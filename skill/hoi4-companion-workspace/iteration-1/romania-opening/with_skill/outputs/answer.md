# Romania 1936 — Opening Focuses and Priorities

## Starting position (why Romania plays the way it does)

Romania (tag **ROM**) starts as a constitutional monarchy under **King Carol II**, ruling party **Partidul Național Liberal** (60% popularity), democratic ideology. It has 12 states, 18.07M core population, and a **20-factory industrial base (11 civilian / 7 military / 2 naval dockyards)**, 40 building slots, 3 research slots (2 more unlockable via focuses). Resource-wise its standout asset is **44 units of oil** — its most valuable strategic resource, since Germany and the USSR will both want it later. It starts with essentially no aluminum/rubber/tungsten/chromium and only 3 steel and 5 coal, so early industry focuses matter a lot.

It starts with two national spirits:
- **King Carol II's Lifestyle** (hedonism, dampens stability)
- **Neutral Foreign Policy** — a genuinely costly spirit: +100% join-faction tension limit, +100% guarantee tension limit, +250% embargo tension limit, and a −100 AI "desire to join/expand a faction" modifier. This is the single biggest early diplomatic drag and several branches exist specifically to shed it.

Romania is bordered by Hungary (west), Poland (north/northwest, friendly), the USSR (north/northeast), Czechoslovakia (north), Bulgaria (southeast, little threat in 1936), and Yugoslavia/Greece (south, allied via the Balkan Pact). It starts with **guarantees given to Czechoslovakia, Yugoslavia, Turkey and Greece** (Little Entente/Balkan Pact) but no faction membership.

## The core early decision: which root focus?

Romania's focus tree has **two mutually exclusive root political focuses**, and this is the first real choice:

| Root focus | What it does | Playstyle |
|---|---|---|
| **Preserve Greater Romania** | National spirit: Preserve Greater Romania (Guarantee tension limit −100%, guarantee cost −50%). Opens branches toward the USSR (A Deal With the Devil → Comintern), Germany (Trade Treaty With Germany → Axis), or the West (Renew Romanian-Polish Alliance → Cordon Sanitaire or Allies). | Diplomatic/alliance-based — pick a great-power patron or build the Cordon Sanitaire with Poland/Czechoslovakia. |
| **Balkans Dominance** | National spirit: Balkans Dominance (war goal justify time −25%, can create factions). Immediately **replaces the Balkan Pact (+25 opinion) with "Incongruous Neighbors" (−30 opinion)** toward Bulgaria and Hungary, and every country that had Balkan Pact with Romania loses it. Opens the regional-conquest branch (Puppet Bulgaria → Secure Greece → Secure the Bosporus → Divide Yugoslavia, plus Align Hungary → Split Czechoslovakia). | Aggressive regional hegemon — subjugate Bulgaria, Greece, Turkey, Yugoslavia, pressure Hungary. |

Both roots unlock the shared **Civil Works / National Defense Industry** economic sub-branch, so the economic path below is available either way — but note Balkans Dominance's conquest focuses have steep gates (e.g. **Puppet Bulgaria** needs 150%+ army strength vs. Bulgaria and 400K+ manpower in divisions, **Secure Greece** needs 500K+ manpower in divisions), so it's a mid-game payoff, not something you complete in month one.

## Recommended first several focuses

### 1. Pick your root focus first
Take **Preserve Greater Romania** if you want to play it safe and choose a faction later (or build the Cordon Sanitaire), or **Balkans Dominance** if you want to grow Romania territorially early. Either way, this is worth taking turn one since it's the gateway to everything else.

### 2. Institute Royal Dictatorship (parallel branch, take early)
Available immediately (only requires: independent, and less than 50% support for PCR/Garda de Fier/FRN). Effects: **+120 political power**, Carol II becomes leader (Non-Aligned, Hedonist), stability −5%, FRN becomes ruling party (no elections), and — importantly — **removes the Neutral Foreign Policy spirit**, shedding those punishing tension-limit penalties. The stability hit is real, but the +120 PP and removal of Neutral Foreign Policy make this a strong early pick regardless of which root you took.

### 3. Civil Works (if you took Preserve Greater Romania) / National Defense Industry (if you took Balkans Dominance)
- **Civil Works** (prereq: Preserve Greater Romania): 2×100% research bonus for Industry.
- **National Defense Industry** (prereq: Balkans Dominance): adds building slots + military factories in Oltenia/Moldova.

Either feeds into the shared industrial sub-branch: **Agrarian Reform → Danubian Transport Network → MALAXA (or Invite Foreign Motor Companies) → Hunedoara Steel Works → Invest in the IAR → Expand Ploiesti Oil Production.**

This last one is the payoff worth planning toward early: **Expand Ploiesti Oil Production** (requires both Hunedoara Steel Works AND Invest In the IAR, plus full control of Muntenia) adds **+24 oil production** to Muntenia — a huge boost on top of Romania's already-strong 44 base oil. After that, **Expand the University of Bucharest** (needs 50+ factories) grants a research slot, and **Exploit the Baita Mines** adds +25% nuclear reactor construction speed if you're going long-game.

### 4. Army Maneuvers and/or Expand the Air Force (parallel, root-level, no prerequisites)
Both are available turn one and don't compete with the political branches for focus slots' prerequisites (only your focus queue is limited to one at a time). Recommended early picks:
- **Army Maneuvers** → **Army War College** (National spirit: Army leader cost −30%, spawns a 2-skill general) → **The ZB-53** (100% research bonus to Infantry Weapons) — cheap, immediate value for a country with only 7 military factories.
- **Expand the Air Force** → grants +25 air experience and 6 extra air base levels split across Muntenia/Moldova — useful since Romania only starts with 7 air base levels.

### 5. Naval branch (lower priority)
**Expand the Galati Shipyards** (root, no prereqs) grants +25 navy experience and 2 extra naval dockyards — worth taking eventually given Romania's 12 naval base levels on the Black Sea, but with only 2 starting dockyards this is a longer-term investment, not a first-five-focuses pick unless you specifically want Black Sea Dominance/Coastal Defense Navy MIOs.

## Non-focus priorities for the opening (generic HOI4 openers, still applicable to Romania)

- **Research slots are the highest-ROI early unlock.** Romania starts with only 3 (Expand the University of Bucharest adds a 4th later; The Royal Foundation, under Institute Royal Dictatorship → Revise the Constitution, also grants a research slot). Prioritize unlocking these over rushing military tech.
- **Production lines:** with only 7 military factories at game start, don't spread across 5+ equipment types — concentrate on Infantry Equipment I for reinforcements plus 1–2 Support Equipment lines (cheap, no strategic resources, reaches its production cap in ~30–50 days) before branching into artillery or armor.
- **Efficiency discipline:** military factories start at 10% efficiency and take ~300+ days to approach the ~50% cap: don't switch an established line unless facing an actual shortfall, since switching cuts efficiency by 70–90%.
- **Stability:** starting at a deficit already (King Carol II's Lifestyle, and −5% more from Institute Royal Dictatorship if you take it) — stability below 50% costs 1% factory output per 1% shortfall, so watch this before it erodes your already-small 20-factory base.
- **Conscription law:** Romania starts democratic (Volunteer Only, 1.5% recruitable population) unless/until you shift ideology via the focus tree; don't try to rush higher conscription levels before war support rises.
- **Don't declare war early without justification** — Balkans Dominance's conquest focuses (Puppet Bulgaria, Secure Greece, etc.) gate on army-strength percentages and manpower-in-divisions thresholds specifically so you build up first; treat war as the payoff of a focus-tree branch, not a turn-30 decision.

## Summary opening order (example, Balkans-Dominance lane)

1. **Balkans Dominance** (root)
2. **Institute Royal Dictatorship** (removes Neutral Foreign Policy, +120 PP)
3. **National Defense Industry** (military factories + building slots)
4. **Army Maneuvers** → **Army War College**
5. **Agrarian Reform** → **Danubian Transport Network** → toward MALAXA/Hunedoara Steel Works/Invest in the IAR → **Expand Ploiesti Oil Production**
6. **Align Hungary** or **Puppet Bulgaria** once army-strength/manpower thresholds are met, to start the regional conquest payoff.

(Swap step 1 for **Preserve Greater Romania** and step 6 for the alliance branch of your choice — Germany/Trade Treaty With Germany, USSR/A Deal With the Devil, or West/Renew Romanian-Polish Alliance — if you'd rather play the diplomatic lane.)
