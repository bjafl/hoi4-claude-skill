# Man the Guns Achievements

The Man the Guns DLC adds **21 achievements** spanning multiple nations and challenge types — diplomatic objectives (forming federations, hosting governments-in-exile), military goals (inflicting casualties, controlling ships and mines), and special scenarios (the 30 Minutes of Hel speedrun challenge). All of them require a **single-player Ironman game with a 1936 start, on regular difficulty or higher** (this is the general HOI4 achievement requirement, reflected in the source file's summary).

Data below is from game version 1.19 (Man the Guns), sourced from the HOI4 wiki's Achievements page.

## United States

**Team America** — Drop a nuclear bomb on Paris.
As a democratic nation, build strategic bombers and fighters and rush nuclear technology. Once Germany controls Paris (typical in historical play), move your planes to airfields in the British Isles, set them to mission over Northern France, achieve air superiority, and deploy the nuke.

**History Repeated Itself** — Trigger the Second American Civil War, and defeat the Confederacy of American States (it must not exist afterward).
Completing either Full Desegregation or Union Representation Act triggers the civil war. Complete the WPA branch and Guarantee the American Dream beforehand to help the US win.

**Georgia On My Mind** — Own and control Georgia (state 365), Georgia (231), and South Georgia (720).
Easiest via collaborating with the Axis: help them capitulate the Allies and Comintern, then grab South Georgia (720) and Georgia (231). South Georgia (720) can alternatively be obtained via the Reaffirm Monroe Doctrine focus.

**To Arms in Dixie!** — Trigger the Boston Rebellion.
Complete America First and hire the fascist advisor, then complete Ally With the Silver Shirts to start the civil war chain. For best results, also complete Work With the Bund and Recruit the Free Corps.

**Arsenal of Democracy** — As the ruling Democratic party, reach more than 300 military factories.
Play historical and build as many military factories as possible. The Arsenal of Democracy focus branch gives free military factories; the WPA branch also boosts industry.

**Underpaid, Undersexed, and Under Eisenhower** — Be in a faction with the United Kingdom, be faction leader, and receive at least 50 expeditionary forces from the UK.
Play historical and join the Allies. You'll need ~50% more factories and fielded manpower than the UK, which happens naturally after a few years. Once faction leader, launch an invasion and request 51 expeditionary forces — the UK accepts if it has roughly 160 divisions.

## United Kingdom

**Last for a Thousand Years** — Complete the Imperial Federation focus.
Can be done as any ideology. Save 300–700 political power before starting Hold the Imperial Conference, and improve relations with the dominions before starting that focus sequence.

**Britzkrieg** — Complete The King's Party focus, be in a faction with Germany, ensure France has capitulated (or doesn't exist), and have full control of Ile de France (state 16).
Follow the focus tree to Alliance with Germany, justify against France, and naval invade Normandy (calling in Germany isn't required). If France is puppeted rather than annexed, the achievement won't fire — annex all cores, territories, and colonies instead.

**Nothing Wrong with our Bloody Ships** — Have at least 9 Battlecruisers.
Battlecruisers use Heavy Ship Hulls with Battlecruiser armor. Build 6 more alongside the UK's starting 3 to reach 9, assigning all dockyards to speed construction.

**William Wallis** — Complete The King's Party focus, make Scotland and the United States subjects of the UK, and install an American monarchy.
Declare war on the dominions (not Canada directly — they'll join the US side). Occupy at least one province each from Australia, New Zealand, and South Africa. Train 72–96 divisions and declare war on the US via Canada in late 1938/early 1939 to exploit their low conscription. After annexing/puppeting all US territory, the decision to install an American Monarchy appears.

**Crush the Dream** — As the ruling Communist party, complete the Crush the Dream focus and make the United States a UK subject.
Switch to communist ideology, complete Crush the Dream, and puppet the US via peace negotiations or diplomacy.

## Netherlands

**Treading the Narrow Path** — Without completing Cave to the Germans, complete Continue the Zuiderzee Works, keep all starting states, and survive to after Jan 1st, 1945.
Losing a majority of a state's victory points counts as losing it (losing a few provinces is fine). Defend your continental territory against German pressure for 9 years.

**Putting the Raid on the Medway to Shame** — Leave the United Kingdom with no capital ships (carriers, battleships, battlecruisers, heavy cruisers).
Fully annexing the UK is easier than hunting down individual capital ships — annexation counts for this achievement.

**Bevrijding** — As the ruling Democratic party, complete the Liberation focus.
Play historical down the Continue the War in Batavia branch. Don't defend your European lands (that branch requires capitulation) — build up your Asian possessions instead. Once Germany is defeated, your homeland returns to you. Complete War on Pacifism to remove the Shell-shocked Spectator of the Great War spirit, then complete Liberation.

## Mexico

**Mi Casa es tu Casa** — Have any country exiled in Mexico.
Reach the Realpolitik focus, create a "Bastion of Liberty" faction, and use your first initiative point to allow hosting exiled governments. Guarantee Finland, accept their Call to Arms when the Winter War starts, improve relations and send an attaché, then invite them to your faction before they white-peace with the Soviets.

**The New Home of the Revolution** — Have Lev Trotsky as your country leader and make the Soviet Union a Mexican subject.
Invite Trotsky via the "Trotsky seeks refuge" event. Follow the rightmost focus branch to go communist and install Trotsky. Join Germany's war against the USSR via Smash the Bureaucrats, then puppet the Soviets at the peace conference.

## Minor/Other Powers

**Assuming Direct Control** — As a non-major power (not US, UK, Germany, Italy, France, USSR, or China), assume faction leadership taken from the UK, Germany, or USSR.
Play as Romania down the Balkan Dominance branch. Build up army and industry, defeat Hungary and Bulgaria, join the Axis but avoid participating in their wars — when they're defeated, you become faction leader.

**Vive la Jeune Ecole** (France) — After Jan 1st, 1945, have at least 150 Destroyers and have researched Modern Ship Torpedo Launcher.
Turn fascist via National Regeneration, join the Axis but stay out of their wars. Rush Modern Ship Torpedo Launcher research, build as many naval dockyards as possible, take focuses that boost research/construction/dockyard output, and assign all dockyards to destroyer production.

**30 Minutes of Hel** (Poland) — Starting Aug 3rd, 1939, inflict more than 1.80M casualties on Germany.
Replace Cavalry Recon with Engineer Companies in your templates and trim excess cavalry to manage supply. Build two stacks of 24 divisions with high-defense generals, construct level-1 land forts and anti-air along a fallback line from Warsaw to Łódź, and research Engineer Company II, Improved Radio, and infantry upgrades. Join the Allies when Germany attacks and hold the line — casualties pile up fast on German offensives, and you'll hit 1.8M well before the Soviets get involved.

**Pride and Extreme Prejudice** (any non-UK country) — Sink the British Pride of the Fleet (HMS Hood).
Japan is the fastest route: choose Purge the Kodoha Faction and justify on the UK or British Malaya. Ask Germany for docking rights and stage your fleet at their nearest port. Use it as a strike force with destroyer patrols around the UK; declare war once justification finishes, accept a German Axis invitation for airfield use, and sink capital ships until the Hood goes down.

**Do You Mined?** (Italy) — Get an enemy at war with you to have more than 999 mines on their coastline.
Remove divisions from Eritrea (550) or Somaliland (559), keeping the ports. Lay mines on the Ethiopian coast with your starting navy until you hit 1000 mines. If you lose all your territory in the process, Ethiopia will typically offer white peace.
