# Man the Guns Achievements — Hearts of Iron IV

*Answered from general/training knowledge only (no wiki or external lookup used). Man the Guns (MTG) was the expansion that reworked the naval system (ship designer, admirals traits, new naval doctrines) and gave the United Kingdom and France full alternate-history national focus trees. The achievements tied to this DLC generally require the DLC to be enabled and are earned by playing as the UK or France down one of the new focus-tree branches, or by exploiting the new naval systems.*

## Achievements associated with Man the Guns

### United Kingdom focus-tree achievements
- **Restore the Monarchy path** – Complete the UK branch that removes the sitting government and restores Edward VIII to the throne. Unlocked by following the monarchist restoration chain of focuses (deposing the establishment, reinstating the king, then completing the follow-on focuses that branch has).
- **Splendid Isolation / neutrality path** – Take the UK down the isolationist branch, deliberately avoiding joining/leading the Allies and staying out of continental entanglements for as long as the path allows, then completing that branch's final focuses.
- **Imperial Federation / Commonwealth unification** – Follow the path that turns the British Empire into a federated/unified state, integrating Dominions (Canada, Australia, South Africa, etc.) more tightly, and complete the tree's endpoint.
- **Naval supremacy as the UK** – Build and maintain the single largest navy in the world (by total tonnage or hull count) while playing the UK, leaning on MTG's new ship designer to min-max fleet composition.

### France focus-tree achievements
- **Authoritarian/fascist France path** – Complete France's Action Française–style branch that installs an authoritarian government, reworking France's political and military structure before/around the historical collapse.
- **Popular Front / leftist France path** – Complete the branch that pushes France toward a stronger left-wing government (Popular Front consolidation) rather than the historical drift toward defeat.
- **Free France / continued resistance path** – As France, avoid the historical 1940 collapse (or recover from it) by completing the relevant chain of focuses that keeps France as a major belligerent rather than falling to Germany and surrendering.

### General naval-rework achievements (usable by any major naval power)
- **Biggest navy in the world** – Achieved by out-tonnaging every other nation using MTG's new ship modules/hull designer, typically requires the DLC active since the redesign is what the achievement is testing.
- **Admiral/ship-design flavor achievement** – Something along the lines of designing and fielding a fleet built entirely (or mostly) around custom hull/module combinations rather than the pre-MTG stock ship types, reflecting the new "build your own warship" system.

## How to unlock them (general approach)
1. **Enable the Man the Guns DLC.** Achievement-locked content requires the expansion to be active; several MTG achievements also require you *not* to use debug/console commands and to play with default/ironman-compatible settings (no unsupported mods) — Steam achievements in HOI4 are typically disabled if achievement-incompatible mods or the console are used during that session.
2. **Pick United Kingdom or France** for the focus-tree-specific achievements, since MTG's headline content is the alt-history rework for those two majors.
3. **Follow one specific branch of the reworked tree to its end** rather than mixing branches — most of these achievements trigger off completing the terminal focus(es) of a particular path (monarchist restoration, isolationism, imperial federation for UK; authoritarian, popular front, or resistance/continuation for France).
4. **For the naval ones**, use the new ship designer to build custom hulls, keep dockyards pumping out ships, and track total fleet tonnage against the AI's fleets — you're aiming to have the largest navy in the world at some point in the game, or to field a fleet dominated by your own custom designs.
5. **Play in Ironman with no incompatible mods enabled** — as with all HOI4 achievements, using the console, achievement-incompatible mods, or non-Ironman saves in a way that disables achievements will prevent these from popping even if you meet the in-game condition.
