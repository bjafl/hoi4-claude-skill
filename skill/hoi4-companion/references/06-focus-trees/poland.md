## Summary

Poland gets a unique national focus tree as part of the free Poland: United and Ready DLC, updated with No Step Back. The tree spans 10 major branches covering industrial development, naval policy, military preparation, constitutional choices, and ideological paths. Poland must navigate between Sanation factions, peasant unrest, and potential monarchy restoration, while preparing defenses against historical threats from Germany and the Soviet Union.

See [Poland](../05-countries/poland.md) for the country's 1936 starting profile. See [National Focus and Continuous Focus](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md) for generic/continuous focuses.
