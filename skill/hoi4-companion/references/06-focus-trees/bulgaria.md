## Summary

Bulgaria receives a unique national focus tree with the Battle for the Bosporus DLC. The tree contains two main branches: the economic/military "Acquire Modern Tools" branch focusing on industrial development, and the political "Oppose the Royal Dictatorship/Power to the Tsar" branch allowing Bulgaria to shift ideologies and align with major powers. See [Bulgaria](../05-countries/bulgaria.md) for the country's 1936 starting profile.
