## Summary

Germany's national focus tree is one of the most extensive and historically detailed in the game. The main tree (base game) consists of 6 major branches emphasizing military development, economic growth, and diplomatic expansion. The industrial/historical branches (base game, expansion across a second page of content) offer deep military doctrines (Blitzkrieg vs. Bewegungskrieg), airforce specialization, and naval strategy. The opposition branches (requires Götterdämmerung DLC) unlock civil war paths to communism or monarchy, bypassing the fascist historical route entirely. See [Germany](../05-countries/germany.md) for the country's 1936 starting profile.

**Generic and continuous focus information:** See [01-mechanics/national-focus.md](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md) for generic/continuous focuses — this file documents only Germany's unique national tree branches.

---

## Main Historical Path (Base Game)

Germany's base-game tree branches into three ideological/strategic routes via the **Remilitarize the Rhineland** branch:

1. **Von Neurath path** (peaceful/cautious): Integrates Czechoslovakia diplomatically, focuses on alliances
2. **Von Ribbentrop path** (aggressive/aggressive expansion): Prioritizes conquest, enables Reichskommissariat creation, and anti-Soviet positioning
3. **Communist/Monarchist paths** (requires Götterdämmerung): Accessible only after completing opposition civil-war branches

## Key Numbers & Modifiers (Exact as Sourced)

- **Political Power costs:** Standard 70 days = 70 PP (day 1: −1 PP per day).
- **Command Power gains:** +25.0 typical for major branches; +15.0 for sub-focuses.
- **Army/Air/Navy experience:** +25 typical for primary branch starts, +15 for development focuses.
- **Research bonuses:** 1× 50% (1 research point at 50% speed reduction), 2× 25% (2 points at 25% each), 1× 100% (1 research at no speed penalty), 2× 100% (2 researches), etc.
- **Morale/Stability:** ±5.00%, ±10.00%, ±15.00% common; Some focuses grant ±2.50%.
- **Construction/Production speed:** +5.0%, +10.0%, +15.0%, +20.0%, +25.00% typical ranges.
- **Combat modifiers:** +5.0%, +10.0%, +15.0% attack/defense/breakthrough; −5.0%, −10.0% penalties.
- **AoT reduction:** −1.0 year typical (advances tech by 1 full year), −2.0 years available for powerful focuses.
- **Doctrine cost reduction:** −10%, −15%, −25%, −50% common for land/air/naval doctrines.

## Strategic Notes

1. **Early game (1936–1937):** Prioritize Develop Modern Maneuver Warfare or Expanding the Luftwaffe to unlock army/air bonuses; simultaneously pursue Prioritize Economic Growth or Four-Year Plan for industrial strength.
2. **Mid-game (1937–1939):** Complete either Blitzkrieg (armor) or Bewegungskrieg (speed) path; secure Remilitarize Rhineland branch to access diplomatic choices; build Kriegsmarine via Plan Z or Trade Interdiction depending on strategy.
3. **Late game (1939+):** Access Totaler Krieg for total mobilization; leverage completed focuses to unlock Wunderwaffe projects; if Götterdämmerung: consider opposition branches for alt-history communist/monarchist routes.
4. **Opposition branches:** Civil war diverts army to internal conflict; requires careful timing if committing to Oppose Hitler or Rally the Wehrmacht after 1936.
5. **Götterdämmerung expansion:** Rally the Wehrmacht path opens communist/socialist routes; Oppose Hitler (base game) path locked by Götterdämmerung; two branches are mutually exclusive at the start.

## Abbreviations & Terms

- **AoT:** Ahead-of-Time penalty reduction (technology research speed advantage)
- **MIO:** Military Industrial Organization research bonus
- **Wunderwaffe:** Wonder Weapons special projects (nuclear, rockets, advanced tanks)
- **Reichskommissariat:** Nazi occupation/colonial administration (unlocked via focus decisions)
- **Politbüro:** Communist ruling body modifier (granted in communist branches)
- **MEFO Bills / Economy of Conquest / Recovering Economy / Wirtschaftswunder:** Four mutually-exclusive economy law modifiers; many focuses reference modifying these.
- **Mastery:** Doctrine subdoctrine bonuses granted temporarily (e.g., +10.00% mastery for 240 days to all Armor tracks).
- **Bypassed focus:** Automatically completes if condition met (e.g., Motorization Effort if Truck already researched).
