## Summary

Iraq gets a unique national focus tree with the Graveyard of Empires expansion. The tree comprises five main branches covering industrial decoupling, oil nationalization, military building, sunni political dominance, and Kurdish identity, plus the joint Saadabad Pact with Iran, Turkey, and Afghanistan. See 01-mechanics/national-focus.md for generic/continuous focuses.

See [Iraq](../05-countries/iraq.md) for the country's 1936 starting profile.

## Overview

The Iraqi focus tree is divided into six major branches:

- **Decouple from the Pound** — Industrial development, dams, irrigation, and currency control.
- **Nationalize/Expand Anglo-Iraqi Oil** — Mutually exclusive oil policy branches.
- **Royal Iraqi Army** — Military capability modernization.
- **Ensure Sunni Dominance** — Political paths for monarchy, nationalism, democracy, and pan-Arabism.
- **Promote Kurdish Identity** — Transform Iraq into Kurdistan.
- **The Saadabad Pact** (joint) — Regional cooperation for Middle Eastern dominance.
