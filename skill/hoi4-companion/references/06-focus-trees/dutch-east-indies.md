## Summary

The Dutch East Indies (Indonesia) unique focus tree (requires Thunder at Our Gates DLC) follows a branching path centered on independence and nation-building. When controlled by the Netherlands as a subject, the tree provides options for revolutionary activities leading to the **Declaration of Independence** and formation of the **Republic of Indonesia**. After independence, Indonesia faces political choices among democracy, socialism, communism, fascism, and guided democracy, plus military development (army/navy branches) and economic policies. See [Dutch East Indies](../05-countries/dutch-east-indies.md) for the country's 1936 starting profile.

> Note: This tree documents the Indonesian branch that unlocks upon independence. The Netherlands retains control of the Dutch East Indies as a puppet subject unless the independence path is pursued.

## Independence & Revolution Branch

The core of the Indonesian focus tree revolves around declaring independence from Dutch rule. The Underground Revolution path and Seize the Opportunity path both lead to The Declaration of Independence, which triggers the formation of the Republic of Indonesia and begins a National Revolution (low-intensity conflict). All subsequent focuses are locked to the Republic of Indonesia.

## Overview & Strategy Notes

The Dutch East Indies tree is fundamentally about **independence and post-revolution state-building**. Key branching decisions:

1. **Independence Path:** The underground revolution path is locked until Volksraad debate concludes; The Jewel in the Crown is an alternative historical continuation as Netherlands puppet (base-game generic tree).

2. **Military Structure:** Choice between Sudirman (mobility/training) and Oerip (assault/quality) determines army doctrine; Navy is split between captured ships (cheap navy) vs. future shipbuilding (divert funds).

3. **Post-Independence Politics:** Three competing narratives—democratic (fair elections, multiple PMs), authoritarian (Guided Democracy + militarism), and communist (Tan Malaka's coup)—each with cascading economic/military effects.

4. **Militia vs. Army:** Freedom Fighter militia cap grows via Laskar branch; formal TKR grows via military branches. Both are essential during the 10-year National Revolution.

5. **Economic Choices:** Align with Allies (trade/faction), pursue export economy restructuring (self-management), or orient toward militarism (war effort). Consumer goods factor varies sharply by ideology.

6. **Territorial Expansion:** Destroy Pacific Colonialism unlocks limited wars against British Malaya; The Timor Issue creates a war goal against Portuguese Timor (East Timor).
