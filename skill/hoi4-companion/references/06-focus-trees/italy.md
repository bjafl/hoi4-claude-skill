## Summary

Italy, as one of the seven major powers, has a unique national focus tree with eight primary branches and fifteen sub-branches. The tree is heavily shaped by the outcome of the Second Italo-Ethiopian War, which determines political and military paths ranging from fascism to democracy, monarchy, and communism. Key branches cover colonial expansion, industrial development, military modernization, and international relations. See [Italy](../05-countries/italy.md) for the country's 1936 starting profile.

For shared/continuous focuses and generic focus mechanics, see [National Focus and Continuous Focus](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md).

## Overview of branches

| Branch | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Ethiopian War Logistics | Colonial infrastructure, naval bases, units in East Africa |
| Italian Highways | Homeland industry, research slot, regional development (north/south) |
| Army Primacy | Army experience, unit modifiers, Regio Esercito/Esercito Italiano spirit |
| Air Innovations | Air experience, airbases, Regia Aeronautica/Aeronautica Italiana spirit |
| Naval Power Projection | Naval experience, dockyards, Regia Marina/Marina Italiana spirit |
| Abyssinian Fiasco/Solid Progress/Struggle in Ethiopia | Political path (fascist/democrat/communist/monarchist), intelligence agency, Emperor title |
| Italian Social Republic | Civil war (fascist puppet path); available via Balance of Power |
| Italian Liberation War | Civil war (communist/monarchist puppet path); requires By Blood Alone DLC |
