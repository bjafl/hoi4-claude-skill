## Summary

Ethiopia has a unique focus tree available with the **By Blood Alone** DLC expansion. The tree comprises six main branches and fifteen sub-branches, four of which are hidden and triggered by in-game events (government-in-exile formation, ideology shifts). Major themes include modernizing the military (Ecole Militaire Haile Selassie 1er), industrial and infrastructure development (Develop Shewa), defensive measures against Italy (The Second Italo-Ethiopian War), and three ideological paths (Monarchist, Fascist, Communist) with distinct political advisors and military bonuses. Ethiopia starts with handicaps in military tradition and naval capacity but can progressively upgrade them through focus choices.

See [Ethiopia](../05-countries/ethiopia.md) for the country's 1936 starting profile.

**For generic/continuous focuses applicable to all countries, see [National Focus and Continuous Focus](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md).**
