## Summary

The Chinese Soviet Republic's national focus tree varies by DLC availability. The modern version (requires 'No Compromise, No Surrender') features a sprawling single-branch tree centered on power struggles in the Politburo between Mao Zedong and the 28½ Bolsheviks faction, with sub-branches for military modernization, industrial development, and diplomatic alignment. The earlier base-game version provides a simpler three-branch structure covering internal administration, land redistribution policy, foreign investment, and military preparedness. Both paths emphasize securing control over Chinese territory, managing the Japanese threat, and navigating relations with the Soviet Union and Nationalist China.

See [Chinese Soviet Republic](../05-countries/chinese-soviet-republic.md) for the country's 1936 starting profile.

See [National Focus mechanics](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md) for details on focus duration, cost, prerequisites, and bypasses.
