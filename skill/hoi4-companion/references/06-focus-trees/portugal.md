## Summary

Portugal's unique focus tree (requires La Résistance) contains five main branches and five sub-branches that permit political transformation across all ideologies, colonial empire management, industrial and military buildup, and intervention in or neutrality toward the Spanish Civil War. The tree offers monarchist, communist, democratic, and fascist paths with varied Spanish Civil War responses (intervention on either side, strict neutrality, or royal wedding alliance). Key decisions branch on ideology: Estado Novo (fascist) or Popular Front (communist) form the primary ideological split.

**See [Portugal](../05-countries/portugal.md) for the country's 1936 starting profile.**

For generic and continuous focuses common to all countries, see [National Focus and Continuous Focus](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md).

## Popular Front / Estado Novo branch

**Primary ideological split.** Two mutually exclusive root focuses:
- **Popular Front**: +5% communism, +5% democracy; national spirit *Internationalism* (daily communism support +0.10); PP +100; Maria Lamas (political advisor) dailies +0.05 democracy; removes Fernando dos Santos Costa, Rolao Preto, José Adriano Pequito Rebelo.
- **Estado Novo** (default 1936 path): consumer goods −1%, civilian factory construction +10%; PP +100; Júlio Botelho Moniz (army reformer) army experience +10%; removes Álvaro Cunhal.

Branches lead to five distinct sub-trees: Nation in Arms (domestic communist), Support the Spanish Republic (interventionist communist), Strict Neutrality (democratic pro-Allies), Support the Spanish Nationalists (fascist/non-aligned), A Royal Wedding (monarchist). Only one Spanish path can be taken (all mutually exclusive at the highest level).
