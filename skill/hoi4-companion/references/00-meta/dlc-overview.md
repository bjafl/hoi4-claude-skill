Hearts of Iron IV has received 8 expansions, 5 country packs, 1 focus pack, and 1 theater pack over its development lifecycle. This index catalogs major feature additions and points to where deep mechanical documentation lives elsewhere in this corpus.

## Waking the Tiger (Patch 1.5)

Released March 8, 2018. First full-fledged expansion.

- **Chinese National Focus Trees** (Nationalist and Communist China with political support point system) — See `05-countries/china.md`
- **German and Japanese Focus Tree Reworks** (alternate history paths for major powers) — See `05-countries/germany.md`, `05-countries/japan.md`
- **General Traits and Command Power** (trait system for flexible command abilities) — See `02-land-warfare/generals-and-commanders.md`
- **Decisions and Missions System** (new way to spend political power on special projects) — See `01-mechanics/decisions-and-missions.md`
- **Chain of Command** (field marshals commanding generals for stacked bonuses) — See `02-land-warfare/organization-and-command.md`

## Together for Victory (Patch 1.3)

Released December 15, 2016. First country pack (now free, base game post-1.14.3).

- **Commonwealth Dominion Focus Trees** (Canada, Australia, New Zealand, British Raj, South Africa) — See `05-countries/canada.md`, `05-countries/australia.md`, `05-countries/british-raj.md`
- **Autonomy System** (subject state degrees of independence) — See `03-diplomacy/autonomy-and-subjects.md`
- **Lend-Lease Expansion** (improved resource sharing with allies) — See `03-diplomacy/trade-and-lend-lease.md`
- **Spearhead Battleplan Command** (grand encirclement and breakthrough mechanics) — See `02-land-warfare/battleplans.md`
- **Technology Sharing** (Commonwealth tech catch-up) — See `01-mechanics/technology.md`

## Death or Dishonor (Patch 1.4)

Released June 14, 2017. Second country pack (now free, base game post-1.14.3).

- **Eastern European Focus Trees** (Hungary, Romania, Czechoslovakia, Yugoslavia) — See `05-countries/hungary.md`, `05-countries/romania.md`, `05-countries/czechoslovakia.md`, `05-countries/yugoslavia.md`
- **Equipment Conversion** (repurposing captured or outdated equipment) — See `01-mechanics/equipment-and-production.md`
- **New Subject Levels** (Reichskommissariat and other puppet control types) — See `03-diplomacy/autonomy-and-subjects.md`
- **New Diplomatic Interactions** (military tech licensing and sales) — See `03-diplomacy/trade-and-lend-lease.md`

## Man the Guns (Patch 1.6)

Released February 28, 2019. Second expansion (now free, base game post-1.17).

- **Ship Designer** (modular naval vessel construction with component research) — See `04-naval-warfare/ship-designer.md`
- **Admiral Traits and Skills** (trait-based naval leadership) — See `04-naval-warfare/admirals-and-captains.md`
- **USA, UK, Netherlands, Mexico Focus Trees** (with alternate historical paths) — See `05-countries/united-states.md`, `05-countries/united-kingdom.md`, `05-countries/netherlands.md`, `05-countries/mexico.md`
- **Governments in Exile** (exiled governments providing manpower and troops) — See `03-diplomacy/autonomy-and-subjects.md`
- **Naval Mines and Naval Treaties** (mine laying/sweeping and treaty compliance) — See `04-naval-warfare/naval-operations.md`
- **Fuel Resource** (consumption by mechanical units) — See `01-mechanics/resources.md`

## La Résistance (Patch 1.9)

Released February 25, 2020. Third expansion.

- **French Focus Tree Rework** (Free France and Vichy paths, monarchist alternative) — See `05-countries/france.md`
- **Spanish Civil War Expansion** (Republican and Nationalist Spain focus trees with wider conflict potential) — See `05-countries/spain.md`
- **Portuguese Focus Tree** (navigation of world war pressure and Spanish conflict involvement) — See `05-countries/portugal.md`
- **Espionage System** (intelligence agency with spies performing specialized missions) — See `06-espionage/espionage-and-operations.md`
- **Support Resistance Operations** (assisting allied resistance movements) — See `06-espionage/espionage-and-operations.md`
- **Collaboration Governments** (preparing collaborators to fill power vacuums) — See `06-espionage/espionage-and-operations.md`
- **Code Cracking and Recon Units** (scout planes and armored cars for intelligence gathering) — See `06-espionage/espionage-and-operations.md`

## Battle for the Bosporus (Patch 1.10)

Released October 15, 2020. Third country pack.

- **Balkan and Turkish Focus Trees** (Bulgaria, Greece, Turkey with alternate paths) — See `05-countries/bulgaria.md`, `05-countries/greece.md`, `05-countries/turkey.md`

## No Step Back (Patch 1.11)

Released November 23, 2021. Fourth expansion.

- **Soviet Focus Tree** (largest in game; historical and alternate paths including monarchism) — See `05-countries/soviet-union.md`
- **Polish and Baltic Focus Trees** (Poland, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania with shared alternate histories) — See `05-countries/poland.md`, `05-countries/baltic-states.md`
- **Army Officer Corps** (general staff system with personnel management) — See `02-land-warfare/organization-and-command.md`
- **Army Spirits** (unit-type specific bonuses and army characteristics) — See `02-land-warfare/army-spirits.md`
- **Tank Designer** (modular tank construction with speed/firepower/armor/production tradeoffs) — See `02-land-warfare/tank-designer.md`
- **Supply System Overhaul** (logistics with scorched earth tactics and special supply units) — See `01-mechanics/supply-and-logistics.md`
- **Railway Guns** (specialized artillery for fortified positions) — See `02-land-warfare/equipment.md`

## By Blood Alone (Patch 1.12)

Released September 27, 2022. Fifth expansion.

- **Italian Focus Tree Rework** (Mussolini's path, monarchy restoration, Communist revolution, democracy) — See `05-countries/italy.md`
- **Ethiopian Focus Tree** (defeat Italian invader or resist foreign conquest with exile abilities) — See `05-countries/ethiopia.md`
- **Swiss Focus Tree** (neutrality balancing between France, Germany, Italy) — See `05-countries/switzerland.md`
- **New Peace Conference System** (demilitarization, capital ship bidding, reparations claiming) — See `03-diplomacy/peace-conferences.md`
- **Aircraft Designer** (interceptor to bomber spectrum with customizable traits) — See `03-air-warfare/aircraft-designer.md`
- **Unit Medals and Division Commanders** (tracking division accomplishments and awarding honors) — See `02-land-warfare/organization-and-command.md`
- **Embargo Diplomatic Interaction** (prevent enemy trade with sufficient world tension) — See `03-diplomacy/trade-and-lend-lease.md`

## Arms Against Tyranny (Patch 1.13)

Released October 10, 2023. Sixth expansion.

- **Nordic Focus Trees** (Finland, Norway, Denmark, Sweden with alt-history paths emphasizing neutrality and defense) — See `05-countries/finland.md`, `05-countries/norway.md`, `05-countries/denmark.md`, `05-countries/sweden.md`
- **Military Industrial Organizations (MIO)** (weapons manufacturer specialization improving production and research) — See `01-mechanics/production-and-industry.md`
- **International Market** (arms trading and neutral nation profiteering from war) — See `03-diplomacy/trade-and-lend-lease.md`
- **Support Company Specialization** (equipment support unit customization) — See `02-land-warfare/support-companies.md`
- **Special Forces Doctrines** (specialized division training and deployment) — See `02-land-warfare/special-forces.md`
- **Joint Focus Trees** (shared alternate history branches between nations) — See `05-countries/` (nordic nations)

## Trial of Allegiance (Patch 1.14)

Released March 7, 2024. Fourth country pack.

- **South American Focus Trees** (Brazil, Argentina, Chile with continental hegemony and alliance options) — See `05-countries/brazil.md`, `05-countries/argentina.md`, `05-countries/chile.md`
- **Paraguay and Uruguay Content** (minor focus trees and formable/releasable nations) — See `05-countries/south-america.md`

## Götterdämmerung (Patch 1.15)

Released November 14, 2024. Seventh expansion.

- **German Focus Tree Rework** (expanded alternate histories: monarchist, democratic, communist alongside historical fascist path) — See `05-countries/germany.md`
- **Austrian and Hungarian Focus Trees** (restored independence, Austro-Hungarian Empire reform, Reichskommissariat mechanics) — See `05-countries/austria.md`, `05-countries/hungary.md`
- **Belgian and Congo Focus Trees** (Belgian choice on German supremacy, Congo's colonial economy and independence paths) — See `05-countries/belgium.md`, `05-countries/congo.md`
- **Special Projects System** (scientific facilities and scientist assignment for nuclear, aircraft, rocketry, tanks, artillery, naval, electronics research) — See `01-mechanics/special-projects.md`
- **Raids Mechanic** (targeted strikes on ports, industries, research facilities, resources, dams with bombing/commando/paratroop options) — See `03-air-warfare/strategic-bombing.md`
- **Landmarks and Dams** (map-visible strategic locations affecting operations) — See `01-mechanics/map-and-terrain.md`

## Graveyard of Empires (Patch 1.16)

Released March 4, 2025. Fifth country pack.

- **Indian Raj Focus Tree** (independence struggle with East India Company revival, Princely States, Pakistan/Bangladesh formation) — See `05-countries/british-raj.md`
- **Iranian Focus Tree** (great power potential resisting Soviet and British imperial pressure, Persian Empire restoration) — See `05-countries/iran.md`
- **Iraqi Focus Tree** (client state escape and managing ethnic/religious factions with oil resources) — See `05-countries/iraq.md`
- **Afghan Focus Tree** (isolation and neutrality versus rapid industrialization and mountain power) — See `05-countries/afghanistan.md`

## No Compromise, No Surrender (Patch 1.17)

Released November 20, 2025. Eighth expansion.

- **Japanese Focus Tree Rework** (Pacific seization with multiple ideological endstates) — See `05-countries/japan.md`
- **Chinese Focus Trees** (Communist China and Nationalist China unified under single nation with dual paths) — See `05-countries/china.md`
- **Philippines Focus Tree** (island defense, invasion resistance, independence and resource leverage) — See `05-countries/philippines.md`
- **New Military Doctrines System** (reworked doctrine mechanics with nation-specific paths) — See `02-land-warfare/doctrines.md`
- **Expanded Factions System** (greater alliance control with defined goals and rules) — See `03-diplomacy/factions.md`
- **Naval Special Projects and Carrier Mechanics** (improved naval research and aircraft carrier behavior) — See `04-naval-warfare/naval-combat.md`

## Peace for Our Time (Patch 1.18)

Released April 22, 2026. First focus pack.

- **Czechoslovakian Focus Tree Rework** (Democratic, Communist, Monarchist, Fascist paths facing overwhelming odds) — See `05-countries/czechoslovakia.md`
- **Divided Nation Mechanic** (managing faction divisions and political stability) — See `01-mechanics/politics-and-stability.md`
- **Army Readiness and Balance of Power** (military support requirements for government stability) — See `02-land-warfare/organization-and-command.md`
- **Arms Industry Specialization** (weapon production and export focus) — See `01-mechanics/production-and-industry.md`

## Thunder at Our Gates (Patch 1.19)

Released June 11, 2026. First theater pack.

- **Australian Focus Tree** (Allied support versus domestic political instability and independence) — See `05-countries/australia.md`
- **Siam Focus Tree** (rival manipulation, great power exploitation, Southeast Asian empire) — See `05-countries/siam.md`
- **Indonesia Focus Tree** (Dutch rule continuance, solo independence, or new patron-seeking) — See `05-countries/indonesia.md`
- **Military HQs** (general deployment with local command synergies and proximity bonuses) — See `02-land-warfare/organization-and-command.md`
- **Ship Captains and Promotions** (named naval leaders with skill progression and admiral promotion) — See `04-naval-warfare/admirals-and-captains.md`
