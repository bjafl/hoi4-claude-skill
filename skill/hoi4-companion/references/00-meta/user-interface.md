Summary: Hearts of Iron IV's interface is divided into three main areas: the top information bar displaying key national metrics, the left-side national management menu, and the right-side military information. The menu bar provides hotkey access to all primary systems (government, decisions, research, trade, construction, production, recruitment, and logistics). Understanding this layout is essential for managing all aspects of country and military operations.

## Top information bar

The top of the screen displays critical national metrics from left to right.

**Political power**
  Accumulated political capital spent on national focuses, hiring advisors, taking decisions, changing laws, and diplomatic actions. Each country gains +2 political power per day (modified by nation leader traits, advisors, stability, national spirits, national focus selection, and events).

**Stability**
  Measure of government support on a scale (average 50%). Values above 50% yield bonuses to factory/dockyard output, political power gain, and consumer goods. Below 50% produces penalties. Also affects occupied territory resistance gain.

**War support**
  Measure of public support for war (0–100%). Directly affects mobilization speed, command power gain, attack and defense on core territory. Defensive wars provide positive buff; offensive wars negative buff. Influenced by bombing, enemy convoy raiding, operative propaganda, and hero casualties.

**Manpower**
  Available men for military units, based on controlled territories (core vs. occupied giving different yields) and affected by conscription laws, national spirits, and units under construction.

**Factories**
  Three separate numbers: military factories, naval dockyards, and civilian factories available for new orders.

**Fuel**
  Generated from excess oil up to storage capacity (increased via silos and infrastructure). Generation affected by oil availability (domestic and traded), research, and economy laws.

**Logistics Fulfillment**
  Capacity representation showing satisfaction of supply lines. Insufficient trains, trucks, or convoy ships result in reduced unit supply and penalties.

**Convoys**
  Number of available transports. Each overseas strategic resource trade requires convoy allocation. Ocean/sea crossings for land units also require transport allocation.

**Command power**
  Ability to bypass chain of command for military operations and general promotion. Capped at 200 (increase cap via doctrines and national spirits).

**Experience (Army, Navy, Air)**
  Accumulated by units in combat or exercise. Army experience: unit designer modifications. Air experience: aircraft modifications (speed, firepower). Naval experience: ship modifications or ship designer. All three: 100 XP for 100% research speed bonus in respective doctrines.

**Nuclear weapons**
  Icon with number appears when nuclear research is completed.

**World tension**
  Globe icon (upper right) with percentage indicating global war proximity. Increased by diplomatic/military actions, historical events, declarations of war. Clickable for event list and ongoing wars.

## Main menu bar (hotkeys)

Nine icons (10 with La Résistance DLC) provide direct access to primary menus.

| Hotkey | Q | Shift+Q | Shift+W | W | E | R | T | Y | U | I | Shift+E |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Menu | Government | Decisions | Intelligence agency | Research | International Market | Trade | Construction | Production | Recruit & Deploy | Logistics | Officer Corps |

### Government (Q)

Displays national status: leader portrait, political system and ideology support levels, national spirits, and three modification rows for laws and advisors. Includes buttons for managing occupied territories, exiled governments, and subjects.

**National Focus:** Most take 70 days at 1 political power per day. Form trees controlling country direction (industrial, technological, or diplomatic growth). Mutually exclusive paths exist (e.g., German Reich cannot ally both China and Japan simultaneously).

**Laws & Government:**
- Conscription law: Affects manpower availability, factory/dockyard output, construction speed, training time.
- Trade law: Affects resource market access, factory/dockyard output, construction speed, research time.
- Economy law: Affects consumer goods factory dedication, manpower availability, factory construction speed, conversion speed.

**Advisors:** Up to three political advisors hired for bonuses. Design companies (tank, ship, aircraft, material, industrial) reduce research time, increase equipment production, or boost combat abilities. Theorists reduce doctrine/rocket/nuclear research costs or increase armor max speed 10%. Military staff: Chief of Army/Navy/Air Force and up to three high command members.

### Decisions (Shift+Q)

Gavel-marked button opens Events and Decisions menu. Enable special projects (ideology switching, border wars, banning parties, press censorship, resource excavation). Typical cost: 25–150 political power. Middle ground between focus trees and events.

### Intelligence Agency (Shift+W)

Eye-marked button. Recruit operatives (spies), expand intelligence capabilities, conduct espionage missions.

### Research (W)

Flask-marked button. Two to four research slots available (additional slots unlocked via national focus). All trees marked on historical timeline. Researching before historical date takes more days; at/after date takes fewer (penalty modified via national focus).

**Nine research categories:**

- **Infantry:** Better weapons/equipment, specialized division types (mobile/paratroopers/marines/mountain/motorized rocket artillery/amphibious vehicles). Armored cars require La Résistance.
- **Support Companies:** Support equipment and companies (engineers, recon, military police, maintenance, field hospitals, logistics, signal). Train engines and railway guns require No Step Back.
- **Armor:** Light/medium/heavy/super heavy/modern tanks and variants (tank destroyer, self-propelled gun, self-propelled anti-air). No Step Back adds tank chassis and module technologies.
- **Artillery:** Anti-tank, anti-air, rocket artillery. No Step Back adds cannon module technologies.
- **Naval:** Destroyer/Cruiser/Heavy Ship/Carrier/Submarine hull technologies and ship modules.
- **Naval support:** Naval weaponry, training, transport ships, mine-warfare.
- **Air:** Fighter, heavy fighter, close air support, naval bomber, tactical bomber, strategic bomber (all with carrier variants and jet versions).
- **Engineering:** Electrical engineering (radio, radar, computers), atomic energy (reactors, nuclear bombs), experimental rockets (rocket engines, jet engines).
- **Industry:** Military factories/dockyards output/efficiency, construction speed, resource extraction, synthetic refineries.

### Diplomacy (R; may display as International Market with button below)

Hand-marked button. List of nations filterable by region (Africa, Asia, Europe, Middle East, North America, Oceania, South America), ideology (Communist, Democracy, Fascist, Non-Aligned), major countries, or faction (Allies, Axis, Comintern). Shows leader portrait, relationship flags, and available actions (limited for democracies below high world tension). Hover options for descriptions and conditions.

### Trade (R)

Box/exchange-arrows-marked button. Tabs and sortable columns list resources (oil, aluminum, rubber, tungsten, steel, chromium), convoy requirements, and trading partners (filterable by region, neighbor, subject). Resources traded in units of 8. Each trade (except overland) requires ≥1 convoy. Every 8 units imported costs 1 civilian factory (power transferred to exporting nation). Tip: trading with puppets yields >8 resources per civilian factory.

### Construction (T)

Excavator-marked button. Assign civilian factories to state improvements: infrastructure, factories, defenses. Maximum 15 factories per project.

**State structures** (own level/max per state, no shared slot cost):
- Infrastructure: Supply rating, unit movement speed, shared building construction speed, resource output.
- Air base: Aircraft capacity/housing.
- Anti-air: Defense from enemy strategic bombing.
- Radar station: Detect/engage enemy air wings, detect enemy fleets, gain intel on nearby enemy troops.

**Shared structures** (limited by state slots, expandable via Industry research):
- Military factory: Weapons, equipment, planes, vehicles.
- Civilian factory: Industrial capacity.
- Synthetic refinery: Synthetic fuel/rubber.
- Naval dockyard: Ships, convoys (coastal states only).
- Rocket site: Rocket production/launches.
- Nuclear reactor: Nuclear weapons.

**Province buildings:**
- Naval base: Fleet station/repair, supply/resource convoys. Level increases supply capacity/throughput.
- Land fort: -15% attack per level (multi-direction reduces effect).
- Coastal fort: -15% naval invasion attack per level.

### Production (Y)

Wrench-marked button. Assign military factories/dockyards to equipment, vehicles, aircraft, ships. More factories = more output. Output depends on strategic resource availability and efficiency cap. New lines take time reaching peak efficiency; adding factories reduces efficiency. Can be improved via Industrial research.

### Recruit & Deploy (U)

Tank-marked button (army planner). Choose division types trained. Equipment/vehicles from production menu fill these divisions. Army experience designs new templates or edits existing ones. Set reinforcement priorities (default: equal weight with new units).

### Logistics (I)

Paper/pen-marked button. Overall summary of equipment stockpiles, shortages, missing strategic resources from production lines.

**Logistics screen columns:**
- Average production efficiency (yellow bar shows per-type average).
- Equipment type: name and picture.
- Amount produced: per day.
- Status: green bar showing production-to-need ratio, slider indicating surplus (left) or deficit (right).
- Need: daily requirement.
- Balance: surplus (+Amount) or deficit (-Amount).
- Stored: green for stockpile or red for shortage.
- Resources: current resource consumption for production.

Bottom summary: Military factory percentage in use, naval dockyard percentage, six resource icons (oil, rubber, steel, aluminum, tungsten, chromium) showing surplus (green) or deficit (red).

**Stockpile screen:** Accessed by clicking equipment on logistics screen. Graph shows 30-day, 12-month, or 7-year stockpile change (red line). Mini overview shows equipment variants and stored amounts per variant.

### Officer Corps (Shift+E; La Résistance adds to hotkey bar)

Hat-marked button. Summary of command power allocation. Manage military leadership. Unlock military spirits, military doctrines, and preferred tactics.
