## Summary

Comprehensive roster of in-game commanders, military advisors, theorists, and political advisors available across all countries. Includes skill levels (1–5), trait-based bonuses, military high command specialists, military chief roles, doctrine experts, and political advisors with their effects. Trait mechanics are defined separately in Officer Corps documentation; this file is the who's-who and availability reference.

## Generals and Admirals

All countries have access to predefined commanders (generals/admirals). Each commander has:
- **Skill level** (1–5): Base command power and combat effectiveness
- **Trait stats**: ATK (attack), DEF (defense), PLN (planning), LOG (logistics) ranging 1–6
- **Traits**: Special abilities granting combat bonuses and mission modifiers

## Availability & Recruitment

- **Commanders** spawn pre-assigned to countries; additional commanders can be recruited via military XP spending.
- **Military High Command** are selected individually from country pool (one per combat domain).
- **Military Chiefs** (one per branch: Army, Navy, Airforce) gain XP and can be replaced/promoted.
- **Theorists** unlock via research or political decisions; some locked behind technology trees or DLC.
- **Political Advisors** vary by country and political alignment; recruitment cost based on political power and alignment distance.

Consult [Officer Corps and Commanders mechanics](../01-mechanics/officer-corps-and-commanders.md) for trait calculation details, recruitment costs, and XP gain formulas.
