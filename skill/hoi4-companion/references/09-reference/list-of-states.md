## Summary

Hearts of Iron IV contains 1,081 distinct states representing all political divisions in the game world. Each state has a unique ID, owner (in 1936), population, infrastructure level, and strategic resources (oil, aluminum, rubber, tungsten, steel, chromium). States are categorized by size (wasteland through megalopolis) and include building slots for factories and air/naval bases.
