Military Industrial Organizations (MIOs) are design bureaus and manufacturers that develop and improve equipment through trait specializations. Each MIO falls into one of ten categories, has an initial trait, and can unlock specialized traits via branching tech trees. See [Military Industrial Organizations](../01-mechanics/military-industrial-organizations.md) for mechanic details.
