## Summary

Two adjacent research folders. **Infantry technology** covers infantry equipment, support weapons, night vision, the motorized/mechanized/amtrac/armored-car lines and the special forces units. **Support companies technology** is rooted in a single **Support Equipment** technology and branches into Engineers, Recon, Military Police, Maintenance, Field Hospital, Logistics and Signal, plus the Trains line. Support Artillery, Support Anti-air and Support Anti-tank companies come from the [artillery tree](artillery-technology.md), not this one.

Base research times are given in days before research-speed modifiers; years are the historical unlock years used for the ahead-of-time penalty.

## Part 1 — Infantry technology

## Infantry weapons

Infantry Equipment is the basis of any army and is the primary — often only — source of stats for most unit types, so fielding a newer tier than the enemy is a large advantage. Later tiers add hard attack and a little piercing; Support Anti-air/Anti-tank companies still give far more piercing because 40% of a division's piercing comes from its highest-piercing brigade or company.

**Basic Infantry equipment** starts researched for all countries; all majors and many minors also start with **Infantry Equipment I**.

## Motorized

Faster than foot units, slightly harder, more breakthrough; more expensive, less reliable, worse on rugged terrain and they burn fuel. Motorized technology also enables and improves **motorized logistics**, extending supply-hub and naval-base range at the cost of motorized equipment lost to breakdowns and enemy logistics strikes.

## Amphibious Tractors (Amtracs)

Special-forces mechanized infantry for wet terrain and naval invasions — **they consume [special forces cap](special-forces-doctrine.md)**. Less breakthrough than mechanized, much more fuel, cannot be discounted with army experience, but they get marsh/river/amphibious bonuses and benefit from special-forces attack bonuses (doctrines, Commando high command). These technologies also benefit from motorized, mechanized and naval-transport technology bonuses.

## Armored Cars (requires La Résistance)

Weaker, softer, much faster and slightly cheaper than light tanks. With No Step Back they are **not** designable in the tank designer, so obsolete armored cars cannot be repurposed the way obsolete light tanks can become TDs/SPGs/SPAA. With Arms Against Tyranny they also need their own dedicated MIOs which cannot be shared with tank equipment. These technologies benefit from motorized and armor technology bonuses.

## Research priorities

**Inferred:**
- **Support Equipment** (110 days, 1918) is the cheapest high-value technology in the game for a new player — it gates every support company.
- **Engineer Company I** and **Recon Company I** at 137 days are the two most commonly worth taking immediately: entrenchment/terrain and tactic selection respectively.
- **Logistics Company** is the answer to attrition and supply overload; **Maintenance Company** is the answer to equipment losses (and unlocks the excellent Easy Maintenance tank module at tier II).
- **Signal Company** matters most for invasions and for defensive stacks; skip it in a low-division-count game.
- On the infantry side, the **Improved Infantry Equipment** upgrade techs (+5% soft attack each) are cheap but modest; **Infantry Anti-Tank I and II** (+25% hard attack and +100% piercing each) are the ones that change what infantry can fight.
- **Mechanized Equipment I** is worth researching even for a motorized army — it gives all motorized battalions **Hardness +100%**.
