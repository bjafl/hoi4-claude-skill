## Summary

Special forces doctrine (requires **Arms Against Tyranny**) lets a nation specialise its elite units. It has two **Grand Doctrines** (Expanded Special Forces, Elite Special Forces) and **two Doctrine Tracks**, each of which can hold any one of the eight subdoctrines — two each for Mountaineers, Marines, Paratroopers and Rangers (Rangers requires **No Compromise, No Surrender**). Opening a tree costs a **special forces branch specialization (SFBS)**, of which every country starts with exactly one, and 60 experience of the matching type. Without Arms Against Tyranny the equivalent content is the **Special Forces Concentration** technology line on the Infantry technology tree (documented below).

## Special forces cap

Applies with or without DLC. Special forces = Marines, Mountaineers, Paratroopers, Rangers, Amphibious Tractor (Amtrac) and Amphibious Tank battalions.

- The cap is **the greater of 24 battalions or 5% of your total non-SF battalions** in deployed divisions.
- Divisions **cannot** be trained, deployed, changed to a different template, or have their template edited if doing so would exceed the cap.
- Battalions counted do **not** need to be fully equipped or at full strength — they only need to be part of deployed divisions.
- The 5% rule only overtakes the flat 24 at **480 non-SF battalions**. After that you need **+20 non-SF battalions per extra SF battalion**: 500 → 25 SF, 520 → 26 SF, 540 → 27 SF, and so on.

### Raising the cap

| Source | Effect |
|---|---|
| Political advisors, military high command, national focuses, decisions/national spirits | Various flat or percentage bonuses to special forces cap (country-specific) |
| **Expanded Special Forces Program** technology (no AAT required) | Special Forces Capacity: +10% |
| Grand Doctrine **Expanded Special Forces** (AAT) | Special Forces Capacity Multiplier: +4.0% |
| Expanded Special Forces track milestones (AAT) | +4.0% per mastered track (both tracks: +8.0% total) |
| Grand Doctrine **Elite Special Forces** track milestones (AAT) | +2.0% per mastered track (both tracks: +4.0% total) |
| Subdoctrine "Cap Contribution" reductions (AAT) | Reduce how much each battalion of that type *counts against* the cap — effectively multiplying how many you can field |

**Cap Contribution reductions** are the strongest lever. Each subdoctrine's base effect gives its unit type **-25% cap contribution**, and several milestones give another **-25%**. Stacking them means a Mountaineer/Marine/Paratrooper/Ranger battalion can eat far less than one cap slot.

| Sabotage Parties (Paratroopers) | -50% | base -25%, 300 Mastery -25% |

## Special Forces Branch Specialization (SFBS)

- Opening the **first** doctrine of a special-forces tree requires (a) the corresponding unit technology and (b) one available SFBS.
- Every country starts with **exactly one** SFBS. No country starts with any branch unlocked in either the 1936 or 1939 scenario.
- **Spending an SFBS is permanent and irreversible** — the tree stays unlocked and the specialization stays spent forever.
- A second SFBS comes from the **Diversify Elite Forces** decision, available to all **major** countries **after 1 January 1940** that do not have a focus granting one.

## Track and Mastery rules

- Unlike land/air/naval doctrine, special forces tracks are **not** split by category. Each of the two tracks can pick any of the eight subdoctrines, **except** that both tracks cannot take subdoctrines of the *same* special-forces type (e.g. you may not run Cold Weather Specialists and Elevate the Mountaineers together).
- Grand Doctrine does not restrict which subdoctrines are available.
- **Mastery source and experience cost are set by the subdoctrine**, not by the track:

| Subdoctrine pair | Gains Mastery from | Cost to unlock |
|---|---|---|
| Cold Weather Specialists / Elevate the Mountaineers | All Mountaineers | 60 Army experience |
| Marine Commandos / Expeditionary Units | All Marines and Amphibious Units | 60 Navy experience |
| Sabotage Parties / Concentrated Drops | All Paratroopers | 60 Air experience |
| Jaeger Traditions / Rangers Jungle Training | All Rangers | 60 Army experience |

- Mastery is gained when the relevant units engage in action, or at a reduced rate while training.
- If no subdoctrine is selected in a track, up to **200 Mastery** is held in reserve.
- Milestones are at **60 / 120 / 180 / 240 / 300 Mastery** (cumulative) — cheaper and shorter than land doctrine's 100–500.
- Doctrine costs are modified by spirits, advisors and focus/event/decision bonuses, including the corresponding land/naval/air doctrine cost modifiers.
- Mutually exclusive choices within a tree can be switched, but all bonuses unlocked after the switched choice are lost and must be re-bought with experience. **The SFBS itself is never refunded.**

## The three (four) unit types — what they are for

Special forces units use ordinary **Infantry Equipment** but need more of it per battalion than Infantry. Amtracs need their own motorized equipment; Amphibious Tanks use armor technology (and, with No Step Back, Light/Medium/Heavy amphibious chassis in the tank designer).

### Marines
Trained for wet terrain and amphibious invasions.

| Technology | Year | Base time | Prerequisites | Effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Marines I** | 1936 | 220 days | – | Enables Marines. Manpower 1000, Recovery Rate 0.40, Supply Use 0.05, Organization 70, Weight 0.5, Training time 120, Breakthrough 0.3, Suppression 1.0, HP 20.0.<br>Terrain: Marsh Attack +30%, River Attack +30%, Amphibious Attack +50% |
| **Marines II** | 1939 | 165 days | Marines I | Marines: Soft attack +5%, Organization +5 |
| **Marines III** | 1943 | 220 days | Marines II | Marines: Organization +5 |

**Use:** naval invasions (the +50% amphibious attack is the single biggest invasion modifier available to a division), river crossings, marsh and island fighting. Pair with **Engineers / Pioneers / Armored Engineer / Assault Engineer** support companies, which add their own river/amphibious bonuses.

### Mountaineers
Excel in rough terrain.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mountain Infantry I** | 1936 | 220 days | – | Enables Mountaineers. Manpower 1000, Recovery Rate 0.40, Supply Use 0.05, Organization 70, Weight 0.5, Training time 120, Breakthrough 0.3, Suppression 1.0, HP 20.0.<br>Terrain: Hills Movement +10%, Attack +20%, Defense +5%; Mountain Movement +20%, Attack +35%, Defense +10% |

**Use:** attacking into or holding hills and mountains — Alps, Caucasus, Balkans, Italy, Norway, Spain, China. Mountains are the terrain where ordinary infantry attacks stall hardest, so a handful of mountaineer divisions unblocks fronts that nothing else will move.

### Paratroopers
The only unit that can be paradropped, via transport plane.

| Technology | Year | Base time | Prerequisites | Effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Paratroopers I** | 1936 | 220 days | – | Enables Paratroopers. Manpower 1000, Recovery Rate 0.40, Suppression 1.0, HP 22.0, Training time 150, Supply Use 0.05, Organization 70, Weight 0.5 |
| **Paratroopers II** | 1939 | 165 days | Paratroopers I | Paratroopers: Soft attack +5%, Organization +5 |
| **Paratroopers III** | 1943 | 220 days | Paratroopers II | Paratroopers: Organization +5 |

**Use:** seizing ports, airfields, river crossings and encirclement chokepoints behind the line. Constraints:
- A division that **mixes Paratroopers with other line battalions cannot be paradropped** — keep drop templates pure paratrooper (support companies are fine).
- Requires transport planes and air superiority over the drop zone.
- The AI fights with paratroopers but **never paradrops them**.

### Amphibious Tractors and Amphibious Tanks
Also count against the special forces cap. Amtracs behave like Mechanized (own motorized equipment); Amphibious Tanks are armor. Marine Commandos' 300-Mastery milestone is the main doctrine support for them.

## Picking a branch — practical guidance

**Inferred:**

- **You get one SFBS at the start and it is permanent.** Decide based on your war plan, not on flavour. If you will run naval invasions, take Marines. If you will fight in the Alps/Caucasus/China, take Mountaineers. Paratroopers are the weakest default pick because the drops need transport planes plus air superiority plus a pure template.
- **Majors** should wait for **Diversify Elite Forces** (1 Jan 1940) or their focus if they want a second branch — check the table above before spending.
- **Expanded Special Forces** grand doctrine is the pick if the goal is *quantity* (+4% cap base, +4% per mastered track, -20% training time). **Elite Special Forces** is the pick if you will only ever field the 24-battalion minimum and want them individually strong (+10 Org, +24h supply grace).
- With cap-contribution reductions of -50% per type stacked on top of capacity multipliers, an AAT nation committed to special forces can field roughly double the raw battalion count its cap number suggests.
- **Marine Commandos 180 (Demolition Experts)** destroys one level of every strategic building in the state after a successful naval invasion — a useful way to cripple enemy industry you do not intend to hold.
- **Expeditionary Units** is the better marine branch for repeated large invasions (+3 total naval invasion plan capacity, +2 divisions per invasion, -5 days prep, +20% invasion planning, +15% shore bombardment).
