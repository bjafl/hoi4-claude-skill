## Summary

The artillery tree has four branches — **Artillery**, **Anti-air**, **Anti-tank** and **Rocket artillery** — all rooted in **Towed Artillery** (1934). Besides the towed/support/motorized battalions it feeds, this tree is also where **almost every tank gun module** comes from with No Step Back, so an armour nation researches it as hard as an artillery nation does. All artillery and anti-tank technologies count as **land warfare** special-project techs; anti-air counts as both **land warfare** and **aerodynamics and avionics**; rocket artillery counts as **aerodynamics and avionics**.

Key division mechanic repeated across the page: **40% of a division's piercing comes from the single brigade or support company with the highest piercing**. One anti-tank battalion or support company can therefore give an entire division the ability to pierce enemy armour.

## Anti-air branch

Very high air attack; hard attack and piercing better than plain artillery but worse than anti-tank; soft attack is a fraction of artillery. Units with air attack also contest enemy air superiority in their strategic region and shoot down enemy CAS.

## Research priorities

**Inferred:**
- **Towed Artillery → Inter-War Artillery** early is close to mandatory: it is the root of the whole tree and gates the first tank cannons.
- The **Improved/Advanced Artillery Upgrade** technologies are cheap (110 days) percentage boosts that apply to line, motorized, support and self-propelled artillery simultaneously. They are among the best research-time-to-combat-power in the game for an infantry-plus-artillery army.
- **Anti-tank branch to at least Improved Anti-Tank** is the insurance policy against enemy armour, because of the 40%-highest-piercing rule. One Support Anti-tank company per infantry division is the cheapest way to apply it.
- **Anti-air branch** does double duty: air attack for your divisions plus the tank AA guns and, via Shock-Hardening Techniques → **Variable Time Fuze Shells**, the biggest single artillery soft-attack modifier.
- **Rocket artillery** is a luxury: higher soft attack and breakthrough per unit, but higher cost, more Tungsten and no anti-armour value. Take it only when you already have artillery saturation.
