## Summary

Naval doctrine specialises how a nation fights at sea. Like land and air doctrine since patch 1.17, it is bought from the **Officer Corps** with **Navy experience**, split into three mutually exclusive **Grand Doctrines** (Fleet In Being, Trade Interdiction, Base Strike) and four **Doctrine Tracks** (Submarines, Screens, Capital Ships, Carriers), from each of which one **Subdoctrine** may be active. Subdoctrine milestones unlock at cumulative **Mastery** thresholds (100/200/300/400/500), earned when relevant ship types see combat or training. **Base cost: 100 Navy experience per grand doctrine, 50 or 100 per subdoctrine.**

---

## How the system works

- **Grand Doctrine**: exactly one active at a time. It gives immediate flat bonuses and unlocks unique **milestones** for each track when that track's subdoctrine is fully mastered.
- **Switching Grand Doctrine wipes all Naval Mastery.** Commit early.
- **Doctrine Tracks** (Submarines, Screens, Capital Ships, Carriers) each hold one active Subdoctrine. Grand Doctrine does **not** restrict which Subdoctrines are available.
- **Mastery** is the currency inside a track. Each milestone costs **100 Mastery, cumulative** (100 / 200 / 300 / 400 / 500).
- Mastery is generated when ships of the track's type **engage in combat**, or at a reduced rate while training.
- If **no Subdoctrine is selected** in a track, up to **200 Mastery** is held in reserve.
- Completing a track (mastering its subdoctrine) unlocks that Grand Doctrine's **track milestone**.

## Terminology and ship modifiers

### Ship stats

- **Armor**: Protection from enemy attacks. Armor ≥ enemy piercing reduces damage taken.
- **Attack**: Firepower or damage capability. Adds positive modifier to "to hit" roll for large friendly ships.
- **HP**: Hit points / health. Damage absorption before destruction.
- **Organization**: Combat readiness. Higher = longer fight duration and smaller effectiveness drops from enemy attacks. Low/zero organization = cannot fight/move effectively.
- **Carrier overcrowding**: Reduces penalty for having too many planes on a carrier.
- **Enemy fleet size penalty**: Increases penalty the enemy gets when they have too many ships in combat.
- **Naval AA attack**: Planes shot down on interception, reduces damage from Naval Strikes and Port Strikes.
- **Surface detection**: Ability to find enemy surface fleets and avoid them.
- **Sub detection**: Ability to find enemy submarines in and out of combat.
- **Surface visibility**: How hard a ship is to hit in combat; how well hidden outside combat.
- **Sub visibility**: How hard a submarine is to hit with depth charges; how well hidden in/out of combat.
- **Torpedo reveal chance**: Probability submarines reveal themselves when firing. Subs base at 3.5%, modified by visibility and positioning.

### Mission modifiers

- **Escort efficiency**: How quickly escorted convoys get defensive support. 100% = immediate.
- **Minelaying efficiency**: Overall efficiency of naval minelaying.
- **Naval air agility from carriers**: Agility modifier for naval-strike planes from carriers.
- **Naval targeting**: Hit chance for Naval Strike and Port Strike planes against ships.
- **Port strikes**: Damage multiplier against fleets in port.
- **Raiding efficiency**: How quickly ships on Convoy Raiding gather on a target.
- **Sortie efficiency**: How many carrier planes participate per attack. Typically starts at 50%.
- **Strike force org loss**: Organization lost by ships moving to engage while on Strike Force missions.

## Choosing a Grand Doctrine

| Grand Doctrine | Core strengths | Best for | Poor for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fleet In Being** | Capital Ship Armor +10%, Battleship Org +15%, Naval AA +10%, milestones improve battleship sustainability and armor raiding | Heavy battleship fleets, defensive postures, navy built around big guns | Nations with weak battleship/cruiser industries; little payoff for light-ship-focused fleets |

| **Base Strike** | Port Strikes +40%, Carrier Org +20%, milestones boost carrier sortie efficiency and naval targeting | Carrier-dominated fleets, naval air superiority, striking ports and stationary targets | Nations with weak carrier industries; battles far from ports |
