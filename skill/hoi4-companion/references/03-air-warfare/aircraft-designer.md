## Summary

**(requires By Blood Alone.)** The aircraft designer replaces fixed plane models with modular designs: pick an **airframe** (Small, Medium or Large, in one of five generations), bolt on an **engine**, a **primary armament** that determines the plane's role, and fill the remaining slots with weapons, special modules and electronics. Without By Blood Alone you instead research whole planes — see [Air technology](air-technology.md) for the legacy models and their stats.

**The primary armament — the weapon in the leftmost armament slot — decides the plane's role**, and therefore which missions it may fly and how it counts for air superiority.

| Airframe size | Roles available (set by primary armament) |
|---|---|
| **Small** | Fighter, Close Air Support, Naval Bomber |
| **Medium** | Heavy Fighter, Tactical Bomber, Scout |
| **Large** | Strategic Bomber, Naval Patrol Bomber |

Aircraft built on **carrier airframes** can be stationed on aircraft carriers. All airframes have **80% reliability** unless noted otherwise.

> The "Modules" column below is the airframe's module slot count exactly as the wiki lists it (e.g. `4/5`). The wiki does not explain the two numbers; treat the second as the airframe's maximum slots.

## Engine modules

**Design cost: changing the engine costs 6 air experience. Upgrading the engine one level while keeping the same engine count costs only 3 air experience.** Plan engine counts up front and you halve the XP bill for every later refit.

Engines supply **Thrust**; the airframe plus all modules supply **Weight**. Thrust versus weight is what actually sets a design's speed, on top of the engine's flat `Max Speed` percentage bonus.

> The exact thrust-to-weight speed formula is _not covered on the wiki._ Treat every "Max Speed %" figure below as a bonus applied on top of the airframe's base speed, and treat weight as the thing that eats it back.

- More engines = more thrust (to lift weight) but also linearly more cost and fuel.

**Reading it:** **2x Cannon II** is the best air-attack-per-agility-loss gun in the game (+24 attack for only −1 agility). Before it, **2x Cannon I** (+20 / −2) is the standard interceptor gun and **4x Heavy MG** (+18 / no agility loss) the standard dogfighter gun. See [Air combat](air-combat.md): agility only mitigates *incoming* damage, so cannon-heavy designs are correct against bombers and MG designs are correct in fighter-vs-fighter grinds.

## Designer companies

Aircraft designer companies are hired as ideas. **Companies typically also give +15% or +10% air research speed** on top of the trait below. With **Arms Against Tyranny** they are replaced by the military industrial organization system.

| Trait | Effects |
|---|---|
| Aircraft Designer | None (research speed only) |
| Light Aircraft Designer | Fighter: Agility +10%, Max Speed +10%. Carrier Fighter: Agility +10%, Max Speed +10% |
| Medium Aircraft Designer | Heavy Fighter: Reliability +20%. Tactical Bomber: Reliability +20%. Scout Plane: Reliability +20% |
| Heavy Aircraft Designer | Strategic Bomber: Strategic Bombing +10% |
| CAS Designer | Close Air Support: Ground Attack +10%, Reliability +10%. Carrier CAS: Ground Attack +10%, Reliability +10% |
| Naval Aircraft Designer | Naval Bomber: Range +10%, Naval Attack +10%. Carrier Naval Bomber: Range +10%, Naval Attack +10%. Carrier Fighter: Range +10%, Agility +10%. Carrier CAS: Range +10%, Naval Attack +10% |

**Inferred:** Light Aircraft Designer is the default pick for any country whose air force is fighters (agility and speed both feed the [air combat](air-combat.md) damage formula directly). Naval Aircraft Designer is the strongest single trait for a carrier navy because it buffs three carrier plane types at once.

## Worked example designs

Each build lists the module loadout and the **additive stat contributions**. Final Max Speed depends on thrust vs. weight, whose formula is not published; treat the engine's percentage as the headline.

### 1. Air-superiority fighter (1936 → 1940)

**Frame:** Basic Small Airframe (2/3 slots), later refit to Improved Small Airframe (3/4).

| Slot | Module | Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Engine | 1x Engine II (later III, then IV) | Thrust 16 → 25 → 30; Max Speed +20% → +30% → +45%; cost 14 → 16 → 18 |
| Primary armament | 4x Heavy Machine Gun (or 2x Cannon I) | Air Attack +18, no agility loss (Cannon I: +20, −2 agility) |
| Special | Self Sealing Fuel Tanks | Air Defense +6, weight 1, +1 rubber |
| Special (Improved frame onward) | Extra Fuel Tanks | Range +50%, Air Defense −2, weight 2 |

**Basic Small Airframe totals with 4x HMG + Self Sealing Fuel Tanks:** Agility 40, Air Defense 9 + 6 = **15**, Air Attack **18**, base range 550 km, weight 4 + 5 + 1 = **10**, production cost 5 + 14 + 3 + 1 = **23**.

**Inferred:** guns before agility. Agility only mitigates damage you receive; air attack and the speed ratio decide what you deal. Add Extra Fuel Tanks the moment your fighters cannot cover the whole strategic region — range shortfall is a direct multiplicative cut to mission efficiency and is usually worth more than any stat.

**Late-war interceptor variant:** Advanced or Modern Small Airframe + 1x Engine IV (or Axial Jet) + **2x Cannon II** (+24 attack, only −1 agility) + Self Sealing Fuel Tanks + Armor Plates + **Air-Air Radar II** (night penalty −40%, applies on Interception). This is the anti-bomber build: cannons and speed beat unagile bombers, and radar keeps it working at night.

### 2. Close air support plane (1936 → 1940)

**Frame:** Basic Small Airframe, then Improved Small Airframe.

| Slot | Module | Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Engine | 1x Engine II → III | Thrust 16 → 25; Max Speed +20% → +30% |
| Primary armament | Heavy Bomb Locks | Ground Attack +8, Agility −20 on mission, weight 0 (6 on mission), cost 1 |
| Slot 2 | Rocket Rails | Ground Attack +4, **no agility penalty**, cost 1 |
| Slot 3 (Improved frame) | Rocket Rails again, or Dive Brakes | +4 more Ground Attack, or +4 Air Defense and +6 Naval Targeting on CAS/Naval/Port Strike |
| Later | Armor Plates / Self Sealing Fuel Tanks | Air Defense +4 / +6 |

**Improved Small Airframe with Heavy Bomb Locks + 2x Rocket Rails:** Ground Attack **8 + 4 + 4 = 16**, Agility 45 − 20 = **25 on mission**, Air Defense 11, production cost 6 + 16 + 1 + 1 + 1 = **25**.

**Inferred:** CAS damage scales with `ground attack × number of planes`, and **nothing about the plane affects divisional AA losses**, so the correct CAS is *cheap and numerous with maximum ground attack*. Rocket Rails are the best value in the game for this. Skip Anti-Tank Cannons unless facing heavy armour — 8 weight for +8 ground attack is worse than two Rocket Rails.

### 3. Naval bomber (1940)

**Frame:** Improved Medium Airframe (4/6 slots) for range, or Improved Small Airframe if you need carrier compatibility.

| Slot | Module | Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Engine | 2x Engine III | Thrust 38, Max Speed +30%, fuel 0.32, cost 32 |
| Primary armament | Medium Torpedo Mountings (Heavy from 1944) | Naval Attack +17 (+22), Naval Targeting +8 (+10), Agility −12 on mission |
| Slot 2 | Air-Ground Radar I → II | Surface Detection +10 → +20, Sub Detection +5 → +10, night penalty −30% → −40% |
| Slot 3 | Self Sealing Fuel Tanks | Air Defense +8 |
| Slot 4 | Extra Fuel Tanks | Range +50%, Air Defense −6 |
| Spare | Heavy MG Defense Turret | Air Defense +2, Air Attack +1.5, Max Speed −3.5% |

**Improved Medium Airframe totals with the above (excluding turret):** Naval Attack 1 + 17 = **18**, Naval Targeting 1 + 8 = **9**, Air Defense 20 + 8 − 6 = **22**, Range 1100 × 1.5 = **1650 km**, cost 10 + 32 + 4 + 2 + 1.5 + 2 = **51.5**.

**Inferred:** naval strike needs to *find* ships (base 4% fleet detection), so Air-Ground Radar is not optional. Torpedo mountings all cost 4 — always fit your newest. On a **carrier** naval bomber use a Small carrier airframe and drop the extra fuel tanks; carrier wings are 10 planes and fight in the 5× carrier damage regime, so survivability modules pay off more than range.

### 4. Strategic bomber (1940 → 1944)

**Frame:** Improved Large Airframe (5/7 slots), later Advanced Large Airframe (5/7).

| Slot | Module | Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Engine | 4x Engine III → IV | Thrust 64 → 75, Max Speed +30% → +45%, cost 64 → 72 |
| Primary armament | Large Bomb Bay | Strategic Bombing +15, Agility −15, weight 2 (8 on mission), cost 9 |
| Slot 2 | Self Sealing Fuel Tanks | Air Defense +10, weight 1 |
| Slot 3 | Bomb Sights I → II | Strategic Bombing +4 → +6 |
| Slot 4 | Radio Navigation I → II | Strategic Bombing +4 → +6, night penalty −10% → −20% |
| Slot 5+ | Cannon Defense Turret 2x | Air Defense +8, Air Attack +6, Max Speed −7.5% |

**Improved Large Airframe totals with Large Bomb Bay + SSFT + Bomb Sights I + Radio Navigation I + one Cannon Defense Turret 2x:** Strategic Bombing 15 + 4 + 4 = **23**, Air Defense 35 + 10 + 8 = **53**, Air Attack **6**, Agility 30 − 15 = **15 on mission**, Range 2000 km, production cost 20 + 64 + 9 + 2 + 1.5 + 1 + 8 = **105.5**.

**Inferred:** stack **air defense** above all else on strategic bombers. State AA damage divides by the wing's air defense and floors at one plane lost per hit, so a high-defense bomber effectively caps its own AA losses. Air defense also feeds the disruption-negation formula, buying back bombing output that enemy fighters stripped. Bomb Sights and Radio Navigation are pure strategic-bombing value for almost no weight. Late war, swap Large Bomb Bay for **Heavy Rocket-Propelled Bombs** (+16 strategic bombing and +18 naval attack for port strikes) if you can afford 16 production per bomber.
