## Summary

There are **two entirely different air technology trees**. With **By Blood Alone** you research **airframes and modules** that feed the [aircraft designer](aircraft-designer.md). Without it you research **whole plane models** in eight fixed roles. This file documents both, with years, research times, prerequisites, unlocks and full plane stat tables.

Since **Götterdämmerung** and Patch 1.15, **all** air technologies also generate **aerodynamics and avionics breakthrough progress** while being researched, and the **Jet Engine** and **Axial Jet Engine** special projects act as prerequisites for many late technologies in both trees.

---

## Part 1 — By Blood Alone airframe tree

**(requires By Blood Alone.)** Research is split into **airframes** (by size and generation), **weaponry**, and **engines**. Modules unlocked by a technology can be fitted to any eligible airframe.

## Research priorities

**Inferred, By Blood Alone:**
1. **Small airframes** every generation — fighters win air superiority, and superiority is what feeds the land war (−0.7% enemy defense and breakthrough per point).
2. **Engines II → III → IV** on schedule; engine level is the single biggest speed driver and speed is a pure offensive multiplier in air combat.
3. **Survivability Studies (1939)** early — Self Sealing Fuel Tanks are the cheapest air defense in the game.
4. **Cannons I (1939)** for interceptors; **Range Improvements (1936)** if your air regions are large.
5. Medium and Large airframes only if you are actually committing to tactical or strategic bombing — large airframes contribute 0.01 air superiority and will not help you hold the sky.

**Inferred, legacy tree:** research Fighter I/II/III on time and let CAS lag one generation; CAS damage scales with numbers and ground attack, not with tech tier, whereas losing the fighter tech race loses the whole air war.
