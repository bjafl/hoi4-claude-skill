Base game achievements in Hearts of Iron IV. These 31 achievements cover fundamental gameplay mechanics across all nations, from faction diplomacy and research to military conquest and ideological conversion. Difficulties range from AFK-able tasks to extremely challenging global objectives.

## Friend in Need

**Condition:** Has joined a faction

## Time is on our side

**Condition:** After Dec 31st, 1947

## Canada First

**Condition:** In faction with Germany, Germany is a faction leader

## We'll Build it in a Day

**Condition:** Has researched Construction V

## Transport Tycoon

**Condition:** Has at least 1000 convoys

## Destroyer of Worlds

**Condition:** Has used a nuclear bomb

## Wunderwaffen

**Condition:** Has completed special project Nuclear Fission Bomb Project, Has completed special project Me 163 Komet (Rocket Interceptor), Has completed special project Jet Engine

## United Netherlands

**Condition:** Owns and controls: Vlaanderen (6), Wallonie (34), Antwerpen (977), Ardennes (980), Luxembourg (8), Brabant (35), Holland (7), Friesland (36)

## Vive la France

**Condition:** Has not surrendered, Has not capitulated, After Dec 31st, 1947

## Master of War

**Condition:** Has completed doctrine track: Infantry, Has completed doctrine track: Artillery & Combat Support, Has completed doctrine track: Armor, Has completed doctrine track: Operations, Has completed doctrine track: Capital Ships, Has completed doctrine track: Carriers, Has completed doctrine track: Screens, Has completed doctrine track: Submarines, Has completed doctrine track: Fighter Aircraft, Has completed doctrine track: Strike Aircraft, Has completed doctrine track: Medium Aircraft, Has completed doctrine track: Heavy Aircraft

## Mine is bigger than yours

**Condition:** Is Faction leader, Faction has at least 20 members

## No country for old men

**Condition:** Has conscription law Scraping the Barrel, Has 0 manpower

## Poland Can Into Space

**Condition:** Has completed special project PZL Bielik (Rocket Interceptor)

## True Blitzkrieg

**Condition:** Before Jan 1st, 1940: France does not exist or has capitulated or has surrendered, Poland does not exist or has capitulated

## Northern Light

**Condition:** Is a faction leader, In faction with: Norway, Denmark, Sweden, Finland

## Nobody expects...

**Condition:** Ruling party is Fascist, In faction with Germany, Germany is a faction leader, Controls Ile de France (16)

## The bell tolls for us

**Condition:** Ruling party is not Fascist, Won the Spanish Civil War, Ruling party is Communist or Democratic

## The Revolution Triumphant

**Condition:** Germany is a puppet of the Soviet Union

## Once More Mate

**Condition:** Controls Edirne (341)

## Forge of Victory

**Condition:** Has at least 50 factories

## Sunset Invasion

**Condition:** Has not joined a faction with non-South American members, Controls any coastal state on the European continent

## Duce Nuked'em

**Condition:** Ruling party is Fascist, Has dropped a nuclear bomb in California

## Operation Sea Lion

**Condition:** Controls: South-West England (123), Sussex (127), Gloucestershire (338), Greater London Area (126), East Anglia (125), Wales (122), West Midlands (128), East Midlands (129), Lancashire (132), Yorkshire (130), Northumberland (131), Lanark (133), Lothian (121), Aberdeenshire (136), Scottish Highlands (120)

## One Empire

**Condition:** Is a faction leader, Every state in the world is controlled by you or a member of your faction

## Party like it's 1520

**Condition:** Controls Södermanland (141), Ruling party is Communist

## Med plutonium...

**Condition:** Has dropped a nuclear bomb on Denmark

## The Revenge of Montezuma

**Condition:** Controls: California (378), Nevada (379), Utah (380), Arizona (377), New Mexico (376), Texas (375)

## Big Entente

**Condition:** Is a faction leader, Has completed focus Strengthen the Little Entente, You or a member of your faction owns all cores of Germany

## New World Order

**Condition:** Ruling party of every country is Fascist

## Bearer of Artillery

**Condition:** Has Vojtek as a commander

**Strategy note:** Requires Poland to trigger the event "Wojtek Never Drops A Crate" by: staying non-aligned (can form any kingdom), avoiding Soviet faction/puppet status, being at war with Italy with a division in Latium/Abruzzo/Tuscan, completing focus Artillery Modernization, and controlling Iranian Hamadan (owned by you or faction member).
