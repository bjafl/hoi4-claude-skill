## Summary

The Man the Guns DLC adds 21 achievements spanning multiple nations and challenge types. These range from diplomatic objectives (forming federations, hosting governments-in-exile) to military goals (inflicting casualties, controlling ships and mines) and special scenarios (the 30 Minutes of Hel speedrun challenge). Achievements require single-player Ironman with 1936 start, regular or higher difficulty.

## Team America

**Condition:** Playing as United States; Dropped a nuclear bomb on Paris

As a democratic nation, build strategic bombers and fighters, and rush nuclear technology. When Germany controls Paris (which they will in historical play), move your planes to airfields in the British Isles, set them to mission over Northern France, achieve air superiority, and deploy the nuke.

## History Repeated Itself

**Condition:** Playing as United States; The Second American Civil War has started; Confederacy of American States does not exist

Completing either Full Desegregation or Union Representation Act triggers civil war with the Confederacy. Complete the WPA branch and Guarantee the American Dream beforehand to help the United States win.

## Georgia On My Mind

**Condition:** Playing as United States; Owns and controls Georgia (365), Georgia (231), and South Georgia (720)

Collaborating with the Axis is simplest: help them capitulate the Allies and Comintern, grab South Georgia (720) and Georgia (231). Alternatively, South Georgia (720) can be obtained via the Reaffirm Monroe Doctrine focus.

## To Arms in Dixie!

**Condition:** Playing as United States; The Boston Rebellion has begun

Complete America First and hire the fascist advisor. Complete Ally With the Silver Shirts to trigger the civil war chain. For best results also complete Work With the Bund and Recruit the Free Corps.

## Arsenal of Democracy

**Condition:** Playing as United States; Ruling party is Democratic; More than 300 military factories

Play historical and build as many military factories as possible. The Arsenal of Democracy branch gives military factories for free; also complete the WPA branch to boost industry.

## Underpaid, Undersexed, and Under Eisenhower

**Condition:** Playing as United States; Is in faction with United Kingdom; Is Faction leader; Received at least 50 expeditionary forces from United Kingdom

Play historical and join the Allies. You need 50% more factories and fielded manpower than the United Kingdom; this happens naturally after a few years. Once faction leader, create an invasion and request 51 expeditionary forces. The UK will accept if they have ~160 divisions.

## Last for a Thousand Years

**Condition:** Playing as United Kingdom; Has completed focus Imperial Federation

Can be done as any ideology. Save 300-700 political power before starting Hold the Imperial Conference, then improve relations with the dominions before starting the focus sequence.

## Britzkrieg

**Condition:** Playing as United Kingdom; Has completed focus The King's Party; Is in faction with Germany; France has capitulated or does not exist; Has full control of Ile de France (16)

Follow the focus tree to Alliance with Germany. Justify against France and launch a naval invasion of Normandy (not necessary to call Germany in). If France is puppeted, the achievement will not fire; annex all cores, territories, and colonies instead.

## Nothing Wrong with our Bloody Ships

**Condition:** Playing as United Kingdom; Has at least 9 Battlecruisers

Battlecruisers are built with Heavy Ship Hulls and Battlecruiser armor. Build 6 simultaneously with the United Kingdom's starting 3 to reach 9 total, assigning all dockyards to speed construction.

## William Wallis

**Condition:** Playing as United Kingdom; Has completed focus The King's Party; Scotland is a subject of United Kingdom; United States is a subject of United Kingdom; Installed an American monarchy

Declare war on the dominions (not Canada directly, as they'll join the US side). Occupy at least one province from Australia, New Zealand, and South Africa. Train 72-96 divisions and declare war on the US via Canada in late-1938/early-1939, capitalizing on their low conscription. After annexing/puppeting all US territory, the decision to install American Monarchy will appear.

## Crush the Dream

**Condition:** Playing as United Kingdom; Ruling party is Communist; Has completed focus Crush the Dream; United States is a subject of United Kingdom

Switch to communist ideology, complete the Crush the Dream focus, and puppet the United States as part of peace negotiations or through diplomacy.

## Treading the Narrow Path

**Condition:** Playing as Netherlands; Has not completed focus Cave to the Germans; Has completed focus Continue the Zuiderzee Works; Has not lost starting states; After 1st Jan, 1945

Losing control means losing a majority of a state's victory points; losing a few provinces is acceptable. Defend your continental territory while resisting German pressure for 9 years.

## Putting the Raid on the Medway to Shame

**Condition:** Playing as Netherlands; United Kingdom has no capital ships (carriers, battleships, battlecruisers, heavy cruisers)

It is easier to fully annex the United Kingdom than to hunt individual capital ships, and annexation will count for this achievement.

## Bevrijding

**Condition:** Playing as Netherlands; Ruling party is Democratic; Has completed focus Liberation

Play historical and go down the Continue the War in Batavia branch. Do not defend your European lands (Continue the War in Batavia requires capitulation); instead build up your Asian possessions. Once Germany is defeated, your homeland returns to your control. Complete War on Pacifism to remove the Shell-shocked Spectator of the Great War spirit, then complete Liberation.

## Mi Casa es tu Casa

**Condition:** Playing as Mexico; Any country is exiled in Mexico

Reach the Realpolitik focus and create a "Bastion of Liberty" faction, then use your first initiative point to allow hosting exiled governments. Guarantee Finland, accept their Call to Arms when the Winter War starts, improve relations and send an attaché, then invite them to your faction before they white peace with the Soviets.

## The New Home of the Revolution

**Condition:** Playing as Mexico; Lev Trotsky is the current country leader; Soviet Union is a subject of Mexico

Invite Trotsky in the "Trotsky seeks refuge" event. Follow the rightmost focus tree to become communist and put Trotsky in power. Join Germany's war against the USSR via Smash the Bureaucrats, then puppet the Soviets in the peace conference.

## Assuming Direct Control

**Condition:** Not playing as a major power (US, UK, Germany, Italy, France, USSR, Japan, China); Has assumed faction leadership from UK, Germany, or USSR; Is Faction leader

Play as Romania and go down the Balkan Dominance branch. Increase your power by focusing on army and industry, then defeat Hungary and Bulgaria. Join the Axis but never join their wars; when they are defeated, you will become faction leader.

## Vive la Jeune Ecole

**Condition:** Playing as France; After Jan 1st, 1945; Has at least 150 Destroyers; Has researched Modern Ship Torpedo Launcher

Turn fascist via the National Regeneration focus. Join the Axis but do not participate in wars. Rush research toward Modern Ship Torpedo Launcher, build as many naval dockyards as possible, and take focuses that improve research, construction speed, and dockyard output. Assign all dockyards to destroyer production.

## 30 Minutes of Hel

**Condition:** Playing as Poland; Start date is Aug 3rd, 1939; Has inflicted more than 1.80M Casualties on Germany

Modify division templates: replace Cavalry Recon with Engineer Companies; delete excess cavalry to manage supply. Create two stacks of 24 divisions with high-defense generals. Construct land forts (level 1) and anti-air along a fallback defensive line from Warsaw to Łódź. Research Engineer Company II, Improved Radio, and infantry upgrades. When Germany attacks, join the Allies and hold your lines; casualties accumulate rapidly on German offensives. You will reach 1.8M casualties long before the Soviet Union gets involved.

## Pride and Extreme Prejudice

**Condition:** Not playing as United Kingdom; Sank the British Pride of the Fleet (HMS Hood)

Japan can do this quickly. Choose Purge the Kodoha Faction and justify on the UK or British Malaya. Ask Germany for docking rights and move your fleet to their nearest port. Use your fleet as a strike force with destroyer patrols around the UK. When justification finishes, declare war. Accept a German Axis invitation to use their airfields. Sink capital ships until the Hood goes down.

## Do You Mined?

**Condition:** Any country at war with player has more than 999 mines on their coastline

Play as Italy. Remove divisions from Eritrea (550) or Somaliland (559), keeping ports for efficiency. Lay mines on the Ethiopian coast with your starting navy until you reach 1000 mines. If you lose all territory, white peace with Ethiopia will be offered.
