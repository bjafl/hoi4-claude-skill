Death or Dishonor DLC adds 10 achievements focused on Central European nations (Hungary, Romania, Czechoslovakia, Yugoslavia, Sweden). Difficulty ranges from Very Easy to Very Hard, with several requiring complex territorial control or diplomatic maneuvering.

## Miklos Horthy and the Habsburg Prince

Playing as Hungary. Restore Austria-Hungary.

**Condition:** Has cosmetic tag Austria-Hungary. Owns and controls: Oberösterreich (152) Tirol (153) Niederösterreich (4) Vorarlberg (848) Burgenland (975) Steiermark-Kärnten (976) North Sudetenland (69) Bohemia (9) Moravia (75) Czeské Slezsko (74) Tešínsko (72) Western Slovakia (70) Southern Slovakia (664) Eastern Slovakia (71) Podkarpatská Rus (73) Jordan (455) Palestine (454) Niederschlesien (66) Oberschlesien (67) Toscana (162) Franche-Comte (17) Lombardia (159) Emilia Romagna (161).

**Strategy:** Two main paths exist. Method 1: accept Germany's Sudetenland ultimatum, build air force, conquer Balkans for resources (Yugoslavia, Romania, Greece), then backstab Germany when they war the Soviets. Method 2: refuse Sudetenland with Austria and Czechia as puppets, attack Germany immediately while they move on Poland. Either way, avoid doing Reverse the Risorgimento focus—it puppets Italian states but you need to own them directly. Franche-Comte (eastern France) and Jordan (UK puppet) are the hardest to acquire; most others come via war goals or conquest of neighbors.

## Better than the Szent István

Playing as Hungary. Have Miklos Horthy as a Naval commander and build a battleship.

**Condition:** Has the unit leader Miklos Horthy. Has at least 1 Battleship.

**Strategy:** With Götterdämmerung DLC, complete End the Regency focus to gain Horthy as an advisor. Otherwise, complete Invite the Habsburg Prince. Then pursue any path under Reinstate the Pragmatic Sanction of 1713 for wargoals on Yugoslavia or Romania. Build industry, army, research and deploy a battleship, then assign Horthy to command that fleet.

## Death or Dishonor or Cake

Playing as Romania. Own a slice of all your starting neighbors.

**Condition:** Carol II is the current country leader. Has not completed any of the focuses: Force Abdication, Handle the King, All Parties Must End. Owns at least one core state each of: Bulgaria, Soviet Union, Hungary, Czechoslovakia, Yugoslavia, Poland.

**Note:** Mutually exclusive with other Romanian achievements that require different country leaders. Achieving the required core states from six different neighbors while maintaining Carol II is a complex balancing act.

## Neither Death nor Dishonor

Playing as Romania. Make it to 1942 with all the states you started it with and control either Moscow or Berlin.

**Condition:** Has not lost a starting state. Controls either Moscow (219) or Brandenburg (64). After 31st Dec, 1941.

**Strategy:** Save political power early for Institute Royal Dictatorship and Revise the Constitution (using their discount). Conscript heavily until you have 400k manpower deployed. Puppet Hungary and Bulgaria via Align Hungary and Puppet Bulgaria focuses—they rarely refuse. Build land forts on Soviet/Polish borders and coastal forts on Black Sea (including Bulgaria). Take Fortify the Borders focus. When Soviets demand Bessarabia around mid-1940, refuse. Join Axis and refuse calls to Allied wars. Let Germans push Soviets while you defend your borders (do not lose any states). Help Germans invade but keep coast/border secure. Push into Soviet territory to control Moscow, or trade captured territory for it in peace deal.

## The Munich Disagreement

Playing as Czechoslovakia. Occupy Munich while at war with Germany.

**Condition:** At war with Germany. Controls Oberbayern (52).

## Czechmate

Playing as Czechoslovakia. Occupy Canberra while at war with the Allies.

**Condition:** At war with United Kingdom. United Kingdom ruling party is Democratic. Controls New South Wales (285).

## Huge-oslavia

Playing as Yugoslavia. Occupy all your neighbouring countries (Austria, Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria, Albania, Italy and Greece).

**Condition:** Controls all core states of: Italy, Austria, Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria, Greece (except Edirne (341)), Albania. (Achievement only requires control, can be gained while at war.)

**Strategy:** Go communist (40% support to Abolish the Monarchy). Rush Austria early before Anschluss. Declare on Greece after Austria to prevent their Allied guarantee (tight timing required). Annex both for industry. Wait for Soviet pressure on Bessarabia mid-1940, then justify on Italy and call Soviets. Use Zara exploit: attack Italians there repeatedly without occupying the port; they keep landing with low organization. Build defensive line from Fiume through Zagreb. After Italy exhausts, capture before Allies do to puppet them. Then conquer Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria using concentrated forces to find encirclement opportunities. Germany will be weakened fighting Soviets; push north to finish. Use autonomy system to annex conquered nations post-war.

## The Balkan Powder Keg

Playing as Yugoslavia. Start the Second World War (be part of either Axis or Allies and at war with the other faction in a war you started).

**Condition:** Declared war on Axis or Allies while in either Axis or Allies.

**Strategy:** Pick fascist demagogue as first political power spending, flip fascist via decision (skip civil war), complete Invite German Military Mission focus, join Axis manually. Justify and declare war on United Kingdom. If on historical mode, signing Tripartite Pact will trigger civil war; flip via decision and join Axis manually instead.

## En Svensk Tiger

Playing as Sweden. Acquire a production license for a Tiger or Tiger II tank from Germany.

**Condition:** Is licensing from Germany either: Heavy Tank II (Panzerkampfwagen VI Tiger), Heavy Tank III (Panzerkampfwagen Tiger Ausf. B), Improved Heavy Tank Chassis, Advanced Heavy Tank Chassis.

**Strategy:** Pick fascist demagogue as first advisor and turn fascist (avoid civil war if possible). Join Axis and help in their wars. Germany must survive until late 1943 to research Tiger tank. Note: Due to a bug, this achievement cannot be obtained with No Step Back enabled, as Germany never progresses beyond Basic Heavy Tank.

## Lethal Straight

Have a production license from at least 5 of the 7 major powers.

**Condition:** Is licensing from 5 of the below: United Kingdom, Germany, United States, Soviet Union, Japan, France, Italy.

**Strategy:** Play as a non-aligned nation to avoid diplomatic penalties from ideology differences. Improve relations with five major powers, then request production licenses for basic infantry equipment (even majors readily license outdated tech). With La Résistance DLC, use agents with diplomatic pressure to expedite licensing requests.
