## Summary

The Götterdämmmerung DLC adds 20 achievements focused on Central European powers (Austria, Belgium, Hungary, Germany), special projects (Manhattan Project, thermonuclear bombs, ballistic missiles), and landmark/dam raiding mechanics. Many achievements concentrate on ambitious territorial claims and multi-country Habsburg dynasty placements.

## Antischluss

**Condition:** As Austria, control all German starting states (Ermland-Masuren, Königsberg, Hinterpommern, Ostmark, Oberschlesien, Niederschlesien, Sachsen, Brandenburg, Vorpommern, Mecklenburg, Schleswig, Holstein, Hannover, Thüringen, Franken, Niederbayern, Oberbayern, Württemberg, Baden, Moselland, Hessen, Rhineland, Westfalen, Weser-Ems).

## No Friends, Only Family

**Condition:** As Austria or Hungary led by a Habsburg Monarch (Otto von Habsburg or Joseph August von Habsburg), control the Swiss Plateau and have at least three other countries be led by a member of the Habsburg family (Austria, Hungary, Poland, Czechoslovakia, Ukraine, Spain, Mexico, Grand Duchy of Tuscany, Kingdom of Lombardy-Venetia, or Croatia).

**Strategy:** Annex Hungary and Czechoslovakia, create Austria-Hungary, then release them. Use decisions to restore Habsburgs to their thrones across multiple countries.

## Edelweiss Seedelweiss

**Condition:** As Austria have a coastline, at least 10 Battleships and have Georg von Trapp as an Admiral.

**Strategy:** Georg von Trapp joins Austria via event, 15–20 days after completing the Promote Admirals focus.

## Otto-man

**Condition:** As Austria or Hungary led by Otto von Habsburg, control starting states of Austria, Hungary, Czechoslovakia, Romania, Bulgaria, Greece, Turkey, Polish Galicia, Slovenia, and Croatia (55 specific states listed).

**Strategy:** Note: Requires the states of Lombardy-Venetia even though not mentioned in the achievement description. Focuses on establishing a Central European Habsburg hegemony with Otto von Habsburg as leader.

## Spaak of Genius

**Condition:** As Belgium with Paul-Henri Spaak as country leader, research at least 10 different Special Projects.

**Strategy:** Germany should not go historical. Rush towards The Council of Europe and invite as many countries as possible. Naval and aerospace facilities have the shortest special projects.

## Brentry

**Condition:** As Austria, Belgium, or the Netherlands be the leader of the EU and have the United Kingdom join your faction.

**Strategy:** Varies by country. Belgium/Austria: complete The Council of Europe or Via the Danube to Europe focus. Netherlands: complete Foundations for a European Union focus.

## The 501st Legion

**Condition:** As Belgium have the National Spirit "Rexist Legion" and control all starting neighbors' capitals (Ile de France, Luxembourg, Holland, Brandenburg).

**Strategy:** Go fascist via the rexist path, justify on the Dutch East Indies, annex Luxembourg and the Netherlands. Join the Axis and attack Germany. Achievement fires while at war if all required provinces are occupied.

## Congolese Belgium

**Condition:** As Belgian Congo break free and control all Belgian Congo starting states (Ardennes, Wallonie, Antwerpen, Vlaanderen).

## The Lenin Boys are Back in Town

**Condition:** As Communist Hungary complete Reinstate the Lenin Boys focus, control Leningrad fully, and be communist.

**Strategy:** Reinstate the Lenin Boys and then capture Leningrad (state 195).

## A Great Hunger

**Condition:** As Hungary proclaim Greater Hungary and control all historical states (Southern Slovakia, Podkarpatská Rus, North Transylvania, Transylvania, Crisana, Banat, Backa, Burgenland).

**Strategy:** Requires the Götterdämmmerung version of Proclaim Greater Hungary focus; will not trigger without the DLC.

## Weltpolitik

**Condition:** As Germany take control of all former German colonies (Cameroon, Togo, Tanganyika, Rwanda, Burundi, Otjozondjupa, Kunene, Khomas, Karas, Qingdao, Palau, Kaiser-Wilhelmsland, Bismarck, Caroline Islands, Marshall Islands, Saipan, Samoa, Nauru).

## Naval Arms Race

**Condition:** As Germany led by an Admiral (Erich Räder) capitulate the United Kingdom.

**Strategy:** Follow the Oppose Hitler focus branch. Fail the timed event during the focus progression to keep Erich Räder in power. Justify war goal on UK and capitulate them. Achievement grants even if the UK collapses from its own civil war rather than direct capitulation.

## Woman in a High Castle

**Condition:** As Germany with a female leader (Victoria or Eva Braun) annex the USA (including Alaska and Hawaii) or have a US puppet that owns all American starting states including Alaska and Hawaii.

**Strategy:** German female leaders available include Victoria and Eva Braun. Can fulfill via puppet instead of direct annexation.

## American Prometheus

**Condition:** As the USA have Robert Oppenheimer complete the Manhattan Project Special Project.

**Strategy:** Build Nuclear Research Facilities and hire scientists. Rush Manhattan Project (fastest via Neutrality Act). Research Atomic Research and computing ahead of time. Assign scientists to Pile-1, but must choose Oppenheimer for The Manhattan Project. Keep Oppenheimer if an event asks whether to dismiss him. Achievement triggers on completion.

## Tsar Bomba

**Condition:** As Tsarist Russia (after completing Romanov Reconstruction focus) research and complete the Thermonuclear Bombs Special Project.

**Strategy:** Win the Civil War. Build Nuclear Research Facilities and hire scientists with good traits for breakthroughs. Germany's historical status affects difficulty. Research the tech to completion.

## Super Heavy Metal

**Condition:** Complete Special Projects for Intercontinental Bombers, Land Cruisers, and Super Heavy Battleships.

**Strategy:** Easiest as the USA. Note: Intercontinental Bomber requires Improved Large Airframe research; Land Cruiser requires Super-Heavy Tank Chassis research.

## Backfire

**Condition:** As the United Kingdom complete the Medium Range Ballistic Missiles Special Project, own at least 1 rocket site, and be at war with Germany.

**Strategy:** Build Aviation Research Facilities and man them with scientists. Research Ballistic Missile, then MRBM. Build a rocket site. Easily achieved if following historical path (will be at war with Germany).

## Dam It!

**Condition:** Successfully execute a Dam Busting Raid.

**Strategy:** Can be done as Germany by executing a raid on the Belgian dam.

## Bullseye

**Condition:** Successfully launch a raid on a Landmark.

## The True Successor

**Condition:** As Luxembourg led by Charles Marx control Brussels and Trier.

**Strategy:** A challenging achievement requiring control of specific Belgian and German cities as the small Luxembourg nation.
