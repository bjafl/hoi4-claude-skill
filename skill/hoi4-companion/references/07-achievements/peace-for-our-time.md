Achievement group for the Peace for Our Time DLC, focused on Czechoslovak national spirits, faction play, and regional politics. All achievements require Ironman mode with regular or higher difficulty and all game rules enabled for achievements.

## I'm playing both sides, so I always come out on top

Condition: One of the following must be true: Czechoslovakia has (via the Škoda weapons sales decisions) sold weapons to a country that is at war with another country they also sold weapons to; OR any country has an international market buy contract with a country that is at war with another country they also have a buy contract with.

Strategy: As Czechoslovakia, complete Expand Škoda's Offices to unlock weapons sales decisions, then sell to countries in conflict with each other (easiest as part of the Spanish Civil War via Profit off the Spanish Civil War).

## I didn't hear no bell

Condition: Has had the cosmetic tag Czecho-Slovak Republic at least once; Has capitulated Germany.

Strategy: Go democratic, complete Truth Prevails and Forming a Federation before Munich, select "We need radical changes" in the Fall of the First Republic event to gain the cosmetic tag, then build 54-60 infantry divisions and 800 fighters, hold Germany's initial assault with your fighters and state AA, then push through and capitulate them.

## Premyslid's Dream

Condition: Has completed focus The Legacy of Ottokar; Has completed focus Proclaim Restoration of the Bohemian Kingdom.

Strategy: Rush the monarchist focus path to unlock both required focuses; The Legacy of Ottokar requires conquering Austria, while the Bohemian Kingdom requires owning Silesia. Ahistorical AI makes this easier.

## Kuťáci

Condition: Owns at least 1 of each facility type.

Strategy: No need to build the facilities yourself; just own one of each type. Requires acquiring a coastal province to build naval facilities.

## First train home

Condition: Owns and controls one of: Moscow (219), Ivanovo (253), Gorky (252), Chuvashia (256), Kazan (249), Udmurtia (399), Perm (398), Zlatoust (573), Sverdlovsk (653), Chelyabinsk (572), Tyumen (403), Omsk (571), Novosibirsk (570), Khakassia (569), Krasnoyarsk (568), Bratsk (567), Irkutsk (566), Chita (563), Amur (561), Birobidzhan (657), Khabarovsk (409), Vladivostok (408).

Strategy: Ahistorical Soviet Union is typically weaker; join the 2nd Russian Civil War via Intervene in the Soviet Civil War to claim territory in a peace deal.

## Ain't like that now

Condition: Has completed focus Return to Democracy.

Strategy: Go fascist and complete Expand the Rome Protocols before late 1937 to invite Austria into your faction and prevent Anschluss; hold Germany's attack with fighters and state AA while destroying their air force, then push and capitulate them; after reaching 1941 at peace, complete Return to Democracy.

## Czechoslovaustrohungarian Union

Condition: Has completed focus Expand the Union; Hungary is a subject of Czechoslovakia and has the "Integration efforts" national spirit; Austria is a subject of Czechoslovakia and has the "Integration efforts" national spirit.

Strategy: Go communist, conquer or puppet Austria and Hungary (if they refuse the puppet event, attack them manually), then complete Expand the Union and grant both the "Integration efforts" national spirit via decision.
