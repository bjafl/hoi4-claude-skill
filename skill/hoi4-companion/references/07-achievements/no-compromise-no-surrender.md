No Compromise, No Surrender achievements focus on the Pacific theater and Asian nations—particularly China's civil war, Japanese expansion, and Philippine unification. These 17 achievements range from simple territorial control to complex diplomatic and military sequences involving infiltration, fascist conversions, and nuclear weapons.

## Battle Royale

**Condition:** Playing as Chinese Soviet Republic. Has declared war on China and Japan within 90 days of another, before 01.01.1940. Has capitulated China and Japan.

**Strategy:** Requires rapid military conquest while managing two simultaneous wars before 1940.

## The Winner takes it all

**Condition:** Playing as Chinese Soviet Republic. Owns and controls all starting core states of Chinese Soviet Republic.

**Strategy:** Can be easily accomplished on historical mode, as long as Sinkjiang doesn't become a Soviet puppet. Note that this achievement requires ownership of your cores instead of just occupation. Although this could potentially mean a total war with the allies, the PRC actually has a focus to just request these treaty ports from their occupiers. If you use the tree to switch to Democratic and don't cause much world tension by justifying on anything, it seems that they'll give them up easily to you.

## Master Infiltrator

**Condition:** Playing as Chinese Soviet Republic. Has entered a war with a country that controls at least 5 Chinese Soviet Republic core states with infiltration level 3. Is not at war with any Chinese country except the Reorganized National Government (before the war started). Does not control any states other than Yulin (1046), Shaanbei (622), and Yan'an (1032) (when the war starts).

**Strategy:** Very easily obtained by taking the historical Mao path with historical focuses on. Just take as many advisors that give you PP as possible. You need to make sure China does well enough to not die to Japan though - Japanese occupation prevents you from infiltrating states/will reset your infiltration progress. Don't bother infiltrating Warlord states, as both the warlord and China have decisions to remove your infiltration, meaning it gets removed much faster than just infiltrating China's states. You don't even have to actually win against Japan; if you progress far enough down the tree you'll be able to leave the United front and make your own faction, letting you declare war and get the achievement earlier.

## Its all coming together

**Condition:** Playing as China. You or your subjects fully control all starting cores of China.

## The Coast is Clear

**Condition:** Playing as China. After Sep 2nd, 1945. Has not lost Qingdao (743), Jiangsu (598), Suzhou (1034), Shanghai (613), Zhejiang (596) or Fujian (595).

**Strategy:** Most easily done with historical focuses turned off; Japan can potentially go Hokushin-ron, which leads to them making a non-aggression pact with you and attacking the USSR instead. Can be combined with "It's all coming together" and "A Fullmetal Brotherhood" if played safely. Keep in mind that it's just China's starting coastal states & not all their cores, so you can afford to lose coastal states that you conquer later (be careful with Qingdao though, it's a one province state so it can easily be lost by mistake).

## A Fullmetal Brotherhood

**Condition:** Playing as China. Ruling party is Fascist. Extracting at least 300 Steel. Extracting at least 300 Tungsten. Extracting at least 300 Chromium.

**Strategy:** China has enough tungsten and steel to fulfill the requirements. Chromium will have to be conquered.

## Paradisus Paradoxum

**Condition:** Playing as Japan. Has cosmetic tag Jobukkokudo.

## An Equivalent Exchange

**Condition:** Playing as Japan. Ruling party is Democratic. Has completed focus "Foreign Exchange Allocation Policy". Has capitulated Fascist Germany.

**Strategy:** As with all capitulation achievements, you need to be careful; Even if you've occupied most of a country's territory, they can capitulate to another country if that country has a greater war contribution score. The Germans will not begin Operation Barbarossa so long as France hasn't capitulated, so France's survival is your best bet to ensure Soviet neutrality. Germany's main strength lies in their air force, so build plenty of high-quality fighters and equip divisions with anti-air guns. You can't get this achievement if Germany isn't fascist anymore, so don't try it on ahistorical.

## The Sun Will Never Set

**Condition:** Playing as Japan. Has completed focus The Eternal Empire. Fully controls all your cores.

## The Juannines

**Condition:** Playing as Philippines. Fully controls and has cores on all cores of Spain.

**Strategy:** You need to go down the "Election Overturned" Hispanic road. After that, complete the rest of the Hispanic focuses and take decisions with Hispanic countries with the Hispanic Overtures focus. I recommend improving relations and using a diplomatic pressure agent. After completing the "War of Independence", you need to hold the USA for a year to get the white peace event. Then, take the "Reino de Filipinas" focus and unify with the faction member countries, in that specific case, with Spain. Because of the civil war, you need to core half of Spain manually when you reach the necessary compliance level.

## Declare Military Emergency

**Condition:** Playing as Philippines. Has gotten the event "The Military Emergency".

**Strategy:** For this you need to complete the Assassination of Quezon focus after the focus Police Chief Eisenhower has been completed previously. Make sure to select the "Police Chief Eisenhower steps in" option in the assassination event. The achievement will fire after Macarthur seizes power. Since all events in the chain have a MTTH, it can take a while after taking the assassination focus before you get your achievement.

## Cobalt Sea

**Condition:** Playing as Philippines (Götterdämmerung is enabled, No Compromise, No Surrender is enabled). Has launched a Nuclear Missile Strike or Thermonuclear Missile Strike from a Nuclear Missile Submarine with Success or Critical Success outcome.

## The Neverending Story

**Condition:** Playing as Guangxi Clique, Yunnan, Shanxi, Qinghai Ma, Sinkiang, Guangdong Clique, Shandong Clique, Sichuan Clique, Ningxia Ma, Gansu Ma, Northeastern Army, Khotan Ma, or Kumul Khanate. Has completed focus "Restoration of the Chinese Empire". Fully controls all starting cores of Chinese Soviet Republic.

## Genghis Khan't

**Condition:** Playing as China, Chinese Soviet Republic, Guangxi Clique, Yunnan, Shanxi, Xibei San Ma, Qinghai Ma, Sinkiang, Guangdong Clique, Shandong Clique, Sichuan Clique, Ningxia Ma, Gansu Ma, Northeastern Army, Khotan Ma, Kumul Khanate, Hebei-Chahar, Reorganized National Government, or Manchukuo. Owns and controls all cores of Mongolia.

**Strategy:** It's not difficult to do this in a regular historical game by just beating Russia with the Axis after pushing out Japan. However, it can be made incredibly easy in non-historical: if Russia does any of their alt history paths but especially if they go Tsarist, then Mongolia gets spit out of their faction and is easily conquered.

## I Have Avenged You, Father!

**Condition:** Playing as Northeastern Army. Zhang Xueliang is the current country leader. Fully controls Liaoning (716). Has completed focus "Revive the Beiyang Government".

**Strategy:** Dont go historical. Restart until Japan goes against the military. Manchukuo will become independent and you can beat them 1v1.

## Manifesting a Better Tomorrow

**Condition:** Faction manifest fulfillment is greater than 99%.

**Strategy:** Can be very easily and quickly completed by playing as Czechoslovakia and completing the focus An Entente of Our Own, the faction will start with 100% manifest.

## Doctrine of Choice

**Condition:** Has completed any of the same subdoctrine track more than two times.

**Strategy:** The description of this is somewhat obtuse, but what it actually requires is; fully complete any subdoctrine path, then swap it out to a different subdoctrine & complete it again 2 different times. (ex: Fully fill out "Defensive Postures" subdoctrine under Infantry, then swap to Assault Infantry, etc).
