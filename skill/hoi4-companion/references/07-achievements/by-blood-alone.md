## Summary

The By Blood Alone DLC introduces 19 achievements focused on Switzerland, Ethiopia, Italy, Brazil, and general mechanics. Achievements span difficulty from Medium to Very Hard, requiring territorial conquest, faction manipulation, and specific mechanical conditions.

## …and you get a canton, everybody gets a canton!

**Playing as:** Switzerland
**Condition:** Owns more than 24 states
**Strategy:** For detailed strategies, see the individual achievement page.

## I'm not locked in here with you…

**Playing as:** Switzerland
**Condition:** Has declared war on Germany AND has won the war against Germany
**Strategy:** After the Anschluss, justify a quick war goal on Germany using the claim on Vorarlberg. Launch the attack once the Allies have made successful landings or achieved air superiority, then fall back to a defensive line and wait for Germany to capitulate.

## Swiss Cheese

**Playing as:** Switzerland
**Condition:** Has 5 states not contiguous to each other after a peace conference
**Strategy:** On historical difficulty, rush the Fascist path and join the Axis. Immediately justify and declare war on Vichy France. Islands count as non-contiguous states.

## You Shall Not Pass

**Playing as:** Switzerland
**Condition:** Has won a defensive war without losing Western Swiss Alps (847), Eastern Swiss Alps (151), or Ticino (846)
**Strategy:** For detailed strategies, see the individual achievement page.

## The Lion that Roared

**Playing as:** Ethiopia
**Condition:** Forced the Italians to make peace (while not in a faction)
**Strategy:** Either push Italy into the seas or hold territory until the AI offers peace. Alternatively, complete the focus Fait Accompli to end the war.

## The Lion King

**Playing as:** Ethiopia
**Condition:** Has completed focus The King of Kings AND Haile Selassie is the current country leader AND controls Tanganyika (546), Mombasa (905), Garissa (903), Nyanza-Rift Valley (904), Nairobi (547)
**Strategy:** For detailed strategies, see the individual achievement page.

## This time for Africa

**Playing as:** Ethiopia
**Condition:** Has completed focus Towards African Unity AND is in a faction with at least 13 of the following countries: South Africa, Somali Sultanate, Sultanate of Aussa, Djibouti, Eritrea, Sudan, Kingdom of Egypt, Kingdom of Libya, Tunisia, Algeria, Kingdom of Morocco, Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic, Mauritania, Mali, Senegal, The Gambia, Guinea-Bissau, Guinea, Upper Volta, Niger, Kingdom of Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Togo, Dahomey, Nigeria, Chad, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Zaire, Congo-Brazzaville, Angola, Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, Kenya, Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Mozambique, Zimbabwe, Botswana, Namibia, Republic of Madagascar
**Strategy:** For detailed strategies, see the individual achievement page.

## Stalin, how many guns does he have?

**Playing as:** Italy
**Condition:** Play as Italy and capitulate the Soviet Union while having the Pope in power
**Strategy:** Continue after completing Holy, Roman, and an Empire. After forming the Roman Empire, you will have sufficient resources and a large navy (take France and UK navies in the peace deal) to defeat the USSR.

## Holy, Roman, and an Empire

**Playing as:** Italy
**Condition:** The Pope has taken over Italy AND Italy has formed the Roman Empire
**Strategy:** For detailed strategies, see the individual achievement page.

## Pizza Time!

**Playing as:** Italy
**Condition:** Controls New York (358), Illinois (395), Hawaii (629)
**Strategy:** Continue after completing Holy, Roman, and an Empire. The Roman Empire's resources and navy are sufficient to invade and occupy the specified US territories.

## Collect all the Romes

**Playing as:** Italy
**Condition:** Controls Lazio (2), Istanbul (797), Moscow (219)
**Strategy:** Continue after completing Holy, Roman, and an Empire. After forming the Roman Empire, you will have enough military power to capture Istanbul and Moscow.

## Nothing personal, Adolf

**Playing as:** Italy
**Condition:** After Jan 1st, 1945 AND Italy has never joined a faction with Fascist Germany AND owns Niederösterreich (4), Oberösterreich (152), Tirol (153), Vorarlberg (848), Burgenland (975), Steiermark-Kärnten (976) AND Germany has not achieved Anschluss
**Strategy:** Justify a war goal on Austria as early as possible; their military is weak and Germany will not attack Austria while you occupy it. Annex Austria, then wait until after January 1st, 1945 for the achievement to fire. Never join a fascist Germany faction.

## Not today

**Playing as:** Italy
**Condition:** Ruling party is Communist AND Antonio Gramsci is the current country leader AND has completed focus Il Sol dell'Avvenire
**Strategy:** Rush the Communist focus tree directly toward Il Sol dell'Avvenire.

## This time it will stick

**Playing as:** Any Allied Nation (in faction with democratic Britain)
**Condition:** The Rhineland has been disarmed and demilitarized (both Rhineland and Moselland states must be demilitarized)
**Strategy:** Enforce a peace deal on Germany after victory, specifying demilitarization of the Rhineland.

## By Beer Alone

**Playing as:** Germany
**Condition:** You or a faction member controls Bohemia (9), Leinster (113), Qingdao (743)
**Strategy:** For detailed strategies, see the individual achievement page.

## By merit alone

**Condition:** Any unit leader has been promoted from the ranks with Skill Level more than 8
**Strategy:** Generals or field marshals directly commanding divisions gain experience much faster than field marshals commanding armies. Can be done in parallel with Sun Tzu Reborn while doing Battlecry or Awake and Angry, or with Holy, Roman, and an Empire.

## Snakes on a plane

**Playing as:** Brazil
**Condition:** Has captured Lazio (2) with paratroopers
**Strategy:** On historical difficulty, join the Allies. Allocate a factory to transport planes and train paratroopers. Increase special forces cap via focuses and technology, training additional regular troops. When Allies invade Italy and push inland, launch paratroopers on Rome using Allied air superiority.
