Fourteen achievements gated behind the Together for Victory DLC, spanning colonial liberation, dominion conquest, and faction manipulation across the British Empire and its members.

## Braaaaains!

**Condition:** Playing as Canada, complete the Send in the Zombies focus.

Strategy note: Rush economic focuses; when Germany invades Poland, join the war and push down to Send in the Zombies.

## District 9

**Condition:** Playing as South Africa, have at least 9 civilian factories in Transvaal (275).

Strategy note: Convert military factories or follow the focus tree for free civilian factories; if building slots fill, research Dispersed or Concentrated Industry to unlock more slots.

## Our Words Are Backed With Nuclear Weapons

**Condition:** Playing as British Raj, complete one of Quit India Movement, Self Rule - Purna Swaraj, or Graveyard of Empires focus; develop and deploy a nuclear bomb.

Strategy note: Head for Quit India Movement, develop nukes and strategic bombers for air superiority. Viable targets: Romania (via Turkey joining Allies) or Siam (via Japan).

## Crusader Kings

**Condition:** Playing as South Africa, complete A King for our People focus and control Palestine (454) while having Edward VIII national spirit.

## Crusader Kings 2

**Condition:** Playing as South Africa, complete Anti-Colonialist Crusade focus, release Angola, Mozambique, Zimbabwe, Kenya, and Zaire via event (not occupation screen), become faction leader, and control Greater London Area (126).

Strategy note: Requires careful timing and military preparation. Liberate Belgian Colonies focus is broken since Götterdämmerung; disable that DLC or manually justify war on Belgian Congo. Research marines, submarines, naval invasions early. Conquer Portugal by 1938, then Belgium, then coordinate with Germany's invasion to eventually invade and capitulate Britain; alternatively, boost Communist influence in Britain and trigger civil war while invading from France.

## Rule Britannia

**Condition:** Playing as Australia, British Raj, Canada, New Zealand, or South Africa (fully independent), control all 16 British states: South-West England (123), Sussex (127), Gloucestershire (338), Greater London Area (126), East Anglia (125), Wales (122), West Midlands (128), East Midlands (129), Lancashire (132), Yorkshire (130), Northumberland (131), Lanark (133), Lothian (121), Aberdeenshire (136), Scottish Highlands (120).

## Cut The Strings

**Condition:** Starting as a puppet state, become fully independent.

Strategy note: As Manchukuo, rush down the Assertiveness path toward Independence War.

## It's 1812 All Over Again

**Condition:** Playing as Canada, control Maryland (361).

Strategy note: Pick Patriation and Swastika Clubs as first focuses; when switching ideology, select Support a Synarchist Baja and recruit Mexico into your faction to attack the United States on the East Coast.

## The weapons of the Third Emu War

**Condition:** Playing as Australia, drop a nuclear bomb on a core Australian territory.

Strategy note: Develop fighters, strategic bombers, and nukes; allow Japan to conquer one Australian state and nuke it (use your own fighters or wait for Allied air superiority support).

## I Am the Tong Master

**Condition:** Playing as South Africa, become fully independent and have at least one subject.

Strategy note: Release Namibia as a puppet at game start.

## The Empire Strikes Back

**Condition:** Playing as United Kingdom, declare war on one of your former subjects (South Africa, Canada, British Raj, Australia, New Zealand, British Malaya).

Strategy note: Follow any sub-branch under A Change in Course; the Dominions will break ties automatically.

## The Puppetmaster

**Condition:** Have at least 51 subjects.

Strategy note: Play as Communist Britain, declare war on France, puppet as many of their colonies as possible, then release subjects via focus tree.

## Freegypt

**Condition:** As Egypt, progress through autonomy system: reach Colony Status, Dominion Status, then Freedom Status.

Strategy note: Play as Britain and build up Egypt with factories. Release as puppet when full of military factories (check "Play as"). Increase autonomy via lend-lease (trains and convoys recommended), continuous focus, and military attaché.
