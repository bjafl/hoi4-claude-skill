The Battle for the Bosporus DLC adds 24 achievements focused on Eastern European, Turkish, and Middle Eastern nations. Achievements include reshaping the Balkans, controlling key strategic territories, and navigating complex political systems in Turkey and Yugoslavia.

## Finnish Him!

**Condition:** Playing as Finland; Has defeated the Soviet Union without joining a faction.

## I Captured the Bosporus and All I Got Was This Lousy Achievement

**Condition:** Playing as Greece, Bulgaria, or Turkey.
- If Greece or Bulgaria: Owns Istanbul (797).
- If Turkey: Has Bulgaria as a subject or owns all Bulgarian core states; Has Greece as a subject or owns all Greek core states (except Dodecaneso (164)).

## Istanbul is Constantinople. Again.

**Condition:** Playing as Greece; Owns Istanbul (797); Istanbul has been renamed to Constantinople.

## Peter's Pride

**Condition:** Playing as Yugoslavia; Peter II is the current country leader.

Strategy: Complete a focus such as End the Regency to put Peter in charge.

## This is Madness!

**Condition:** Playing as Greece; Has formed Greater Greece.

Strategy: The Megali Idea involves coordinating with UK and France to split Turkey; you gain guaranteed land in the peace deal. UK and France may bail individually after the focus completes, but Turkey is beatable with one or both. Greater Greece can be formed via Nenikikamen (if leader is not Georgios Kosmidis), The Modern Movement (with Ioannis Metaxas), or Prepare for the Great Anti-Fascist War.

## Nobody's Business but the Turks

**Condition:** Playing as Turkey; Has completed focus Return of the Sultan.

Strategy: Win a small civil war to access the focus.

## House of Kurds

**Condition:** Original country is Kurdistan or has become Kurdistan; Controls Hakkari (352), Diyarbakır (350), Van (800), Tunceli (353), Malatya (344), East Azerbaijan (1000), West Azerbaijan (419), Kurdistan (1001), Ilam (421), Deir-az-Zur (680).

Strategy: As of patch 1.16.9, must control Malatya (344) which is only a claim, not a core. Release Kurdistan after seizing required states; achievement fires upon control of all listed states.

## Hellenic Civility

**Condition:** Playing as Greece; Has won the Greek Civil War before January 1st, 1937.

Strategy: Delete all land units pre-war and build a single cavalry division. Deploy in Athens post-war start and cap all victory points. Naval invade Crete (182) if necessary. With Battle for the Bosporus, trigger civil war via The Right to Rule path; time Reorganize the Royal Guard to complete one day after civil war starts. If units are pre-deleted, the two granted units from the focus suffice to win immediately.

## Hoofin' It

**Condition:** United Arabia with 30 Camelry Divisions; Owns all of North Yemen (293), Nejd (292), Al-Qassim (857), Najiran (858), Jawf (854), Tabuk (855), Asir-Makkah (856), Dammam (859), Muscat (294), Abu Dhabi (658), Qatar (765), South Yemen (659), Madinah (679), Bahrain (1014), Musandam (1013), Oman (1015), Dhofar (1016), Province of Aden (992); More than 4499 infantry equipment in armies; More than 29 Camelry battalions.

Strategy: Easiest as United Kingdom (already controls most territory). Take The King's Party focus route, justify and annex Saudi Arabia, Oman, Yemen. Build camel template and train 30 divisions (unlocked by default). Territory must be owned directly, not puppeted or occupied. May need to annex British Raj if Aden is still held. Camelry unlocks: Ethiopia (Animal Husbandry, requires By Blood Alone), Turkey (Desert Camel Corps and Tuz Gölü Training Facility, requires Battle for the Bosporus), Sultanate of Aussa (at game start).

## We're Putting the Band Back Together

**Condition:** Playing as Turkey; Is in a faction with Germany and Austria-Hungary.

## Dracula's Revenge

**Condition:** Playing as Yugoslavia; Transylvania owns all Romanian core states.

Strategy: Pre-1.12 / By Blood Alone: Go communist toward Pan-Balkan Initiative, instigate uprising in Romania, justify and declare war on them after they flip communist, puppet Transylvania in peace deal and award them all Romanian territory. Post-1.12: Either go Western and release Transylvania as puppet via Autonomy for Slavic Transylvania before defeating Romania, or go Communist and defeat Romania twice (once to create puppet, once to give them remaining cores). Communist route races against Soviet Union demanding Bessarabia mid-1940.

## Prussia of the Balkans

**Condition:** Playing as Bulgaria; Is at war with Turkey; Turkey has capitulated; Controls all core states of Romania, Yugoslavia, Greece (except Dodecaneso (164)), and Albania.

Strategy: Only need to occupy states, not own them. Go communist, rally Balkan nations (prioritize Romania and Greece). Annex via The Unification of the Balkans focus. Attack remaining Balkan holders and capitulate Turkey. Turkey must be in a faction when it capitulates to satisfy "at war" and "has capitulated" requirements; otherwise the achievement will not fire.

## Balkan Problem Solved

**Condition:** Playing as Bulgaria; Ruling party is Communist; Is faction leader; Has completed focus The Unification of the Balkans; Controls all Bulgarian core states.

Strategy: Do not need to own all cores of Romania, Greece, Yugoslavia, Albania; only need to control all Bulgarian cores. Ensure control of Dodecaneso (164).

## Bad Romeance

**Condition:** Playing as Greece; Byzantine Empire has Soviet Union, Romania, and Italy as subjects.

## Totally Not Cool, Bromania

**Condition:** Playing as Romania; Romania has switched sides and capitulated a former ally (which had not capitulated at the time of the switch).

Strategy: Revise the Constitution → Balkans Dominance (500k+ manpower). Align Hungary (force if refused), Split Czechoslovakia (Germany agrees). Appoint Pro-Axis Government → Fascist Demagogue → Force Abdication (flip to fascism, remove demagogue). Wait for Germany to attack Poland and Bulgaria to join Axis; join the war but exclude Hungary and Slovakia. Place army on Bulgarian border. King Michael's Coup (flip to democratic); wait for Poland to capitulate and USSR to take Eastern Poland. Join Allies, capitulate Bulgaria.

## Freedom or Death

**Condition:** Playing as Greece; Is at war with Italy; Has full control of Northern Epirus (805), Central Albania (44), and Shkodër (934).

Strategy: Go fascist, justify on Albania. Italy guarantees Albania so will join on your declaration. Rush for the port to prevent Italian troop landings. Capitulate Albania and annex all their land.

## Now This is Getting Childish

**Condition:** Playing as Bulgaria or Yugoslavia; Bulgaria is at war with Yugoslavia; Bulgaria: Regency Council is the current country leader; Yugoslavia: country leader has the Underage Monarch trait.

Strategy: Bulgaria: Go through Form a Regency Council focus, then Plot Against Boris (kill him via decisions), pick pro-Axis regent, join Axis. Yugoslavia: Peter II loses Underage Monarch trait in September 1941 (turns 18); Tsar Boris III dies in 1943 under historical AI. Unclear if this is achievable as Yugoslavia due to timing mismatch. Prince Paul becomes Peter II in February 1941; Germany attacks Yugoslavia soon after. Join the war to get the achievement.

## Hardly Anything Sèvres

**Condition:** Playing as Turkey (Ottoman Sultanate or Ottoman Empire cosmetic tag, or ideology variants); Controls Ile de France (16), Greater London Area (126), Lazio (2), and Kanto (282).

## One Nation Under Atatürk, Indivisible

**Condition:** Playing as Turkey; Battle for the Bosporus is enabled; Ruling party is Non-Aligned; Has zero negative state modifiers: Kurdish Agitation, Kurdish Separatism, Kurdish Rebellion, Traditionalist Opposition, Traditionalist Sedition, Traditionalist Insurgency, Kemalist Schemers.

Strategy: Go Kemalist route via Continue the Policy of Etatism and Peace at Home focuses. Use Silent Workhorse advisor and Secularism decision repeatedly to block Islamist expansion. Türk Ulusu focus pacifies Kurdish states; handle Islamist opposition via Counter-Fundamentalist Operation or compromise decisions (Kemalist must remain loyal). Achievement fires when all Islamic states are Placated and Kurdish states reach Separatist Fatigue or are cored. Requires only political power, not warfare. Alternative: follow Ottoman route, participate in WW2, lose; Kurdistan separates and removes Kurdish negative modifiers.

## Master Puppeteer

**Condition:** Playing as Bulgaria; Has completed focus The Fate of the Balkans; At least 6 of the following are subjects: Romania, Yugoslavia, Hungary, Greece, Albania, Serbia, Turkey, Slovenia, Bosnia, Herzegovina, Macedonia, Independent State of Croatia, Kosovo, Republic of Montenegro.

Strategy: After completing The Fate of the Balkans, peacefully puppet Balkan nations in your faction or force them into faction first. Puppet Yugoslavia can be integrated with constituent states released as puppets (Macedonia, Serbia). Seize state 803 (106) from Yugoslavia via decision. To get states to join faction via decision, deploy more manpower to field; puppet expeditionary forces count toward this total.
