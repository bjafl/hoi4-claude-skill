The Graveyard of Empires DLC adds 18 achievements focused on Middle Eastern and South Asian powers: Afghanistan, Iran, Iraq, the British Raj, and the cosmetic tags they can unlock (Mughal Empire, East India Company, Khalistan, Hashemite Arab Federation).

## Graveyard of Empires

As Afghanistan, Iran, Iraq or the British Raj, control all of the other three nations' starting provinces.

**Condition:** Playing as Afghanistan, Iran, Iraq or British Raj.
- If playing as Afghanistan: Has capitulated Iraq, Iran and British Raj and fully controls their cores.
- If playing as Iraq: Has capitulated Afghanistan, Iran and British Raj and fully controls their cores.
- If playing as Iran: Has capitulated Afghanistan, Iraq and British Raj and fully controls their cores.
- If playing as British Raj: Has capitulated Afghanistan, Iraq and Iran and fully controls their cores.

**Strategy:** Play as British Raj. Take the focuses for Mughal rebellion to seize territories during civil war. Use focuses to attack and annex Afghanistan, Sinkiang, Iran, and Iraq in this order. Main difficulty is acquiring Iraq by late 1940 before the UK puppets it.

## This achievement is dedicated to the brave soldiers of...

As Afghanistan, capitulate the Soviet Union.

**Condition:** Playing as Afghanistan. Capitulate the Soviet Union.

**Strategy:** Must have the highest war score of all participants. May be easier to annex a single Soviet state, release it, then justify a second war against the Soviets alone.

## I have the high ground

As Afghanistan, control all Himalayan states.

**Condition:** Playing as Afghanistan. Controls: Northern Kashmir (787), Kashmir (441), East Punjab (986), United Provinces (438), Nepal (323), Sikkim (985), West Bengal (431), Bhutan (324), Assam (432), Arunachal Pradesh (434), Manipur (990).

## Savior of the holy lands

As Afghanistan, become the owner of Palestine and Mecca.

**Condition:** Playing as Afghanistan. Owns Palestine (454) and Asir-Makkah (856).

## Cradle of Civilization

As Iraq, build the maximum infrastructure level in all core states and achieve 100% stability.

**Condition:** Playing as Iraq. More than 99.9% stability. All passable cores have at least 5 infrastructure.

**Strategy:** Take decisions to improve worker conditions and anti-fascist raids for stability. Build max infrastructure in all states. Take Construction Effort and Infrastructure Effort focuses.

## Oil Sheiks

As Iraq, have 500 oil production.

**Condition:** Playing as Iraq. At least 500 oil production.

## Revenge for 1925

As Iraq, form the Hashemite Arab Federation and defeat Saudi Arabia.

**Condition:** Playing as Iraq. Has cosmetic tag Hashemite Arab Federation. Has capitulated Saudi Arabia.

**Note:** Hashemite Arab Federation cosmetic tag can only be gained by completing the Hashemite Arab Federation national focus.

## Avenging the Sack of Baghdad

As Iraq, control the capital of Mongolia.

**Condition:** Playing as Iraq. Fully controls Ulaanbaatar (330).

**Strategy:** Go fascist and join Germany in attacking the Soviet Union. Mongolia should be in their faction, requiring minimal war score.

## That's Sikh!

As Khalistan, control all of the starting Raj states.

**Condition:** Playing as Khalistan. Control all of India's starting states.

**Note:** Province of Aden (992) is not required.

## Pesky Greeks

As Iran, become the owner of all Greek core states.

**Condition:** Playing as Iran. Owns all cores of Greece.

**Strategy:** Can be done together with The Legacy of Cyrus the Great.

## From Tehran with Love

As Iran, guarantee Poland's independence and ensure it does not capitulate by 1945.

**Condition:** Playing as Iran. After Jan 1st, 1945. Has guaranteed Poland. Poland: Exists, is fully independent, has not capitulated.

## It's Just Good Business

As the East India Company, own Suez and Panama.

**Condition:** Playing as British Raj (has cosmetic tag East India Company). Owns Suez (446) and Panamá Canal (685).

**Strategy:** Beeline for the Hostile Takeover focus. Use decisions to buy Suez and Panamá Canal from the UK and USA. Rush civilian factory focuses to accumulate enough resources by end of 1938.

**Note:** East India Company cosmetic tag can only be gained by completing the Incorporation of the East-India Company national focus.

## Media Mughal

As the Mughal Empire, complete Agency Upgrade Psychological Warfare, become Spy Master and have at least 8 operatives.

**Condition:** Playing as British Raj (has cosmetic tag Mughal Empire). Has completed Agency Upgrade Psychological Warfare. Is Spy Master. At least 8 operatives.

**Note:** Mughal Empire cosmetic tag can only be gained by completing the Unite the Subcontinent focus before completing the Expand the Silk Road focus. Can be combined with I shall either find a way or make one. Guarantee Greek or Yugoslav independence to join Allies in defensive war and become Spy Master.

## Oh, how the turntables

As India puppet the UK with Churchill as its leader.

**Condition:** Playing as British Raj. The United Kingdom is your subject. United Kingdom: Either has the winston_achievement flag set or has Winston Churchill as current country leader.

## I'm just passing by

Own a state on each continent while being at peace.

**Condition:** Playing as British Raj. At peace. Owns a state in: Europe, Asia, Africa, Middle East, North America, South America, Australia.

**Strategy:** Easiest as the East India Company. Use the Just Good Business focus to get Dutch New Guinea (Australia), Curaçao (South America), and Netherlands states (Europe). Purchase Bermuda (North America), French Somaliland or Madagascar (Africa), and Alexandretta (Middle East).

## I shall either find a way or make one

Control Rome and have an elephant unit in the state.

**Condition:** Playing as British Raj. Fully controls Lazio (2). Has a division with elephantry battalion in Lazio (2).

**Note:** Elephants are only available to the Mughal path. Can be combined with Media Mughal.
