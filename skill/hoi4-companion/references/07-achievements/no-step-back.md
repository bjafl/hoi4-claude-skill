No Step Back DLC achievements spanning Eastern European focus trees, Soviet mechanics, Baltic state campaigns, and specialized conditions like railway networks and propaganda campaigns.

## At least they run on time!

**Condition:** Playing as Italy; has railway of level 5 in all core states (Sardegna, Piemonte, Lombardia, Alto Adige, Trentino, Veneto, Litorale, Istria, Emilia Romagna, Abruzzo, Toscana, Lazio, Campania, Calabria, Puglia, Sicilia).

Only a single province per state needs a level 5 railway; connecting level 5 rail through two states counts for both.

## Not much fun in Stalingrad

**Condition:** Playing as Germany; capitulate the Soviet Union without taking Stalingrad or having troops in any province in its state.

Use spies to create a Collaboration Government and lower Soviet stability to decrease surrender threshold. Most easily done with historical focuses on; immediately justify on Poland, then once that war concludes, justify on the Soviets.

## No more Partitions

**Condition:** Playing as Poland; fully independent; Germany either does not exist or is in faction with Poland; Soviet Union either does not exist, is in faction with Poland, or does not control all Poland's core states.

## Habsburgs. Habsburgs everywhere

**Condition:** Playing as Poland; Karl Albrecht I is the current country leader; Hungary has Otto von Habsburg as the current country leader; Poland is in faction with Hungary.

Elect Karl Albrecht I by choosing Assemble the Regency Council and Fulfil the 5th of November Act. Requires No Step Back DLC to unlock the Hungarian focus tree branches that install Otto von Habsburg.

## The Pope? How many Divisions does he have?

**Condition:** Playing as USSR; controls Lazio (Rome).

Can be done relatively easily by defeating the Axis with the Allies and simply taking Lazio in the peace deal.

## This is going to be LIT

**Condition:** Playing as Lithuania; controls Rīga (Latvia), Warszawa (Poland), Brandenburg (Germany), and Kaunas (Lithuania's capital).

## It has my name on it

**Condition:** Playing as Estonia, Latvia, or Lithuania; controls the entire Baltic Sea shoreline: Sjaelland, Jylland, Holstein, Schleswig, Mecklenburg, Hinterpommern, Gdynia, Danzig, Ermland-Masuren, Königsberg, Klaipeda, Žemaitija, Kurzeme, Rīga, Pärnu, Vidzeme, Harju, Saaremaa, Virumaa, Luga, Leningrad, Norrbotten, Gävleborg, Södermanland, Småland, Västergötland, Karjala, Uusimaa, Vaasa, Lappi, Turku, Kymi, Oulu.

## This Achievement is Cheesy

**Condition:** Playing as Latvia; has completed focus Ostland; controls Norrbotten.

The achievement description suggests Västerbotten, but the actual requirement is Norrbotten. Requires No Step Back DLC.

## Esti is Scandi

**Condition:** Playing as Estonia; you or your puppets control all of Scandinavia: Oslofjord, Vestlandet, Trøndelag, Nordland, Norrbotten, Gävleborg, Södermanland, Småland, Västergötland, Skåne, Gotland, Sjaelland, Jylland.

## Not a step back!

**Condition:** Playing as Soviet Union; after 1945, has not lost a core state.

Do not take the focus Reconcile Japan since North Sakhalin is a Soviet core. Turn off historical German focuses while keeping all others on historical. Alternatively, declare war on Poland early, releasing Belarus and Ukraine as puppets while retaining your own cores and leaving them outside the war for an easier defensive line.

## One Step Forward

**Condition:** Playing as Soviet Union; is at war with Poland; is at war with Germany; Germany did not attack Poland first.

Set up a frontline along the Polish border. Justify on Poland, then declare war. Justify and declare war on Germany. Achievement triggers when at war with both simultaneously.

## The Soviet Onion

**Condition:** Playing as Soviet Union; every neighbouring country is your subject (puppet).

## Crusader Kings III

**Condition:** Playing as Poland; has elected a Monarch; controls Palestine (Jerusalem).

Elect the Fascist king, join the Axis, and help them in the Africa campaign. You only need to control Jerusalem; gain enough war score and ask for Palestine in the peace deal. Requires No Step Back DLC.

## The Romanovs laugh last

**Condition:** Playing as Soviet Union; either has national spirit Tsar Vladimir I or Vladimir III is the current country leader; you or your subjects control Brandenburg, Bohemia, Niederösterreich, Northern Hungary, Sofia, Ankara.

Requires No Step Back DLC for the Soviet monarchy branch.

## We don't really like statistics

**Condition:** Playing as Soviet Union under Stalin; has defeated Germany while taking less than 1 million casualties total.

Germany must capitulate to you (if it capitulates to another faction without a popup, the achievement does not trigger). This casualty count applies only to casualties from Germany; you may take any number of casualties from other countries. Achievable in historical playthroughs; easier when Germany goes Oppose Hitler—attack the Monarchist or both sides before they recover from the civil war.

## Race for Germany

**Condition:** Playing as Soviet Union; conquered Germany after the fall of France before any other country took a German core state.

When Germany capitulates, all their core states must fall under Soviet control. France must be in the Allies and capitulated. Attack earlier for better odds. Go down Collective Security and pick Eradicate Western Fascism (mid-1939 for the attack bonus). Refuse the Molotov-Ribbentrop pact and attack Germany when they close in on Paris. A Collaboration Government in Germany can help, or alternatively help Germany kill the Allies by naval invading the UK after France falls, then kill Germany—safer but harder.

## Siberian Tiger

**Condition:** Playing as Tannu Tuva; has formed Siberia.

Requires No Step Back DLC for the Tannu Tuva focus tree.

## Just proper gander

**Condition:** Playing as Soviet Union; completed at least 30 propaganda campaigns before 1945.

Complete "The Path of Marxism Leninism", "Addressing Internal Affairs", "Expand the Agitprop", "The Komsomol" and then either "Positive Heroism" or "Collectivist Propaganda" to unlock Agitprop decisions with three slots. Continuously take these decisions to reach 30 campaigns within five years; start by 1939 to ensure completion before 1945.

## Around Eurasia in 80 days

**Condition:** Railway connection exists between Gibraltar and Singapore.

For railways to connect, provinces must be owned by members of the same faction. Shortest route: Gibraltar–Spain–France–Germany–Poland–Soviet Union–Iran–British Raj–British Burma–Siam–British Malaya.

## Poland Peasant Revolution

**Condition:** Playing as Poland; has completed focus Organize the Peasant's Strike; not in a civil war; ruling party is Democratic or Communist.

Take focus Organize the Peasant's Strike and then take decisions to claim provinces and expand the strike to factories. Even a few provinces allow easy civil war victory. Requires No Step Back DLC.
