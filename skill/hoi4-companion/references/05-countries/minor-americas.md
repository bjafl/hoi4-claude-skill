## Bolivian Republic

### Summary

Bolivia is a minor South American country with a population of 2.38 million, bordered by Brazil, Peru, Paraguay, Argentina, and Chile. Politically divided between democratic, communist, fascist, and non-aligned factions, Bolivia begins recovering from the Chaco War (1932–1935) against Paraguay, a burden reflected in its national spirits. It is guaranteed independence by the United States via the Monroe Doctrine.

## Colombia

### Summary

Colombia is a minor South American nation in a favorable geographical position bordering both the Caribbean Sea and Pacific Ocean. It begins as a democracy under the Liberal Party with limited military and industrial capacity but significant oil reserves. The country is guaranteed independence by the United States via the Monroe Doctrine, constraining regional expansion but providing diplomatic security.

### Government and political situation

Colombia is ruled by the **Partido Liberal Colombiano (PLC)** with 99% popularity, led by **Alfonso López Pumarejo** in 1936. (Eduardo Santo assumes leadership in 1939.) Elections occur every four years with the next scheduled for February 1938.

Opposition parties exist but hold minimal support: **Partido Comunista Colombiano (PCC)** 1%, Conservador 0%, Fascist 0%. The Liberal party aligns with democratic ideology; shifting to communism or fascism requires significant political effort via national focus or appointed advisors. Switching to fascism avoids civil war if done via "population reforms" around the 1938 election; communism yields the alternate name "Colombian Socialist Republic," while fascism becomes "Nueva Gran Colombia" (referencing the historical Gran Colombia period when Venezuela and Ecuador were included).

### National spirits

**Monroe Doctrine** — Colombia's starting national spirit. The independence of Colombia is guaranteed by the United States, meaning US military protection against invasion by other nations.

> ⚠️ The Monroe Doctrine spirit was extracted from the wiki infobox which uses template artifacts `[Monroe Doctrine icon]`. The guarantee mechanism and restrictions are inferred from the Diplomacy section.

### Unique mechanics

No unique mechanics documented on the wiki. Colombia uses the generic national focus tree, which offers five main branches: Army Effort (army experience/research), Aviation Effort (air experience/bases/research), Naval Effort (navy experience/dockyards/research), Industrial Effort (factory construction, infrastructure, extra research slots), and Political Effort (ideology conversion options).

See [01-mechanics/national-focus.md](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md) (generic tree) for the full focus tree structure.

### Geography and terrain

Colombia occupies a mountainous region with jungle provinces and difficult terrain causing high attrition. The Andes mountains run through Cundinamarca; most provinces feature jungle or mountain terrain except the La Guajira Desert in the north (represented as forest in-game) and plains in portions of La Libertad and Meta. The capital Bogota sits in mountainous Cundinamarca.

| State | Cities | Terrain Composition | Population | Region |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cundinamarca | Bogota | 1 Jungle, 6 Mountain | 4,953,049 | Urban Region |
| La Libertad | Cartagena, Medellín | 1 Forest, 1 Hills, 3 Jungle, 4 Mountain, 2 Plains | 2,826,781 | Developed Rural Region |
| Meta | — | 5 Jungle, 2 Plains | 213,160 | Pastoral Region |

Major rivers: Cauca and Magdalena flow north into the Caribbean (La Libertad); Meta flows northeast to Orinoco; Amazon Basin rivers flow east to Brazil.

### Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Land borders:** Venezuela (northeast), Panama (northwest), Ecuador (southeast), Peru (south), Brazil (east/southeast)

**Sea borders:** Caribbean Sea (north), Western Canal Zone (Pacific, west; controlled by United States)

**Diplomatic status:** Colombia's independence is guaranteed by the United States under the Monroe Doctrine. All neighboring countries (Panama, Ecuador, Peru, Venezuela) are similarly guaranteed. However, the US guarantee applies primarily to European intervention in the Americas; war between Latin American nations is not automatically prevented.

**Regional context:** Panama (Democratic) is directly northwest, split by the strategically important Panama Canal held by the United States with strong defensive forces. Venezuela (Fascist) lies to the northeast. Ecuador (Democratic) and Peru (Democratic but with rising Fascist influence in 1936, ~51% Fascist party popularity) are southern neighbors. Brazil (Non-Aligned) borders to the east. The British, Dutch, and French colonies in the Caribbean and Guiana region are nearby but not guaranteed by the US, making them potential targets if exploited during major-power conflicts.

### Military overview

**Army:** 6 infantry brigades (Brigada del Ejército type), each with 3 infantry battalions, 1 cavalry battalion, and engineer support. Total manpower: ~25.8K.

**Navy:** 2 destroyers of the Antioquia-class (Early Destroyer hull type, Light II engine); 15 convoys for trade supply. Total manpower: 500.

**Air Force:** 30 P-6 fighter aircraft (without By Blood Alone DLC; Inter-War Fighter if that DLC is active).

**Doctrine:** Uses generic doctrines (Grand Battleplan, Fleet in Being, Strategic Destruction).

### Law and economy

|---|---|---|

The low industrial capacity and mandatory civilian factory allocation mean early gameplay focuses on research (especially Industrial Effort focus branches) and trade to acquire missing resources. Coal and oil are the only domestically available resources; all others must be traded for via civilian factories or convoys.

## Dominican Republic

Dominican Republic is a small Caribbean island nation with 1.25M population controlling one state (Hispaniola) alongside Haiti. It begins 1936 as a fascist dictatorship under Rafael Trujillo with minimal industry and military, guaranteed independence by the United States via the Monroe Doctrine. The country lacks a unique focus tree and relies on the generic tree for all ideological and military advancement paths.

**Tag:** DOM

**Economy:** Civilian economy law; Volunteer Only conscription; Export Focus trade law (+10% construction and research speed, +10% factory output, 50% resources to market).

### Government and political situation

Dominican Republic starts as **Fascist** with no elections. The ruling party is **Partido Dominicano** (100% popularity) led by **Rafael Trujillo**.

Opposition parties present but suppressed:
- **Democratic** (0%, leader Jacinto Gonzáles) — becomes ruling ideology if player chooses democratic path
- **Communist** (0%, leader Fernando Bayo) — becomes ruling ideology if player chooses communist path
- **Non-Aligned** (0%, leader Horacio Cabral) — alternative fascist/despotic path

Possible future leader: **Jacinto Peynado** (Fascist, available 1939) under Partido Dominicano.

Available government ministers include industrial/diplomatic organizers (Elias Brache, Manuel de Jesús Troncoso, Jacinto Bienvenido Peynado) and military staff across doctrine, maneuver, naval, and air specializations. No leader traits are listed for Trujillo or Peynado.

### National spirits

Dominican Republic starts with one national spirit:

**Monroe Doctrine**
- Effect: Independence guaranteed by the United States. (In-game: prevents United States from freely justifying war goals against DOM without triggering guarantor mechanics; solidifies US diplomatic protection.)

This guarantee persists unless the USA is conquered or DOM joins an opposing faction.

### Unique mechanics

No unique mechanics documented on the wiki. Dominican Republic uses the **generic national focus tree** for all advancement paths:

- **Army/Aviation/Naval Effort branches** grant experience and unlock research bonuses for respective military domains, plus construction of bases.
- **Industrial Effort branch** unlocks civilian and military factory construction (even beyond building-slot limits) plus infrastructure and additional research slots (+3 total).
- **Political Effort branch** enables ideological conversion and path selection (Democratic, Communist, Non-Aligned, or deeper Fascism).

See [01-mechanics/national-focus.md](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md) (generic tree) for the full generic tree.

Dominican Republic has no unique cosmetic unit names or equipment designer options beyond the generic military industrial organization menu.

### Neighbours and geography

**Direct neighbours:**
- **Haiti** (shares island, west of DOM)
- **Cuba** (nearby to the north, across water)

**Faction status (1936):** Unaligned. The Monroe Doctrine guarantee from the USA is diplomatic, not a faction alliance.

**Strategic position:** Island nation in the Caribbean. Borders on weak states (Haiti) and regional powers (Cuba, eventually United States involvement if war spreads). Suitable for regional conquest focused on Caribbean/Central American states (Costa Rica, Honduras, Nicaragua, Panama) and mainland South American expansion, though minimal starting military makes early expansion slow without focus-tree military buffs.

Historical note: After 1941, historical Dominican Republic declared war on Japan and joined the Allies; in-game, this is determined by player choice via political focuses and the faction system.
