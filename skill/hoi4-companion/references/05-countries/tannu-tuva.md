## Summary

Tannu Tuva is a minor communist country in East Asia bordered by the Soviet Union to the north and Mongolia to the south. It begins the game as part of the Comintern with only 1 state, 85,000 population, and minimal industry. While it uses the generic focus tree, its geographic position and Soviet alignment offer opportunities to expand into Chinese warlord territory with Soviet support.

### Military

Tannu Tuva starts with a single infantry division (Strelkovaya Polk) containing 2 battalions, using 2,000 manpower. It has no navy or air force.

## Government and political situation

Tannu Tuva is ruled by the **Communist Party of the Soviet Union** at 100% popularity, with Salchak Toka as the starting leader. The communist ideology grants access to the following capabilities: force government on other countries, puppet nations, and send volunteer forces.

Three other political parties exist but have 0% popularity:
- **Democratic** (Generic leader; country name: Tuva Independent Republic)
- **Fascist** (Generic leader; country name: Tuvan Nationalist Republic)
- **Non-Aligned** (Generic leader; country name: Tannu Uriankhai)

## National spirits

Tannu Tuva does not have any national spirit.

## Unique mechanics

Tannu Tuva lacks a unique national focus tree and instead uses the **generic focus tree** with five main branches: Army Effort (army experience and research), Aviation Effort (air experience, air bases, air research), Naval Effort (navy experience, naval dockyards, navy research), Industrial Effort (civilian and military factories, infrastructure, extra research slots), and Political Effort (ideological choices).

No other unique decisions or events specific to Tannu Tuva are documented on the wiki.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

Tannu Tuva is bordered by:
- **Soviet Union** (north)
- **Mongolia** (south)

The country is a member of the **Comintern** alongside the Soviet Union and Mongolia. The Soviet Union can demand annexation of Tannu Tuva via their national focus tree, making Tannu Tuva vulnerable to absorption but also positioning it as a potential staging ground for Chinese expansion with Soviet military backing.

### Economic and Military Laws

**1936 Starting Laws:**
- **Conscription:** Limited Conscription (2.5% recruitable population)
- **Trade:** Export Focus (+10% construction speed, +5% research speed, +10% factory/dockyard output, 50% resources to market, +20% civilian intelligence to others, +10% navy intelligence to others)
- **Economy:** Civilian Economy (35% factories on consumer goods, +30% factory conversion cost, –30% factory energy consumption, –40% fuel gain per oil, –25% fuel capacity, –30% factory construction speed)

These laws remain unchanged between 1936 and 1939.

### Technology

Tannu Tuva begins research with access to Basic Infantry Equipment and uses cosmetic Soviet-style names for equipment (e.g., M-N 1891/30 in 1918, SVT-38/40 in 1936). It can unlock 3 additional research slots through the generic focus tree beyond its starting 2 slots.
