## Summary

The Chinese Soviet Republic (PRC, Communist China) is a minor communist nation in eastern Asia, controlling only the small, mountainous states of Shaanbei and Yulin. Severely weakened after the Long March, it begins with minimal industry, just 2.33M core population, and a population of 7.01M total. Despite its weakness, the mountainous terrain, starting forts, and large pool of potential recruits provide defensive strength. The country can pursue multiple paths: Soviet-aligned Marxist Orthodoxy, nationalist Maoism, or coexistence with Nationalist China via Social Democracy. It has the potential to emerge as an unlikely victor in the Chinese Civil War.

## Government and political situation

The Communist Party of China (CPC) holds 100% political power at game start under **Mao Zedong** as de facto leader (with the title De Facto Leader, giving +10% same-ideology monthly opinion, +10% Stability, +5% war penalty stability, +8% division recovery rate). An alternate starting leader under Mao is available when No Compromise, No Surrender is disabled: **Mao Zedong (Cornered Fox)**, granting +15% division defense and +10% division recovery rate.

Alternative communist party leaders are available but not in power:
- **Wang Ming:** Stalinism, can become leader via Marxist Orthodoxy focus path (Faction Diplomat trait: +2.5% contribution influence gain)
- **Zhang Lan:** Socialism/Social Democracy path (Pragmatic Socialist trait: -5% war support, +10% stability, -20% political advisor cost, -35 acceptance of communist diplomacy)

**Political situation:** The country is locked in internal power struggles after the Long March (see National spirits). Multiple paths are available via the national focus tree:
1. **Marxist Orthodoxy (Soviet alignment):** Join the Comintern, pursue Soviet aid, replace Mao with Wang Ming
2. **Agrarian Socialism (Maoism):** Maintain Mao's leadership, pursue independent Chinese path, form own faction
3. **Social Democracy (Coalition):** Cooperate with Nationalist China, eventually annex them via political power struggle

Alternative ideology paths exist (Democratic, Fascist, Non-Aligned) but are not pursued historically and require significant focus investment.

## Unique mechanics

**Power Struggles in the Central Committee:** When pursuing certain focus paths (Marxist Orthodoxy, Agrarian Socialism, Social Democracy), the player unlocks a series of **Power Struggles** decisions. Different paths empower different leaders (Mao, Wang Ming, Bo Gu, Zhang Guotao, or a shared Leninism diarchy) and unlock different capability trees (guerilla warfare bonuses, orthodox Soviet alignment, or rapid army reforms).

**Chinese Power Struggle (Social Democracy path only):** If pursuing Social Democracy, the player can engage in a sustained political competition with Nationalist China. By building support from Warlords (via expensive Political Power commitments) and competing for control of individual states, Communist China can accumulate enough national support to peacefully annex Nationalist China without direct military conflict. This is the fastest and safest path but requires significant Political Power investment.

**Focus tree:** Communist China has a large and branching national focus tree (integrated from Waking the Tiger and expanded by No Compromise, No Surrender). Key branches include:
- **Put an End to Sufan Branch:** Early stabilization and resource gathering
- **Know the Enemy (political path):** Border wars with nearby warlords
- **Burn the Paper Tigers (historical path):** Chinese United Front formation
- **Leadership decisions (Mao vs. 28½ Bolsheviks vs. Diarchy):** Long-term political direction
- **Military recovery (Long March to Recovery, PLA Ground/Air/Navy):** Gradual military modernization
- **Industrial path (Rally the Industry):** Choice between rapid industrial plans vs. sustained growth; agrarian or urban focus

See [06-focus-trees/chinese-soviet-republic.md](../06-focus-trees/chinese-soviet-republic.md) for the full tree.

**Technology cosmetic names:** Infantry equipment is labeled Type 24 (1936), TsingTao MP18 (1939), PPSh-41 (1942). Tank destroyer/SPG equipment has unique Type designations (e.g., Type 36 PTZ 36 in 1936). Aircraft have Chinese designations (J-1 fighter, Q-1 CAS, H-2 tactical bomber, Y-1 naval bomber, JH-1 heavy fighter in 1936).

**Starting military laws:**
- Conscription: Service by Requirement (10% recruitable population, –10% construction, +20% training time, +20% factory output)
- Trade: Closed Economy (0% resources to market, +40% lend-lease tension limit)
- Economy: Partial Mobilization (25% consumer goods factories, –10% factory conversion cost, +10% fuel per oil, +10% military factory construction speed)

No unique mechanics are documented for economic companies or design firms beyond generic options and the content available to Nationalist China and other Chinese nations.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering countries:**
- Shanxi (north and east)
- Ningxia Ma (west)
- Northeastern Army (south)
- Japan may justify war via Chinese Border Incident

**Alliances and factions:** Communist China starts with no alliances or faction membership. Its diplomatic path depends on ideology choice:
- **Marxist Orthodoxy:** Can and will join the Comintern if player accepts Soviet aid focus
- **Agrarian Socialism:** Forms its own faction ("The People's Front") and may recruit sympathetic warlords or other communist states
- **Social Democracy:** Forms a coalition with Nationalist China (if Nationalist China also completes the Coalition Government focus)

**Initial stability and war support:** 57% Stability, 52% War Support.

**Initial starting position:** Communist China is typically at peace in 1936. It begins a Civil War with Nationalist China around 1936–1937 (player-dependent). In 1939, on Historical AI, it joins the Chinese United Front faction with China (Nationalist) in active war against Japan.

**Strategic note:** Communist China is surrounded by hostile or neutral warlords; most will not volunteer to join its faction. Border wars are an early expansion path. The country is also a target for Japanese aggression via the Chinese Border Incident.
