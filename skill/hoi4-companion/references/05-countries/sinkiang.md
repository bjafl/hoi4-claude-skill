## Summary

Sinkiang is a Chinese warlord state in northwestern China, the only warlord that starts Communist. With a population of 3.88 million across four northwestern states and minimal industry (1 military factory, 4 civilian), it is among the weakest warlords militarily. However, it possesses unique advantages: Communist ideology allows early Comintern membership and unrestricted war justification, and it has an exclusive focus tree branch emphasizing Soviet cooperation and territorial expansion toward Central Asia.

## Government and political situation

Sinkiang is ruled by Sheng Shicai of the Communist Xinjiang People's Anti-Imperialist Association. As the only Communist warlord, it begins at 100% Marxism popularity.

**Alternative ideologies available:**
- **Conservatism:** Kwong Jin (China Democratic League)
- **Fascism:** Luo Wei (Blue Shirt Society)
- **Democracy:** Liang Jiang (Xinjiang Clique)

Completing the "Secure Internal Politics" focus allows Sheng Shicai to shift toward Conservatism or Despotism. The three main focus branches—Cooperation with the Nationalists, Cooperation with the Communists (with Soviet support), and Opposition (toward independence or Japanese alignment)—eventually grant access to full Nationalist or Communist Chinese focus trees.

## Unique mechanics

### Sinkiang-Exclusive Focus Branch (requires No Compromise, No Surrender)

**"Office of Sinkiang Border Defense / Cooperate with the USSR"** — Three sub-branches:

1. **Office of Sinkiang Border Defense:** Miscellaneous bonuses.
2. **Cooperate with the USSR:** Industrial bonuses; military aid from Soviet Union.
3. **March into Khotan:** Wars against Khotan Ma and releases Kumul Khanate as enemy. Post-war options:
   - Become a Soviet puppet (Sinkiang Autonomous SSR)
   - Install Sheng Shicai as King
   - Join China (Non-Aligned or aligned against China)
   - Join the Comintern

### Communist Warlord Advantages

As the only Communist warlord, Sinkiang can:
- Join the Comintern at game start (unrestricted war justification regardless of world tension)
- Replace Communist China via the Opposition focus branch without triggering Soviet tribute demands

### DLC Differences

**Without No Compromise, No Surrender:** Sinkiang's starting territory includes all states of Khotan Ma in addition to its four core states.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering countries:**
- Gansu Ma (southeast)
- Khotan Ma (south)
- British Raj (southeast)
- Afghanistan (southeast)
- Soviet Union (northwest)
- Mongolia (northeast)

Sinkiang begins at war with Japan and its puppets in 1939 (as part of the Chinese warlord situation). It can conduct border conflicts with other warlords—Xibei San Ma is the initial target, with expansion toward the Nationalists' core heartland possible through subsequent border clashes. Soviet support enables territorial expansion with minimal resource expenditure.

## Military

- 1 additional Soviet Volunteers unit appears in 1939 start

**Navy:** None

## Technology & Research

Research starts at 2 slots; 1 additional slot can be unlocked via focus tree. Starting techs include Basic Infantry Equipment, Mountain Infantry I, and Towed Artillery.

Sinkiang has cosmetic names for tank and aircraft equipment reflecting Chinese/Soviet design influences (Type 34/36/39 designations for tank destroyer variants; Hawk fighters, Vultee aircraft, N-series naval bombers).

## Strategies

_Not covered on the wiki as formal mechanics;_ player guides describe leveraging Soviet military support to expand through China and eventually form a "Turkic Union" through Central Asian conquest once the Soviet Union is weakened (typically post-1943 after German invasion, or post-civil-war fragmentation).
