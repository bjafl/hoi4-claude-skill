## Summary

Ireland is a minor democratic nation in Western Europe sharing the island with Northern Ireland (United Kingdom-controlled). In 1936 it has limited industrial capacity (1 military factory, 8 civilian factories) and manpower, but maintains a core claim on Northern Ireland. Ireland uses the generic national focus tree rather than a unique one, and begins the game with no national spirits.

## Government and political situation

Ireland starts under **Fianna Fáil, The Republican Party** (Democratic ideology) with 98% popularity. The ruling leader is **Éamon de Valera**, a historical anti-treaty republican who came to power in 1932 and shaped Irish neutrality policy throughout WWII.

Elections occur every 4 years; the next scheduled election is January 1937.

### Alternative Ideologies

Political parties available for conversion:

- **Communist**: Páirtí Cumannach na hÉireann (CPI) — James Larkin — 1% popularity
- **Fascist**: Páirtí Náisiúnta Corparáidíoch (PNC) — Eoin O'Duffy — 1% popularity
- **Non-Aligned**: Páirtí an Lucht Oibre — William Norton — 0% popularity

## National spirits

Ireland does not start with any national spirits.

## Unique mechanics

Ireland has no unique mechanics. It uses the **generic national focus tree** shared with many minor nations. The generic tree offers five main branches:

- **Army Effort**: Army experience and research bonuses
- **Aviation Effort**: Air experience, air bases, air research bonuses
- **Naval Effort**: Navy experience, naval dockyards, navy research bonuses
- **Industrial Effort**: Civilian and military factory construction, infrastructure, extra research slots
- **Political Effort**: Choices on political ideology path

See [01-mechanics/national-focus.md](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md) (generic tree) for the full focus tree structure.

## Diplomacy and Neighbors

Ireland has a **core claim on Northern Ireland**, which is controlled by the United Kingdom. The island neighbor is also the United Kingdom. 

Historically, Ireland remained neutral throughout WWII despite pressure from both the Allies and Axis. The game reflects possible paths toward fascism or communism, though the default democratic stance emphasizes neutrality. Players can justify wars against the UK or other nations through the political focus tree.

## Strategies

### Fascist Path

Recruit divisions early, hire a Fascist Demagogue, and pursue Political Effort → Fascist focuses. Once fascist, justify war goals to attack larger neighbors (UK, France, Spain). Joining the Axis provides access to German airbases. An alternative aggressive strategy uses collaboration governments to puppet smaller nations (Benelux, Hungary) for manpower, then uses paratroopers to invade the UK before Germany moves on Poland.

### Allied Path

Build naval and air capacity to supplement weak ground forces (limited manpower). Approximately 16 divisions suffice for territorial defense; focus on subs, destroyers, and heavy cruisers for navy, and fighters + naval bombers for air. Provide convoy raiding and air support to Allied operations rather than large ground offensives. Coastal forts and AA defend Dublin against German invasion.
