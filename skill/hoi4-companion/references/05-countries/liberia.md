Liberia is a minor independent democratic nation in West Africa with a population of 750K. As one of only three fully independent African countries in 1936 (alongside Ethiopia and the Sultanate of Aussa), it occupies a unique geopolitical position bordering British and French colonial territories. Though poorly industrialized, Liberia would gain strategic importance to the Allies as a rubber supplier when Japanese expansion cut off Southeast Asian plantations. The country uses the generic national focus tree and has minimal starting military strength.

## Government and political situation

Liberia starts as a democratic nation under the True Whig Party with elections scheduled for January 1939. Edwin Barclay serves as the ruling leader with a Conservatism trait. The country maintains a neutral foreign policy stance in 1936.

Political ideologies available for conversion:
- **Communist:** Liberian Commune (0% starting popularity)
- **Fascist:** Reorganized Liberian Free State (0% starting popularity)
- **Non-Aligned:** Independent Liberia (0% starting popularity)

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Borders:**
- United Kingdom (colonial territories to the northwest)
- France (colonial territories to the north and east)

**Diplomatic stance:** Neutral foreign policy. Historically aligned with the Allies during the war, granting access to territory and serving as a rubber source and transit point for North Africa operations. Despite early cooperation, Liberia remained reluctant to declare war until January 27, 1944.
