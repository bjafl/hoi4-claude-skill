This page covers all releasable African nations outside of Ethiopia (listed separately under Horn of Africa nations). These minors are cores of European colonial powers and can be released as puppets. Most gain independence at game start if "Africa Decolonized" custom game rule is enabled. Release mechanics and conditions vary by owner nation and DLC.

## Release mechanics

Most African releasables share common requirements:
- **Standard release:** Released as puppet of colonial owner (France, UK, Portugal, Italy, Spain, Belgium, South Africa) by player decision or AI wargoal.
- **Decolonization path:** Many UK and French colonies require the "Decolonization" focus to be released. UK puppets become Democratic Dominions if "Self Government For Africa" is completed, unless a regional breakaway (e.g., Biafra) has already been released.
- **Spanish Civil War gate:** Spanish colonies (Equatorial Guinea, Morocco, Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic, The Rif) cannot be released until Spanish Civil War concludes.
- **Africa Decolonized rule:** Custom game rule enables release at 1936 start for most nations; changes some starting ideologies (noted per country).

## Notable releasables by region and condition

### Belgian Congo territories
Burundi, Katanga, Rwanda, and Congolese State are cored in Belgium (Congo). Under Götterdämmerung DLC, they are owned by Belgian Congo (existing at 1936 start) and cannot be released from Belgium.

### British dominions (Africa Decolonized rule or Self Government For Africa focus)
UK territories that become Democratic Dominions: Botswana, Egypt, Ghana, Kenya, Malawi, Nigeria, Sierra Leone, Sudan, Tanzania, The Gambia, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe. These release from UK but remain allied.

### Strategic multi-core releasables
- **Barotseland:** Shares core with Zambia; releasing one does not prevent the other.
- **Biafra:** Shares core with Nigeria. If released, Nigeria cannot complete as Democratic Dominion via Self Government For Africa.
- **Djibouti:** Shares cores with Aussa and Somalia.
- **Katanga:** Shares core with Congolese State; both releasable from Belgium (except under Götterdämmerung).
- **Sokoto Caliphate:** Shares cores with Nigeria and Niger; not released via standard decolonization.
- **The Rif:** Shares core with Morocco; locked behind Spanish Civil War.

### Formation decisions
Releasables can form larger nations via decisions:
- **Al-Andalus:** Algeria, Libya, Morocco, Mauritania, Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic
- **Arabia:** Algeria, Egypt, Libya, Mauritania, Morocco, Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic
- **East Africa:** Burundi, Kenya, Rwanda
- **Horn of Africa:** Djibouti, Eritrea
- **Maghreb:** Algeria, Libya, Mauritania, Morocco, Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic
- **Mutapa:** Barotseland, Botswana, Mozambique
- **United States of Latin Africa:** Angola, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad, Congo-Brazzaville, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Katanga, Rwanda
