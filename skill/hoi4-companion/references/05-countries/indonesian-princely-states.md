Indonesian Princely States are a group of releasable minor nations with cores in the Dutch East Indies. Most start as Non-Aligned sultanates and kingdoms with unique leaders and national characteristics. Most are not released by default with the "Asia Decolonized" game rule; only Timor-Leste and West Papua are affected by this rule. These nations can be released through liberation or independence wars.
