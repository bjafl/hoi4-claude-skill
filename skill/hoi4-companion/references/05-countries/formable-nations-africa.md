Formable nations represent historical or alternate-history states that players can create by meeting specific territorial and political requirements. This page covers formable nations primarily formed through decisions in African regions, including North Africa, the Horn of Africa, East Africa, Central Africa, and Southern Africa. Formation requirements vary—some require focus tree completion, others only territorial control.

## Aksumite Empire

**Tag:** AKS  
**DLC:** By Blood Alone  
**Formable by:** Ethiopia

| Ideology | Country name |
|---|---|
| Democratic | Aksumite Empire |
| Communist | People's Union of Aksum |
| Fascist | Aksumite Empire |
| Non-Aligned | Aksumite Empire |

Ancient regional empire historically spanning the eastern coast of Africa and parts of southern Arabia. Ethiopia's first formable nation, unlocked after retaining Emperor Selassie and completing the focus "Restore the Empire of Aksum."

**Formation requirements:**
- Visibility: Has completed focus "Restore the Empire of Aksum"
- Prerequisites: Control all Ethiopian core states (Shewa, Hararghe, Bale, Sidamo, Illubabor-Kaffa, Welega, Gojjam, Begemder, Tigray, Wello) plus Eritrea and North Yemen
- Effects: Gains cores on South Yemen (659), Eritrea (550), North Yemen (293), Socotra (906), French Somaliland (268), Kassala (883), Khartoum (551), Blue Nile (886), Province of Aden (992), and Afar (908)

**Optional secondary decision:** Move capital to Tigray (costs 50 political power; adds 5 VP to Axum; upgrades state development level; only once per game).

## Al-Andalus

**DLC:** La Résistance  
**Formable by:** Morocco (and other Iberian nations)

| Ideology | Country name |
|---|---|
| Democratic | Islamic Republic of Al-Andalus |
| Communist | People's Republic of Andalusia |
| Fascist | Caliphate of Cordoba |
| Non-Aligned | Al-Andalus |

Moorish restoration of medieval Iberian sultanate. Upon formation, enables a cascade of decision chains to restore a unified Umayyad Caliphate across North Africa, the Mediterranean, and eventually the Middle East and Persia.

**Formation (Restore Al-Andalus):**
- Prerequisites: Fully independent; control Granada (173), Córdoba (789), Murcia (168), Valencia (167), Ciudad Real (175), Extremadura (170), Sevilla (169) from Spain; Beja (179), Santarém (795) from Portugal
- Effects: Gains cores on all Spanish states (18 total) and Portuguese states (7 total); capital moves to Córdoba (789)

**Post-formation decision chains:**
- "Restore the Sultanate of Africa" — gains cores on Moroccan, Tunisian, Algerian, and Libyan states
- "Restore the Emirate of Sicily" — gains cores on Mediterranean islands (Sicilia, Malta, Dodecanese, Crete, Cyprus, Sardegna, Corsica)
- "Recreate the Umayyad Caliphate" (requires both prior decisions) — gains cores on all Middle Eastern and Egyptian states
- "The Rashidi Expansion" — gains cores on Iran, Afghanistan, and Pakistan
- "The Northern Expansion" — gains cores on Caucasus and Anatolia

## Congolese Kingdoms (Six variants)

**DLC:** Götterdämmerung  
**Formable by:** Belgian Congo

Belgian Congo gains independence and completes "Assemble a Regency Council" focus, then receives events for selecting a historical king. Each kingdom follows a unique path:

### Kingdom of Kongo

| Ideology | Country name |
|---|---|
| Democratic | Congo-Léopoldville |
| Communist | Democratic Republic of The Congo |
| Fascist | Zaire |
| Non-Aligned | Kingdom of Kongo |

Expansionist southern Congo kingdom. Can claim and core Cabinda (796) and Luanda (540). Allows supporting rebels in northern Angola for 3 months, then peaceful integration via decision. Unique bonuses: political power gain, division offense/defense, faster war goal justification, ability to reduce "An Uncontrollably Vast Nation" national spirit twice (vs. once for other kingdoms).

### Kingdom of Bakuba

| Ideology | Country name |
|---|---|
| Democratic | Congo-Léopoldville |
| Communist | Democratic Republic of The Congo |
| Fascist | Zaire |
| Non-Aligned | Kingdom of Bakuba |

Interior-development focused kingdom. Provides population growth, construction speed, maximum factories, and supply efficiency buffs through "The Children of Woot" national spirit. Military penalties: 5% attack and defense reduction. No unique territorial expansion or war goals.

### Kingdom of Loango

| Ideology | Country name |
|---|---|
| Democratic | Congo-Léopoldville |
| Communist | Democratic Republic of The Congo |
| Fascist | Zaire |
| Non-Aligned | Kingdom of Loango |

Alliance-focused western Congo kingdom. Can seek alliance with France or Portugal. Gains claims on and enables peaceful annexation of Middle Congo (772) and Cabinda (796). Provides substantial rubber production and bonuses to factory output, division breakthrough, and stability.

### Kingdom of Burundi

| Ideology | Country name |
|---|---|
| Democratic | Congo-Léopoldville |
| Communist | Democratic Republic of The Congo |
| Fascist | Zaire |
| Non-Aligned | Kingdom of Burundi |

Eastern expansion-focused kingdom. Cores Burundi (769) and enables peaceful annexation if not already part of kingdom. Provides war goals around the Great Lakes and Lake Malawi. Can eventually form into a unified central African empire.

### Kingdom of Rwanda

| Ideology | Country name |

| Communist | Democratic Republic of The Congo |

Eastern Africa expansion path. Cores Rwanda (768) and enables annexation. Provides faster war justification at lower world tension threshold. Unlocks decision to unify into East Africa. Forms when Belgian Congo chooses Rwanda-focused king and completes "Unify East Africa" focus (requires Götterdämmerung).

### Kingdom of Mittelafrika

| Ideology | Country name |

Pro-German collaborative path. Can become a German protectorate or establish an independent German-inspired colonial state. Enables reconquest and integration of former German colonial territories. Available as either Fascist or Non-Aligned.

## East Africa

**DLC:** By Blood Alone  
**Formable by:** Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Burundi (standalone); or Belgian Congo if it forms Kingdom of Rwanda

| Ideology | Country name |
|---|---|
| Democratic | East African Federation |
| Communist | East African Union |
| Fascist | East African Empire |
| Non-Aligned | East Africa |

Regional union modeled on the 1967–1968 East African Community. Represents unification of East African nations.

**Formation (Form East Africa):**
- Prerequisites: Control Tanganyika (546), Nairobi (547), Mombasa (905), Nyanza-Rift Valley (904), Uganda (548)
- Effects: Gains cores on the above 5 states; gains claims on Rwanda (768), Burundi (769), Jubaland (844)

**Post-formation decisions:**
- "Integrate Ruanda-Urundi" (requires controlling Rwanda 768 and Burundi 769) — gains cores
- "Dominate Lake Malawi" (requires controlling Malawi 770 and Zambezia-Moçambique 897) — gains cores
- "Liberate the Sidama" (requires controlling Sidamo 837) — gains core
- "Unite the Somalis" (requires controlling 7 Somali-inhabited states including Jubaland, Somaliland, British Somaliland, Bale, Hararghe, French Somaliland, Garissa) — gains cores on all, plus Socotra (906)
- "Conquer the Comoros" (requires controlling Comoro Islands 708) — gains core

## Greater Ethiopian Union (and regional variants)

**DLC:** By Blood Alone  
**Formable by:** Ethiopia, Gojjam, Jimma

| Ideology | Primary | Gojjami variant | Jimman variant |
|---|---|---|---|
| Democratic | East African Federation | East African Federation | East African Federation |
| Communist | East African Socialist Republic | East African Socialist Republic | East African Socialist Republic |
| Fascist | Greater Ethiopian Union | Greater Gojjami Union | Greater Jimman Union |
| Non-Aligned | Greater Ethiopian Union | Greater Gojjami Union | Greater Jimman Union |

Ethiopia can follow a fascist path by focusing on "Reach out to the Italians" and "Declare for Italy," triggering a civil war. Choosing different Italian collaborators allows Ethiopia to split into Gojjam or Jimma as independent entities. Rising from Italian control via "Negus of Shewa" and completing "Reclaim the Empire" and "Consolidate East Africa" unlocks integration decisions to core Ethiopian, Sudanese, Kenyan, and Ugandan states.

**Formation (Proclaim East African Unity):**
- Prerequisites: Fully independent; must core and control 17 states across Sudan (Blue Nile, Kassala, Khartoum, North Darfur, Kurdufan, South Darfur, Upper Nile, Bahr al Ghazal) and East Africa (Nyanza-Rift Valley, Garissa, Nairobi, Mombasa, Jubaland, Somaliland, British Somaliland, French Somaliland, Eritrea)
- Effects: Triggers 1-day event; transforms into Greater Ethiopian Union (or Greater Gojjami Union / Greater Jimman Union if currently those nations)

**Optional secondary decision:** Move capital to Jerusalem (requires owning Palestine 454; 50 political power cost; adds 9 VP; Fascist or Non-Aligned only; once per game).

## Horn of Africa

**DLC:** By Blood Alone  
**Formable by:** Ethiopia, Eritrea, Somalia, Tigray, Aussa, Harar, Oromia, Sidamo, Gambela, Benishangul-Gumuz, Qemant State, Djibouti

| Ideology | Country name |
|---|---|
| Democratic | The Great Federation of the Horn of Africa |
| Communist | The Union of Soviet Socialist Republics of Eastern Africa |
| Fascist | The Unitary State of Horn of Africa |
| Non-Aligned | The Horn of Africa |

Regional union of the easternmost African Horn (Somalia, Ethiopia, Djibouti, Eritrea). Available to Somalia, Eritrea, Djibouti without focus requirements. Ethiopia requires "Develop the Horn of Africa" focus; minor nations require "Consolidate East Africa" focus.

**Formation (Unify the Horn of Africa):**
- Prerequisites: Depends on nation. If Ethiopia: has completed "Develop the Horn of Africa" focus. If Aussa, Oromia, Qemant, Tigray, Benishangul-Gumuz, Harar, Sidamo, or Gambela: has completed "Consolidate East Africa" focus. All must control 16 states: Tigray, Wello, Afar, Begemder, Gojjam, Welega, Illubabor-Kaffa, Sidamo, Shewa, Bale, Hararghe, Eritrea, French Somaliland, British Somaliland, Somaliland, Jubaland
- Effects: Gains cores on all 16 states plus Socotra (906)

**Post-formation decisions:**
- "Conquer Socotra" (requires controlling Socotra 906) — gains core
- "Integrate the Southern Territories" (requires controlling Garissa, Nairobi, Mombasa, Nyanza-Rift Valley, Uganda) — gains cores on all 5
- "Control the Upper Nile" (requires controlling Blue Nile 886 and Upper Nile 884) — gains cores on both
