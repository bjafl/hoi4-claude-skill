## Summary

Guangdong Clique (tag GDC) is a Chinese warlord state controlling the southern province of Guangdong with 38.20M population and 4 core states. It is ruled under Despotism by Chen Jitang and suffers from severe internal administrative malaise (corruption, ineffective bureaucracy, warlord instability). The clique is a minor industrial power with significant tungsten and steel resources but limited military capability, requiring the "No Compromise, No Surrender" DLC to play as.

## Government and political situation

**Ruling party:** Southwest Political Council and the Southwest Headquarters of the Kuomintang Central Executive Committee (Despotism, 100% popularity)  
**Ruling leader:** Chen Jitang (ATK 2, DEF 3, PLN 3, LOG 2)  
**Alternative leaders available via event:**
- Yu Hanmou (Reckless Commander, via "Desertion and a Traitor" event)
- Li Zongren (Fierce Leader, via "Leadership of the Liangguang Clique" event)

**Ideological pathways:** Via the "Secure Internal Politics" focus, Chen Jitang can lead the state toward Socialism (China Democratic League), Anti-Revisionism (Communist Party of China), or Rexism (Blue Shirt Society), each retaining his trait.

## Unique mechanics

**Warlord-specific focus tree:** Guangdong Clique uses the shared Warlords national focus tree plus a unique joint branch with Guangxi Clique covering the **Liangguang Incident** (a challenge to Chiang Kai-shek's authority). The focus tree offers three major pathways: cooperation with Nationalist China, cooperation with Communist China, or opposition (siding with Japan or proclaiming a rival government).

**Border conflicts:** Warlords can seize territory from other Chinese states without declaring war via Border Conflicts events, a key economic/territorial expansion mechanic.

**Technology path:** Guangdong starts with Basic Infantry Equipment, Inter-War Small Airframe, Transport Plane, Bombs, Light MG, and Engines I. With "By Blood Alone" DLC absent, access to Inter-War Fighter, Inter-War Bomber added; these are restricted under baseline game.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering countries:** Guangxi Clique (west), China/Nationalist China (north)  
**Alliances:** None at start. Part of no faction.  
**Diplomatic isolation:** Guangdong is a minor warlord state without formal guarantees or faction ties. Its survival depends on internal consolidation and avoiding simultaneous conflicts with Nationalists and external powers (primarily Japan).

**Note on 1939 start:** Guangdong Clique does not exist in the 1939 start date but can be released through annexation reversals or focus decisions.

See [06-focus-trees/china-warlords.md](../06-focus-trees/china-warlords.md) for the full Warlords national focus tree and Liangguang Incident branch details.

> ⚠️ This country requires the "No Compromise, No Surrender" DLC to play as. Guangxi Clique can be played without this DLC and shares much of the same focus tree except the Liangguang Incident joint branch.
