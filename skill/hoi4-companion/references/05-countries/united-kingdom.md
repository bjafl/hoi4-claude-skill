The United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland is the principal Allied power in Western Europe in 1936, controlling a vast empire spanning Africa, Asia, the Pacific, and the Caribbean. As a major industrial democracy with the world's largest navy (restricted by the London Naval Treaty), the UK begins with extensive resources, factories, and research capabilities. The nation faces internal political divisions over appeasement toward Germany and the future of its empire.

## Government and political situation

The UK begins as a constitutional monarchy under King George V with a parliamentary democracy. The Conservative Party holds 97% popularity, led by Prime Minister Stanley Baldwin. The current government pursues **appeasement** of Germany while maintaining formal commitments to the League of Nations and existing guarantees (notably to Poland).

### Political Parties and Opposition

| Party | Popularity | Leader | Ideology |
|-------|------------|--------|----------|
| Conservative Party | 97% | Stanley Baldwin | Democracy |
| Communist Party of Great Britain | 1% | Harry Pollitt | Communism |
| British Union of Fascists | 2% | Oswald Mosley | Fascism |
| House of Windsor | 0% | Horatio Dundas | Non-Aligned |

Elections are held every four years; the next scheduled for 1939. Due to **British Stoicism** (+50% ideology drift defense), AI-driven ideology spread is significantly slower than normal.

### Government Mechanics

- **Stability:** 60% base (89.5% after modifiers)
- **War support:** 10% base (15% after modifiers)
- **Diplomatic rules:** Can guarantee other ideologies, join any faction, create factions, lower world tension with guarantees. Cannot declare war on democratic nations without a war goal, puppet countries, or justify against nations that haven't raised world tension.

## Unique mechanics

The UK has **no unique national decisions or events** documented on the wiki. However, several focus tree branches provide major strategic choices:

- **Steady As She Goes (Democratic):** Maintains democracy, brings Winston Churchill into power early, ends appeasement, mobilizes the nation, and secures war goals against Axis powers.
- **A Change in Course (Ideology Shift):** Allows pivot to non-aligned (King Edward VIII path with monarchy), fascism, or communism. The communist path requires decolonization within 2 years of becoming communist or triggers civil war.
- **Reinforce the Empire (Colonial Development):** Develops colonial infrastructure, influences China, coerces Balkan/Turkish nations, and can annex dominions into an Imperial Federation.
- **Revisit Colonial Policy (Decolonization):** Dismantles the empire in exchange for manpower and industry at home, converting colonies to puppets.
- **Cryptologic Bomb/Limited Rearmament:** Technology research bonuses, factories, and an extra research slot. Includes Intelligence Agency option (requires La Résistance DLC).

## Geography and Borders

The UK lies in Western Europe, comprising England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland, and the Isle of Man. It is **directly bordered only by Ireland to the west**; European access is sea-only across the North Sea and English Channel.

The empire controls vast overseas territories:
- **Mediterranean:** Malta, Gibraltar (borders Spain), Suez Canal (Egypt), Cyprus
- **Africa:** Large holdings including Egypt, Sudan, Kenya, Uganda, Nigeria, Ghana, Sierra Leone, Gold Coast, British Somaliland, Rhodesia, Tanganyika, and others
- **South Asia:** British Raj (India, Burma, Ceylon)
- **Far East & Pacific:** British Malaya, Hong Kong, Singapore, Fiji, Gilbert Islands
- **Atlantic/Americas:** Newfoundland, British Guyana, Jamaica, Bermuda, Bahamas, Trinidad
- **Other:** Falkland Islands, various small island territories

**Neighboring powers (direct and colonial):** Ireland (land border), Spain (Gibraltar), France (across Channel and in Africa), Italy (in Africa and Mediterranean), Egypt (Suez Canal shared control).

**Dominions and Subjects** grant the UK strategic reach but represent potential political complications:
- Dominions: Canada, Australia, New Zealand, South Africa (more autonomy)
- Direct colonies: British Raj, British Malaya, British Burma, and dozens of smaller territories
- All dominions and colonies start as subjects of the United Kingdom

## Navy

The UK's greatest asset is its fleet—currently the largest in the world but legally constrained. The **London Naval Treaty Signatory** national spirit limits capital ship construction costs (heavy ships capped at 10,000 production cost), reflecting the treaty restrictions on naval rearmament. The UK has **19 naval dockyards** and **183 naval base levels** distributed globally, supporting fleet operations across all theaters.

## Notable Characteristics

- **Imperial overextension:** 73 colonial non-core states dilute legitimacy and complicate industrial scaling.
- **Manpower constraints:** The War to End All Wars spirit imposes -30% recruitable population, reflecting WWI losses.
- **Naval dominance but limited rearmament:** Largest fleet but handicapped by treaty until diplomatically resolved.
- **Industrial capacity:** Balanced but not dominant (14 military factories); room to expand through focus tree or conquest.
- **Research advantage:** 4 starting research slots (1 more unlockable), facilitating technology parity with major powers.
