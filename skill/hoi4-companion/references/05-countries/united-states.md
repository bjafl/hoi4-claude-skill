## Summary

The United States enters 1936 as a major democratic power with the world's largest industrial capacity (161 total factories, 1,727 total resources), though heavily constrained by isolationist sentiment and the lingering effects of the Great Depression. Despite vast resources and territory spanning 54 states, the nation is restricted politically by neutrality laws and national spirits that prevent early intervention in foreign conflicts. With the right political choices, however, the US can eventually remove these restraints and leverage its economic dominance to become the decisive force in any global conflict.

## Government and political situation

The United States begins under the **Democratic Party** with **Franklin Delano Roosevelt (FDR)** as leader, holding 99% party popularity. The political system features mandatory elections every 4 years (1936, 1940, 1944), with the player choosing outcomes at each cycle.

**Electoral system:**
- **1936:** FDR (incumbent Democrat) vs. Alf Landon (Republican) — each offers distinct bonuses
- **1940:** FDR vs. Wendell Willkie (Republican)
- **1944:** FDR vs. Thomas E. Dewey (Republican)
- If a Republican wins, they become incumbent for the next cycle; FDR always runs as Democrat if unseated

**Opposition parties:**
- Communist Party USA (1% support, led by Earl Browder)
- Silver Legion of America (0% support, led by William Dudley Pelley; fascist faction)
- Non-Aligned (0% support, led by Carnot Wheeler)

**Political branches:** The national focus tree offers three major ideological paths:
- **Democratic:** The historical path; improves economy and maintains democracy
- **Communist (requires "Suspend the Persecution" focus):** Leads to civil war and formation of Communist States of America
- **Fascist (requires "Reestablish the Gold Standard" focus):** Leads to civil war and formation of Confederated States of America under William Dudley Pelley

**Civil war mechanics:** When pursuing Communist or Fascist branches, a timer triggers: industrialists move factories to preferred regions (North/South), government arsenals are raided, tax income withholds, and National Guard units mobilize. Upon secession declaration, war begins. Unless specific focuses are taken, Illinois, Indiana, Michigan, Minnesota, Wisconsin, and Ohio declare "neutrality," forming the **Neutral States of America** (independent faction; they sell weapons to both sides). Upon civil war victory, neutral states rejoin the winner.

**Base stability and war support:** 75% Stability / 0% War support (modified to 89.8% / 5% with national spirits).

## National spirits

The United States begins with four national spirits:

### London Naval Treaty Signatory
Restricts naval construction. Effects:
- Carrier Max Production Cost: 9,500
- Cruiser Max Production Cost: 5,300
- Heavy Ship Max Production Cost: 10,000

### Monroe Doctrine Enforcer
Affects diplomatic interactions in the Americas.

### The Great Depression
Severe economic penalty representing the 1929 crash aftermath. Effects:
- Daily Political Power cost: +1.00
- Resource Gain Efficiency: −20.00%
- Recruitable population Factor: −50%
- Consumer Goods Factories factor: 60.0% (60% of factories produce consumer goods)
- Join faction tension limit: +20.0%

### Home of the Free
Grants diplomatic independence but prevents factional alignment outside democracy. Effects:
- Ideology drift defense: +20.0%
- Cannot join factions led by another ideology
- Can create factions

## Unique mechanics

**Electoral system:** The US holds mandatory elections every 4 years with player choice over outcomes, each candidate offering different research bonuses and stability modifiers.

**Isolationist restrictions:** National spirit "Undisturbed Isolation" (from the focus tree) raises world tension requirements for declaring war, guaranteeing independence, and joining factions.

**Diplomatic rules (from national spirits):**
- Cannot declare war on democratic countries without a war goal
- Cannot join factions led by non-democratic powers (Home of the Free)
- Can create and lead factions
- Can guarantee independence of any ideology (Democracy rule)

**Civil war branching:** Pursuing either communist ("Suspend the Persecution") or fascist ("America First") focus branches triggers a Second American Civil War with unique state behavior (Neutral States of America).

**Focus tree branches:** The US focus tree comprises 4 major branches plus sub-branches:
1. **Continue the New Deal / Reestablish the Gold Standard** — Economy recovery and political ideological shifts (splits into Democratic, Communist, or Fascist paths)
2. **War Plans Division** — Planning speed bonuses and war preparations against major powers; democratic faction formation
3. **War Department** — Army and air force doctrines and production discounts
4. **Two Ocean Navy Act** — Naval dockyards, ship tech, and naval production improvements
5. **Establish the ABDACOM** — Noted on wiki but details incomplete

See [06-focus-trees/united-states.md](../06-focus-trees/united-states.md) for the full focus tree.

**Research and technology:** Begins with 4 research slots (expandable to 6 via focuses). Unique technology names reflect US military conventions (e.g., M1 Garand, P-40 Warhawk, B-17 Flying Fortress, Sherman tanks). By Blood Alone DLC affects some aircraft options (Inter-War Fighter availability); La Résistance DLC affects encryption/decryption techs.

No decisions or event-based unique mechanics beyond the focus tree system are documented on the wiki.

## Geography and Diplomatic Position

**Borders:**
- North: Dominion of Canada (British subject; can guarantee independence from UK via focus)
- South: Mexico (independent; grants oil to USA)
- Overseas: Numerous island possessions and territories (Philippines, Guam, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, Panama Canal, etc.)

**Starting faction:** None (non-aligned); can create own faction via "Form a Faction" focus.

**Key city/victory point clusters:**
- New England (120 VP): Boston, Providence, Bangor
- New York (33 VP): New York City, Buffalo
- Pennsylvania (20 VP): Philadelphia, Pittsburgh
- Ohio (10 VP): Cleveland, Columbus
- California (30 VP): Los Angeles, Sacramento, San Francisco, San Diego
- Illinois (30 VP): Chicago
- Texas (10 VP): Dallas, Houston, San Antonio
- Florida (5 VP), Virginia (15 VP), Michigan (20 VP), Maryland (5 VP), Missouri (10 VP) — major secondary centers

Major strategic assets: Panama Canal (5 VP), Hawaii (3 VP), Guam (1 VP), Puerto Rico (1 VP).
