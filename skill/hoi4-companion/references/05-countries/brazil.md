Brazil is a regional South American power with the largest population on the continent (44.02M) and borders almost all neighboring countries. Although it begins relatively weak economically, its size and strategic position make it well-positioned to dominate the Americas. The country starts as a democratic state under Getúlio Vargas, with internal tensions between democratic, fascist, and communist factions.

## Government and political situation

Brazil starts 1936 as a democratic nation under the **Provisional Government (UDN)** with Getúlio Vargas as the populist leader. The ruling party has 49% popularity. A general election is scheduled for July 1938.

**Starting leader:** Getúlio Vargas (Provisional Government / UDN, ideology Populism), with trait Authoritarian Populist.
- Effects: Political Power Gain +10%, Daily Support for Unaligned +0.01, Party Popularity Stability Modifier +5%

**Competing parties (1936):**
- Military (Non-Aligned, 25%, leader Arlindo Veiga dos Santos)
- Ação Integralista Brasileira (Fascist, 14%, leader Plínio Salgado)
- Partido Comunista Brasileiro (Communist, 10%, leader Luís Carlos Prestes)

**Ideological paths:**

- **Democratic:** Can remain democratic via focus Repeal the National Security Laws, electing conservative reformers (Armando de Sales Oliviera, Anísio Teixeira, Lindolfo Collor) or remaining on the UDN path.
- **Fascist:** Via focus Estado Moderno, installing Getúlio Vargas under the Ação Integralista Brasileira or Plínio Salgado as dictator.
- **Communist:** Via focus Second Intentona Comunista, triggering a civil war led by Luís Carlos Prestes (Stalinism), Lampião (Anarcho-Communism), or the Council of Aliança Nacional Libertadora (Marxism).
- **Monarchist:** Via focus Romanticize Imperial History, installing a member of the House of Orléans-Braganza (Pedro de Alcântara or Pedro Henrique) as king and potentially uniting with Portugal.
- **Non-Aligned:** Sustained through Estado Novo (Estado Moderno under Vargas/Integralists) or the Tribunal de Segurança Nacional path.

Elections every 4 years if remaining democratic.

## Unique mechanics

**National focus tree:** Brazil has a unique focus tree (requires Trial of Allegiance) with four main branches:

1. **Second Intentona Comunista** — Communist civil war option, allowing the player to choose a communist leader (Luís Carlos Prestes, Lampião, or the Council of Aliança Nacional Libertadora) and then spread communism throughout South America via the Comintern or a Latin American Socialist Cooperative.

2. **End the State of Emergency** — Democratic or Monarchist path, removing the State of Emergency spirit. Democratic path centralizes or federalizes and compromises/collaborates with the military. Monarchist path installs a monarch and potentially unites with Portugal.

3. **Tribunal de Segurança Nacional** — Maintains Getúlio Vargas and the Estado Novo authoritarian regime, allowing industrial buildup. Fascist sub-branch (Estado Moderno) can install the Integralists under Plínio Salgado.

4. **Aftermath of the Coffee Crisis** — Industrial branch to remove neglected-state modifiers and relocate the capital from Rio de Janeiro to Brasília.

**Shared military branches** (Trial of Allegiance) offer additional national spirits and military bonuses depending on ideology chosen.

**Diplomatic options:**
- **Good Neighbor Policy** sub-branch (Democratic/Non-Aligned only) — Cooperate with the US and allow South American nations to voluntarily join a United States of South America.
- **Domination of the Americas** sub-branch (Fascist/Non-Aligned only) — Unite South America by conquest with German and Italian support.

See [06-focus-trees/brazil.md](../06-focus-trees/brazil.md) for the full focus tree.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Land borders:** Uruguay (south), Argentina (southwest), Paraguay (south), Bolivia (west), Peru (west), Colombia (northwest), Venezuela (north).

**Colonial neighbours:** British Guiana, Dutch Guiana, French Guiana (north, claimed/controllable via focus tree).

**Diplomatic position:** Starts Non-Aligned (per master table) but as a democratic nation via the Provisional Government. Independence is guaranteed by the United States under the Monroe Doctrine, providing significant diplomatic protection in 1936.

**Factions:** Brazil starts unaligned. Can join the Allies (US faction, especially via Good Neighbor Policy), the Axis (via German/Italian military support in Domination of the Americas), or the Comintern (via communist focuses).

**Trial of Allegiance note:** Much of Brazil's unique content (focus tree branches, national spirits, unique ministers, and special military bonuses) requires the Trial of Allegiance DLC. Without it, Brazil uses the generic focus tree but retains the Monroe Doctrine guarantee.
