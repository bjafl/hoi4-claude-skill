Costa Rica is a small Central American nation with minimal military and industrial capacity. It begins under democratic rule with a guarantee from the United States via the Monroe Doctrine. With limited manpower and factories, Costa Rica relies on political maneuvering and can form the United States of Central America by conquering its neighbors.

## National spirits

**Monroe Doctrine** — Costa Rica's independence is guaranteed by the United States. This national spirit prevents any nation from freely conquering Costa Rica, providing strategic protection despite its military weakness.

## Unique mechanics

Costa Rica lacks a unique national focus tree and uses the generic focus tree instead, which provides five branches:

- **Army Effort** — army experience and research bonuses
- **Aviation Effort** — air experience, air base construction, air research bonuses
- **Naval Effort** — navy experience, naval dockyard construction, naval research bonuses
- **Industrial Effort** — civilian and military factory construction, infrastructure, extra research slots
- **Political Effort** — political ideology choices

**Formable nations:** Costa Rica can form the **United States of Central America** by controlling all independent Central American countries (Nicaragua, Honduras, Guatemala, El Salvador) excluding Panama. This grants cores on conquered territories and British Mexico (state 311).
