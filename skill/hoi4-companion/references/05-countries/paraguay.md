Paraguay is a landlocked minor country in South America bordered by Bolivia (north), Argentina (south), and Brazil (east). Recovering from the 1932–1935 Chaco War and the earlier War of the Triple Alliance, it has minimal industry and manpower but access to niche resources. Politically unstable, it can pursue democratic, communist, or fascist paths; its unique focus tree (requires Trial of Allegiance) enables multiple ideological pivots and intervention from either the Allies or Comintern.

## Government and political situation

**Starting government:** Democratic — Partido Azul (55% popularity) led by **Eusebio Ayala** (The President of Victory). Elections occur every 4 years (next in January 1940), though the election event does not always trigger.

**Opposition parties:**
- **Communist:** Partido Revolucionario Febrerista (PRF, 30%), led by **Rafael Franco** — can seize power via event; gains ideology drift defense and division defense on core territory.
- **Fascist:** Guión Rojo (5%), led by **Juan Natalicio Gonzalez** — grants division attack and recruitable-population bonuses.
- **Non-Aligned:** Gobierno de Morínigo (10%), led by **Higinio Morinigo** — military government path.

**Political paths:** The unique focus tree (Trial of Allegiance DLC) offers branches toward communism (via Rafael Franco reforms) or fascism (via military intervention), plus alignment toward either the United States or the Soviet Union. Without DLC, Paraguay uses the generic focus tree.

**Notable government transitions:**
- Rafael Franco can take power in 1939 (starting leader if communist path is chosen).
- **José Félix Estigarribia** — a Militaristic Nationalist alternative with war support +10% and higher surrender limit.
- Continued military influence under Higinio Morinigo (fascist sympathizer).

## Unique mechanics

**Focus tree:** Paraguay gains a unique national focus tree with Trial of Allegiance DLC. Key branches include:

- **Febrerista Land Reforms:** Allows Rafael Franco to take power, pushing communist government with military reforms.
- **Pull Back Troops From the Chaco:** Counters Febrerista path; reforms toward fascism and Axis alignment.
- **Revenge for the Chaco War:** Pushes communist popularity; grants war goals against Chile and Peru.
- **Cult of Personality / Mythologize the Father of the Nation:** Shared focuses between fascist and communist paths grant leader traits and national spirits.
- **Reach out to American Contacts for Aid:** Aligns Paraguay toward the United States in civil war; provokes Bolivia into attacking communist Paraguay. Offers Italian planes, factories, and land forts in Humaitá (province 301).
- **Accept American Loans:** Shared section permitting alignment with either the Allies or the Comintern (Allies path grants more bonuses).
- **Paraguay/Uruguay Shared Branches:** Provide expansion opportunities, industrial buildings, and research bonuses; Paraguay gains special foreign bonuses.

Without Trial of Allegiance DLC, Paraguay uses the generic focus tree.

## Research and technology

Paraguay begins with 2 research slots. Up to 3 additional slots can be unlocked via focus tree. Starting technologies include Infantry Equipment I, Support Weapons I, and early construction/industry techs (no inter-war fighter without By Blood Alone DLC).

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Immediate neighbours:**
- **Bolivia** (north) — historically adversarial (Chaco War ended 1935); can be invaded early if pursuing communist ideology (can justify wars from 1936).
- **Argentina** (south) — major power; aggressive annexation risky without significant military buildup.
- **Brazil** (east) — regional power; conquest inadvisable without controlling at least 2 other neighbors first.

**Diplomatic guarantees:** Paraguay's independence is guaranteed by the United States (Monroe Doctrine). Early attacks by communist Paraguay on democratic/fascist neighbors will not automatically trigger US intervention, but US support can be secured via focus tree.

**Faction alignment:** Can join the Comintern directly as communist (default diplomatic orientation), or pivot toward the Axis (via fascist path) or Allies (via American loans focus). Alignment timing affects neighbor reactions and internal stability.
