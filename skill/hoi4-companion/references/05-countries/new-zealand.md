New Zealand is a minor democratic British Dominion in Oceania with limited military resources but a comprehensive focus tree. It starts with substantial naval infrastructure relative to its size and can pursue independence or strengthen Commonwealth ties. Its greatest constraints are manpower and initial factory capacity, offset by access to Commonwealth research bonuses as a subject state.

## Government and political situation

**Ruling ideology:** Democratic

New Zealand starts under democratic rule governed by the New Zealand Labour Party, led by **Michael Joseph Savage** (1936–starting event ~1940). Savage provides +5% Civilian factory construction speed and +5% Stability. A second leader, **Peter Fraser**, takes power via an event following Savage's death in-game, offering +5% Research Speed and daily Fascism support reduction.

**Electoral cycle:** Elections occur every 3 years; next election November 1938. Opposition parties include the Communist Party of New Zealand (Elsie Farrelly), New Zealand Legion (John Ormond, Fascist), and New Zealand National Party (Sidney Holland, Non-Aligned).

**Political choices:** New Zealand's focus tree offers branches to remain within the Commonwealth, or pursue independence as Democratic, Fascist, or Communist. The "Constitution Amendment Act" and other focuses enable ideology shifts via minister appointments (Democratic Reformer, Communist Revolutionary, Fascist Demagogue).

- Construction Speed: –5%

**British Commonwealth Loyalty**  
- Stability: +10%

All three spirits are active at game start. Independence via focus tree removes British Commonwealth Loyalty and may trigger others depending on ideology choice.

## Unique mechanics

**Focus tree structure (five branches):**  
1. **The First Labour Government** — Political tree handling Commonwealth ties, independence, and ideology paths (Democratic, Fascist, Communist).
2. **Transfer the New Zealand Division** — Naval tree with dockyards and ship/naval doctrine bonuses.
3. **Expand the NZPAF** — Air doctrine and aircraft research bonuses.
4. **Bureau of Industry / Ministry of Public Works** — Industrial capacity, resource extraction, and research slot unlocks.
5. **Army Reforms** — Infantry, armour, and artillery bonuses; military factory construction.

**Puppet mechanics:** The Samoa state can be released as a puppet to gain access to generic focus-tree bonuses without consuming manpower; it returns factories and naval dockyards if re-annexed later.

**Company slots & designers:** New Zealand has access to industrial companies (Wairarapa Ranches, New Zealand Railways, Electronics Company) and design companies for armor (New Zealand Railways, Christchurch Workshops), naval (Saint Mary's Bay Shipbuilding, Port Chalmers Shipbuilding), and air equipment. All cost 150 political power to hire.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Direct borders:** Australia (to the west). Nearby British-aligned territories include Fiji (British) and New Caledonia (French).

**Faction:** New Zealand starts as a subject of the United Kingdom in the British Commonwealth faction. It is guaranteed against invasion by the UK (as a dominion) and receives Commonwealth research bonuses. Independence via focus tree allows alignment with other factions or remaining non-aligned.

**Starting military (1936):**  
- Army: 3 Militia Divisions (3 infantry + 3 cavalry each), 18K manpower used
- Navy: 70 convoys, 0 capital ships (2 light cruisers from 1939 start scenario onward)
- Air: 12 naval bombers

New Zealand's small military is offset by reinforcement from Commonwealth allies if war breaks out. The focus tree emphasizes naval and air superiority in the Pacific theater.

**Diplomacy:** New Zealand can declare war independently (historically on 3 September 1939 following UK declaration). Focus tree choices determine long-term alignment.
