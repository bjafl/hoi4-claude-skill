Ningxia Ma is a Chinese warlord state in northwestern China. Part of the Ma Clique, a confederation of four related warlord states, it controls key territory and has limited but recoverable industrial capacity. Ruled by Ma Hongkui, Ningxia Ma can cooperate with other warlords to form a united front or pursue independent ambitions via focus tree branching.

## Government and political situation

Ningxia Ma is ruled by **Ma Hongkui**, leader of the Ma Clique. The starting government is Despotism; through focus tree completion (Secure Internal Politics), Ma Hongkui can shift the country toward Socialism (China Democratic League), Anti-Revisionism (Communist Party of China), or Rexism (Blue Shirt Society), each with identical trait effects. His ability grants +8% Division Attack and Defense on core territory, −40 acceptance for Fascist and Communist diplomacy.

The Ma Clique, founded by Muslim generals who served the Qing dynasty (notably the Kansu Braves during the Boxer Rebellion), consists of four warlords: Ningxia Ma, Gansu Ma, Khotan Ma, and Qinghai Ma. These states share a joint focus tree branch and can collectively form the Chinese United Front faction through the "Develop Inter-Province Ties" branch, ultimately creating the nation Xibei San Ma. Alternately, Ningxia Ma can pursue nationalist cooperation, communist annexation (absorbing the Chinese Soviet Republic), or independent opposition.

## Unique mechanics

Ningxia Ma shares the Warlords national focus tree with other warlords, plus exclusive branches:

- **Secure Internal Politics:** Political branch allowing cooperation with Nationalist China (replacing focus tree with Nationalist Chinese Warlord tree), communist annexation of the Chinese Soviet Republic (replacing with Communist Chinese Warlord tree), or opposition/border clashes leading to an independent rival government.
- **Develop the Capital State:** Industrial investment branch.
- **Strengthen Warlord Authority:** Modifies Warlord State spirit and offers bonuses to cavalry or mountaineers.
- **Sideline Family Conflicts:** Exclusive to Ma Clique warlords. Permits demands or war goals against other Ma Cliques; Gansu Ma and Ningxia Ma do not have their own exclusive sub-branches.
- **Develop Inter-Province Ties:** Shared Ma Clique branch modifying the Ma Clique Collective modifier and forming the Chinese United Front faction, preparing formation of Xibei San Ma.

Access to shared Nationalist and Communist focus trees is granted after the main warlord tree completion. Ningxia Ma also accesses military industrial organizations (Sino-German Technical Mission, Morozov Design Bureau, naval and air designers) and industrial companies conditional on controlling specific provinces or focus completion, many gated by No Compromise, No Surrender DLC.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Borders:** Shanxi (east), Chinese Soviet Republic (east), Northeastern Army (south), Qinghai Ma (west), Mongolia (north).

**Factions:** Part of the Ma Clique confederation; potential member of Chinese United Front (via focus) or aligned with Nationalist/Communist blocs depending on focus choice.

**Military position:** Ningxia Ma begins in a landlocked, mountainous region with minimal resources. Coal is its only trade good. The state lacks air and naval forces; its cavalry-heavy starting army reflects historical warlord mobility. Victory in internal warlord conflicts requires outmaneuvering and supply route management, particularly in mountainous terrain where mountaineer divisions offer tactical advantage.
