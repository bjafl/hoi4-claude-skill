## Summary

The Kingdom of Afghanistan is a landlocked minor nation in Central Asia, positioned between the British Raj, Iran, and the Soviet Union. It starts as a Non-Aligned state under King Mohammed Hashim Khan with a population of 6.61 million and modest industrial capacity. Afghanistan's geographic isolation and strong cavalry traditions are reflected in its military units and national spirits, making it a challenging but rewarding nation to play.

**States and geography:**
Afghanistan controls seven states: Farah (281K pop), Herat (1.0M), Kabul (2.0M), Khyber Pass (950K), Maymanah (915K), Qandahar (562K), and Qataghan (925K). Terrain is predominantly mountainous and desert, with the Khyber Pass providing a critical but defensible choke point against the British Raj to the east.

## Government and political situation

Afghanistan is a Non-Aligned monarchy under King Mohammed Hashim Khan of the Barakzai royal family. The Non-Aligned (Barakzai) party dominates at 75% popularity in 1936, with Communist (10%), Fascist (15%), and Democratic (0%) opposition. 

The country can transition to other ideologies via its focus tree. Democratic paths lead to a parliamentary system; Communist paths unlock a Socialist Coup (Nisar Muhammad Yousafzai as leader); Fascist paths can install either Faqir of Ipi or Amanullah Khan. Non-Aligned paths allow maintaining the monarchy with gradual modernization or balancing between Axis and Allies.

Key alternative leaders include:
- Mohammed Zahir Shah (Non-Aligned, stability +15%)
- Faqir of Ipi (Fascist/Non-Aligned, religious movement leader)
- Nisar Muhammad Yousafzai (Communist, +2% research speed)
- Amanullah Khan (Fascist, modernizing reformer, -25% justify war goal time)

Pashtunwali and Subsistence Economy form the early impediment to industrial growth, but provide stability. The Quami spirit supports irregular and mountainous warfare. The Turkish Military Mission reflects historical military cooperation.

## Unique mechanics

Afghanistan has an extensive unique focus tree divided into five main branches:

- **Expand the Telegraph Network** — Industrial bonuses, research slots, factories, railroads; can lead to Socialist Coup or oil sales agreements
- **Support King Zahir** — Maintains non-aligned monarchy; sub-branches offer modernization, foreign capital visits, or balancing act between Allies/Axis
- **Against Kabul** — Alternative monarchist paths: Contact Amanullah Loyalists (aggressive expansion into India, Iran, Middle East), or Crown the Golden Emir (Faqir of Ipi, creates Emirate of Pashtunistan)
- **Maintain Quami/Adopt Nufus** — Military spirit improvements; Quami focuses on cavalry/irregular infantry/equipment capture, Nufus on infantry equipment/armor/planes
- **Align with Allies/Pursue Own Agenda/Seek Alternative Partnerships** — Foreign policy branches allowing Operation Countenance participation, Iran expansion, or Axis alignment; includes joint Saadabad Pact branch with Iran, Iraq, Turkey

Afghanistan receives a unique national focus tree as part of Graveyard of Empires; without it, uses the generic focus tree. Multiple focuses grant war goals on neighboring states (Sinkiang, India, Soviet Union) or unlock territorial claims on Pakistan and Pashtunistan.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering nations:**
- North: Soviet Union
- West: Iran
- South/East: British Raj
- Far northeast: Sinkiang

As Non-Aligned, Afghanistan has +20 opinion with other Non-Aligned nations (except Austria, Finland, Greece, Hungary, Latvia, Lithuania, Romania, Poland: +10), and -10 with everyone else (Allies, Axis, Comintern).

Afghanistan begins at peace with all factions and has no guarantees or alliance commitments. Its strategic position along the Silk Road makes it valuable as a buffer state and military springboard for regional campaigns. The Khyber Pass to the east is a natural defensive bottleneck against the Raj.

**Military starting strength:**
- 10 divisions total: 9 Infantry (Ferqhah), 1 Royal Guard (mixed cavalry/tank/artillery)
- 67.5K manpower committed
- No navy, no air force
- Irregular infantry and cavalry traditions reflected in high-command traits

Without Graveyard of Empires DLC, Afghanistan uses generic concerns and designers; with it, gains unique industrial organizations (Herat Armories, Afghan Shipyards, Afghan Aviation Company, Kabul Rifle Company, Kabul Foundry) supporting armor, naval, air, rifle, and artillery research.
