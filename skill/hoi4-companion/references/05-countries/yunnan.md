Yunnan (tag YUN) is a Chinese warlord state controlling southwestern China. Historically a mutually hostile clique, it maintained autonomy under Long Yun despite nominal alignment with the Nationalist government.

## Government and political situation

Yunnan starts under Kuomintang rule with Long Yun as both civilian leader and military commander (skill 3 across attack/defense/planning; logistics 2). The ruling party holds 80% popularity; the remaining 20% is split among China Democratic League, Communist Party of China, and Blue Shirt Society (each hypothetically 0% in the initial state beyond BSS at theoretical cap).

Long Yun carries the trait "King of Yunnan," providing construction speed +10%, daily political power gain +0.10, and resource gain efficiency +10%.

Alternative leaders available through focus completion include Mao Dong (China Democratic League, Conservatism), Cheng Peng (Communist Party, Anarchist Communism), and Hsia Wu (Blue Shirt Society, Falangism).

## National spirits

Yunnan begins with severe internal handicaps:

- **Government Corruption:** Political power gain -10%, stability -10%
- **Ineffective Bureaucracy:** Daily political power gain -0.25
- **Warlord State:** Political power gain -5%, recruitable population +5%, division attrition -5%, supply consumption -3%, stability -5%, research speed -3%, command power gain multiplier -15%. Removed upon completing any of: Power Struggle, The Yan'an Incident, or Proclaim Rival Government focus.

With No Compromise, No Surrender enabled, Yunnan also starts with:
- **Commonplace Looting:** Consumer goods factories +10%, division recovery rate +5%, construction speed -5%

## Unique mechanics

Yunnan shares the Warlords national focus tree with other Chinese warlords. The tree branches into:

- **Secure Internal Politics:** Opens political focuses allowing Yunnan to align with Nationalist China (replacing focus tree with Nationalist Chinese Warlord tree) or Communist China (integration into Communist focus tree).
- **Develop the Capital State:** Industrial branch for economic development.
- **Strengthen Warlord Authority:** Modifies the Warlord State spirit and grants bonuses to cavalry or mountaineer divisions.

With No Compromise, No Surrender, the tree is expanded and grants access to new shared Nationalist and Communist focus trees at the end.

The "Strengthen Burmic Ties" focus (NCNS only) grants a war goal against British Burma and, if Burma is later decolonized, an opportunity to annex it for additional states, manpower, resources, and access to southern ports.

See [06-focus-trees/china-warlords.md](../06-focus-trees/china-warlords.md) for the shared warlord focus tree.

## Military

**Starting army (1936):** 6 Infantry divisions (Juntuán pattern with 4 infantry battalions each), consuming 24K manpower.

**Navy:** None.

**Air force:** None.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

Yunnan borders:
- **East:** China (Nationalist), Guangxi Clique
- **South:** French Indochina (French colony)
- **West:** British Burma (British colony)
- **North:** Tibet, Sichuan Clique

Like all Chinese warlords, Yunnan enters 1939 at war with Japan and Japanese puppets (Manchukuo, Mengjiang). No guaranteed alliances are established at game start. Cooperation paths exist via national focus to align with either Nationalist or Communist China during the civil war, or to remain independent and challenge both.
