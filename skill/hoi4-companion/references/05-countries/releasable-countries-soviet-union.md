Releasable minor nations and autonomous republics within Soviet territory, most originating as Soviet autonomous republics. All are released automatically if "Soviet Union Fragmented" is enabled; some release if "Soviet Union Dissolved" is active. Manual release by Soviet Union requires completing focus "Autonomous Soviet Republics" or "Reconvene the Zemsky Sobor". Many can form regional multi-nation unions via decision.

## Abkhazia

**Tag:** ABK | **Capital:** Sukhumi

Released at game start: No custom rules apply.

Starts democratic (60% ruling party; elections every 4 years, next January 1940). Can shift to communist (Kirill Bechvaya, Stalinism) or other ideologies. Shares cores with Georgia. Can form Mountainous Republic of the Northern Caucasus.
