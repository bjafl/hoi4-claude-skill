## Summary

Honduras is a small Central American nation with minimal industry and military capacity. Starting as Democratic under Tiburcio Carías Andino, it begins with only 1 state, a population of 0.86M, and a single infantry brigade. Its independence is guaranteed by the United States under the Monroe Doctrine, but it can shift ideology and potentially form the United States of Central America by annexing neighboring countries.

## Government and political situation

Honduras begins as a **Democratic** nation under the **PLH (Partido Liberal de Honduras)** with mandatory elections every 4 years (next scheduled October 1936). President **Tiburcio Carías Andino** is the starting leader.

Alternative ideological paths are available via the generic focus tree:

| Ideology | Party | Leader | Country name |
|----------|-------|--------|--------------|
| Democratic | PLH | Tiburcio Carías Andino | Honduras |
| Communist | Partido Comunista de Honduras (PCH) | Lorenzo Ferrera | Honduran Commonwealth |
| Fascist | Fascist | Pedro Figueroa | Corporatist Honduras |
| Non-Aligned | Non-Aligned | Lisandro Machado | Atlas Honduras |

Converting ideology requires acquiring political power and winning elections or mounting coups, depending on the target ideology. The generic focus tree provides political effort branches to shift allegiances.

## National spirits

**Monroe Doctrine** — Honduras' independence is guaranteed by the United States under the Monroe Doctrine national spirit. This provides security against unprovoked aggression but does not commit Honduras to join any faction or provide aid.

## Unique mechanics

**No unique mechanics documented on the wiki.** Honduras uses the generic national focus tree, which provides five branches: Army Effort, Aviation Effort, Naval Effort, Industrial Effort, and Political Effort. The Industrial Effort branch can build factories even beyond available building slots.

**Formation opportunity:** Honduras may form the **United States of Central America** if it controls all independent Central American countries except Panama (requires Guatemala, El Salvador, Nicaragua, and Costa Rica). This grants cores on the required territories plus British Mexico (state 311), dramatically expanding Honduras' power base and manpower.

See [01-mechanics/national-focus.md](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md) (generic tree) for the full generic focus tree.

## Strategies

Honduras is severely limited by its 1 state, minimal industry, and small manpower pool (0.86M). Viable long-term strategies require:

1. **Ideology shift:** Use the generic tree's Political Effort branch to shift to Fascism or Communism for access to better government bonuses and more aggressive expansion options.

2. **Central American union:** Justify wars on neighbors (Guatemala, El Salvador, Nicaragua, Costa Rica) via the Political Effort focus branch. Annexing all four (excluding Panama) forms the **United States of Central America**, which provides cores and significantly boosts Honduras' industrial base to levels comparable to Nepal.

3. **Regional dominance:** After forming Central America, expand southward into South America (particularly Brazil and Argentina) to access greater industry and resources, or expand into Mexico if able to overcome its larger military.

4. **Avoid early US conflict:** The Monroe Doctrine guarantee means direct war with the USA will result in devastating defeat. Focus on regional consolidation first.
