Romania is a middle power in Eastern Europe that begins as a constitutional monarchy under King Carol II. Its position grants it the unusual flexibility to shift between multiple ideologies via the focus tree, allowing it to pursue varied diplomatic strategies—from membership in the Little Entente to the Axis or Comintern. Bordered by three major powers (Germany, Soviet Union, and Hungary to the west/north/east) and facing existential territorial threats, Romania must navigate carefully or risk dismemberment.

## Government and political situation

Romania starts under **King Carol II** as a constitutional monarchy in the democratic ideology. The ruling party is the **Partidul Național Liberal** (60% popularity), led by **Gheorghe Tătărescu**.

However, Romania's focus tree grants unusual political fluidity via the "Institute Royal Dictatorship" branch, which can shift the government toward despotism while maintaining the monarchy. The nation can then branch further into:
- **Fascism** (Garda de Fier / Iron Guard, led by Corneliu Codreanu initially, later Ion Antonescu)
- **Communism** (Partidul Comunist Român, led by Constantin Ion Parhon)
- **Non-Aligned/Despotism** (Frontul Renașterii Naționale, led by Armand Călinescu or Carol II)
- **Democracy** (via the "King Michael's Coup" focus, restoring liberal rule)

Romania's unique flexibility lies in the fact that these ideological shifts are **not mutually exclusive** in the focus tree—the player can shift between them based on political priority, allowing rapid pivots in foreign policy.

## Unique mechanics

### 1. Institute Royal Dictatorship Path
Romania can convert from constitutional monarchy to absolute monarchy via the "Institute Royal Dictatorship" focus (available as early as 1936, though historically occurred in 1938). This enables despotism while preserving the Crown, permitting Romania to pursue authoritarian policies without adopting fascism or communism outright.

### 2. Ideological Flexibility
The focus tree allows Romania to shift ideologies multiple times without the usual political cost caps. This is unusual for HOI4 and allows Romania to:
- Join the Comintern, Axis, or Allies (or create the Cordon Sanitaire)
- Pivot between support for Iron Guard (fascism) and Securitate (communism)
- Execute the "King Michael's Coup" to restore democracy after any ideological swing

### 3. Balkan Dominance
Romania can pursue regional hegemony by puppeting, annexing, or declaring war on neighboring Balkan states: Bulgaria, Greece, Yugoslavia, Turkey, Hungary, and Czechoslovakia.

### 4. Preserve Greater Romania
This branch centralizes Romania's diplomatic options: joining factions (Comintern, Axis, Allies) or forming the **Cordon Sanitaire** (an alliance with Poland and Czechoslovakia to resist German and Soviet pressure).

### 5. Naval and Military Development
- **Expand the Galati Shipyards** branch upgrades naval construction and grants naval spirits. With Arms Against Tyranny enabled, choices between military industrial organizations (MIOs) appear.
- **Army Maneuvers** branch grants free division templates and army officers.
- **Expand the Air Force** branch improves air capacity and enables licensing of foreign aircraft.

> ⚠️ Arms Against Tyranny is required for full naval MIO mechanics. Without it, naval improvements are still available but with fewer choices.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

### Bordering States
- **West:** Hungary (disarmed in 1936, but poses long-term threat)
- **North/Northwest:** Poland (friendly, but expected to fall to Germany; see Polish-Romanian Alliance)
- **North/Northeast:** Soviet Union (soon undertaking military purges, but a long-term existential threat)
- **North:** Czechoslovakia (preoccupied with its own crises)
- **Southeast:** Bulgaria (poses little threat in 1936)
- **South:** Yugoslavia (faces significant internal instability)
- **South:** Greece (allied via Balkan Pact)

### Starting Alliances and Guarantees
- **Guarantees given:** Czechoslovakia, Yugoslavia, Turkey, Greece (as part of Little Entente and Balkan Pact)
- **Guarantees received:** France, Czechoslovakia (April 1939 in historical gameplay)
- **Faction membership:** None initially; can be recruited to Little Entente if France creates it

### Strategic Position
Romania occupies a precarious middle position in a region of great-power competition:
1. **German pressure from the west:** Will likely demand northern Transylvania and other territories
2. **Soviet pressure from the north/east:** Will demand Bessarabia and northern Bukovina
3. **Hungarian and Bulgarian claims:** On Transylvania, Banat, and Dobruja respectively

Romania's primary strategic options historically were:
- **Alliance with the West:** Join the Little Entente / Entente alliance (France, Czechoslovakia, Poland)
- **Submission to the Axis:** Accept German hegemony and receive guarantees in exchange for territorial losses
- **Resistance via Cordon Sanitaire:** Form an independent bloc with Poland and Czechoslovakia

### Historical Outcome (For Reference)
In history, Romania accepted German hegemony after France's collapse in 1940, suffered territorial dismemberment (losing Transylvania to Hungary, Dobruja to Bulgaria, and Bessarabia to the Soviet Union), underwent a military coup installing Ion Antonescu, and joined the Axis in November 1940. Romania became a major Axis power with the fourth-largest army by 1942.
