## Government and political situation

**Ruling ideology:** Non-Aligned  
**Ruling party:** União Nacional (National Union)  
**Government leader:** António de Oliveira Salazar (Conservative Nationalist trait: +10% Stability, +20% Infrastructure construction speed)

Portugal's government is the Estado Novo ("New State") regime established in 1933. The regime, based on Catholic traditionalism, authoritarianism, and staunch anti-communism, is led by economist and Prime Minister António de Oliveira Salazar. The regime maintains a neutral foreign policy stance and has no elections.

**Alternative political parties and paths:**
- **Aliança Republicana e Socialista (Democratic):** Led by José Norton de Matos; can reach power via "Allow Free Elections" focus.
- **Partido Comunista Português (Communist):** Led by Bento Gonçalves; can reach power via "Reorganization of the Communist Party" focus.
- **Movimento Nacional-Sindicalista (Fascist):** Led by Francisco de Barcelos Rolão Preto; can reach power via "Ditadura Militar" focus.
- **Causa Monárquica (Despotist/Monarchist):** Led by Dom Duarte Nuno; can reach power via "Restoration of the Monarchy" focus.

Portugal can adopt 8 different country names depending on ideology and focus tree choices: Portuguese Republic, Maximalist Portugal, Greater Portugal, Fifth Portuguese Empire, Kingdom of Portugal, Anarchist Commune of Portugal, and puppet states.

## Unique mechanics

Portugal receives a unique focus tree with La Résistance DLC, divided into 5 branches and 5 sub-branches:

**Colonial Assimilation Policy branch:** Manages Portuguese colonies and their industries; unlocks core claims and compliance bonuses on African territory.

**Continue the Public Works branch:** Expands industry; grants additional research slots and land/air military research boosts.

**Second Navy Reequipment branch:** Naval industry expansion; grants additional research slot and navy research boosts.

**Army Reorganization branch:** Military infrastructure and land doctrines.

**Popular Front/Estado Novo branch:** Allows choice of ideology and stance on Spanish Civil War; leads to five sub-branches—Communist (Nation in Arms), Communist (Support Spanish Republic), Democratic (Strict Neutrality), Fascist/Non-Aligned (Support Nationalists), Monarchist (Royal Wedding).

**Formable nations:**
- Iberian Socialist Union (requires Communist ideology and "The Iberian Socialist Union" focus).
- United Kingdom of Portugal and Brazil (requires "The Kingdom Reunited" focus).

**Colonial releases:** Portugal may release or return Angola, Mozambique, Cabo Verde, Timor-Leste, Guinea-Bissau as puppets. Macau can be returned to China; Goa can be returned to British Raj.

**Colonial coring:** Via "Luso-Tropicalism" focus, Portugal can core several African states: Cabinda, Luanda, Zambesi, South West Angola, Lourenço Marques, Manica e Sofala, Zambezia-Moçambique.

**Military industrial organizations:** Portugal has unique designers including OGMA (light aircraft), OGME (motorized), and specialized manufacturers for artillery and naval equipment, unlocked through focus tree completion.

Without La Résistance, Portugal uses the generic focus tree.

## Geography and neighbours

Portugal occupies the western Iberian Peninsula and controls extensive colonies. Its mainland borders only Spain. Colonial holdings span Africa (Angola, Mozambique, Portuguese Guinea, Cape Verde, Sao Tome), Asia (Macau in China, East Timor, Goa in India), and Atlantic islands (Azores, Madeira).

Core states (7): Lisbon, Porto, Guarda, Santarém, Beja, Madeira, Azores.

**Diplomatic position:**
- Anglo-Portuguese Alliance: +30 opinion modifier with United Kingdom (starts with).
- Fascist leanings: +15 opinion modifiers from Germany and Italy (La Résistance).
- Pursues neutrality in World War II unless influenced by focus tree path.

## Military

**Army (1936):** 18 divisions (15 Infantry, 3 Cavalry), ~65K manpower.  
**Navy (1936):** 9 ships (6 Destroyers Douro-class and Guadiana-class, 3 Submarines Delfim-class), 150 convoys.  
**Air Force (1936):** 24 planes (12 Close Air Support, 12 Tactical Bombers).

Portugal's military is small but functional. The army is underequipped relative to neighboring Spain, making immediate large-scale war inadvisable. The navy is primarily coastal defense-oriented.
