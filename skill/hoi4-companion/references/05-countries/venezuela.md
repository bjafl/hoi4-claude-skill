## Government and political situation

Venezuela starts under fascist rule with President Eleazar López Contreras, a former general who inherited an authoritarian system and focused on infrastructure development and constitutionalization of the government. The ruling Consejo de Ministros party holds 80% of political support.

**Political parties and leaders:**
- Consejo de Ministros (Fascist, 80%, ruling) — Eleazar López Contreras
- Partido Comunista de Venezuela (Communist, 15%) — Juan Bautista Fuenmayor
- Copei (Democratic, 5%) — Luis Figueroa
- Acción Democrática (Non-Aligned, 0%) — Patricio Franco

**Possible ideological paths:** Venezuela can shift toward communism, democracy, or non-aligned via its political parties. Alternative leaders available for each ideology include Juan Bautista Fuenmayor (Communist), Luis Figueroa (Democratic), and Patricio Franco (Non-Aligned, with traits: Armaments Organizer, Financial Expert).

## National spirits

**Blockade Inactive:** Foreign powers can blockade Venezuela's oil shipments to Fascist-aligned nations if they control more than 2 capital ships in the Caribbean Sea. This exposes Venezuelan oil exports to interference.

**Monroe Doctrine:** Venezuela's independence is guaranteed by the United States, providing automatic diplomatic protection under the U.S. sphere of influence.

**Diplomatic Trade:** Venezuela is historically significant as a major oil exporter; it continues selling oil to Axis and Allied powers during WWII until Allied pressure forces cessation in 1941. U.S. pressure leads to modernization via aircraft and naval purchases.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Borders:** Colombia, Brazil, British Guiana, and straits to Netherlands and United Kingdom.

**International Position:** Guaranteed by the United States under the Monroe Doctrine. Venezuela remains officially neutral in 1939 but later declares war on the Axis in February 1945. The nation serves as a critical oil supplier to Axis powers until 1941, when British blockades and U.S. pressure force Venezuela to cease trade with Germany and grant Allied military access to its airfields and harbors.
