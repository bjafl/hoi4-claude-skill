Iraq is a medium-sized non-aligned state in West Asia with significant oil resources. Starting as a kingdom under the Hashemite Dynasty, Iraq faces internal pressure from the Golden Square military faction and entanglement with British interests through the Anglo-Iraqi Treaty. The Graveyard of Empires DLC provides unique mechanics; without it, Iraq uses the generic national focus tree.

## Government and political situation

Starting government is the Hashemite Dynasty (Non-aligned/Despotism) under King Ghazi I (or Yasin Al-Hashimi with Graveyard of Empires). Iraq begins with no elections and cannot hold them without ideological change.

### Political parties at 1936 start

Notable historical context: Following the 1941 coup attempt by Rashid Ali al-Gaylani and the Golden Square, Iraq sees competing power centers — the monarchy and British-backed Regent 'Abd al-Ilah, versus nationalist and military factions pushing for independence and anti-British policy.

### Ideology branching

The focus tree (Graveyard of Empires DLC) offers multiple paths:
- **Strengthen Monarchy:** Maintain Hashemite rule and non-aligned status or pivot to conservatives (British-aligned).
- **Golden Square / Fascism:** Support Rashid Ali al-Gaylani and the nationalist military faction, potentially joining the Axis.
- **Communist path:** Support Yusuf Salman Yusuf and the Iraqi Communist Party.
- **Kurdish autonomy:** Launch a civil war to form Kurdistan as a separate state.
- **Arab League:** Form an independent faction with other Arab nations.

## National spirits

Iraq begins with four major national spirits (Graveyard of Empires DLC only):

### The Golden Square
- Political Power Gain: –20%
- Stability: –20%
- Daily Fascism support: +0.02

Military and nationalist faction within the armed forces, undermining governmental authority and driving pro-Axis leanings.

### Anglo-Iraqi Treaty
- Political Power Gain: –10%
- Stability: –20%
- Attack/Defense bonus against Iraq (United Kingdom): –10%

Formal treaty with Britain governing oil rights and military basing, constraining Iraqi sovereignty while offering some defensive guarantees against external powers.

### Agrarian Society
- Recruitable population factor: –20%
- Consumer Goods Factories factor: +15%
- Construction Speed: –10%
- Factory output: –15%

Iraq's economy remains agricultural and underdeveloped; industrial capacity is limited.

### Royal Iraqi Army
- Army experience Gain: –3%
- Division Organization: –5%
- Recruitable population factor: –5%
- Planning Speed: –10%
- Hot acclimatization gain factor: +15%

Armed forces lack professional training and doctrine but gain bonuses in desert warfare and hot climates.

## Unique mechanics

### Graveyard of Empires content (DLC required)

**Unique focus tree branches:**
- **Decouple from the Pound:** Industrial development, dam construction, reduction of Agrarian Society penalties.
- **Nationalize Anglo-Iraqi Oil Company / Anglo-Iraqi Oil Expansion:** Seize or negotiate expanded British oil rights; significant ideological consequences.
- **Ensure Sunni Dominance:** Choose between monarchy/democracy or fascism; dictatorial path available via Bakr Sidqi.
- **Promote Kurdish Identity:** Civil war leading to formation of Kurdistan (removes Kurdish revolt, British resource rights, cores Kurdish territory).
- **Saadabad Pact:** Joint branch with Turkey, Iran, Afghanistan to build regional alliance.
- **Camelry availability:** Battle for the Bosporus DLC enables camelry unit type for Iraq.

**Exclusive industrial and military companies** (requires specific focus completions):
- Iraqi State Railways: Railway/supply hub construction +15%, train production cost –25%
- State Company for Iron and Steel: Steel output +10
- Iraq Petroleum Company: Oil output +20, synthetic resources research +15%
- British Overseas Airways Corporation: Air range +5%, supply range +5% (at peace with UK)
- Nairn Transport Company: Desert supply penalty –15%, supply consumption –5%

### Historical events and decisions

Iraq can receive British ultimatum events relating to the Anglo-Iraqi War (May 1941 in-game equivalent). Bakr Sidqi, Rashid Ali al-Gaylani, and other historical figures trigger branching political paths via national focuses and events.

### Diplomatic context

Iraq grants oil to the United Kingdom (Battle for the Bosporus DLC). Initially tied to Britain via treaty and de facto subject status, Iraq is a primary target for:
- **Soviet Union:** May seek war goal via "Southern Thrust" focus.
- **United Kingdom:** Can justify "Securing Iraq" focus; initially protective via treaty.
- **Nationalist/Axis actors:** Golden Square and fascist factions push Iraq toward Germany and Italy.

The Kingdom of Iraq can form the **Arab League** (independent Arab faction) or dissolve into **Kurdistan** (if Kurdish path is chosen). Limited major-power status; remains a minor nation unless industrialized significantly or enters a powerful faction.
