## Summary

The Sultanate of Muscat and Oman is a British protectorate in the Middle East with approximately 490K core population spread across four states (Muscat, Dhofar, Musandam, and Oman). It uses the generic national focus tree and begins as a Non-Aligned state under the Al Said Dynasty. It is one of the smallest nations with minimal starting industry (0.13M factories) and limited military forces (one infantry division).

## Government and political situation

Oman starts as a **Non-Aligned protectorate** under British control. The ruling party is the **Al Said Dynasty**, led by **Said bin Taimur** (Despotic ideology). The starting government name is "Sultanate of Muscat and Oman."

**Available political parties:**
- Non-Aligned (Al Said Dynasty) — 100% popularity, ruling
- Democratic (Ahmad ibn Ja'far, Liberalism)
- Communist (Halil ibn Halil, Anarchist Communism; PFLO)
- Fascist (Iskandar ibn Ali)

**Alternative government names:**
- Republic of Oman (Democratic)
- People's Republic of Oman (Communist)
- Omani Empire (Fascist)

**Key ministers:**
- Rashid al-Kharusi — Political advisor, +15% Political power gain
- Khalfan bin Master — Industrial advisor, +10% construction speed (civilian/military/railway/refinery)
- Marhoon Muhaid — Diplomatic advisor, +25% improve relations opinion

**Starting traits (Said bin Taimur):** Inexperienced Sultan (Stability +5%, Justify War goal time -30%, Unaligned Diplomacy +30).

## National spirits

Oman does not have any starting national spirits.

## Unique mechanics

_No unique mechanics documented on the wiki._ Oman lacks a unique national focus tree and uses the generic tree instead, with branches for Army, Aviation, Naval, Industrial, and Political effort. The political branch allows ideological path-switching.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Borders:** Saudi Arabia (northwest), United Kingdom (west and north).

**Diplomatic status:** British protectorate (subject state). Oman is a releasable tag from British control and may form the unified Arabia nation if it controls the necessary states.

**Starting economy:** Volunteer conscription, Export focus trade law, Civilian Economy. Expected consumer goods load: 35% of factories. Trade law grants +10% factory output and +5% research speed.

**Starting ideology support:**
- Non-Aligned: 100% (ruling)
- Democracy, Communism, Fascism: 0% each

Historical context: The sultanate was historically divided in 1920 by the British-brokered Treaty of Seeb, with the interior ruled by Ibadite imams and coastal areas by the Sultan. In 1936, it remains a protectorate. The Sultan declared war on Germany on 10 September 1939.
