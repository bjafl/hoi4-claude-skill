El Salvador is a small Central American nation with a fascist government under Maximiliano Hernández Martínez. With limited industry and resources but decent starting military strength, it is positioned to expand within Central America. Its independence is guaranteed by the United States via the Monroe Doctrine, but this does not prevent internal conflicts or diplomatic maneuvers. El Salvador lacks a unique national focus tree and must use the generic tree.

## Government and political situation

El Salvador starts as a **Fascist dictatorship**. Maximiliano Hernández Martínez is the sole ruling leader with no government positions occupied. The country can switch to Democratic (leader: Victoriano Ubico; country name: Republic of El Salvador), Communist (leader: Benito Ubico; country name: Central American Socialist Republic), or Non-Aligned (leader: Bartolomé Vasconcelos; country name: Teocracia de El Salvador) via the generic focus tree's Political Effort branch, with elections scheduled for January 1939.

Available ministers and military staff include industrial organizers (Oscar A. Bolaños, Salvador Castañeda Castro, Jose Maria Cuellar), doctrine theorists (Reinaldo Galindo Pohl for naval, José Angél Castillo for air, Manuel Jose Maria Lemus Lopez for land), and various military commanders and designers.

## National spirits

**Monroe Doctrine:** Independence guaranteed by the United States. This spirit provides protection from external intervention but is not a defensive alliance. The guarantee does not prevent El Salvador from being attacked or from declaring wars of its own.

## Unique mechanics

No unique mechanics documented on the wiki. El Salvador uses the generic national focus tree with five main branches: Army Effort, Aviation Effort, Naval Effort, Industrial Effort, and Political Effort.

**Notable diplomatic option:** El Salvador may form the **United States of Central America** if it controls all other independent Central American countries (excluding Panama). This grants cores on the required territories plus British Honduras (state 311).

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering countries:** Honduras (north), Guatemala (west).

**Diplomatic situation:** Independence guaranteed by United States (Monroe Doctrine). Starting faction: none (independent). El Salvador begins as the only fascist nation in Central America, giving it a potential path to regional dominance if it can unite the region under the United States of Central America focus path.

**Starting military:** 3 infantry divisions (Brigada de Infanteria; 9.0K manpower), 5 civilian convoys, no air force or navy.
