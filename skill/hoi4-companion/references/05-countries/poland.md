## Summary

Poland is a regional power in Central Europe with a population of 31.97M, caught geographically between the German Reich and Soviet Union—two rapidly militarizing superpowers. While its industry is decent with potential for growth, it cannot match the armament pace of either neighbor. Poland has guarantees from France and the United Kingdom but lacks the war-supporting domestic consensus to rely on them, forcing it to navigate a perilous political situation while preparing for inevitable conflict.

## Government and political situation

Poland begins under Sanacja (Non-Aligned), the ruling party with 65% support led by Ignacy Mościcki. The political landscape is fractious: multiple factions compete for influence, including the Sanation Left, Sanation Right, Peasant movement (Polskie Stronnictwo Ludowe), Communist Party (2% support), and Fascist movements (15% combined). Several opposition parties exist with alternative leaders (Stanisław Mikołajczyk for democrats, Władysław Gomułka for communists, Bolesław Piasecki for fascists, and monarchist claimants including Habsburgs, Hohenzollerns, and Romanovs).

Poland is guaranteed by France and the United Kingdom, offering diplomatic protection but not reliable military support given their low war enthusiasm in 1936.

## Unique mechanics

Poland has an extensive unique national focus tree (free DLC; expanded with No Step Back) divided into 10 major branches:

- **The Four Year Plan:** Factories, research slots, railways, infrastructure; removes Peasant's Strike penalties
- **Develop Polish Ship Building / Clamp Down on Danzig:** Naval capacity and industry transfer options
- **Prepare For The Next War:** Army and air force tech bonuses; Plan East/Plan West sub-branches allow preparation against Soviet or German threat (mutually exclusive until one is completed)
- **Complete the April Constitution:** Balance Sanation Left/Right factions with risk of civil war; sub-branches offer Democratic (Baltic Alliance path), Fascist, or Castle (monarchist) routes
- **Restore the Sejm:** Choose Democratic, Fascist, or Communist ideologies (No Step Back disabled only)
- **Organize the Peasant's Strike:** Align with Peasant Revolt for Democratic or Communist paths; Communist can choose Soviet alignment or independence; Democratic can join Allies or form faction with France; unlock colonial holdings in Liberia or Madagascar
- **Assemble the Regency Council:** Form a Monarchy; sub-branches include Habsburg (personal union with Czechoslovakia), Hohenzollern (personal union with Romania/Hungary, form Poland-Romania), Commonwealth Claimant (personal union with Lithuania, form Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth), Romanov (secret, requires Anastasia Romanov event; gives war goals vs. Soviet Union and Yugoslavia, enables Slavic Union), Cossack-King (Pavel Bermondt-Avalov; switches to Fascism; war goals vs. Germany or becomes Reichsprotektorate; enables Don Host/Kuban Host)
- **A Nationalist Constitution:** Fascist branch; option to become German Reichsprotektorate or remain independent; can form Falangist faction
- **The Between The Seas Concept (Międzymorze):** Ally Finland, Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Czechoslovakia, Austria, Hungary, Yugoslavia, Greece, Turkey, or Italy
- **Prepare for the Inevitable:** Continue war effort after capitulation with off-map factories and manpower; includes Expand Polish Intelligence sub-branch (requires La Résistance DLC)

Without DLC, Poland uses the generic focus tree.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering countries:** German Reich (west), Soviet Union (east), Czechoslovakia (south), Lithuania (northeast), Romania (southeast)

**Diplomacy:** Guaranteed by France and United Kingdom; in Non-Aligned faction with no formal alliance. Poland's diplomatic position is defensive: it lacks the industrial capacity to match either major neighbor in an arms race and must choose between appeasing one power to focus on the other, or maintaining precarious independence through military preparation. Internal political friction between Sanation factions further constrains rapid rearmament.
