A collection of formable nations that can be created by various North American, Central American, and Caribbean countries through decisions and focus tree branches. These range from nation-states formed by unification (Antillean Confederation, United States of Central America) to puppets released by major powers (Dominion of North America, United Kingdom of America).

## Antillean Confederation

**Tag:** Non-aligned form uses tag ANT.

A regional confederation of Caribbean island nations. Can be formed by Haiti, Dominican Republic, Puerto Rico, Cuba, Jamaica, Bahamas, Guadeloupe, or British Antilles by controlling Dominican Republic (319), Haiti (318), and Cuba (315).

**Formation decision:** "Unite the Antilles" — requires controlling all three core states above. Additional decisions available after formation allow coring of Curaçao (695), Trinidad (691), and surrounding Caribbean territories.

**Ideology names:**
- Democratic: Federative Republic of the Antilles
- Communist: Union of Antillean Soviet Socialist Republics
- Fascist: Antillean Empire
- Non-Aligned: Antillean Confederation

**Core states on formation:** Dominican Republic (319), Haiti (318), Cuba (315), Jamaica (689), Northern Bahamas (690), Southern Bahamas (693), Puerto Rico (686), French Caribbean (694), Leeward Islands (308).

## Confederated Canadian First Nations

**Tag:** Trial of Allegiance decision (Canada tag with special government).

A reorganization of Canadian territory under indigenous leadership. Formation available only to Chile when it follows the Mapuche State path and completes the "Crusade Against Imperialism" focus. Chile can then trigger a decision to reorganize Canada into this form.

**Ideology names:**
- Democratic: Federation of Canadian First Nations
- Communist: Union of Canadian First Nations
- Fascist: The Great Canadian First Nations
- Non-Aligned: Confederated Canadian First Nations

## Confederated States of America

**Tag:** CSA.

A fascist American nation formed when the United States follows its fascist branch during the American Civil War. Specifically requires the Boston Rebellion to occur, the Loyalists to gain control, and completion of the "Honor the Confederacy" focus and its associated decisions before civil war ends. The decision series includes renaming the capital to Richmond, enshrining states' rights, and constitutional convention changes.

A Communist version (Confederacy of American Workers / Confederacy of American States) also appears in the same civil war when the US goes communist.

**Ideology names:**
- Democratic: Confederacy of American States
- Communist: Confederacy of American Workers
- Fascist: Confederated States of America
- Non-Aligned: Loyalists / Confederation of America

**Known bug:** Granting statehood to Alaska, Hawaii, or Puerto Rico after formation causes the nation to revert to "Free American Empire" (CSA fascist) or "United Kingdom of America" (Loyalists non-aligned) with default USA flag.

## Dominion of North America

**Tag:** DAN.

A British dominion created by the United Kingdom as part of Imperial Federation formation. The UK must complete "Unite the Anglosphere" focus, control all continental US territory plus Alaska, and unlock the "Create Pan-North American State" decision. When this decision is taken, Canada annexes all US states and becomes the Dominion of North America. Available even if USA still exists, as long as UK controls the continental territory.

**Formation note:** UK must not incite a pro-imperial civil war in Canada, as this causes a new Canada entity that blocks the decision. If already done, reduce the new Canada's autonomy, annex it, re-release as puppet, then retry.

**Core states on formation:** All of Canada and all of United States core territory.

**Ideology names (as Dominion state):**
- Democratic: Canada
- Communist: Canada Popular State
- Fascist: Unitary Canada
- Non-Aligned: Free Canadian Republic
- Special (Dominion alignment): Dominion of North America

## Hispaniola

**Tag:** HAI (as Haiti) or DOM (as Dominican Republic) before formation; becomes HSP or similar.

Unification of the island of Hispaniola. Can be formed by Haiti or Dominican Republic by controlling both Haiti (318) and Dominican Republic (319).

**Formation decision:** "The Unification of Hispaniola" — requires control of both states. Haiti and Dominican Republic cannot both be part of the Federative Republic of the Antilles when this decision is taken.

Chile can also create it as a monarchist puppet via the "Satellite Kingdoms" focus; in that case it gains additional cores on Cuba, Jamaica, Puerto Rico, and North/South Bahamas.

**Ideology names:**
- Democratic: Federation of Hispaniola
- Communist: Hispaniolan People's Republic
- Fascist: New Chiefdom of Hispaniola
- Non-Aligned: Hispaniola

**Core states on formation:** Dominican Republic (319), Haiti (318).

## United Kingdom of America

**Tag:** UKA.

A North American monarchy created by the United Kingdom. Formation requires UK to be non-aligned (monarchist path), force the marriage of Edward VIII and Wallis Simpson (non-morganatic), empower the King's Party, complete "Unite the Anglosphere" focus, and control all starting US core territory. Then unlock and take "Declare American Monarchy" decision.

**On formation:** USA is released as a UK puppet, renamed to United Kingdom of America, ideology shifts to non-aligned with Wallis Simpson (as Queen Wallis I) as leader. Washington D.C. is renamed to Wallington and Maryland is renamed Duchy of Wallington.

**Special modifiers for Wallis I:** +10% factory output, -25% trade/economy law costs, -30% political power gain, -20% stability. +0.05 daily non-aligned support.

**Ideology names (as puppet state):**
- Democratic: United States
- Communist: Communist States of America
- Fascist: Free American Empire
- Non-Aligned: United Kingdom of America

## United States of Central America

**Tag:** UCA.

A regional confederation representing the Federal Republic of Central America (historical 1823–1841). Can be formed by Guatemala, Belize, El Salvador, Honduras, Nicaragua, or Costa Rica by controlling all six states.

**Formation decision:** "Reconstitute the Federal Republic" — requires controls of Guatemala (313), El Salvador (314), Honduras (312), Nicaragua (317), Costa Rica (316), and Belize. Upon formation, gains cores on all above plus British Honduras (311). Additional decisions available to core Panamá (304), Panamá Canal (685), and Yucatán (474).

**Ideology names:**
- Democratic: United States of Central America
- Communist: Central American Socialist State
- Fascist: Central American Empire
- Non-Aligned: Federal Republic of Central America

**Core states on formation:** Guatemala (313), El Salvador (314), Honduras (312), Nicaragua (317), Costa Rica (316), British Honduras (311).
