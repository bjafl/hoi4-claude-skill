Lebanon is a French colony in the Middle East with 1.10 M population and one state (Beirut). Located on the Mediterranean coast, it borders French-controlled Syria to the east and British Mandatory Palestine to the south. It starts with no army, no air force, only 5 convoys, and is currently subject to France. Like most minors, it uses the generic national focus tree and can pursue the path to form Arabia if it gains independence and conquers the required territories.

## Government and political situation

Lebanon starts as a Democratic state governed by the Constitutional Bloc under Prime Minister Abdullah Bayhoum. Elections are held every 4 years (next in January 1940). The state is a French colony with no independent diplomatic status.

## National spirits

Lebanon does not have any national spirits.

## Unique mechanics

Lebanon can attempt to form **Arabia** (along with Mesopotamia) by conquering the requisite territories as an Arab country. This is a formable nation available through conquest; the specific focus or decision mechanics are not detailed on the wiki.

Lebanon uses the generic national focus tree, which offers five branches: Army Effort, Aviation Effort, Naval Effort, Industrial Effort, and Political Effort. Political Effort can unlock paths to other ideologies (Democratic, Communist, Fascist, Non-Aligned).

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering states:**
- Syria (French puppet) to the east
- Mandatory Palestine (British controlled) to the south
- Mediterranean coast to the west

**Faction:** None at start (subject to France).

**Note:** Lebanon is a one-province minor with no standing military, making early expansion extremely difficult without external support (lend-lease, allies). Historical independence was declared in November 1941 under French pressure; in-game, independence requires breaking free from France through conquest, civil war, or diplomatic manipulation.

Lebanon's historical status as a French colony and its minimal starting military make it one of the weakest starting positions in the game. Surviving the early game requires careful diplomacy and significant reliance on ideology shifts and external support to gain any military footing.
