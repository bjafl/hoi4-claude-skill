A minor Non-Aligned monarchy in East Africa starting at war with Italy. Ethiopia possesses limited industry and resources, with significant national spirits reflecting economic backwardness and military weakness. Its unique focus tree offers multiple ideological paths: remaining monarchist under Haile Selassie, collaborating with Italy, or pursuing communism, with branching events that unlock different governments and allow formation of alternative East African states.

## Geography

Ethiopia owns 10 states distributed across the East African highlands: Bale, Begemder, Gojjam, Hararghe, Illubabor-Kaffa, Shewa (containing the capital Addis Ababa with VP 5), Sidamo, Tigray, Welega, and Wello. Terrain is predominantly mountains and hills with minimal desert; several states are classified as "Pastoral Region" or "Rural Region" with limited infrastructure.

## Government and political situation

Haile Selassie rules as absolute monarch under the Solomonic Dynasty (ruling party), maintaining traditional despotic authority. Alternative political movements exist: the Non-Aligned Solomonic Dynasty (70%), the Fascist Gugsa Line (20%, led by Haile Selassie Gugsa), the Communist Black Lions (5%, led by Alemework Beyene), and the Democratic EDU (5%, led by Imru Haile Selassie). Shifts to other ideologies unlock different leaders and country names (Ethiopian Empire, People's Democratic Republic of Ethiopia, Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia).

Haile Selassie begins with bonuses to ideology drift defense (+10%), political power gain (+5-10%), and mobilization speed (+5%), with progressive unlocks of stability gains and command power increases through the tech tree.

## Unique mechanics

Ethiopia's unique focus tree (By Blood Alone required) is structured in six major branches offering distinct strategic paths:

- **Ecole Militaire Haile Selassie 1er:** Military modernization, enabling removal of Traditional Warfare, Air Force in Infancy, and No Naval Tradition spirits. The Chitet conscription law can be upgraded.
- **Develop Shewa:** Industrial expansion and railroad/dockyard construction; removes Corrupt Bureaucracy and allows formation of the Horn of Africa faction. Foreign military designers available.
- **The Second Italo-Ethiopian War:** Defensive bonuses and fortification focuses against Italy. Bonuses to unit attack and penalties imposed on Italy.
- **Rally Around the Emperor (Monarchist):** Maintains Haile Selassie. Branches unlock government-in-exile appeals to Germany, United Kingdom, Sweden, or Japan (each granting unique bonuses). Also enables formation of alternative nations (Empire of Aksum, Empire of Solomon with Caribbean expansion, Empire of Zion). Post-war reconstruction and federalization unlock formation of African Union or Organization of African Unity.
- **Reach Out to the Italians (Fascist):** Triggers civil war with opportunity to become Italian collaborator as Governorate of Ethiopia, Gojjam, or Jimma. Allows rejection of annexation in exchange for subordinate status and Italian resource/designer transfers.
- **Military Communism (Communist):** Flips Ethiopia communist. Branches unlock appeals to global communist nations, Spanish Civil War intervention (La Résistance required), and decision to form anarcho-communist or centralized Workers Party variants. Soviet puppet option available, with conditional independence war decision.

Additional decisions unlock federation of Horn of Africa nations and formation of continental African factions post-war.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Borders:** British Somaliland (UK colony) to the northeast; French Somaliland (France) to the north; Sultanate of Aussa to the northeast; British Kenya (UK) to the west; Italian Eritrea and Somalia to the north and south (across active war border).

**Diplomatic status:** Non-Aligned in 1936, starting in active defensive war against Italy (begun 1935, historical Second Italo-Abyssinian War). Has positive relations with other Non-Aligned nations (+20 peace, +10 if at war together); negative relations with aligned powers (-10 baseline). Begins without formal alliances but can seek military access, alliance, or puppet agreements depending on ideology and focus path chosen. European powers may accept government-in-exile status if Ethiopia pivots ideologically.

**Alliance options:** Can eventually join Comintern (communist path), form African faction (monarchist path post-war), or become Italian satellite (fascist collaborator path).
