## Summary

Bolivia is a minor South American country with a population of 2.38 million, bordered by Brazil, Peru, Paraguay, Argentina, and Chile. Politically divided between democratic, communist, fascist, and non-aligned factions, Bolivia begins recovering from the Chaco War (1932–1935) against Paraguay, a burden reflected in its national spirits. It is guaranteed independence by the United States via the Monroe Doctrine.

## National spirits

**Monroe Doctrine** (all games)  
Independence guaranteed by the United States; permanent.

**The Toll of the Chaco War** (requires Trial of Allegiance DLC)  
Remnants of the 1932–1935 war against Paraguay. Expires 1 November 1939 (day 1400).
- Recruitable population: −15%
- War support: −15%
- Division Attack: −10%
- Justify War goal time: +30%

**The Era of National Regression** (requires Trial of Allegiance DLC)  
Lasting economic and political damage from the Chaco defeat. Removed only if Bolivia restores "Greater Bolivia" by controlling Acre (940), Antofagasta (506), and Chaco Boreal (688) simultaneously.
- Political Power Gain: −20%
- Stability: −10%
- War support: −5%

## Unique mechanics

Bolivia has no unique national focus tree; it uses the generic focus tree instead. The generic tree provides five main branches:

- **Aviation Effort** — air experience, air bases, and air research
- **Naval Effort** — navy experience, naval dockyards, and naval research
- **Industrial Effort** — civilian/military factory construction, infrastructure, and extra research slots

**Diplomatic Mechanics:**  
Bolivia has a core claim on Chaco Boreal (688), currently controlled by Paraguay. It also holds claims on Argentina's Formosa (957) and Los Andes (959), and Chile's Antofagasta (506). A unique decision allows Bolivia to form the **Peru-Bolivian Confederation** after conquering Peru, granting cores across South America and removing the Bolivian Irridentism debuff.

**Available Cosmetic Names:** None unique; uses generic equipment designations.

**Alliances & Guarantees:**

**Historical Note:** Bolivia recovered from the Chaco War (1932–1935) against Paraguay and, historically, would remain neutral or non-combatant when declaring war on Germany in 1943, instead exporting tin to the Allies.
