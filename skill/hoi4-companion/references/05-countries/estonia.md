A minor non-aligned nation on the Baltic coast of Northern Europe with a population of 1.1 M. Estonia borders Latvia to the south and the Soviet Union to the east. It has a unique national focus tree (requires No Step Back) and faces existential pressure from Soviet expansion; its survival depends on allying with a major power. Estonia can form the Baltic Federation or pursue fascist unification of the Finno-Ugric peoples.

## Government and political situation

Konstantin Päts (Oligarchic, ruling) leads the Isamaaliit party. Estonia is constitutionally non-aligned with a fragile political balance constrained by two competing national spirits that limit stability and recruitment. Other political parties present: Rahvuslik Keskerakond (Democratic, 20%), Eestimaa Kommunistlik Partei (Communist, 5%), and Eesti Vabadussõjalaste Liit / Vapsid (Fascist, 15%).

### Possible regime changes

- **Democratic:** Jaan Tōnisson (Conservatism) via focus Break the Silence
- **Communist:** Johannes Kabin (Stalinism) or Jaan Anvelt (Marxism with +10% division attack/defense on core territory) via focus Overthrow Isamaaliit, contingent on whether Enter Opposition is taken
- **Fascist:** Artur Sirk (Fascism) via focus The March on Tallinn, may trigger civil war

> ⚠️ These competing spirits leave Estonia with -25% total stability at game start, severely constraining military buildup and requiring focus completion to improve the situation.

## Unique mechanics

**National focus tree** (No Step Back): Estonia has a unique focus tree divided into four main branches:

- **Rejoin the Railways:** Shared Baltic branch upgrading industry and military; left side requires investment from a Fascist or Democratic major.
- **Restore the Worker's Republic:** Shared Baltic branch enabling Communist path; allows puppet status under Soviet Union or independence, plus expansion to other Baltic states and Scandinavia.
- **The Era of Silence:** Historical branch providing military/civilian factories and industrial bonuses; choice between Democratic path or staying Non-Aligned; removes Vaps threat and enables puppet surrender option.
- **Rally the Nation:** Fascist branch triggering possible civil war; enables flipping Finland Fascist and annexation, gaining war goals against Scandinavian nations or USSR; paths to form Nordic League or Estonia-Finland League, eventually the Empire of Finno-Ugra.

Sub-branches: Formalize the Baltic Entente (shared, allowing Baltic alliance and UK alignment), The Threat on Our Border (land forts with Germany or USSR specialization), and Prepare for the Inevitable (post-capitulation army buildup).

Without No Step Back, Estonia uses the generic focus tree.

**Formation options:**
- Baltic Federation (with Latvia and Lithuania)
- Estonia-Finland League (with annexed/cored Finland)
- Empire of Finno-Ugra (completing full Finno-Ugric unification via Nordic League or parallel branch)

**Political parties and support mechanics:** Democratic Opposition and Low Vaps Tension spirits impose stability/recruitment penalties and drift daily support, making ideology transitions slower than other nations without focus completion.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Borders:** Latvia (south), Soviet Union (east)

**Diplomatic stance:** As non-aligned, Estonia maintains overall positive opinion toward non-aligned nations and slightly negative opinion toward democratic, communist, and fascist nations. No starting alliances or guarantees.

**Strategic vulnerability:** The Soviet Union historically poses the primary existential threat; Estonia cannot defend against Soviet expansion alone and must ally with Axis or Allies to survive. The Ribbentrop-Molotov pact (which historically placed Estonia in the Soviet sphere) is a major game event.

## Starting military (1936)

**Army:** 9 divisions (3 × Infantry, 6 × Cavalry); 24.54K manpower

**Navy:** 5 convoys (0 combat ships in 1936; by 1939 gains 2 × Kalev-class submarines with Torpedo Tubes I and Minelaying Tubes)

**Air Force:** 18 fighters; 360 manpower

## Technology and research

Estonia begins with 3 research slots (2 more unlocked via focus tree). Starting techs include Infantry Equipment I, Support Equipment, Towed Artillery/Anti-Air, Early Destroyers/Submarines, Naval Gunnery, Contact Mines, and basic air/land/naval doctrines (Grand Battleplan, Fleet in Being, Strategic Destruction). By Blood Alone adds Inter-War Fighter to baseline. Estonia has no unique cosmetic equipment names.

## Economy

**Conscription Law:** Limited Conscription (2.5% recruitable population)  
**Trade Law:** Export Focus (10% construction speed, 5% research speed, 10% factory output, 50% resources to market, 20% civilian/10% navy intelligence to others)  
**Economy Law:** Civilian Economy (35% expected consumer goods; 30% factory conversion cost; 40% factory energy reduction)

**Starting factories (1936):** 2 military, 0 naval, 4 civilian (—2 in red for consumer goods), 13 building slots.

## Leaders and personnel

**Government Ministers:** Jüri Uluots (Captain of Industry, +10% civilian/infrastructure/railway/synthetic refinery construction speed) available immediately. Ideology-specific ministers available after focus completion (Johannes Käbin/Communist Revolutionary, Jan Tõnisson/Democratic Reformer, Andres Larka/Fascist Demagogue, all +0.10 ideology support; others: Boris Kumm/Backroom Backstabber with -25% subversive cost; August Rei/Popular Figurehead with +15% stability).

**Military Theorists:** Juhan Tuuling (Land Doctrine Cost -10%), Alex Nirk (Naval Doctrine Cost -10%)

**Army Commanders (General/Field Marshal):** Johan Laidoner (Army Drill Expert, —10% division training time), Alfons Rebane (Army Morale Expert, —6% out-of-supply penalties). Both grant +0.30 daily army experience.

**Naval/Air Commanders:** Officers with various specialties (Oskar Eiriksson/Naval Reformer, Richard Tomberg/All-Weather air, etc.)

**Specialists:** Nine divisional commanders (Andres Larka, Johan Laidoner, August Kork, Jakob Palvadre, Herbert Brede, Otto Heinze) with Old Guard and Communist Sympathizer traits.

**Design companies:** Industrial Company (6 coal, +5% coal efficiency, +15% industrial research), Electronics Company (+15% electronics research, —10% factory energy), plus infantry/armor/air/naval/motorized designers (many gated behind focus completion or requiring British/US peace: RSAF Enfield, Royal Arsenal, Paldiski Port, Ford Motor Company, Crossley Motors, Royal Air Force [medium aircraft], etc.).
