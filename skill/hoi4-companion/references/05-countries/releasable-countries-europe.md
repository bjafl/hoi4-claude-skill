Releasable countries located in Europe outside the Soviet Union. These nations can be released at game start via Custom Game Rules or through focus tree decisions by their puppet master. Most are minor powers that serve as puppet/vassal states or regional rivals.

## Baden-Württemberg

**Tag:** WUR  
**Starting government:** Non-Aligned (elections every 4 years)

Part of the German Reich territory. Cannot be released by Germany. Can form Germany. First election: January 1940.

## Basque Country

**Tag:** NAV  
**Starting government:** Democratic (elections every 4 years)

Cores in France and Spain. Cannot be released by Spain before the Spanish Civil War concludes. Can appear as Kingdom of Navarra under the "11th of November" Custom Game Rule. First election: January 1940.

## Bavaria

**Tag:** BAY  
**Starting government:** Non-Aligned (elections every 4 years)

Part of the German Reich territory. Cannot be released by Germany. Can form Germany. First election: January 1940.

## Bosnia-Herzegovina

**Tag:** BOS  
**Starting government:** Democratic (elections every 4 years)

Cores in Yugoslavia. Requires "Divide Bosnia" or "Protect Bosnia" focus to be released by Yugoslavia. Shares a core with Herzegovina. Can form Austria-Hungary as Austro-Hungarian puppet. First election: January 1940.

## Brittany

**Tag:** BRI  
**Starting government:** Non-Aligned (elections every 4 years)

Cores in France. No special release restrictions. First election: January 1940.

## Catalonia

**Tag:** CAT  
**Starting government:** Democratic (elections every 4 years)

Cores in Spain. Cannot be released by Spain before the Spanish Civil War concludes. Can appear as Crown of Aragon under the "11th of November" Custom Game Rule (non-aligned, with additional cores in Spain, Italy, and Malta; includes navy and air force). First election: January 1940.

## Corsica

**Tag:** COR

## Croatia

**Tag:** CRO  
**Starting government:** Fascist (no elections)

Cores in Italy and Yugoslavia. Requires "A United Autonomous Croatia" or "A Devolved Croatia" focus to be released by Yugoslavia. Shares a core with Slovenia and two cores with Lombardy-Venetia. Can form Austria-Hungary as Austro-Hungarian puppet. Can join Austro-Hungarian joint focus tree branches.

## Cyprus

**Tag:** CYP  
**Starting government:** Democratic (elections every 4 years)

Cores in United Kingdom. Requires "Decolonization" focus to be released by UK. Released as Dominion if UK completes "Self-Government For the Mediterranean". First election: January 1940.

## Serbia

**Starting government:** Non-Aligned (elections every 3 years)

## Slovakia

**Tag:** SLO  
**Starting government:** Fascist (no elections)

Cores in Czechoslovakia. Can only be released manually by Czechoslovakia on 1 January 1936. Can be released through Germany's "Fate of Czechoslovakia" focus as Reichsprotectorate, Hungary's event as puppet, or Czechoslovakia's "Slovakia Autonomy Agreement" or "Czechoslovak Union" focuses. Shares core with Ukraine. Can form Austria-Hungary as Austro-Hungarian puppet. Can join Austro-Hungarian joint focus tree branches.
