Italy is a major Fascist power in Southern Europe that begins the 1936 scenario already at war with Ethiopia. It possesses a modest industrial base compared to other major powers and suffers from critical shortages of oil and rubber, but benefits from decent aluminum and steel reserves. Italy's most formidable asset is its navy, capable of controlling the Mediterranean; its land and air forces are less developed. The country is subject to the Balance of Power mechanic and the unique Missions from the Duce system, which add layers of complexity to its political and military management.

## Government and political situation

Italy is ruled by **Benito Mussolini** of the **Partito Nazionale Fascista** with overwhelming support (73%). Other parties hold minimal support: Partito Popolare Italiano (20% democratic), Monarchist (5%), and Communist (2%). With the *By Blood Alone* DLC, the **Balance of Power mechanic** can trigger a civil war based on political decisions, allowing Italy to shift toward Communist, Democratic, or Monarchist ideologies with different leadership outcomes. Without DLC, Balance of Power is not active.

Possible alternative leaders include Victor Emmanuel III (Monarchist), Ferruccio Parri (Democratic), and Palmiro Togliatti (Communist), each unlocked through specific national focuses or events. Italy can also form a government under the Pope (Papal States) via the monarchist branch.

## Unique mechanics

**Missions from the Duce:**  
With Mussolini in power, Italy periodically receives timed objectives issued by Il Duce. Typical missions include expanding military strength (army, navy, air force), stockpiling fuel, or raising compliance in subject states. Completing a mission grants temporary national spirits (e.g., fuel consumption reductions, supply range bonuses); failure results in penalties (increased fuel consumption, reduced fuel gain). The focus *To Live As A Lion* shortens mission timers but increases rewards for success. This system is unique to Fascist Italy and adds a resource-management layer to gameplay.

**Balance of Power mechanic** (requires *By Blood Alone* DLC):  
Italy's political factions continuously push for influence via the National Balance of Power bar. Decisions and focuses shift this bar toward Mussolini's Fascists, the Monarchist establishment, or opposition forces. If the bar shifts too far away from Fascism, Italy may enter a civil war, allowing the player to choose outcomes (e.g., Monarchist restoration, Communist revolution, or a military junta). This mechanic does not exist in base game.

**Formable nations:**  
Italy can form **Greater Italy** or the **Roman Empire** (mutually exclusive) via Fascist or Monarchist focuses, or the **European Union** (if Democratic). Forming these nations grants extensive cores on Mediterranean and surrounding territories, but each path has different ideological and focus requirements.

**Start of game:**  
Italy begins the scenario **at war with Ethiopia**, a conflict that will dominate early gameplay. Technological and numerical superiority generally ensures Italian victory, but with *By Blood Alone* DLC, Ethiopia can take the *Boarding the Train* focus to form a government-in-exile if the war drags on, prolonging the conflict.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Borders:**  
Italy borders France (northwest), Austria (north), Switzerland (north), and Yugoslavia (east). The Mediterranean Sea and Adriatic Sea dominate its coastline.

**Colonial holdings:**  
Italy possesses territories in Libya, Eritrea, Somalia, the Dodecanese Islands, and the Adriatic enclave of Zara.

**1936 diplomacy:**  
- **At war with:** Ethiopia (mandatory start)
- **Guaranteeing:** Albania
- **No faction membership** at start, but can join the Axis with Germany, form an independent faction with Nationalist Spain, or court France if it becomes Fascist.

**Key diplomatic paths:**  
- Join the **Axis** with Germany (historical path)
- Form a faction with **Nationalist Spain** and potentially Japan
- Ally **France** if the French government turns Fascist (requires France focus or event)
- Demand territory from Yugoslavia, Greece, and others via national focuses

Italy's position exposes it to multiple potential fronts: France and the Balkans to the north, North Africa to the south, and the Mediterranean contested by British naval power. Strategic success depends on naval superiority in the Mediterranean, quick resolution of the Ethiopian campaign, and careful coordination with Axis allies or independent faction partners.
