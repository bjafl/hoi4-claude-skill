## Summary

Australia enters 1936 as a British Dominion with Democratic governance and a core population of 6.76 million. As a subject of the United Kingdom with significant Commonwealth resources and a focus on Pacific defence, Australia has considerable strategic depth but limited autonomy. The country can pursue independence through its national focus tree, offering paths toward continued democracy, fascism, or communism—each unlocking distinct strategic options across the Pacific theatre.

## Government and political situation

Australia begins as a stable democracy under the **United Australia Party (UAP)** with 98% popularity, led by **Joseph Lyons**. Three other political movements exist with minimal support: the Australian Labor Party (0% initially, but accessible), the Communist Party of Australia (1%), and Australia First fascists (1%).

Opposition leaders available for later recruitment:
- **John Curtin** (Labor, socialist): Available without Thunder at Our Gates DLC; enables Labor path
- **Richard Dixon** (Communist, stalinism): Starting Communist leader; enables communist pathway (requires focus completion)
- **Adela Pankhurst Walsh** (Fascist): Starting fascist leader
- **Earle Page** (Non-Aligned): Starting non-aligned leader (non-aligned path available only through advanced focus mechanics)

**Political path:** The focus tree branches at "Never Another Gallipoli"; players can thereafter pursue "Support the Policy of Appeasement" to remain democratic and allied to the UK, or take "Abandon the Westminster System" toward ideology shifts. Independence from the UK is achieved through "Sever Ties with the UK" or equivalent focuses, but the nation begins in full allegiance to Britain.

## Technology and Research

Australia begins with 3 research slots (expandable via focus). Starting techs include:
- **Army:** Infantry Equipment I, Truck, Engineer Company I, Recon Company I, Civilian Train, Towed Artillery, Towed Anti-Air, Inter-War Tank Development
- **Navy:** Early Destroyer, Basic Depth Charges, Basic Cruiser, Basic Cruiser Armor Scheme, Early Submarine, Naval Gunnery, Basic Torpedo, Transport Ship, Contact Mine, Minelaying Submarine
- **Air:** Inter-War Transport Plane, Blenheim IF, Battle, Wellington (without By Blood Alone: Inter-War Fighter, Inter-War Carrier Fighter, CAS I, Naval Bomber I)
- **Industrial:** Fuel Storage, Army/Naval/Air Doctrines (Grand Battleplan, Fleet in Being, Strategic Destruction)

Australia has unique equipment names for various technologies reflecting Commonwealth conventions (e.g., LSAF rifles, Bren carriers, Vickers tanks, Sentinel and Crusader variants).

## Unique mechanics

**Focus tree:** Australia is set to receive its own national focus tree expansion in "Thunder at Our Gates" DLC. The tree comprises 7 major branches:

1. **Additional Militia Training** — Research bonuses for Land Doctrines, Infantry Equipment, Support Companies, Armor
2. **Expand the RAAF** — Air Force improvements: aircraft and air doctrine bonuses
3. **Cockatoo Island Shipyards / Kangaroo Point Dry Dock** — Naval upgrades: ship and naval doctrine bonuses
4. **Standard Gauge Railway / Industries Assistance Corporation** — Infrastructure and industrial capacity; joint tree with economic focus
5. **Establish Advisory War Council** — Domestic politics: recovery from Great Depression, additional research slots
6. **Support the Policy of Appeasement** — Remain within Allies; Commonwealth technology sharing bonuses
7. **Never Another Gallipoli** — Leave Allies; options to remain Democratic or realign with Fascism or Communism

See [06-focus-trees/australia.md](../06-focus-trees/australia.md) for the full focus tree.

**Military Industrial Organization (MIO) Design Companies:** Australia has access to specialized design companies including Cockatoo Docks and Engineering (naval), Evans Deakin & Company (task force shipbuilding), Commonwealth Aircraft Corporation (fighters, CAS, with restrictions if subject of UK), Lithgow Small Arms Factory (infantry equipment), BHP Steel (industrial), and others providing research speed and equipment bonuses.

No other country-specific mechanics (decisions/events beyond normal dominion mechanics) are documented on the wiki.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Borders:** Australia is bordered directly by the Dutch East Indies (an integrated puppet of the Netherlands) in New Guinea (Papua), and is proximate to New Zealand, UK territories (Christmas Island), French territory (New Caledonia), and Portuguese Timor.

**Diplomatic status:** Subject/Dominion of the United Kingdom; not at war in 1936. Australia is part of the Commonwealth and automatically joins the Allies if the UK declares war (unless the spirit *Unratified Westminster Act* is exploited or the player takes the focus to decline the call). With proper focus progression, Australia can break free and form independent factions or remain with the Allies as a free state.

**Starting military strength:** 5 infantry divisions, 2 cavalry divisions; 2 heavy cruisers (Canberra class), 2 light cruisers (Sydney class), 5 destroyers; 100 convoys; air force varies by DLC (24 CAS or 24 fighters in 1936, expanding to 152 planes by 1939).
