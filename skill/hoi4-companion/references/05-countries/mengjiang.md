## Summary

Mengjiang (also known as Mengkukuo) is a puppet state in Inner Mongolia, initially independent in 1936 but becoming a Japanese Imperial Protectorate by 1939. With minimal industry and only 2.04M population across 3 states, Mengjiang is one of the weakest nations in the game, relying entirely on external support or leveraging its generic focus tree for development.

## Government and political situation

Mengjiang is ruled by **Prince Yondonwangchug** as part of the Mongol Local Autonomy Political Affairs Committee. The government is despotic non-aligned, though the political parties reflect a diversity of ideologies: the committee holds 49% support, House Chinggisid holds 49%, and smaller communist and ultranationalist factions hold 1% each.

Available leaders include Prince Demchugdongrub (Cavalry Leader), Chin Yongchang (Nationalist), and Serengdongrub (Communist). Through the National Effort branch of the generic focus tree, Mengjiang can shift ideologically toward Fascism, Communism, or Democracy.

In 1939, Mengjiang automatically becomes a Japanese subject via the event "The Japanese Empire Demands our Submission" unless prevented by player actions.

## Unique mechanics

Mengjiang uses the **generic national focus tree** (no unique tree), with 5 main branches: Army Effort, Aviation Effort, Naval Effort, Industrial Effort, and Political Effort. The country can lever the Political Effort branch to pursue ideological shifts or the Industrial Effort branch to unlock additional research slots (up to 5 total).

**Civil War Path:** Players can trigger a civil war by shifting ideology and using the Anti-Communist Raids / Ignite Civil War decisions, allowing the communist or nationalist faction to contest Mengjiang's government.

**Formation:** Mengjiang can form Greater Mongolia by conquering the Comintern (Soviet Union, Mongolia, Tannu Tuva) and Sinkiang if it has communist support.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

Mengjiang borders Manchukuo (east), Hebei-Chahar (south), Shanxi (southwest), and Mongolia (north).

**1936:** Independent nation.

**1939:** Becomes a subject of Japan and enters the Second Sino-Japanese War, fighting China and Chinese warlords. The wiki describes Mengjiang as having "the lowest starting industry out of any nation in the game," making it heavily dependent on foreign aid, focus-tree industry bonuses, or ideological shifts to gain military or economic footing.
