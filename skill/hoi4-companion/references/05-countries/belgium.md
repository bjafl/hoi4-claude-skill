Belgium is a minor democratic nation in Western Europe with a troubled post-WWI economy, internal linguistic divisions, and the strategic challenge of maintaining neutrality between German and French power. It controls the Belgian Congo in Central Africa (a joint puppet state with Götterdämmerung enabled). Starting with severe economic and stability penalties, Belgium must choose between reinforcing its historical neutrality or joining the democratic faction to counter rising fascism.

## Government and political situation

Belgium is a **democratic nation with elections every 4 years**, next scheduled January 1940. The **Catholic Block** party dominates with 83–87% popularity, led by either **Paul Von Zeeland** (starting 1936, "Corrupt Old Guard" trait) or **Hubert Pierlot** (starting 1936/1939, "Determined Prime Minister" trait from 1939 onward). Both leaders provide stability bonuses.

**Other starting parties:**
- **Kommunistische Partij van België (KPB)**: 5–6% (leader Émile Vandervelde)
- **Parti Rexiste (Rex)**: 8–11% fascist party (leader Leon Degrelle)
- **House of Saxe-Coburg Gotha (Royalists)**: ~0% non-aligned

**Paths to other ideologies** are available via the focus tree:
- **Democratic**: Constitutional Crisis branch leads to snap election; can join Allies or stay socialist
- **Communist**: Raise the Red Flag path via Vandervelde; can join Comintern or form European Federation
- **Fascist**: Degrelle branch offers Rexist or Vlaamsch Nationaal Verbond paths; VNV can form Burgundy or Belgica and expand into Low Countries

## National spirits

Belgium starts with five debilitating national spirits (Götterdämmerung):

| Spirit | Effects |
|--------|---------|
| **Language Barriers** | −0.10 daily political power; −10% stability; −10% war support |
| **Scars of the Great War** | −15% division organization; −15% recruitable population; −30% war support; −20% military factory construction speed |
| **Corruption** | −0.25 daily political power; −20% stability; −20% construction speed; +0.10 daily fascism support |
| **Economic Downturn** | 25% consumer goods factories required; −20% factory output |
| **Chasseurs Ardennais** | Mountaineers unit type: +10% movement, +20% attack, +20% defense in forests; −10% movement, −20% attack in mountains |

These spirits can be progressively removed via the focus tree, particularly Corruption (Monetary Reconstruction branch) and Scars of the Great War (Defense Bill branch).

## Unique mechanics (requires Götterdämmerung)

**Focus tree:** Belgium's unique 6-branch focus tree replaces the generic tree. Key branches:
- **Perpetual Neutrality/Government Resigns**: Initial choice declaring neutrality and calling snap election
- **Defensive Neutrality**: Builds land forts and defensive modifiers on borders
- **Government of National Unity**: King chooses leadership during government stalemate
- **Paul-Émile Janson (Democratic path)**: Removes economic penalties; unlocks Benelux union → European Union → European Federation formation
- **Degrelle (Fascist)**: Rexist path (industrial bonuses) or Vlaamsch Nationaal Verbond path (cosmetic nation changes: Vlaanderen → Dietsland → Burgundy → Belgica; can annex Low Countries and Dutch colonies)
- **Constitutional Crisis (Democratic/Communist bifurcation)**: Snap election to choose Allies or Communist path
- **Vandervelde (Communist)**: Builds industry, spreads communism into Congo, can join Comintern
- **Belgian Air Force & Naval**: Dedicated air and naval development branches
- **The King Surrenders**: Government-in-exile mechanics when Belgium falls

**Congo joint branch** (shared with Belgian Congo): Allows industry/military buildup in Congo, modifies Force Publique national spirit, manages Congo autonomy and future independence/annexation.

**Snap election mechanics**: Several focuses trigger elections where Belgium's ideology shifts based on player choice (Democratic or Communist).

**Benelux/European Union**: Democratic path can form Benelux union with Netherlands/Luxembourg, then European Union, then European Federation (requires all three Benelux members).

See [06-focus-trees/belgium.md](../06-focus-trees/belgium.md) for the full focus tree.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Land borders:** Netherlands (north), Germany (east), Luxembourg (southeast), France (southwest).
**Colonial borders:** UK and Portugal via Belgian Congo (Central Africa).

**Diplomacy (Götterdämmerung):**
- Belgium **guarantees France and UK**, and is **guaranteed by both**.
- Can form **European Union** if democratic (requires Benelux members).

**Strategic position:** Belgium's neutrality is historically fragile. Germany views it as an invasion corridor to France; France views it as a vital staging ground against German aggression. The focus tree presents a fundamental choice: reinforce neutrality (Perpetual Neutrality branch), join the democratic Allies (Constitutional Crisis → Democratic path), or pivot toward fascism (Degrelle) or communism (Vandervelde).
