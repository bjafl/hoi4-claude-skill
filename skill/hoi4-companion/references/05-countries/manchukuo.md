Manchukuo is a fascist puppet state of Japan in northern Asia. Led by the former Qing emperor Puyi (reigning as the Kangde Emperor), it starts with negligible industry and limited resources but considerable population and expansion potential. Its focus tree centers on Puyi's ambition to restore the Qing Dynasty by conquering China, with paths ranging from strict Japanese loyalty to armed independence and ideological realignment.

## Government and political situation

Manchukuo starts as a fascist state under the Emperor-Fascism variant ideology. In 1936, the country is led by Prime Minister Zhang Jinghui; in 1939, leadership transitions to Aisin Gioro Puyi (Xuantong Emperor). The ruling political party is the Manzhōuguó Xiéhehuì (Concordia Association).

Four ideological paths are available through the national focus tree:

1. **Fascist (Emperor-Fascism):** Starting ideology. Two sub-branches determine trajectory:
   - Strengthen the Concordia Association: Remain a loyal Japanese puppet with increasing industrial investment from Japan; rejects independence.
   - Assertiveness: Gradually gain autonomy and declare war against Japan; unlock Qing restoration path.

2. **Non-Aligned (Despotism):** Achievable through the "Establish a New Constitution" branch after restoring imperial authority. Puyi becomes figurehead emperor with enhanced political power and stability.

3. **Democratic:** Available via the "Question the Emperor's Authority" branch. Puyi is deposed; the nation becomes the Manchu Republic under Ma Zhanshan, with improved garrison defense and recruitable population.

4. **Communist:** Available via the "Question the Emperor's Authority" branch. Puyi is deposed; the nation becomes the Free Democratic People's Republic of Manchukuo under Zhao Shangzhi, allied with Soviet Union and other communist powers, with factory output bonuses and ideology drift defense.

Notable leaders and their traits:
- **Zhang Jinghui** (starting 1936): Japanese Puppet alignment; -5% stability but +0.02 daily fascism support.
- **Aisin Gioro Puyi** (Kangde Emperor): +5% political power gain; "Warrior Code" trait; reduced AI desire to call to arms or join allies.
- **Pujie:** Militarist; -10% land doctrine cost, +0.04 daily army experience; available via "The Question of Leadership" focus.

## National spirits

Manchukuo begins with four national spirits reflecting its Japanese puppet status and low internal legitimacy:

**Banditry**
- Stability: -10%
- War Support: -10%
- *Context:* Reflects bandit raids against factories and infrastructure; can be resolved by completing the "Pacify the Countryside" focus.

**Kwantung Veto**
- Conscription Law change cost: +25%
- Trade Law change cost: +25%
- Economy Law change cost: +25%
- Political Advisor cost: +25%
- *Context:* Japanese military (Kwantung Army) restricts Manchukuo's policy autonomy. Resolved by gaining Japanese recognition as equals or by overthrowing Japanese control.

**Low Legitimacy**
- Stability: -50%
- War Support: -50%
- *Context:* Puyi is a foreign ruler (restored emperor) with limited popular support in a newly constructed state. Resolved by establishing Emperor's independence from Japan.

**The Kangde Emperor**
- Political Power Gain: +5%
- *Context:* Permanent bonus from Puyi's presence.

## Unique mechanics

Manchukuo has an extensive national focus tree with four major branches:

**Pacify the Countryside:** Initial stabilization branch. Unlocks repeatable decisions to either hunt down or pacify bandits, gradually removing the Banditry spirit. Completing pacification branches opens access to Japanese settlers and collective farm focuses for population and building slot gains.

**Invite Foreign Investors:** Shared with other Chinese nations. Pursues industrial partnerships with Germany, Italy, and other nations to build civilian and military capacity. Unlocks diplomatic options and access to foreign military advisors and design companies.

**The Question of Leadership:** Determines long-term government and ideological direction. Two sub-branches:
- Strengthen imperial authority: Keep Puyi in charge; pursue Chinese Empire puppet status or full Qing restoration.
- Depose Puyi: Transition to democratic (Ma Zhanshan) or communist (Zhao Shangzhi) government.

**Assertiveness:** The independence path. Gradually unlocks autonomy focuses, culminating in a decision to declare war on Japan. Once independent, unlocks additional focuses to annex Mengjiang, claim the Mandate of Heaven, and justify wars against all Chinese factions (Nationalist, warlords, communists). Completing "Claim the Mandate of Heaven" raises Puyi to the status of Xuantong (Rexism ideology, +5% political power, persistent Warrior Code bonuses).

Key repeatable decisions:
- **Pacify Bandits:** Station enough units in a state and hold for 180 days to remove Banditry spirit (slower but allows recruiting bandits afterward).
- **Hunt Down Bandits:** Instant removal of Banditry by spending 500 infantry equipment and 1000 manpower.
- **Seize Equipment from Japan:** Unlocked via "Purge the General Affairs Council" focus. Repeatable decision transferring equipment from Japan; each use increases amount and applies temporary penalty to Japanese divisions in Manchuria.
- **Create Underground Workshops:** Unlocked via "Local Arms Procurement" focus when at war with Japan. Grants 3 additional military factories but imposes -40% reliability penalty on infantry equipment (mitigatable with maintenance companies).

Access to foreign military advisors and design companies (German, Soviet, Chinese) becomes available through specific focuses, provided prerequisite conditions are met (peace with relevant nation, focus completion).

See [06-focus-trees/manchukuo.md](../06-focus-trees/manchukuo.md) for the complete focus tree structure.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Borders:**
- North and east: Soviet Union
- West: Mongolia and Mengjiang
- Southwest: Hebei-Chahar
- South and southeast: Japan (by Dalian territory and Korean territory)

**Diplomatic Status:**
Manchukuo is an Imperial Protectorate of Japan at game start. It has a core on Dairen (owned by Japan). If Japan experiences a civil war (via democratic or communist branches of Japan's focus tree), Manchukuo automatically annexes Japanese territories in the region (Kwantung Peninsula, East Hebei) and puppets Mengjiang.

**Faction Alignment:**
Initially aligned with Japan and the Axis. Remaining loyal through "Strengthen the Concordia Association" reinforces this alignment. The Assertiveness branch leads to independence and breaking from the Axis; depending on which ideological path is chosen afterward (or if remaining fascist), Manchukuo may form a new faction or join others.

## Military

Manchukuo starts with a very small army: 11 divisions (7 infantry, 4 cavalry) in 1936, reduced to 9 divisions (5 infantry, 4 cavalry) by 1939. Total starting manpower: 58.6K (1936), 42K (1939). No navy or air force at game start.

**Naval resources:** 5 convoy ships available in both start dates.

Manchukuo possesses no air force at start and must build one from scratch if desired. It can recruit advisors for army, navy, and air doctrine (land doctrine -10%, naval doctrine -10%, air doctrine -10% costs). Foreign military advisors (Alexander von Falkenhausen, Claire Lee Chennault, Aleksandr Cherepanov) can be hired with appropriate focus completion and peace conditions.
