A Chinese warlord state controlling six provinces in central China. Sichuan Clique begins with a decent industrial base (5 factories) and 43.6M population, but labors under five negative national spirits representing internal political fragmentation. Independent until 1939, when it is removed from the game.

## Government and political situation

**Ruling party:** Kuomintang (Liu Xiang, Reckless Commander) with 100% popularity.

**Available leaders:**
- Liu Xiang (Kuomintang, Democratic/Despotism): starting leader
- Yang Sen (China Democratic League/Communist/Fascist/Kuomintang, Taoist Devotee): becomes available after completing "Secure Internal Politics" focus and the "Death of Liu Xiang" event
- Mao Sun (Democratic, Ideological Crusader): Democratic path leader
- Hsia Sun (Communist, Smooth-Talking Charmer / Armaments Organizer): Communist path leader
- Hsia Jin (Fascist, Economist / Silent Workhorse): Fascist path leader

**Paths to other ideologies:** Sichuan Clique can align with Nationalist China or Communist China via the shared Warlords focus tree, or establish a rival government to pursue independent ideology. Ideological conversion happens through completing respective focuses and events.

## Unique mechanics

**Warlords focus tree:** Sichuan Clique uses the shared Warlords national focus tree with an exclusive Sichuan Clique branch providing industrial/military bonuses and bonuses against Tibet, Chinese Soviet Republic, and Japan. The tree's three main paths are:
- Secure Internal Politics (political unification)
- Develop the Capital State (industrial development)
- Strengthen Warlord Authority (army/cavalry/mountaineer bonuses)

At the end of the tree, players can pivot to either Nationalist Chinese Warlord or Communist Chinese Warlord focus trees.

**Border Clashes:** Available as a means to expand against neighboring warlords and weak states (Yunnan, Nationalist provinces, Tibet, Xibei San Ma, Communist China, Sinkiang).

**Ideological alignment:** Sichuan Clique can pursue Democratic, Communist, Fascist, or remain Non-Aligned (Despotism). Each path unlocks different leaders and focus branches.

**Unique designers and industrial companies** (with No Compromise, No Surrender DLC): Sino-German Technical Mission, Morozov Design Bureau, Jiangnan Shipyard Group, Dagu Shipyard, CAMCO, Taiyuan Arsenal, Liaoning Arsenal, Hanyan Arsenal, Mukden Arsenal.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Direct borders:** China (southeast), Yunnan (south), Tibet (west), Qinghai Ma (north), Northeastern Army (north).

**Diplomatic alignment:** Non-aligned; no starting alliances or guarantees. Can be vassalized or absorbed by Nationalist China, Communist China, or form a rival Chinese government.

**Game availability:** Sichuan Clique does not exist in the 1939 start date and cannot be released mid-game.

## Military forces (1936 start)

**Army:** 35 infantry divisions (7 Juntuán with 4×Infantry each, 28 Sanjiao Jun with 6×Infantry each). 196K manpower deployed.

**Navy:** None.

**Air force:** None.

**Terrain advantage:** 6 controlled states contain significant mountainous terrain; starting with some mountainous forts along northern borders.
