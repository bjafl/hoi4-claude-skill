## Summary

The Kingdom of Greece is a minor power in the Balkans with limited industrial capacity and a faction-divided political landscape. Positioned at a strategic crossroads between Europe and the Middle East, it faces territorial pressure from both Italy and Germany in historical scenario. Starting non-aligned, Greece has multiple paths through its unique focus tree, including potential transformation into a democracy, communist state, or rival empire.

## Government and political situation

Greece begins 1936 as a non-aligned monarchy under the authoritarian Laïkòn Kómma Party, with Konstantinos Demertzis as leader. Elections are held annually; the first election scheduled for January 1936. The political landscape is highly fragmented:

- **Ruling party (Non-Aligned):** Laïkòn Kómma, 38% popularity
- **Democratic opposition:** Fileleftheron (Venizelist), 43% popularity, led by Eleftherios Venizelos
- **Communist challenge:** Kommounistikó Kómma Elládas, 17% popularity, led by Nikos Zachariadis
- **Fascist fringe:** Elliniko Ethniko Sosialistiko Komma, 2% popularity, led by George Mercoulis

After Metaxas takes power (via focus tree), political alignments shift significantly: the Laïkòn Kómma rebands as "Kathestós Metaxá" (Metaxism), rising to 42% support, while Democratic support drops to 10%, Communist support rises to 26%, and Fascist support increases to 22%.

## Unique mechanics

Greece possesses a unique national focus tree (requires Battle for the Bosporus DLC; without it, uses the generic focus tree). The tree is structured around four major branches:

**Bring Home the Exiled Republicans** — Allows Greece to pursue Democratic, Byzantine, or Communist ideologies. Includes sub-branches for compromising with Monarchists (forming Democratic Hellas or Byzantine Empire and pivoting Fascist) or requesting Communist support (allying with the Soviet Union, triggering border conflicts with Yugoslavia, or maintaining independence).

**The King's Government** — Centers on establishing George II as an absolute Monarch with risk of civil war, or enabling Metaxas to take control. Includes sub-branches for Non-Aligned military alliances (potentially with Axis or Allies), achieving Enosis with Cyprus, forming the Macedonian Empire, or upgrading Metaxism.

**Devaluating the Drachma** — Focuses on economic stabilization: paying foreign debts, increasing industry, managing foreign monopolies, and choosing between industrialization or maintaining agrarian society.

**Hellenic Armed Forces** — Expands military technology and provides mountain training bonuses to infantry units.

Additional mechanics include the Schachtplan (German economic plan) upgrades and negotiations with foreign majors. See [06-focus-trees/greece.md](../06-focus-trees/greece.md) for the full focus tree.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

Greece is bordered by:
- **North:** Albania (Italian puppet from 1939), Yugoslavia, Bulgaria
- **East:** Turkey

Starting faction: None (non-aligned).

Greece historically faced invasion from Italy (October 1940) after Mussolini's ultimatum to Metaxas, and was later conquered by Germany in April 1941 following German invasion from Bulgaria. The focus tree allows for various diplomatic paths: alliance with the Allies (historically what occurred), cooperation with the Axis, or independent pursuit of regional hegemony via the Macedonian Empire or Hellas.
