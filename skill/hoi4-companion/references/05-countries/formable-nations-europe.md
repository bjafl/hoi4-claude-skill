Formable nations of Europe are countries that can be created during a game via decisions, rather than played from 1936 start. They range from expansionist regional unions (Scandinavia, Greater Italy) to ideologically-variant empires (Alpine Confederation with four forms: Democratic, Communist, Fascist, Non-Aligned) to historically-themed recreations (Austria-Hungary, Roman Empire, Byzantine Empire). Formation typically requires territorial control and specific governmental conditions.

## Overview of formation categories

Formable European nations fall into three categories:

1. **By formable_nation_decisions.txt** (25 nations) — Primary method, triggered by generic "Formable Nation" decisions. Includes Austria-Hungary, Germany (reunified), Greater Germanic Reich, Holy Roman Empire, Roman Empire, and others.

2. **By other decision files** (14 nations) — Formed via decisions in other decision files (Alpine Confederation, Benelux Federation, Pan-Slavic Union, etc.), often tied to specific country focus trees or events.

3. **By other means** (45+ states/entities) — Created via puppet/annexation triggers, autonomous state decisions, or other game mechanics (Alpine Protectorate, British Alpine Territories, various Soviet republics, Papal State, etc.).

Most formable nations require specific DLC: By Blood Alone (Switzerland, Alps, Italy expansions), Arms Against Tyranny (Nordic expansions), No Step Back (Soviet-related), Götterdämmerung (German/Central European), Battle for the Bosporus (Byzantine/Balkans), La Résistance (Iberia, Franco-British).

## Alpine Confederation (and variants)

**Formation:** Primarily via Switzerland; requires By Blood Alone DLC and completion of focus President For Life.

**Ideology variants:**
- Democratic: Alpine Confederation
- Communist: Alpine Union
- Fascist: Alpine Supremacy
- Non-Aligned: Alpine Protectorate

**Requirements:** Switzerland must control at least 7 of: Bouches-du-Rhône (21), Var (851), Savoy (735), Alpes (32), Piemonte (158), Lombardia (159), Trentino (850), Alto Adige (39), Western Swiss Alps (847), Eastern Swiss Alps (151), Ticino (846), Vorarlberg (848), Tirol (153), Oberösterreich (152), Niederösterreich (4). Current ruling party must match ideology; more than 30–50% support for corresponding party.

**Cost:** 100 political power.

**Effects:** Gains cores on approximately 13 states including Alpine and sub-Alpine territories. Switzerland is reformed as Alpine Confederation (or variant).

## Austria-Hungary

**Formation:** Multiple methods depending on source nation (Austria, Czechoslovakia, Croatia, Slovenia, Slovakia, etc.); no specific DLC required for base formable.

**Ideology variants:**
- Democratic: Danubian Confederation
- Communist: Association of Autonomous States
- Fascist: Austro-Hungarian Empire
- Non-Aligned: Austria-Hungary

**Alternate (Czechoslovakia):** Great Moravia variants (Federation of Moravian States, Empire of Great Moravia, etc.).

**Requirements:** Must be fully independent and control entire Austro-Hungarian territory: all 6 Austrian states, all 6 Czechoslovak states, all 5 Hungarian states, plus Slovenia, Dalmatia, Bosnia, Herzegovina, Croatia, Backa, Crisana, North/South Transylvania, West/East Banat, plus Italian Alpine/Adriatic territories (Alto Adige, Trentino, Istria, Zara, Litorale).

**Effects:** Reformation as Austria-Hungary (or Great Moravia if Czechoslovakia) with cores on 30+ states across Central Europe, Balkans, and Northern Italy.

## Baltic Federation

**Formation:** Baltic nations (Lithuania, Latvia, Estonia) or Poland can form via decision; no specific DLC required for base variant.

**Requirements:** Must control all Baltic states — Lithuania, Latvia, Estonia — and be fully independent. Exact prerequisites vary by source nation.

**Effects:** Reformed as Baltic Federation with cores on three Baltic states.

## Belarus-related unions

Several variants exist depending on source and ideology:
- **Baltic-Belarus:** Three closely related formations by Baltic states incorporating Belarus.
- **Estonia-Belarus SSR / Latvia-Belarus SSR / Lithuania-Belarus SSR:** Soviet-themed formations; typically require No Step Back DLC.
- **Pan-Slavic Union:** Broader Eastern European/Soviet union; requires No Step Back.

## Byzantine Empire

**Formation:** Requires Battle for the Bosporus DLC. Can be formed by Greece or Ottoman Empire through religious/historical decision path.

**Requirements:** Must control Constantinople (Istanbul) and Ankara region; exact state list is extensive (Thrace, Bithynia, Cappadocia, Syria, Mesopotamia, etc.).

**Variants by ideology:** Name may vary (Hellenic Empire, Ottoman Sultanate, etc. pending DLC).

**Notes:** One of the most territory-intensive formables; requires control of large swaths of Middle East and North Africa.

## European Federation

**Formation:** Multiple paths — Czechoslovakia can form via focus "Forming a Federation"; democratic nations can pursue via international conferences (La Résistance). No single DLC required for base variant.

**Ideology variants:** Name and exact requirements vary; approximately 14 different decision paths documented.

**Requirements:** Typically requires Czechoslovakia to be independent, control all Czech lands, and puppet or control parts of Austria and Hungary; OR requires a democratic nation to complete specific diplomatic focuses.

**Effects:** Reformed as European Federation with claims on Central European territories.

## Franco-British Union

**Formation:** Can be formed by France or United Kingdom through specific focus decisions or surrender events; no specific DLC required for base variant.

**Requirements:** Requires France and United Kingdom to reach a diplomatic compromise or for one to be occupied/annexed by the other.

**Notes:** Often emerges as a late-game defensive alliance against Axis powers; mechanics vary by DLC (La Résistance affects Spanish/Portuguese pathways).

## Germany (reunified)

**Formation:** Germany can reunify if it has been split (via alternate history paths); no specific DLC required for base variant.

**Requirements:** East and West Germany (or other German splinters) must be reunited under single rule.

**Related:** Greater Germanic Reich (requires expansion into adjacent territories with German cores), Greater German Proletarian State (communist variant; Götterdämmerung).

## Greater Germanic Reich

**Formation:** Nazi Germany path via specific focus tree; no special DLC required for base variant.

**Requirements:** Germany must control extensive Central/Eastern European and Scandinavian territories, typically via the "Lebensraum" focus path.

**Related formables:** Greater German Proletarian State (communist Germany; Götterdämmerung).

## Greater Italy

**Formation:** Requires By Blood Alone DLC. Italy can form via focus tree pursuing Mussolinian expansionism.

**Requirements:** Italy must control Mediterranean territories including parts of North Africa, Balkans, and potentially Greece.

**Variants:** Alpine-related forms (Alpine Supremacy, Alpine Union) can interact with Italian expansion.

## Greater Hungary

**Formation:** Hungary can expand via focus decisions without specific DLC requirement.

**Requirements:** Hungary must control Hungarian-speaking territories, including parts of Romania (Transylvania), Slovakia, and Yugoslavia (Backa, Banat).

## Holy Roman Empire

**Formation:** Can be formed by German states (Germany, Austria, or German splitters) via decision.

**Requirements:** Must control core German territories and often significant portions of Central Europe; decision emphasizes historical "Holy Roman Empire" borders.

**Notes:** Primarily cosmetic/historical formable; no gameplay bonuses beyond territorial cores.

## Iberia (and regional federations)

**Formation:** Requires La Résistance DLC. Spain or Portugal can form via specific focus decisions.

**Ideology variants:**
- **Iberia:** Democratic/non-aligned unification of Spain and Portugal.
- **Kingdom of Franco-Spain:** Spanish Fascist leadership.
- **Regional Defense Council of Iberia:** Communist/Republican form.

**Requirements:** Depends on ideology; typically requires one Iberian nation to control or puppet the other.

## Nordic League (and variants)

**Formation:** Multiple Nordic-themed formables available; most require Arms Against Tyranny DLC.

**Variants:**
- **Nordic League:** Democratic Scandinavian union.
- **Scandinavia:** Unified Nordic states (democratic/non-aligned).
- **Eternal Nordic Empire:** Non-aligned, militarist Nordic expansion.
- **Det Stornordiska Riket:** Swedish Fascist variant (Götterdämmerung).
- **Kingdom of Finland / Red Finland:** Finnish-specific variants (Arms Against Tyranny).
- **Greater Denmark:** Danish expansion (Arms Against Tyranny).
- **Norske Sovjetrepublikken:** Norwegian communist form (Arms Against Tyranny).
- **Reichsprotektorate Sweden / Nordland:** German puppet variants (Arms Against Tyranny).

**Requirements:** Varies by variant; typically Scandinavia (Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland) must be controlled/puppeted by initiating nation. Some require specific ideologies; others require German control/protection.

## Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth

**Formation:** Can be formed by Poland or Lithuania via decision; no specific DLC required for base variant.

**Alternate:** Bemontian/Congressional Commonwealth (No Step Back variant).

**Requirements:** Poland and Lithuania must be under single control (either unified or one puppets the other).

**Effects:** Reformed with cores on Polish-Lithuanian historical borders.

## Roman Empire

**Formation:** Can be formed by Italy, Germany, or Greece via decision; no specific DLC required for base variant.

**Requirements:** Requires control of extensive Mediterranean and European territories: Rome and Italian peninsula, North Africa (Egypt, Maghreb), Balkans, parts of Middle East.

**Notes:** One of the most demanding formables; requires enormous territorial conquest.

## Swiss Protectorates

Multiple Swiss-related formables via By Blood Alone:
- **Helvetic Republic:** Democratic Switzerland path.
- **République Helvétique:** French-aligned democratic form.
- **Schweizerische Reichsprotektorat:** German puppet variant.
- **Nation Hélvetique:** Alternative democratic form.
- **Protettorato Svizzero:** Italian puppet variant (different from "Protectorate").
- **Schweizerische Eidgenossenschaft:** Alternative unified form.
- **Swiss Union:** Non-aligned variant.
- **Repubblica Svizzera:** Italian-aligned form.

**Formation path:** Switzerland follows focus tree in By Blood Alone; specific form depends on which major power (Germany, Italy, France, or UK) Switzerland aligns with or is conquered by.

## Transcaucasia

**Formation:** Requires No Step Back DLC. Can be formed by Soviet Union or regional states.

**Alternate variants:**
- **Confederation of Finno-Russian Republics:** Finnish-Soviet union (Arms Against Tyranny).
- **Estonia-Finland League / Estonia-Belarus SSR / etc.:** Various Soviet autonomous forms (No Step Back).
- **Mountainous Republic of the Northern Caucasus:** Caucasus-focused (No Step Back).
- **Idel-Ural:** Volga-Ural region (No Step Back).
- **Ostland:** German occupation zone for Eastern territories (No Step Back).
- **Empire of Finno-Ugria:** Finnish-Ugric unification (No Step Back).

**Requirements:** Depends on variant; typically Soviet control of regional territories with appropriate autonomous state triggers.

## Third Rome

**Formation:** Requires No Step Back DLC. Soviet Union can form via decision emphasizing Moscow as successor to Byzantine/Russian empire.

**Requirements:** _Not covered on the wiki_ — likely requires Soviet control of Constantinople region and Russian heartland.
