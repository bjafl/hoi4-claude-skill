Latvia is a minor Baltic state in Northern Europe with 1.96 million population and limited industry. It starts non-aligned under oligarchic rule, threatened by both the Soviet Union to the east and the pressures of 1930s fascism and communism. The No Step Back DLC provides Latvia with a unique focus tree offering paths to military dictatorship, alignment with a major power, or communism.

## Government and political situation

Latvia starts under **Kārlis Ulmanis** of the **Tautas Padome (Non-Aligned)** party with 40% popularity. The ruling party grants intelligence bonuses to other nations and increased tension limits but prevents forcing ideology on others.

### Political Landscape

| Ideology | Party | Leader | Popularity | Status |
|----------|-------|--------|-----------|--------|

### Alternative Leaders

With the No Step Back DLC and via focus tree paths, Latvia can switch to:

- **Jānis Balodis** (Non-Aligned, Military Dictatorship path): Veteran with +10% army experience, +10% command power gain
- **Fricis Menders** (Democratic, via Renew the Constitution focus)
- **Augusts Kirhenšteins** (Communist/Stalinist, via Overthrow Tautas Padome)
- **Vilis Lācis** (Communist/Marxist, if Enter Opposition focus taken first)
- **Gustavs Celmiņš** (Fascist, via Seek Alignment with Germany or The Thunder Roars Alone)

## Unique mechanics

Latvia (with No Step Back) has a shared Baltic focus tree with three main branches:

### Rejoin the Railways Branch
Upgrades industry and military. The left side requires support from a Fascist or Democratic major power.

### Restore the Worker's Republic Branch
Allows conversion to Communist, either as Soviet puppet or independent, and expansion toward other Baltic states and Scandinavia.

### Suspend the Constitution of Latvia Branch
Allows transition to Democracy or Non-Aligned/Militarist through restructuring the Saeima (parliament). Includes the **Latvian Military Directorate** path (military dictatorship under Jānis Balodis).

The tree also offers:
- **Formalize the Baltic Entente** (shared sub-branch): Alliance with Estonia and Lithuania; manpower/industry bonuses; option to unite the Baltic States into the Baltic Union; democratic faction alignment
- **Empower the Paramilitarists** (sub-branch): Further Democratic support or Fascist paths (with or against Germany); Dievturība as state religion; creation of Ostland or Baltic Federation
- **The Threat on Our Border** (sub-branch): Select a threat nation, build up defenses after it capitulates or becomes enemy subject

See [06-focus-trees/latvia.md](../06-focus-trees/latvia.md) for the complete focus tree.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering States:** Estonia (north), Lithuania and Poland (south), Soviet Union (east)

Latvia shares good relations with other Baltic states (Estonia, Lithuania) and Finland but is otherwise isolated. All starting diplomatic relations revolve around ideology alignment (Same Ideology, Different Ideology, Same Ruling Party modifiers).

**Starting Diplomacy:** No alliances. Latvia may join the Międzymorze if Poland creates it or can unite the Baltic States via national focus.

**Strategic Position:** Surrounded by larger powers and vulnerable to Soviet expansion under the Molotov-Ribbentrop Pact's secret protocols. Germany or Soviet Union occupation is probable without strong diplomatic or military action early.
