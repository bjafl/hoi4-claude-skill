## Summary

Netherlands is a minor democratic country in Western Europe with a colonial empire centered on the Dutch East Indies as an integrated puppet. Historically neutral but economically fragile, the Netherlands begins 1936 with weak industrial capacity, limited manpower, and a decent navy. It borders Germany and Belgium directly and controls important colonial holdings in Southeast Asia, the Caribbean, and South America.

## Government and political situation

The Netherlands begins 1936 as a democratic country with elections every four years (next scheduled April 1937). The Anti-Revolutionaire Partij (Democratic) holds 93% popularity under Hendrikus Colijn, whose "Strict Austerity Adherent" trait imposes severe maluses: -15% stability, +50% economy law conversion cost, -30% consumer goods factories factor.

Other democratic factions include the Christelijk-Historische Unie (Christian Historical Union) accessible via the "Form New Government" focus, with leader Dirk Jan de Geer ("Defeatist" trait: -10% war support, -25% surrender limit) or Pieter Sjoerds Gerbrandy ("Little Churchill": +5% war support, +15% resistance growth speed in occupied territories).

The Netherlands has no claims or cores on foreign soil. Diplomatic relations are +20 with conservative democracies (Belgium, South Africa), +10 with all other democracies, and -10 with non-democracies.

## Unique mechanics

The Netherlands has a unique national focus tree with 6 main branches and 5 sub-branches, covering colonial development, military reform, economic recovery, ideology shifts, and overseas dominion management.

**Key branches:**
- **Reform the KNIL** and **Dutch East Indies** branches: Develop colonial military and naval infrastructure.
- **Obtain Foreign Colonial Investments** (Base Game or Thunder at Our Gates variant): Expand colonial industry and optionally relocate government to the Dutch East Indies upon European capitulation.
- **Abandon the Gold Standard** and **Overturn Military Budget Cuts** sub-branches: Remove De Crisisjaren and Shell-shocked Spectator spirits, increase military technology research.
- **Form New Government** branch: Choose German, British, or neutral influence paths.
  - **Volk en Vaderland** (Fascist): Ally Germany, pacify Japan, conquer Benelux.
  - **Legacy of the "De Zeven Provinciën" Mutiny** (Communist): Release colonies, join Soviet/French faction, gain war goals on Western democracies.
  - **Oranje Boven!** (Monarchist/Non-Aligned): Make Wilhelmina head of government, gain US guarantees, extract German manpower.
  - **"Unity Through Democracy"** (Democratic): Form Benelux faction, invite democratic nations, create European Union.
- **Reassert Control over the Indies** and **Establish the ABDACOM**: Manage colonial and allied Pacific warfare.

_See [06-focus-trees/netherlands.md](../06-focus-trees/netherlands.md) for the full focus tree._

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Direct neighbours:** Germany (east), Belgium (south/southeast), France (overseas). UK also overseas.

**Colonial neighbours:** Dutch East Indies borders British Malaya, French Indochina, Thailand, and Japanese-controlled territories.

**Faction and guarantees:** Historically neutral (Aloof Neutrality spirit). Can guarantee or join factions via focus tree. Rules the Dutch East Indies as an Integrated Puppet (not a subject), giving indirect access to 228.57K additional non-core population and substantial colonial resources.

**Critical diplomatic constraints:** 
- Guarantee tension limit of +100% (Aloof Neutrality) makes early guarantees of allies difficult without reducing faction tension.
- Germany and Japan pose existential threats if war breaks out; Netherlands lacks manpower and industrial base to sustain prolonged conflict without Allied support.
