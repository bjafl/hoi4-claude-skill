South Africa starts as a British Dominion with 9.75 million population, ruled by a pro-British democratic government but facing severe internal tensions between the ruling faction and Afrikaner nationalist movements. Political divisions and racial segregation policies create deep constraints on national mobilization, while the unique focus tree offers paths to break Commonwealth ties, pursue fascism, communism, or autonomy as a colonial liberator.

## Government and political situation

**Ruling coalition:** United National South African Party under Prime Minister J. B. M. Hertzog, committed to South African independence within the Commonwealth (achieved via Statute of Westminster 1931) but increasingly challenged by Afrikaner nationalist sentiment.

**Starting political parties:**
- United National South African Party (Democratic, 75%) — J. B. M. Hertzog
- Gesuiwerde Nasionale Party / GNP (Fascist, 20%) — D. F. Malan  
- South African Communist Party / SACP (Communist, 5%) — Moses Kotane
- African National Congress (Non-Aligned, 0%) — Pixley Ka Isaka Seme

**Election schedule:** Every 3 years; next election September 1937. Hertzog may be defeated if pro-British or fascist sentiment grows; Jan Smuts becomes PM in 1939 if democracy holds (representing pro-British wing).

**Possible leaders (via focus tree):**
- Conservatism/Democracy: J. B. M. Hertzog (1936), Jan Smuts (1939)
- Fascism: D. F. Malan; also enables Edward VIII (ex-UK monarch) as head of state
- Communism: Moses Kotane
- Non-Aligned: Pixley Ka Isaka Seme

## Unique mechanics

**Focus tree overview:** South Africa has 6 main branches and 2 sub-branches structured around independence decisions and ideological realignment.

**War Measures Act / Support the Policy of Appeasement** — Focuses on war preparation, industrial capacity, and defensive forts; reinforces Commonwealth cooperation. Leads to continued democracy/conservatism path.

**Abandon Westminster** — Independence from Commonwealth with a binary ideological choice: Fascist (via Support the Afrikaner Broederbond) or Communist (via Empower the Workers).

**Support the Afrikaner Broederbond** (Fascist sub-branch) — Opens path to fascist civil war via *Support the German Coup* focus; enables partnership with German Reich or independent fascist state; can select Edward VIII as head of state.

**Empower the Workers** (Communist sub-branch) — Opens two paths: alignment with Soviet Union or the unique **Anti-Colonialist Crusade** path, in which South Africa declares war on colonial powers to "liberate Africa."

**South African Railways** — Boosts railway and infrastructure research, supply hub construction, and provides additional research slots.

**Special Service Battalion** — Bonuses to weapons, artillery, and land doctrine research.

**Commit to the Five-Year Plan** — Bonuses to aircraft and air doctrine research.

**Seaward Defense Force** — Naval focus; bonuses to ship models and naval doctrine research.

**Civil war mechanics:** Choosing the fascist coup triggers a civil war against the democratic government (pro-British faction). Winning requires controlling supply depots and encirclement tactics; typical victory by mid-1939 enables Axis joining.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Borders:** British colonies (Northern Rhodesia, Bechuanaland) and Portuguese East Africa (Mozambique) to the north.

**Diplomatic status:** Subject of United Kingdom (Dominion within British Commonwealth). Guaranteed by UK; British has decision to "Foment South African Imperialist Uprising" to install Non-Aligned leader Seme.

**Faction:** None at start; joins Allies if democratic path holds or Axis if fascist coup succeeds.

**Potential allies:** UK and British subjects (if democratic), German Reich (if fascist), Soviet Union (if communist). Anti-Colonialist Crusade path enables unilateral war declaration on all colonial powers.

## Strategies and guides

**Civil war path (Crusader Kings achievement):** Complete Abandon Westminster → Support the Afrikaner Broederbond → Support Ossewabrandwag → Support the German Coup (triggers civil war mid-1938). Prioritize civilian factories in Transvaal early (enables District 9 achievement: 9 civilians in one state). Use Grand Battleplan doctrine for command power and breakthrough. Win civil war by mid-1939; join Axis. Build 10+ divisions; when Italy joins, establish naval invasion from Taranto to Tel Aviv to complete Crusader Kings (take Jerusalem).

**Anti-Colonialist Crusade path (Crusader Kings 2 achievement):** Complete Abandon Westminster → Empower the Workers → Anti-Colonialist Crusade. Release all European colonies via focus; wage war against colonial powers. Conquer Britain (Rule Britannia) for maximum impact. Alternative achievement: I Am the Tong Master (break free from UK, puppet one nation).
