Bhutan is a landlocked minor nation in the Eastern Himalayas with minimal starting resources and no initial military. The country begins under the rule of the Wangchuk dynasty (Non-Aligned ideology) but has pathways to all other ideologies. Bhutan uses the generic national focus tree and starts with severe economic and military constraints, making early survival heavily dependent on diplomatic alliances and careful economic management.

## Geography and diplomatic position

Bhutan is a landlocked mountain nation bordered by:
- **British Raj** to the south
- **Tibet** to the north
- **Nepal** to the west (separated by the Indian state of Sikkim)

The country pursues a neutral foreign policy with no starting guarantees or alliance memberships. Historically, Bhutan signed the Treaty of Punakha with Britain in 1910 (transferring control of foreign affairs) and a similar treaty with India in 1949 after Indian independence.

**Diplomatic stance:** neutral, no factions or guarantees at start.
