Indian Princely States are a group of releasable minor nations representing historical territories within the Indian subcontinent. All sixteen states are released at game start with the "Princely States Free" custom game rule; several are also released under the "Asia Decolonized" rule. Most are non-aligned; Bangladesh alone starts democratic. These states serve primarily as puppet dependencies or liberated nations during gameplay involving British Raj collapse or decolonization scenarios.

## Balochistan

Balochistan starts with elections every 4 years; the next is scheduled for February 1938. The ruling non-aligned government is supported by 60% popularity.
