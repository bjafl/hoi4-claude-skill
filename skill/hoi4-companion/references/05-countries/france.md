France is a major power in 1936, one of the winners of the Great War and controller of a vast colonial empire spanning Africa, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and the Pacific. Its government is divided and destabilized by political infighting; internal problems and economic inefficiency must be addressed before war becomes inevitable. If properly reformed and rearmed, France is positioned to defeat both Germany and Italy.

## Government and political situation

France starts as a **Democratic** major power under the **Parti Radical** with **Léon Blum** as leader. The country suffers from severe political instability captured in the national spirit **Disjointed Government** (costs +0.80 daily political power; stability −10%; surrender limit −50%). The political landscape is fragmented:

- **Ruling party (Democratic):** Parti Radical, 64% popularity, led by Léon Blum
- **Communist faction:** Parti Communiste Francais, 20% popularity, led by Maurice Thorez
- **Fascist faction:** Mouvement Franciste, 1% popularity, led by Marcel Bucard; also Action Française (15%)

France can shift toward other ideologies through the national focus tree:
- **Communist path:** Via "Invite Communist Ministers" sub-branch or "Leftist Rhetoric" (requires La Résistance DLC not enabled); unlocks cooperation with Comintern.
- **Fascist path:** Via "Utilize the Leagues" or "Right Wing Rhetoric" (requires La Résistance DLC not enabled); allows joining the Axis.
- **Monarchist path (La Résistance required):** Via "The Council of Rambouillet" sub-branch; can restore Orléanist, Legitimist, or Bonapartist monarchy.

Historically France took "Form the Popular Front," which puts Léon Blum in power. The ruling party is currently Democratic.

France's negative spirits are particularly severe early-game; getting rid of them requires 350−490 days and is a critical priority.

## Unique mechanics

**Formation:** France can form the **European Union** if Democratic and in control of its own starting core states plus the core states of Germany, Italy, Netherlands, Belgium, and Luxembourg.

**Colonial integration (La Résistance required):** Via "The Blum-Viollette Proposal," France can core Northern Algeria. Via "French Union," France can gain cores on colonies in Africa, the Middle East, or Indochina (outcome is random).

**Monarchical restoration (La Résistance required):** Via "The Council of Rambouillet," France can restore monarchy as Orléanist, Legitimist, or Bonapartist; the Orléanist branch gives war goals on Germany and Italy; the Legitimist branch allows alliance or annexation of Spain; the Bonapartist branch gives war goals on the UK, Belgium, Luxembourg, Netherlands, Germany, and Soviet Union, and can split Germany into 7−9 countries.

**Spanish Civil War intervention:** France can choose to intervene on either the Republican (Democratic/left-wing paths) or Nationalist/Falangist side (if switched to Fascist), or encourage non-interference for a stability boost.

**Franco-British Union (Democratic requirement):** If France is Democratic, capitulated by non-democratic Germany, and the UK is in the same Allied faction, France can propose a Franco-British Union. If the UK accepts, the game ends for France but the UK gains all French cores, units, ships, generals, and advisors; the Union can later be dissolved at the cost of losing these benefits.

**Vichy France formation:** If France is capitulated by Fascist Germany (while Germany is still at war), Germany can establish Vichy France as a puppet. If capitulated by a non-aligned government or restored Kaisers, France becomes Bourbon France instead.

**Research expansion:** The "Devalue the Franc" branch unlocks +2 research slots (to 5 total).

## Geography and Neighbours

France is located in Western Europe with extensive overseas territories. Mainland France consists of flat, arable land in the northwest and forest/highland in the center, dominated by major rivers: Rhine (northeast border), Rhône (southeast), Seine (northwest), Loire (center), and Garonne (southwest).

**Land borders:** Belgium (north), German Reich (northeast, Alsace-Lorraine region), Switzerland (east), Italy (southeast, Alps-separated), Spain (south, Pyrenees-separated).

**Overseas territories and colonies:** Algeria, Syria, Lebanese Republic (puppet), Madagascar, Indochina (Cambodia, Champa/Vietnam, Laos), Morocco (Casablanca, Fez, Marrakech, Tangier), Tunisia, French Equatorial Africa (Cameroon, Chad, Gabon, Congostan, Ubangi-Shari), French West Africa (Bamako, Dahomey, Ivory Coast, Mauritania, Senegal, French Sudan), and scattered Pacific/Indian Ocean islands.

**Strategic position:** France guarantees Poland's independence (in 1936) and is part of the Allies faction alongside the United Kingdom. France borders Germany's western frontier and controls the English Channel, making it central to any European conflict.

## Diplomatic and Faction Status

France begins in the **Allies faction** and has a guarantee on Poland. It is a major country and receives associated benefits (eligible for various diplomatic decisions and peace-conference cost modifiers). Historical diplomatic paths include forming the **Little Entente** with Czechoslovakia, Yugoslavia, Romania, and Poland, or creating the **Stresa Front** with the UK and Italy (historically unsuccessful).
