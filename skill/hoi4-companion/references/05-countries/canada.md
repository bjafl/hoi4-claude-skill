## Summary

Canada begins in 1936 as a democratic Dominion subject to the United Kingdom, with moderate industrial output and secure geography. Its focus tree offers both a historical path (remaining within the Allies and the Commonwealth) and revisionist options (Patriation, which allows breaking free and joining the Axis or going communist). Defensively sheltered by the United States and oceans, Canada can focus production on supporting its faction or pursuing independent military goals.

## Government and political situation

Canada starts as a **Democratic Dominion** under the **United Kingdom** with the **Liberal Party of Canada** in power, led by **Mackenzie King**. Elections occur every five years (next election: October 1940).

### Status and path to independence

Canada is formally a Dominion of the United Kingdom and begins as a subject state. The **Patriation** branch of the focus tree allows Canada to sever ties with Britain (via event-based referendum, costing 300 political power plus costs for ideology change). Once patriated, Canada can pursue democratic, fascist, or communist paths independently.

Historical path: **Strengthen the Commonwealth Ties** keeps Canada within the Allies and provides bonuses to Commonwealth technology sharing.

## National spirits

Canada begins with three national spirits:

### Great Depression

- Consumer Goods Factories factor: 50.0%
- Factory output: −20.00%
- Dockyard output: −20.00%

**Effect:** Debuffs industrial output and forces higher civilian goods allocation. Can be removed via focus tree.

### Conscription Crisis among French Canadians

- Recruitable population Factor: −30%

**Effect:** Caps manpower pool. Can be mitigated (but not removed) via the "Commit to War" focus; can also be exacerbated or converted via the "Forced Quebec Conscription" focus.

## Unique mechanics

### Focus tree branches

Canada's focus tree offers multiple strategic paths:

- **Industrial and Internal Politics** — Increases industrial capacity, removes Great Depression penalties. Gated behind war/world tension thresholds. Includes "Send in the Zombies" (major manpower unlock) and "National Steel Car" (full industrial unlock).
- **Army Modernization** — Research bonuses for infantry, armor, artillery, and support divisions.
- **Strengthen the Commonwealth Ties** — Historical path. Bonuses to Commonwealth technology sharing and other industrial/resource buffs.
- **Patriation** — Allows Canada to break from the UK, opening three ideological sub-paths (democratic, fascist, communist). Fascist branch includes the option to ally Mexico against the US.
- **RCAF Station Borden** — Research bonuses for radar, aircraft, and air doctrine.
- **Halifax Shipyards** — Research bonuses for ship models and naval doctrine.

### Technology cosmetic names

Canada uses unique historical names for various equipment and unit types (e.g., Ross Rifle for infantry equipment in 1918, SAL Rifle No.4 MkI in 1936). See the full wiki page for the complete equipment cosmetic table.

### Military and diplomatic notes

- Canada begins with small, outdated forces (8 infantry divisions and 5 cavalry divisions in 1936).
- As a subject state, Canada cannot fully control its own diplomacy until Patriation is completed.
- Favorable geography: bordered by oceans (east/west) and the US (south). Geographic isolation makes Canada defensible if it remains in an Allies-aligned faction.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

### Borders

- **United States** — South and west. No war in historical scenario; can be fought if Canada pursues fascist patriation.
- **Newfoundland** — East. British colonial possession; does not border Canada playable states.

### Diplomatic position

- **Faction:** Allies (via UK subject status). Can join independent factions or the Axis only after Patriation.
- **Guarantees:** Inherits British guarantees; no direct guarantees from Canada.
- **Subject status:** Dominion of the United Kingdom. Subject to UK diplomatic decisions; must complete Patriation to achieve full sovereignty.
