## Summary

Hearts of Iron IV has an extensive community vocabulary encompassing acronyms (AA, CAS, NAV), slang terms (blob, cheese, snaking), and country tag abbreviations. This glossary covers all common jargon used in game discussions and strategy documentation, making it essential for interpreting player communication and understanding game mechanics.

## Acronyms and initialisms

**AA**
Anti-air. Used for state anti-air and divisional anti-air (battalions or companies using anti-aircraft artillery and self-propelled anti-air).

**AAA**
Anti-aircraft artillery. Artillery designed to shoot down enemy aircraft.

**AC**
Armored Car (wheeled, lightly armored vehicle for reconnaissance) or Air Controller (dedicated player managing air wings in multiplayer).

**AH**
Alternate History, Austria-Hungary, or Adolf Hitler.

**AAR**
After Action Report. A player's written recounting of their game; can be from personal viewpoint or fictitious narrative perspective. Often posted on HOI4 forums or blogs with screenshots and maps.

**AAT**
The Arms Against Tyranny DLC.

**AI**
Artificial intelligence. Controls all nations not played by humans.

**AIOC**
Anglo-Iranian Oil Company.

**APC**
Armored Personnel Carrier (vehicle for carrying infantry) or Armor Piercing Capped (shell type for anti-tank and naval guns).

**ARM**
Armor. Includes light (LARM), medium (MARM), heavy (HARM), and super-heavy (SHARM) varieties.

**ASW**
Anti-submarine warfare. Use of ships and planes to find and destroy enemy submarines; primary task of destroyer escorts.

**ART**
Artillery, including support, line, or self-propelled varieties (see SPxx). Also called "Arty". See also RART.

**AT**
Anti-tank. Designates units with high hard attack and piercing value; can be artillery or self-propelled.

**BB**
Battleship. Most powerful ship type equipped with large-caliber guns.

**BBA**
The By Blood Alone DLC.

**BBB**
Super heavy battleship. Heavily armored and gunned; sometimes designated SHBB.

**BC**
Battlecruiser. Lightly armored battleship; served historically as fast convoy attack ship but functions as BB in HoI4.

**BFTB**
The Battle for the Bosporus DLC.

**BP**
Battleplan. Mechanic used to simplify defensive or offensive management of divisions.

**Brigade**
Term used in historical unit names and informally for tactical formations of one or more regiments. To avoid confusion with regiment, should not be used interchangeably when describing division template columns.

**BS**
Battlefield Support. Air doctrine tree that benefits Close Air Support the most.

**btn**
Common abbreviation in division design for the smallest unit besides support companies; also abbreviation for battalion.

**CA**
Heavy Cruiser. Large-sized cruiser capable of fitting multiple roles.

**CAG**
Carrier air group. Planes attached to an aircraft carrier.

**CAP/cap**
More commonly: Capitulation or surrender. Less commonly: Combat Air Patrol (fighter planes flying overhead for air defense).

**CAS**
Close air support. Specialized aircraft that inflict organization and hit points damage to enemy divisions during battle.

**CASNAV/NAVCAS**
Naval bomber aircraft with significant close air support weapons, allowing dual CAS/NAV role.

**CAV**
Cavalry. Combat units mounted on horseback; available at war start in many nations and favored for partisan suppression.

**CB**
Casus belli (cause for war; mechanism for justifying war goals like Conquer or Puppet) or Large cruisers (Panzerschiffs and coastal defense ships using cruiser armor with heavy gun batteries).

**CiC**
Construction industrial capacity. Capacity provided by civilian factories for any structure construction.

**CinC**
Commander-in-chief. Not used in-game but players use it to designate Chief of Army and similar positions.

**Civs**
Same as CiC.

**CL**
Light cruiser. Ship used for screening and sometimes anti-air roles.

**CNO**
Chief of Naval Operations. Not used in-game but players use it for Chief of Navy.

**CSC**
Constant Conversion. Cheesy strategy exploiting equipment conversion to produce expensive equipment at a fraction of normal resource costs.

**CTD**
Crash To Desktop. Frequently referenced in patch notes.

**CTF**
Commander of Task Force or Carrier Task Force.

**CUF**
Chinese United Front.

**CV**
Aircraft carrier. Ship transporting fighter, CAS, or naval bomber aircraft.

**CVE**
Escort Aircraft Carrier. Shorter, slower carrier with fewer planes; cheaper and quicker to build. Usually converted from cruiser (requires Man the Guns). Historically for convoy defense but ineffective for that in-game; best for navies unable to afford full carriers or wanting to sidestep carrier stacking penalties.

**CVL**
Light Aircraft Carrier. Faster than CVE; can keep pace with full-size aircraft carriers.

**DC**
Damage Control (emergency ship damage prevention) or Depth Charge (weapon for anti-submarine warfare).

**DD**
Destroyer. Ship for screening role and anti-submarine warfare. Also: Developer Diary.

**DDE**
Destroyer Escort. Destroyer designed to escort ocean convoys and merchant ships against submarines.

**DEI/VOC**
Dutch East Indies.

**DLC**
Downloadable content. Paradox packages adding mechanics, music, and features (compare with mod).

**DMZ**
Demilitarized Zone. Area where treaty prohibits military forces and installations.

**DOD/DoD**
Death or Dishonor. Early DLC since integrated into core game.

**DoW**
Danzig or War (common Germany national focus choice) or Declaration of war (verb: "Japan just DOW'd the USA!").

**DP**
Dual Purpose gun. Light-caliber naval artillery for anti-ship and anti-aircraft.

**ENG**
Engineer support company.

**ETO**
European Theater of Operations. US term for operations in Europe.

**FC**
Fire Control. Method for aiming ship guns accounting for range, elevation, ship speed, and distance to target.

**FCS**
Fire Control System. System aiding ship gun aiming.

**FEX**
Fire Exchange group. Cluster of ships simultaneously engaged in sea battle.

**FM**
Field Marshal. Highest of two possible army leader ranks.

**FOW**
Fog of War. Prevents nations from seeing map areas they don't border or have adjacent army/navy.

**FTR**
Fighter aircraft. Often refers specifically to light fighters (non-heavy fighter research tree).

**GBP**
Grand Battleplan. Available land doctrine.

**GD**
Great Depression or Götterdämmerung DLC.

**GDP**
Gross Domestic Product. Value of all goods and services produced in a country during given year.

**GEACPS**
Greater East Asia Co-Prosperity Sphere. Coalition led by Japan comprising East Asian nations.

**GIE**
Government In Exile. Temporary government formed by exiles hoping to rule former country upon liberation.

**GM**
Game-master. Leader or organizer of multiplayer game.

**GOE/GoE**
The Graveyard of Empires DLC.

**HA**
Hard attack. Hard attack value of unit or division.

**HCmd**
High Command. Same as MHC.

**HFTR**
Heavy fighter.

**HK**
Hunter Killer. Destroyer groups that hunt and kill enemy submarines.

**HOS/HOSP**
Field hospital support company.

**HP**
Hit points. Represents damage a division can take (maximum HP increased by adding regiments). Also represents ship damage before sinking or building damage before destruction.

**IC**
Industrial capacity. Term from earlier HoI installments; equivalent to Factory Output in HoI4.

**IC-days**
Number of days of IC usage. Earlier HoI term; similar to Production Cost in HoI4.

**IJN**
Imperial Japanese Navy.

**INF**
Infantry. Can refer to general infantry category (using infantry equipment) or specifically non-specialized combat infantrymen on foot (distinct from Marines, Paratroopers, Mountain Infantry, Cavalry, motorized, and mechanized infantry).

**JIRA**
Software manager used by Paradox for bug reporting.

**LaR**
La Résistance. Third major expansion by Paradox for Hearts of Iron IV.

**LL**
Lend Lease. Program to send free aid (weapons, tanks, planes) to other nations.

**LNT**
London Naval Treaty. Agreement between United Kingdom, Japan, France, Italy, and United States to limit and reduce naval armament.

**LOG**
Logistics support company.

**MA**
Mass Assault. Available land doctrine.

**MAIN**
Maintenance support company.

**MHC**
Military High Command.

**MiC**
Military industrial capacity. Capacity provided by military factories for equipment construction.

**Mils**
Same as MiC.

**MM/Mass Mob**
Mass Mobilization branch of Mass Assault land doctrine; also refers to flood of small infantry divisions holding frontline enabled by Mass Mobilization bonuses.

**MP**
Multi-player, Military Police, or Manpower.

**MR**
Molotov-Ribbentrop pact. Non-aggression pact between Germany and Soviet Union.

**MRA**
Motorized Rocket Artillery. Rocket artillery on truck chassis.

**MTB**
Motor Torpedo Boat. Small, fast, lightly armed boat for torpedoing enemy ships.

**MTG/MtG**
The Man the Guns DLC.

**MTTH**
Mean time to happen. Metric measuring average time until event triggers.

**MW**
Mobile Warfare. Available land doctrine.

**NAP**
Non-Aggression Pact. Agreement or formal treaty between countries not to attack each other via Declaration of War.

**NAV**
Naval bombers. Used for anti-shipping in sea zones and port strikes over land.

**NF**
National focus. Concept similar to technologies but for political actions.

**NiC**
Naval industrial capacity. Capacity provided by dockyards for ship construction.

**NKVD**
Narodnyy Komissariat Vnutrennikh Del (People's Commissariat for Internal Affairs). Secret police during Stalin regime.

**NS**
National Spirit. Modifier describing unique country advantages or disadvantages.

**NSB**
No Step Back. Sixth expansion by Paradox for Hearts of Iron IV.

**OOB**
Order of Battle. Hierarchical organization, command structure, strength, disposition of personnel, formations, and equipment of armed forces.

**OOS**
Out of Sync. Notorious desynchronization error in multiplayer requiring game reload. Also: Out of Supply.

**OP**
Overpowered or Original Poster (forums).

**OPM**
Overpowered minor.

**PC**
Peace conference. Game phase when country or all faction countries capitulate.

**PH**
Pearl Harbor. Naval base in Hawaii.

**PotF**
Pride of the Fleet. Capital ship chosen to represent country and people at their "best".

**PP**
Political power. Points acquired over time; required for all political actions.

**PTO**
Pacific Theater of Operations. US term for operations in Pacific.

**QRF**
Quick Reaction Force. Military unit responding quickly to military emergency like frontline breach.

**RART**
Rocket Artillery.

**REC**
Recon support company.

**Rgt**
Regiment. Basic military formation of 1+ battalions; in-game division template column with 1-5 compatible battalion types.

**RN**
Royal Navy (United Kingdom).

**RNG**
Random number generator. Mathematical subsystem generating random numbers for event chances, battle results, and other randomized features.

**ROTW/ROW**
Rest of the world. Generally all areas outside Europe.

**SA**
Soft attack. Soft attack value of division or unit.

**SAG**
Surface Action Group. Task force of surface warships with primary mission to attack and sink enemy surface ships.

**SCW**
Spanish Civil War. Event that always happens in 1936 scenario start.

**SEA**
Southeast Asia.

**SIG**
Signal support company.

**SF**
Superior Firepower (available land doctrine) or Special Forces (Marines, Mountain Infantry, Paratroopers) or Strike Force (naval mission).

**shp**
Shaft horsepower. Power delivered to ship propeller shaft.

**SHxx**
Super Heavy [something]. Designates Super Heavy Battleships (SHBB), tanks (SHT), artillery (SHA), tank destroyer (SHTD), or anti-air (SHAA).

**SPxx**
Self-propelled [something]. Weapons on vehicle chassis, including self-propelled artillery (SPART/SPG), tank destroyer (SPTD), and anti-air (SPAA).

**SS**
Submarine.

**SSN**
Nuclear Submarine.

**SSBN**
Nuclear Ballistic Missile Submarine.

**TAC**
Tactical bombers. Aircraft flexible in strategic bombing, ground support, naval strike, and port strike missions.

**TD**
Tank destroyer. Designates armored vehicles with high hard attack and piercing values.

**TF**
Task Force. Temporary grouping of naval assets (ships, planes) for specific mission.

**TfV**
Together for Victory. Early DLC since integrated into core game.

**TPM**
Two/three province minor. Borrowed from EU4; small countries of only 2-3 states.

**TOA**
The Trial of Allegiance DLC.

**TOE**
Table of Organization and Equipment. Specified organization, staffing, and equipment of units in table form.

**USN**
United States Navy.

**VP**
Victory Points. Points on certain provinces (usually capitals and strategic locations) measuring distance to capitulation.

**WAD**
Works/working as designed. Acronym meaning something considered a bug is actually intentional.

**WC**
World conquest. Taking over every inhabitable province; common player goal; represents ahistorical outcome.

**WNT**
Washington Naval Treaty. 1922 treaty among major World War I winners limiting naval construction to prevent arms race.

**WS**
War Support. Willingness of population to endure war privations.

**WT**
World tension. Variable limiting democracy involvement in ongoing events.

**WTT/WtT**
Waking the Tiger. Early DLC since integrated into core game.

**XP**
Experience. Points acquired in three categories (Army, Navy, Air) through combat or other means.

## Slang

**Agency**
Short for intelligence agency.

**Air Controller**
Dedicated multiplayer player focusing attention on micromanaging air wings for their faction; pursues strong air bonuses through national focuses and military high command choices.

**Air Co-op**
Multiplayer player on larger nation focusing on air management cooperatively without full dedication to air-only roles.

**Air Gamble/Air Casino**
Axis multiplayer strategy heavily investing in faction air force, forcing Allies to match investment. Called "gamble" or "casino" due to highly variable results—sometimes strong (preventing naval invasions) and sometimes weak (insufficient land unit production).

**Armor Meme**
Tank design with very high armor used as single battalion in tank division otherwise containing economical tanks; more cost-effective armor provision than Space Marine.

**Asia LARP**
Multiplayer strategy where Allies (mainly UK and US) prioritize fighting Japan in Asia instead of Axis in Europe and Africa.

**Barb**
Shortening of "Operation Barbarossa"; German invasion of USSR/Russia.

**Blob**
Large nation tending to expand unless checked; term derives from irregular borders resembling paint blobs.

**Boosting/Trade Boosting**
Multiplayer practice of deliberately performing actions providing civilian factories to allied country, such as unnecessary trading, production licenses, or equipment purchases through international market.

**Cheese/Cheesy**
Strategy providing notable advantage unlikely to be intentional by developers. Softer than exploits and more fair. Examples: constant conversion, network spam removing enemy planning bonus, submarine spam.

**Civ**
Civilian factory.

**(Country) Can Into Space**
Making a country succeed far beyond expectations. Takeoff on polandball meme.

**Clay**
One's rightful land. Also from polandball.

**Click (Province/Unit)**
Quickly defeating all enemy forces in province without effort besides right-clicking.

**Conductor**
Dedicated multiplayer player producing and micromanaging railway guns (preferably super-heavy); common for minors whose land unit bonuses pale beside teammates (e.g., Romania for Axis, South Africa for Allies).

**Constant Conversion**
Cheesy strategy bypassing expensive tank/aircraft resource costs by simultaneously producing basic outdated designs and converting to final designs, saving civilian factories from resource trading.

**D-Day**
Historically: Allied naval landings in occupied France (Operation Overlord). In-game: any naval invasion of continental Europe from Britain; used as verb ("The Allies are D-Daying!").

**Div(s)**
Division(s). Basic combat unit on map.

**Doomstack**
Extremely large enemy force in single province; seldom used for land in HoI4 but common for navies.

**Double Combat**
Attacking same enemy province from two different provinces as separate combats (by queueing moves carefully), ensuring enemy units receive "in multiple combats" penalty in both combats.

**El Ala**
Abbreviation for El Alamein; notorious choke point for armies in Egypt.

**Excav**
Abbreviation for excavation technologies.

**Flickering/Flicker Bombing**
Rapidly switching air wings (usually bombers) between multiple air zones to abuse naval strike and port strike mechanics or overload enemy air controller micromanagement.

**Gibby**
Abbreviation for Gibraltar; notable choke point controlling Mediterranean/Atlantic access.

**Gorilla/Gorilla Tactics**
Bastardization of "Guerrilla Tactics" combat tactic (unlocked via Mass Mobilization branch of Mass Assault).

**Grinding**
Deliberately keeping units in battles without capturing provinces to generate XP. Early-war practice for leveling volunteer generals; difficult with admirals. Pre-grinds used in multiplayer mods to avoid manual grinding and speed up game.

**Gun**
Abbreviation for infantry equipment. Usually followed by number indicating year: gun 0 (1918), gun 1 (1936), gun 2 (1939), gun 3 (1942).

**Hands**
Multiplayer reference to players actively attending delicate situations and responding (or insult for those who cannot/do not).

**Holding Event**
Deliberately letting event expire or waiting until last moment to select option, delaying undesirable effects (e.g., Allies handing Sudetenland to Germany) by crucial weeks.

**Howies**
Abbreviation for howitzer tanks; howitzer main armament gives high soft attack but minimal hard attack.

**Inf**
Infantry battalion or division.

**Infra**
Infrastructure.

**Juggling/Tech Juggling**
Leaving research slot empty for month before spending accrued progress to complete high-priority tech faster; switch slots strategically to accumulate progress.

**Justifying**
Political action of building propaganda case against country to justify declaration of war.

**Lobby Simulator/Lobby Sim**
Multiplayer time spent in lobby waiting for game start, player reconnections, or hot-join loading.

**Mana**
Slang for numerical Political power, Experience, and other points.

**Mech**
Mechanized battalion or division.

**Mech Marine**
Marine division with few mechanized battalions greatly increasing division HP for longer naval invasion endurance via Force Attack commander ability.

**Mil**
Military factory.

**Macro**
Opposite of "micro"; larger strategy choices outside individual unit control, including production, research, construction, political power, national focus order and timing.

**Micro**
Micromanagement; controlling individual units and attention/skill required under time pressure. **Pause-Micro:** Queueing orders while paused. **Last Stand Micro/Force Attack Micro:** Using dummy order-of-battle with lower-quality generals to dramatically reduce command power cost.

**Mod**
Software package modifying game written by player community (compare with DLC).

**Navy Co-op**
Multiplayer player on larger nation focusing solely on micromanaging that country's navy.

**Navy LARP**
Strategy where country emphasizes (surface) navy far beyond sensible levels for that nation.

**Nuke**
Nuclear bombs or act of using them.

**Org**
Organization; required for division to engage combat. **Deorg:** Division running out of organization, forced to break off attack or retreat when defending.

**Pop Cipher**
After decrypting enemy country cipher for major temporary bonus, needing to re-decrypt after bonus expires.

**PP Guy**
Political advisor increasing country political power gain, usually Silent Workhorse trait.

**Refs**
Abbreviation for synthetic refineries.

**Reinfs**
Reinforcements in manpower, equipment, or both. **Reinforce meme:** Defeating all divisions in combat before reserves can enter, forcing retreat without fighting or destruction if encircled.

**Rehost**
Multiplayer: game host saves, exits (disconnecting players), then hosts new game from save; attempted fix for widespread crashes and out-of-sync.

**Safari**
Axis capturing Egypt and advancing south into Africa.

**Savescumming**
Reloading earlier saves when something goes wrong. Often negative trait. Ironman mode prevents it unless circumvented via manual file copying.

**Shore**
Abbreviation for shore bombardment.

**Shovels**
Engineer support companies.

**Silent Invasion**
Multiplayer exploitation of naval invasion bug allowing launches without visual/audio alert.

**Snaking**
Sending single/small division group racing through breakthrough to capture tiles, VPs, or ports without securing behind, disrupting industry/supply or VP sniping. Often results in unit encirclement and loss.

**Space Marine**
Single armored battalion (heavy tank destroyer or tank) division otherwise lacking armor; provides armor/piercing for low cost via armor/piercing calculation mechanics. Considered overpowered early versions; far less cost-effective currently.

**Stab Guy**
Political advisor increasing country stability, usually Popular Figurehead trait.

**Strat**
Strategic bomber, strategic redeploy, or strategy.

**Tile**
Province.

**Tradebacks**
Multiplayer: one country buying ally resources to offset ally using civvies for first country's resources, even when unused; offsets production license civilian factory cost.

**Vols**
Short for volunteers.

**VP Sniping**
Using paratroopers, fast division snaking, and/or naval invasions to capture enemy victory points causing capitulation, bypassing most army. Unsuccessful attempts risk unit encirclement and loss.

**Wardec**
Declaring war or being declared upon.

**War Support Cruiser**
Cheapest basic heavy cruiser made solely to be designated Pride of the Fleet for extra war support.

## After Action Reports (AAR)

AARs are threads posted to HOI4 forums, personal websites, or blogs documenting player games. Can be written from player personal viewpoint or fictitious narrative perspective like serialized novel; many blend both—metagame discussion plus fictitious narrative in alternate history world.

**Player Point of View**

Personal recounting from player perspective: "I faced coalition attacks, enemy declarations of war mid-battle, and had to manage complex frontlines."

**Fictitious Narrative**

Storytelling format: "It is 1938 and the Munich Conference determines the Sudetenland's fate. Czechoslovakia, prepared with fortifications and full war economy since 1936, refuses surrender. Germany declares war. Czech forces, despite no foreign aid, hold prepared positions through harsh winter, launch counterattacks, and push German forces back through Austria and Germany, reaching Berlin in March 1940 and causing German capitulation by September. Italy enters late and falls by December 1940. Japan's 1941 restoration attempt fails. The course of human history changes—World War 2 never occurs, Allies destroy Soviet Union, Cold War prevented. Czechoslovakia becomes world power. Czechmate."

AARs often include screenshots from games, maps, period art, and other elements adding depth.
