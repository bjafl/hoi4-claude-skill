## Summary

Two reference sets. First, **historical division templates** (1939–1944) recreated in the division designer, each with its full base, combat and cost stat block — useful for flavour play and for calibrating what a "realistic" division of the period looks like. Second, **doctrine sample divisions**: six standard 20-width templates at 1939 technology, showing base, max-planning and max-entrenchment stats under every land doctrine path, which is the cleanest side-by-side comparison of what each doctrine actually buys.

## Doctrine sample divisions

Six standard **20 combat width** templates with **1939 technologies** applied, compared across every land doctrine path. All six include the support companies **engineer, artillery, anti-tank and anti-air**.

Doctrine path notation: `L`/`R` are the left/right choices at each branching point of the doctrine tree, in order.

> ⚠️ Mass Assault reduces the width of infantry battalions, so those divisions come out **under 20 width**. Either add more battalions to compensate or let the game fit more divisions on the front — either way the Mass Assault numbers below are slightly misleading as a like-for-like comparison.

Column groups:
- **Base Values** — Soft Attack, Hard Attack, Breakthrough, Defense, Avg. Org.
- **Max. Planning (Attacker)** — Soft Attack, Hard Attack, Breakthrough at maximum planning bonus.
- **Max. Entrenchment (Defender)** — Soft Attack, Hard Attack, Defense at maximum entrenchment.

## Reading the doctrine tables

**Inferred** from the numbers above:

- **Mobile Warfare** buys **organization** and, on tank templates, **breakthrough** — it is the only path where template 5's breakthrough reaches 427.2 (MW R&R). It adds nothing to soft/hard attack.
- **Superior Firepower** buys **soft attack** (and defence on infantry templates), at a heavy cost in average organization — compare SF L&L's 55.7 org against MW L&R's 76.4 on template 1.
- **Grand Battleplan** buys **planning and entrenchment multipliers**: its base stats are middling, but it has by far the highest max-planning and max-entrenchment columns (e.g. template 1 entrenched defence 547.5 vs 471.4 for Superior Firepower).
- **Mass Assault** gives modest breakthrough and entrenchment and the **lowest organization**, with its real payoff being the infantry combat-width reduction that these tables cannot show.
