## Summary

Naval support technology covers all technologies not directly tied to ship hulls: naval weaponry (batteries, shells, torpedoes), defensive systems (damage control, fire control), invasion logistics (transports), and mine warfare. As of Patch 1.15, all naval support techs generate naval engineering breakthrough progress while researching. Many techs offer optional research bonuses in exchange for navy experience.

## Armaments

| Basic Heavy Battery | 1936 | 110 days | Naval Gunnery | Unlocks Heavy Battery II (30.5cm), Super-Heavy Battery (46cm) |

| Improved Medium Battery | 1939 | 110 days | Armor-Piercing Capped Medium Shell | Unlocks Heavy Cruiser Battery III (20.3cm), Light Cruiser Battery III (15.5cm) |

## Notes

- All naval support techs generate naval engineering breakthrough progress while researching (as of Patch 1.15).
- Optional research bonuses ("50 Navy experience for +150%") allow faster research completion by spending accumulated navy experience.
- Dual-purpose batteries combine anti-ship and anti-air capabilities but provide fewer bonuses in each domain compared to dedicated batteries.
- Fire Control Methods and Damage Control technologies apply globally to your entire navy once researched.
