## Summary

Belgian Congo receives a unique national focus tree with the Götterdämmerung DLC. The tree centers on independence paths and post-independence development. The player can remain colonial under Governor-General Pierre Ryckmans (strengthening the regime, decreasing autonomy, building industry), or pursue independence through democratic negotiation (ABAKO) or communist revolution (Simba movement). After independence, the country develops along democratic (Kasa-Vubu), communist Pan-African (Congo People's Republic), or monarchist paths, with the Regency Council unlocking six historical kingdoms (Kongo, Bakuba, Loango, Burundi, Rwanda, Mittelafrika). War branches activate if Belgium enters conflict.

See [Belgian Congo](../05-countries/belgian-congo.md) for the country's 1936 starting profile. See [National Focus and Continuous Focus](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md) for generic/continuous focuses.
