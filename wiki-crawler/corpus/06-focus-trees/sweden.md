## Summary

Sweden gets a unique national focus tree with the Arms Against Tyranny DLC. Without it, Sweden uses the generic tree. The Swedish tree spans two main branches: the industrial/social Defense Act Branch with 15 sub-branches covering economic, military, and political development, and the joint Form the Nordic Council branch for faction members.

See [01-mechanics/national-focus.md](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md) for generic and continuous focuses. See [Sweden](../05-countries/sweden.md) for the country's 1936 starting profile.

## Form the Nordic Council Branch

The Form the Nordic Council Branch is a joint focus tree shared by Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Norway, and Sweden (requires Arms Against Tyranny). It becomes available to all Nordic faction members when one Nordic country creates a faction with another. For details on the specific focuses in this branch, see the main [Form the Nordic Council](shared-and-joint-branches.md) entry (not yet in this knowledge base).
