## Summary

Hungary's national focus tree, introduced in Death or Dishonor and comprehensively overhauled in Götterdämmerung, contains 238 focuses across 6 major branches. The tree emphasizes Hungary's monarchist traditions, ideological choices (democracy, fascism, communism), territorial revision under the Treaty of Trianon, Austro-Hungarian restoration, and military development. The base game version without Götterdämmerung offers a simpler alternative with 99 focuses.

See [Hungary](../05-countries/hungary.md) for the country's 1936 starting profile. For generic and continuous focuses, see [National Focus and Continuous Focus](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md).

## Götterdämmerung Version (Current)

Requires Götterdämmerung DLC; falls back to base game version if disabled. This tree emphasizes three ideological/governance paths: Monarchist (Habsburg restoration or democratic/fascist kings), Republican (democratic, communist, or socialist paths), and internal administrative/military branches.
