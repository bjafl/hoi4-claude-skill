## Summary

Czechoslovakia's unique focus tree was introduced in Death or Dishonor and extensively revised in Peace for Our Time (1.6.0+). The modern tree features branching political paths reflecting the country's struggles between democracy, monarchy, fascism, and alignment with major powers, plus industrial development programs. Base-game version retained for legacy reference.

See [Czechoslovakia](../05-countries/czechoslovakia.md) for the country's 1936 starting profile. For generic/continuous focus mechanics, see [National Focus and Continuous Focus](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md).
