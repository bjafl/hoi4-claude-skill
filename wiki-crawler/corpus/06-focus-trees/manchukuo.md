## Summary

Manchukuo's unique focus tree offers divergent paths: loyalty to Japan with industrial investment and eventual peer status, or assertive independence pursued through ideological realignment and military expansion. The tree is heavily bifurcated on the choice between obedience and assertiveness, allowing either a gradual climb to autonomy within the Japanese sphere or an armed break-free. The base-game tree contains the same trunk but simpler political branches and fewer advanced military/industrial options.

See [Manchukuo](../05-countries/manchukuo.md) for the country's 1936 starting profile. See [01-mechanics/national-focus.md](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md) for generic and continuous focuses—this document covers only Manchukuo's unique tree.

Pursues either restoration of the Chinese Empire under Zhang Jinghui (and annexation of Mengjiang) or formation of the Empire of Manchuria under Puyi with Shinto incorporation and claims on Soviet Outer Manchuria. Two mutually exclusive paths.

## Shared Branch: Invite Foreign Investors

Both DLC and base-game versions share this branch with all other Chinese nations (China, Chinese Soviet Republic, and Chinese warlords). See the shared Chinese industrial focus documentation for details. This branch pursues industrial investment from major powers to achieve recognized sovereignty over China.

## Key Strategic Notes

**Vassal Path (Obedience/Strengthen the Concordia Association):**
- Maintains peace with Japan; yields steady industrial investment (civilian factories, building slots, research slots).
- Gradual autonomy climb via *The Two Emperors* focus (DLC) or by meeting industrial/military thresholds (base game).
- Locks out independence war but enables territorial claims on all China via *National Cooperation Government* (base game) or *Chinese Leadership* (DLC).
- Industrial research and production are the primary gains.

**Independence Path (Assertiveness):**
- Commits to war against Japan via *Independence War* focus.
- Significant stability and war support penalties before the war (low legitimacy cascades).
- Post-independence, enables rapid territorial claims and annexation or vassalization of Chinese factions.
- Requires strong army and industry before committing; *The Two Emperors* (base game) has a bypass if fully independent, suggesting early breakaway is possible but not recommended without preparation.
- Path variants (Staff the Court vs. Five Equal Peoples) offer minor bonuses (stability vs. war support) but do not branch further in base game.

**DLC Variants:**
- **Question of Leadership** (DLC only) adds ideological depth: Chinese Empire restoration vs. multi-ethnic Empire of Manchuria, with differential claims and cultural identity.
- **Question the Emperor's Authority** (DLC; incomplete data) references democratic/communist paths but is not fully extracted.
- **Assertiveness sub-branches** in DLC include White Russian Advisers and Five People Armies, both offering military XP and manpower gains if Five Equal Peoples is chosen.

**Industrial Gameplay:**
- Builds a comprehensive industrial base: civilian factories (via settlers, investors, textile expansion, Nissan, Showa Steel), military factories (Mukden Arsenal, MAMC, local procurement, Nissan if automotive).
- Railway network (Expand the Railways) requires full control of four states but yields massive infrastructure connectivity.
- Mangyo (DLC) is the capstone industrial focus, merging Anshan and South Manchuria Railway with massive production and consumer-goods penalties.
- Base game's simpler path omits Mangyo but retains steel and aircraft manufacturing.

**Research Acceleration:**
- Law University, Research and Education Department, Study the Gassakusha (DLC), or Social Research Unit (base game) yield +1 research slot each (up to 3 additional slots).
- Research bonuses on Industry, Land Doctrine, Excavation, and aerodynamics are woven throughout.

**Military Options:**
- Mukden Military Academy (command power +25) is foundational.
- Mukden Arsenal and MAMC are the military-industrial nexuses, with Arms Against Tyranny DLC enabling Military Industrial Organizations for advanced aircraft/automotive design.
- Xing'an Army expansion (DLC only) creates elite division template with named generals.
- Independence path (both versions) offers unit creation: Imperial Guards (1 unit, 7 inf + 2 art) and mass conscription via Five People Armies (7 units, 3 inf each).
