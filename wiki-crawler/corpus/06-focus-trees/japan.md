## Summary

Japan is one of the seven great powers and has a unique national focus tree. The tree is divided into 6 main branches and 20 sub-branches, with 3 additional branches when the No Compromise, No Surrender DLC is disabled. Japan's focus tree heavily emphasizes military and political decisions that shape the Imperial government, ranging from militaristic paths (Imperial Way/Tōseiha) to democratic reforms, communist revolution, and economic policies. The Okada's Military Purge Speech leads to a branching path centered on the Kyūjō Incident and cabinet politics; most other branches remain available regardless of ideology.

See [Japan](../05-countries/japan.md) for the country's 1936 starting profile. For shared and generic focus mechanics, see [National Focus and Continuous Focus](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md).

## Focus tree structure and DLC requirements

**Base game + No Compromise, No Surrender DLC enabled:**
- Okada's Military Purge Speech Branch → Purge the Kodoha Faction Branch → The Koiso Cabinet Branch
- Revere the Emperor, Destroy the Traitors Branch (Imperial Way path)
- Ministry of Commerce and Industry Branch
- The Imperial Defense Plan Branch

**Only when No Compromise, No Surrender DLC is DISABLED:**
- The Unthinkable Option Branch (Communist path)
- Strengthen the Civilian Government Branch (Democratic path)
- Support the Kodoha Faction Branch (Non-Aligned military path)

Communist path establishing a communist state in Japan. Civil war erupts; victory leads to loss of navy, colonies (Korea and Mengjiang), and most generals to Manchukuo independence.
