## Summary

Joint and shared national focus branches are cooperative focus trees available to specific groups of countries in multiplayer or coordinated gameplay. Joint branches are usually unlocked by completing prerequisite focuses in one or more countries' main trees, while shared branches provide identical focus chains to multiple nations. This document covers all 13 multi-country focus groups; for generic and country-specific trees, see [National Focus and Continuous Focus](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md).

## Austro-Hungarian Joint National Focus (Götterdämmerung)

**Available to:** Austria, Czechoslovakia, Hungary, Albania, Bosnia, Croatia, Herzegovina, Lombardy-Venetia, Montenegro, Silesia, Slovenia, Slovakia, Transylvania.
**Prerequisite:** Götterdämmerung DLC; Austria completes "The Danubian Federation" or "Demand Hungarian Submission" OR Hungary completes "The Lands of the Crown of Saint Stephen" or "A Danubian Federation".

### Indivisible and Inseparable Branch

Joint focuses for a federated Austria-Hungary. Complex structure with originator (the nation completing the prerequisite) receiving superior bonuses; other contingent states receive baseline bonuses. The branch automatically completes for faction members not actively taking it.

Key patterns:
- Originators receive political power, national spirits with morale/organization bonuses, war support gains
- All members gain political power, stability, or specific capability bonuses (e.g., "improve the Danube crossing")
- Multiple conditional branches based on which country is originator
- Typically grants claims/wargoals on neighboring states (Romania, Yugoslavia, Poland depending on context)
- Creates templates and units for Austro-Hungarian forces
- Late-game focuses may grant research bonuses or specific national spirits (e.g., "Renewed Austro-Hungarian Greatness" with attack/defense on core territory)

**Note:** This branch has 46 focuses organized into an intricate tree with multiple paths; refer to the wiki page for exact prerequisite chains and mutually exclusive choices. Strategic focuses revolve around reintegrating the former Austro-Hungarian empire and managing relationships with contingent states.

## Notes on Focus Mechanics

- **Joint vs. Shared:** Joint focuses involve multiple countries activating the same focus in sequence; shared focuses are identical branches available to multiple nations independently.
- **Originator Bonus:** In joint branches, the nation who first completes a prerequisite and activates the branch (the "originator") typically receives superior bonuses compared to other eligible nations.
- **Automatic Completion:** Some joint branches auto-complete for faction members who do not actively pursue them.
- **Prerequisite Trees:** Many joint branches require completing prerequisite focuses in individual country trees before the joint branch becomes available.
- **Faction Requirements:** Joint branches frequently require the participating nations to be in the same faction.

All exact modifiers, duration costs, and conditional effects are drawn from the wiki; consult individual country pages for integration with their main focus trees.
