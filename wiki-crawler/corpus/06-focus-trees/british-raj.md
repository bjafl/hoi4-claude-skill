## Summary

British Raj has unique national focus trees introduced in Together for Victory (1936–1939 era) and substantially overhauled in Graveyard of Empires (1948 onwards). The base game version emphasizes independence gradualisms and early war participation; the modern version adds complex branching into the Mughal restoration, East India Company corporation, and diverse ideological paths (Gandhian, Bose/Netaji, Hindutva, Communist). Both trees unlock political, military, industrial, and naval branches. See [British Raj](../05-countries/british-raj.md) for the country's 1936 starting profile.

## Base Game Focus Tree (Together for Victory, pre-Graveyard of Empires)

The base game tree has 3 main branches: Provincial Elections (political independence path), Lessons of the Great War (army/air), and Great Indian Peninsula Railway (industry/navy). All focuses are available without additional DLC beyond Together for Victory.

## Modern Focus Tree (Graveyard of Empires 1948+)

Requires the Graveyard of Empires DLC. Substantially expands the tree into 9 major branches with 21 sub-branches: Great Depression Price Controls (Mughal/East India Company), Onto the Road of Independence (Gandhian/Bose/Communist), His Majesty's Loyal Government (Commonwealth loyalty), Keep British Generals/Indianize Army (military command), railways, ammunition factories, artillery, recruitment, air force, and navy.

### Complexity note

This version is approximately 3× the size of the base game tree. Below is a condensed overview; full details are on the wiki. The tree's structure:

1. **Great Depression Price Controls** → Mughal Empire (war vs Britain, Timurid restoration) OR East India Company (corporation mechanics)
2. **Onto the Road of Independence** → Mahatma (Gandhian, split India) → Tryst with Destiny (neutrality or Allies) OR League Against Gandhism (Bose, Hindutva, communism)
3. **His Majesty's Loyal Government** → 3 parallel paths: Politics (choose Gandhi/Nehru/Churchill), Industry, Military
4. **Keep British Generals/Indianize Army** → Choose loyalty or independence
5. **Railways, Ammunition, Artillery, Recruitment, Air, Navy** → Parallel industrial/military branches

### Great Depression Price Controls Branch

Opening focus for two mutually exclusive paths: restore the Mughal Empire (Look to the Past) or transform into the East India Company (Look to the Future).

| Focus | Prerequisites | Key Effects |
|---|---|---|
| **Great Depression Price Controls** | Mutually exclusive with Onto the Road of Independence, His Majesty's Loyal Government | If has Great Depression or Recovering: replace with Depression Price Controls spirit (−15% consumer goods, −2.5% stability); +20 Political Power |
| **Look to the Past** | Great Depression Price Controls; mutually exclusive with Look to the Future | +5% non-aligned support; +5% stability; +5% war support; +80 Political Power |
| **Look to the Future** | Great Depression Price Controls; mutually exclusive with Look to the Past | Switch to East India Company tag; corporation mechanics active |

### East India Company Path (Look to the Future)

East India Company is a corporation tag allowing purchase and coring of land.

| Focus | Prerequisites | Key Effects |
|---|---|---|
| **Form A Security Force Sub-branch** | Look to the Future | Army/navy build-up, industrial bonuses, war goals on India's neighbors |
| **Vertical Integration Sub-branch** | Look to the Future | Tax and tax bracket focuses, war goals on communist powers and UK |
| **Trade Port Sub-branch** | Look to the Future | Industry and defenses in Aden (992) |

### Mughal Path (Look to the Past)

Restore Mughal dominance in the subcontinent; requires securing weapons, triggering uprising, and consolidating empire.

| Focus | Prerequisites | Key Effects |
|---|---|---|
| **Secure Weapons** | Look to the Past | Gain equipment subsidy (if Arms Against Tyranny DLC: −1000 construction cost bonuses; else: 4K Soviet infantry equipment, 800 Soviet artillery, plus tech based on No Step Back availability) |
| **Stir Unrest in the North** | Look to the Past | Trigger anti-British sentiment |
| **The Mughal Uprising** | Secure Weapons AND Stir Unrest in the North | Leave faction if in UK; remove Depression Price Controls; gain Mughal tech and equipment; transform into Mughal empire; gain Agrarian Society, Marginalized Muslim Community (Angry), Princely States, Risk of Famine spirits; UK becomes colony; core all starting Indian states |
| **Sharpen the Tulwar** | The Mughal Uprising | Military training and morale bonuses |
| **Elephants for the Modern Age** | Sharpen the Tulwar | Add Elephantry technology; create Gajhastas units; +70 army experience |

### Onto the Road of Independence Branch

Main path to independence with three broad choices: Mahatma (Gandhi), League Against Gandhism (anti-Gandhi, Bose/Hindutva/communism), or bypass via civil war.

#### Mahatma Sub-branch (Gandhian Independence)

Peaceful independence through British negotiation.

| Focus | Prerequisites | Key Effects |
|---|---|---|
| **Mahatma** | Onto the Road of Independence | Unlock Gandh leadership and peaceful independence path |
| **Tryst with Destiny** | Mahatma | Choose between: Join Allies, remain neutral, or compromise; splits Pakistan/Bangladesh or keeps united; consolidate new state, annex Goa and French India; +research/economic bonuses |
| **I Am Death** | Mahatma; Gandhi survives assassination | Gandhi leads India; +nuclear research bonuses, nuclear-powered army bonuses; democracy path |

#### League Against Gandhism Sub-branch (Subhas Chandra Bose, Hindutva, Communist Paths)

Anti-Gandhi expansion paths.

| Focus | Prerequisites | Key Effects |
|---|---|---|
| **Forward Bloc** | League Against Gandhism | Violent overthrow; infiltrate Aden; white peace with UK; open Netaji/Hindutva sub-branches; option to join Japan |
| **Hindutva Sub-branch** | Forward Bloc | Fascist expansion; form Akhand Bharat (unified Hindu nation); economic and army bonuses |
| **Netaji Sub-branch** | Forward Bloc | Keep Bose as leader; mountain bonuses; choice between authoritarianism or guided democracy; cooperate with USSR (→ Communist) or Axis; war goals on China, Nepal, Bhutan |
| **Coal, Fire and Steel** | Hindutva/Netaji paths | Industrial bonuses (shared branch) |
| **Rani Of Jhansi Regiment** | Various paths | Recruit all-women regiment; +recruitable population; gain female General |
| **Reds in the Shadows** | League Against Gandhism | Communist path after civil war; choose Agrarian Socialism (rural) or Urban Industrialism (urban/Soviet); research sharing with USSR; war goals on imperialist powers |
| **The Five Year Plan** | Reds in the Shadows | Agrarian: peasant armies, production, compliance; Urban: Soviet alignment, liberation army, military factory construction; continue focus multiplies by 30% |
| **The Red Curtain Falls Over Asia** | Reds in the Shadows | Communist expansion; war goals on Pakistan, Burma, Japan |

### His Majesty's Loyal Government Branch

Path for remaining in British Commonwealth; requires completing all 3 sub-branches to gain independence.

| Focus | Prerequisites | Key Effects |
|---|---|---|
| **His Majesty's Loyal Government** | Main branch | Unlock political, industrial, and military loyalty paths |
| **Curtail the Zamindars/Expand the Zamindari System** | His Majesty's Loyal Government | Industrial expansion and famine reduction (sub-branch choice) |
| **Indian Gentleman Officers** | His Majesty's Loyal Government | Military expansion, war with UK enemies, defenses in Aden, possible annexation of British colonies, terrain specialties |

### Keep British Generals / Indianize The Army Branch

Choice between relying on British generals (and receiving equipment/support) or Indianizing (choosing from other major powers' models).

### Railways, Ammunition, Artillery, Recruitment, Air Force, Navy Branches

Parallel industrial and military branches providing factories, dockyards, air bases, recruitment, and research bonuses.

| Branch | Key Purposes |
|---|---|
| **East India Railways / Bombay Baroda and Central India Railway** | Build railways; faster supply hub, railway, infrastructure construction |
| **Ammunition Factory Khadki** | Military factories; unlock MIO traits; upgraded MIO policy |
| **Royal Indian Artillery / Military Pensions** | Upgrade artillery/armor; mountain warfare bonuses; quinine/famine relief |
| **Local Recruitment Offices** | Manpower; reduce Agrarian Society spirit |
| **Indian Air Force** | Aircraft research, air experience, MIO unlocks, paratroopers, unique aircraft |
| **Royal Indian Navy** | Dockyards, naval bases, naval research, naval experience, MIO unlocks, unique ships; choice between British reliance or independence |
