## Summary

Spain starts the game mid-civil-war (1936) with two mutually exclusive national focus trees: **Nationalist Spain** and **Republican Spain**. Both require La Résistance DLC; without it, Spain falls back to the generic tree. The two trees diverge immediately from the 1936 start and lead to entirely different post-civil-war development paths. See [Spain](../05-countries/spain.md) for the country's 1936 starting profile. For generic and continuous focuses available to all nations, see [National Focus and Continuous Focus](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md).

## Nationalist Spain

**Mutually exclusive with:** Republican Spanish national focus tree.  
**Requires:** La Résistance DLC.

The Nationalist Spanish focus tree features three main pre-civil-war political branches (Falangist, Carlist, and Unity-focused), shared military branches, and post-civil-war development paths. Nationalist focuses take **28–35 days** (faster than Republican focuses). The tree culminates in choices between the Falangist, Carlist, or Monarchist post-war state.

## Key Mechanical Notes

1. **Mutual Exclusivity:** Nationalist and Republican trees are completely mutually exclusive; a player picks one at game start based on which focus they take first (A Great Spain vs The Popular Front). Switching is not possible without losing the existing progress.

2. **Civil War Timing:** The Spanish Civil War does not begin automatically. It is triggered when either side completes enough focuses to reach a "civil war declaration" point or when specific focuses are taken. Pre-civil-war focuses can be extended indefinitely to gain more preparation time.

3. **Faction Wars:** Both sides can develop internal faction conflicts:
   - **Nationalist:** Taking the Falangist path forces Carlists to declare war (creating a three-way conflict).
   - **Republican:** Taking the Anarchist path can create third-front conflicts with Communists; taking Communist path with Soviet support creates Anarchist revolts.

4. **Post-Civil-War Paths:** Once the Spanish Civil War ends, post-civil-war sub-branches open up, allowing further ideological/governmental choices (Monarchist, Falangist, Carlist, Soviet Communist, Independent Communist, Democratic, etc.).

5. **Focus Durations:** 
   - **Nationalist focuses:** 28–35 days (faster pre-civil-war preparation).
   - **Republican focuses:** 70 days (slower preparation, offset by war support/division bonuses).

6. **DLC Requirement:** Both trees require La Résistance. Without it, Spain uses the Generic national focus tree.

7. **Foreign Volunteers:** Multiple focuses unlock foreign volunteer bonuses:
   - Germany/Italy support Nationalist Spain.
   - Soviet Union, France, Mexico support Republican Spain.
   - Volunteers provide additional manpower and combat bonuses during the civil war.
