## Summary

Finland has a unique national focus tree with six main branches: Suomalainen Sosialismi (socialist government), Finnish Neutrality (historical non-aligned path), Right-Wing Policies (fascist path), Enhance Southern Infrastructure (industrial bonuses), Increase Military Budget (military focuses), and Form the Nordic Council (joint Nordic faction branch). Most alternate-history branches (socialist and fascist paths) require Arms Against Tyranny DLC. See [Finland](../05-countries/finland.md) for the country's 1936 starting profile. See [National Focus and Continuous Focus](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md) for generic/continuous focuses.

Available without DLC; provides research bonuses and military advisor selections.

| **Suomen Ilmavoimat** | Increase Military Budget | Air experience +5.0%; Carrier damage −10%; Finnish Air Force national spirit: fighter efficiency +5%, naval bomber efficiency +5% |
| **Suomen Merivoimat** | Increase Military Budget | Navy experience +5.0%; Naval invasion terrain penalty −25%; Finnish Navy national spirit: naval attack +10%, naval efficiency +2% |
| **National Armor Focus** | Increase Military Budget | Heavy tank research time −20%; medium tank research time −20%; Armor Experience +15 (hidden focus; requires completing Militarized Society to become visible) |

## DLC branch: Suomalainen Sosialismi (requires Arms Against Tyranny)

Socialist path: choose between communist (with Soviet alignment or antagonism) or democratic socialism. Mutually exclusive with Finnish Neutrality and Right-Wing Policies at the start.

## Form the Nordic Council Branch (requires Arms Against Tyranny; Joint Nordic branch)

Available to all Nordic nations (Finland, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Iceland) when at least two Nordic nations have a faction together. Allows any Nordic faction member to complete these focuses.

| Focus | Prerequisites | Effects |
|---|---|---|
| **Nordic Solidarity** | Nordic nations in same faction | Political Power +100 for all faction members; Nordic unity national spirit: faction members' opinion +50% to each other; military cooperation bonuses |
| **United Nordic Front** | Nordic Solidarity; at war or world tension >75% | Faction members gain +15% defense against all enemies; coordinated military strategy: army bonuses +5% to allied Nordic troops; can share reinforcements |
| **Nordic Defense League** | United Nordic Front; all Nordic nations >50% stability | Establishes coordinated defense: can station units in allied Nordic nations; joint fortification strategy possible |
| **Trade Bloc of the North** | Nordic Solidarity | Trade route multiplier +15% between Nordic nations; can share resources (wool, steel, oil if available); Nordic economic integration |

**Notes:**
- **DLC gating:** Most political (Suomalainen Sosialismi / Right-Wing Policies) and joint (Form the Nordic Council) branches require Arms Against Tyranny. The base-game Increase Military Budget and Enhance Southern Infrastructure branches are available without DLC.
- **Mutual exclusion:** The three core 1936 paths (Suomalainen Sosialismi, Finnish Neutrality, Right-Wing Policies) are mutually exclusive at the root; only one can be taken.
- **Hidden focuses:** Innovative Designs (requires No Step Back DLC) and The Finnish Throne (requires specific conditions) do not appear in the tree initially.
- **Civil war mechanics:** Both communist and fascist paths can trigger internal civil wars; during these, Finland's production and military are heavily penalized.
- **Joint Nordic Council:** The last branch is a shared focus tree that activates when Finland is in a faction with other Nordic nations (Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Iceland).
