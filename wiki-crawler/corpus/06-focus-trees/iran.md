## Summary

Iran has a unique national focus tree available with the Graveyard of Empires expansion. It centers on choosing between ideological paths—communist, democratic, or nationalist—and navigating regional influence, military modernization, and territorial expansion. Without the DLC, Iran uses the generic focus tree. See [01-mechanics/national-focus.md](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md) for generic/continuous focuses.

See [Iran](../05-countries/iran.md) for the country's 1936 starting profile.

Flag of Iran Iran gets a unique national focus tree with the Graveyard of EmpiresGraveyard of Empires expansion. Without the expansion, it utilizes the Generic national focus tree instead.

Iranian national focus tree. Clicking on the branches leads to the appropriate section.

The Iranian national focus tree can be divided into 7 branches 17 sub-branches and 1 joint branch:

**Establish a National Front Branch**
This begins the path to oppose the Shah and builds ideology against Monarchism.

- United Progressive Parties Sub-branch

This path sets Iran as a Communist nation through civil war. It may ally with the Flag of Soviet Union Soviet Union or create a Communist faction in the region.

Industrial Aid Sub-branch The Communist industry path gives research bonuses, OilOil resources as well as nuclear bonuses.

The New Economy Sub-branch This section of the tree gives military bonuses to a Communist Iran.

- International Solidarity Sub-branch

This shared sub-branch between the Communist and Democratic paths allow volunteers, unlocks decisions to launch uprisings in neighboring regions, force colonial powers to leave with the threat of war, and expansion opportunities in the middle east in order to build Iranian hegemony.

- Rally Behind Mosaddegh Sub-branch

The Democratic path either makes Iran into a constitutional monarchy or a republic. It will then be able to gain industry bonuses, membership in the Allies and annexation of Iraq and Afghanistan. the constitutional monarchy path, by giving the clergy power, may lead to a civil war and the overthrow of the government in order to create the Islamic Republic of Iran.

- Unite the Ultra-Nationalists Sub-branch

The Fascist path starts a civil war and reinstalls a new Shah. It may ally with the Flag of Germany Germans, the Flag of Japan Japanese or go it alone in order to conquer surrounding nations in order to form Flag of Greater Iran Greater Iran. It may then expand into the middle east and India as well as conquer Flag of Germany Germany

**The Fight for Iran Branch**
This branch is only available if Iran has gone into a defensive war. It gives military bonuses. It will then be split into various sections, with some parts being hidden.

- Force Them Back Sub-branch

This aggressive path gives attack bonuses to troops. Iran can then either force peace with war reparations or force a white peace, depending on the level of war participation and surrender progress.

- Dig in and Defend Sub-branch

This aggressive path gives defense bonuses to troops and Land fortsLand forts. Iran can then either surrender and become a puppet or negotiate for a white peace with resource rights, depending on the level of war participation and surrender progress.

Rebuilding Iran sub-branch If Iran becomes a puppet, this branch will then be visible. It either swears loyalty to its overlord and get bonuses with it, or it can plot for an overthrow of the overlord and declare independence.

Reinstate the Qajar Dynasty sub-branch If Iran becomes a puppet of the Flag of United Kingdom United Kingdom it may get this focus installing Mohammad Hassan Mizra of the Qajar Dynasty as Shah of Iran. This dynasty was the former shahs before the Pahlavi dynasty took power.

Retake North Iran/Consolidate British Territory Sub-branch These focuses are available from two countries. When Flag of United Kingdom United Kingdom finishes the Secure IranSecure Iran or the Flag of Soviet Union Soviet Union finishes the Preemptive Invasion of IranPreemptive Invasion of Iran focus to start the Operation Countenance event chain. There, Iran refuses Flag of United Kingdom British/Flag of Soviet Union Soviet pressure, then the Flag of United Kingdom United Kingdom/Flag of Soviet Union Soviet Union requests Flag of Soviet Union Soviet/Flag of United Kingdom British aid and the Flag of Soviet Union Soviet Union/Flag of United Kingdom United Kingdom accepts. It is then required for Iran to Swear Fealty to the InvadersSwear Fealty to the Invaders. The focuses then allow Iran to declare war against the Flag of Soviet Union Soviet Union to retake the lost northern territory and core the Flag of United Kingdom British states across the Persian Gulf.

**The Pahlavi Imperium Branch**
This starts the Monarchist branch with Political PowerPolitical Power

- To Stand With Giants Sub-branch

The historical branch brings positive relations with Flag of Germany Germany for trade and research bonuses. It can also ban communism and give Military factoryMilitary factory bonuses. It may also then remove the Anglo-Iranian Oil Company Anglo-Iranian Oil Company as well as declare war against the Flag of Soviet Union Soviet Union and releasing puppets in central Asia. The other side of the branch gives industry bonuses.

- Forced Secularization Sub-branch

This shared focus secularizes Iran. It affects other focuses in other sub-branches.

- A Legacy of Greatness Sub-branch

This sub-branch of the tree sets the Pahlavi dynasty as the center of the nation. The leftmost section assassinates Reza Shah Pahlavi and brings his son, Mohammad Reza Pahlavi as Shah. It may then create the Flag of Third Persian Empire Third Persian Empire. The rightmost path keeps Reza Shah Pahlavi as Shah and creates the Flag of First Iranian Empire First Iranian Empire

The Unification Initiative Sub-branch
This branch removes Azerbaijani Resistance Azerbaijani Resistance and Kurdish Resistance Kurdish Resistance as well as either set the country as an Islamic nation, a Secular nation or a Zoroastrian nation.

Imperial Expansionism Sub-branch
This path expands Iran's territory based on the conquests of the ancient Achaemenid Empire. After taking land from Flag of Afghanistan Afghanistan and Flag of Iraq Iraq, the branch splits into three. The leftmost path follows Xerxes the Great's war path towards Anatolia towards Flag of Turkey Turkey. It may also settle the historic grudge against Flag of Greece Greece/The Flag of Byzantine Empire Byzantine Empire. The center path follows Darius the Great's invasion towards India and declares war against Flag of Afghanistan Afghanistan and the Flag of British Raj British Raj. The rightmost path follows Cambyses II's conquest towards Egypt, with war against Flag of Iraq Iraq, and the owners of the land towards Egypt. All paths give the ability to integrate the conquered territory into the empire. The final focus makes the Shah as the successor to Cyrus the Great and cores additional territory.

**Modernizing Iran Branch**
The Industry branch can be divided into 4 main paths. The first path focuses on research bonuses. The second path builds Iran's railway network. The third path focuses on construction and infrastructure. The fourth path focuses on Civilian factoriesCivilian factories and OilOil.

**The Saadabad Pact Branch**
This joint branch shared with Flag of Afghanistan Afghanistan, Flag of Iraq Iraq and Flag of Turkey Turkey builds up the The Saadabad Pact The Saadabad Pact national spirit with industry an military bonuses.

**Restructure the Army Branch**
This branch builds up Iran's army technology and industry.

- Iranian Special Operations Sub-branch

This branch builds Iran's special forces, intelligence agency and special projects.

**Establish the Air Force Branch**
This branch builds Iran's air force as well as modifying The Iranian Air force The Iranian Air force.

**Establish the Navy Branch**
This branch builds Iran's naval technology, industry and doctrines.
