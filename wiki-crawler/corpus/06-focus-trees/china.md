## Summary

China has two unique national focus trees depending on available DLC. The base-game tree (introduced in Waking the Tiger) focuses on internal political consolidation and economic development, with 4 branches covering political principles, military affairs, industrial planning, and foreign investment. The modern tree (overhauled in No Compromise, No Surrender) adds 7 branches with complex political choices, military doctrines, and unification paths. See [China](../05-countries/china.md) for the country's 1936 starting profile.

For generic and continuous focus mechanics, see [National Focus and Continuous Focus](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md).
