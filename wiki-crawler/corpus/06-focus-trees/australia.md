## Summary

Australia has a unique focus tree introduced in Together for Victory, substantially overhauled in Thunder at Our Gates. The tree divides into seven major branches: army and special forces training, air force expansion, naval construction, industrial development, domestic politics (recovery from Great Depression), Commonwealth alignment, and independence—with the last branch offering fascist, communist, or democratic alternatives. Most branches focus on research bonuses and equipment production; later game paths are locked by ideology and autonomy level, creating distinct diplomatic endgames (Commonwealth loyalty, isolation/neutrality, alignment with the US, Japanese alliance, or communist revolution).

See [Australia](../05-countries/australia.md) for the country's 1936 starting profile.

Note: The base-game version (Together for Victory) is still available if Thunder at Our Gates is disabled. For generic and continuous focuses available to all nations, see [National Focus and Continuous Focus](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md).

## Strategic Notes

**Early Game (1936-1938):**
- All three army/air/navy/industrial branches unlock research bonuses and infrastructure without prerequisites; pick based on tech priorities.
- Early political (Establish Advisory War Council) and defense (Volunteer Defence Corps) focuses offer stability and modifiers to ease Great Depression penalties.
- Ideological commitment (Support Policy of Appeasement vs. Never Another Gallipoli) is not locked early but becomes strongly path-dependent after the autonomy/ideology gates are crossed.

**Mid Game (1938-1940):**
- Complete industrial and domestic branches to maximize factory/dockyard output and remove Depression penalties.
- If remaining Commonwealth-aligned: Adopt Westminster and CSIR unlock faction technology sharing bonuses (3–5% research speed).
- If pursuing independence: Never Another Gallipoli and Sever Ties With UK shift autonomy dramatically; requires careful timing to avoid being locked into a faction before independence is ready.

**Late Game (1940+):**
- Ideological branches (fascist via Centre Party, communist via Empower the Workers, democratic isolation via Woo USA/South-West Pacific Initiative) determine war goals and faction alignment.
- Joint Indonesia/Netherlands support or Japanese alliance (fascist path) can rapidly shift regional control.
- Communist path grants Soviet/Chinese tech sharing if comintern joins; democratic path retains US if in faction.
- War goals (Topple Government, Puppet, Annex) unlock only near end of ideological chains, so total war commitment usually comes in 1941–1942.

**DLC Differences:**
- **By Blood Alone:** Modified fighter/bomber tree (Light Fighter focus, variant creation replaced some AoT reductions).
- **Arms Against Tyranny:** Special Forces Doctrine cost reductions and branch specialisms added to several focuses.
- **La Résistance:** Central Bureau national spirit (Decryption bonus) added to Coordinate Central Bureau.
- **Thunder at Our Gates:** Complete overhaul of focus tree structure and naming; together-for-victory version available only if DLC disabled.
