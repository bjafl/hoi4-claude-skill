## Summary

Lithuania is a minor non-aligned Oligarchic state in the Baltic region with a population of 2.20 million. It begins under the authoritarian rule of Antanas Smetona and the Lietuvių Tautininkų Sąjunga (LTS), with historically limited industry and military capacity. The country features unique mechanics tied to the fascist Iron Wolf movement and possesses a specialized national focus tree (requires No Step Back DLC) offering paths to communism, democracy, fascism, or monarchy.

## Government and political situation

**Ruling ideology:** Non-aligned Oligarchy under Antanas Smetona (Lietuvių Tautininkų Sąjunga, LTS), holding 40% popularity. The ruling party imposes significant diplomatic penalties: forced government conversion blocked, puppeting blocked, and all tension limits reduced by 40% (generate war goal, lend-lease, send volunteers, guarantee, join faction).

**Government cohesion:** Unstable. Smetona rules with less than half the government, opposed by:
- Democratic faction (Lietuvos Krikščionių Demokratų Partija, 30%, leader Kazys Grinius)
- Communist faction (Lietuvos Komunistų Partija, 15%, leader Antanas Sniečkus)
- Fascist faction (Geležinis Vilkas / Iron Wolf, 15%, leader Augustinas Voldemaras)

Alternative leaders accessible via focus tree include the military reformer Stasys Raštikis (Non-aligned Oligarchy), monarchist Mindaugas III (Despotism, House von Urach), and ideological leaders (Kazys Grinius for Democracy/Socialism, Antanas Sniečkus for Communism/Stalinism, Augustinas Voldemaras for Fascism).

## Unique mechanics

Lithuania possesses a specialized national focus tree (requires No Step Back DLC) with five main branches:

1. **Rejoin the Railways Branch** — Shared Baltic industrial/military modernization requiring investment from a Fascist or Democratic major power.

2. **Restore the Worker's Republic Branch** — Shared Baltic path allowing conversion to Communism with options to become Soviet puppet or remain independent, plus expansion to other Baltic states and Scandinavia.

3. **Reconvene the Seimas Branch** — Releases Augustinas Voldemaras, enables Iron Wolf paramilitary organization, fascist conversion, and potential formation of Greater Lithuania with war goals against neighboring states.

4. **Lithuanian Preservationism Sub-branch** — Non-aligned option modifying the Seimas and offering a Military Dictatorship government type.

5. **A King for Our People Sub-branch** — Monarchist path enthroning Karl Gero von Urach as Mindaugas III, establishing a Personal Union with Poland and potentially creating the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth or Kingdom of Livonia.

Additional shared sub-branches:
- **Formalize the Baltic Entente / Beyond the Baltic** — Alliance with Latvia and Estonia with manpower/industry bonuses; option to unify all three Baltic states or ally with UK/other democracies.
- **Integrate the Opposition / Rastiki's Military Reforms** — No ideology requirement; enables Democratic conversion and political advisors; improves/damages relations with Poland, Germany, or USSR.
- **Prepare for the Inevitable / Appease the Russians / Appease the Germans** — Post-capitulation rebuild or puppet submission to major powers.

> ⚠️ Focus tree details are summarized; see [06-focus-trees/lithuania.md](../06-focus-trees/lithuania.md) for the complete tree structure.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Land borders:** Latvia (north), Poland (south), Germany (southwest).

**Maritime:** Coastline on the Baltic Sea with two Level 1 naval bases (subject to German seizure of Memel).

**Starting relations:**
- Latvia: +20 (shared Non-aligned ideology and ruling party)
- Poland: -10 (Poland owns the state of Vilnius, a core of Lithuania)
- Other countries: -10 (different ideology) or +10 (same ideology)

**Starting alliances:** None. Lithuania may join the Międzymorze if created by Poland.

**Stability note:** Poland's occupation of Vilnius core territory creates a permanent source of friction and a potential casus belli for Lithuania once militarily capable.
