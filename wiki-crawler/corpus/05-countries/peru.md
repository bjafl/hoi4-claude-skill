## Summary

Peru is a minor Fascist nation in South America with a population of 6.21M and fair army/navy capacity. It controls five states (Arequipa, Lima, Loreto, Tacna-Moquegua, Ucayali) with limited heavy resources but significant oil reserves. Peru begins guaranteed by the United States under the Monroe Doctrine and uses the generic national focus tree, making it a straightforward expansion candidate for regional conquest.

## Government and political situation

Peru begins as Fascist under **Óscar Benavides** (Constituent Assembly), who holds 51% party popularity. The largest opposition is the Democratic Party (Sebastián Lynch) at 49%, with Communist (Hilarión Lynch) and Non-Aligned (Santiago Ruíz-Tagle) parties at 0%. Elections occur every 8 years, with the next scheduled for October 1939.

## Unique mechanics

Peru lacks a unique national focus tree and unique cosmetic equipment names. It uses the **generic national focus tree** with five main branches: Army Effort, Aviation Effort, Naval Effort, Industrial Effort, and Political Effort. Research slots can be unlocked from 2 to 5 through focus completion.

No unique decisions or country-specific events are documented on the wiki.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering countries:** Ecuador and Colombia (north); Brazil (east); Bolivia and Chile (southeast).

**Diplomatic status:**
- **Independence guaranteed by:** United States (Monroe Doctrine national spirit). This guarantee remains active as long as Peru is not at war with the US.
- **Faction:** None at game start.
- **Alliances:** None at game start.

Peru can pursue fascist expansion within South America, particularly against Ecuador and Bolivia, without triggering US opposition through the guarantee (which only prevents external conquest, not regional war). The focus tree allows transition to democracy or communism but at significant political cost.
