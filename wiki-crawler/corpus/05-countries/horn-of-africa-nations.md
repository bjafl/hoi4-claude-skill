The Horn of Africa nations are eight releasable countries comprising Ethiopia's territorial core. Released via Occupied territories tab (except by Ethiopia with By Blood Alone DLC enabled) or from game start with the "Ethiopia Fragmented" Custom Game Rule, these nations can form the Horn of Africa formable nation through their unique focus tree. The Sultanate of Aussa alone starts independent in 1936; the remainder begin as non-aligned unplayable releasables. All possess shared access to the Horn of Africa national focus tree (requires By Blood Alone).

## Release conditions

**For other nations:** Released via Occupied territories tab like any releasable country (cannot be released by Ethiopia if By Blood Alone DLC is enabled). Released automatically from game start if "Ethiopia Fragmented" Custom Game Rule is enabled.

**Sultanate of Aussa:** Starts as independent country in 1936 and normally annexed by Italy early in historical playthroughs via event. Can also be released as releasable.

## Aussa

**Tag:** AFA  
**Capital:** Aussa  
**Starting ideology:** Non-Aligned  
**Unique trait:** Only Horn of Africa nation starting as independent. Also holds a core on Djibouti. When "Ethiopia Fragmented" rule is enabled, Aussa gains the only Ethiopian core within the custom game rule set.

**Government:** Non-Aligned with no elections.

**Political parties (1936):**
- Non-Aligned (75% — ruling): The Kandhafo Dynasty, led by Mohamed Yayyo
- Fascist (20%): Danakil Unitary Party
- Communist (5%): Afar Revolutionary Democratic Unitary Front
- Democratic (0%): Afar National Democratic Party

**Leader:** Mohamed Yayyo (Non-Aligned, Despotism): +5% factory output, +10% consumer goods factories, +10% political power gain.

**When released/puppeted:** Known as Afar Autonomous Region under Ethiopia (Non-Aligned), or Aussa Governorate under Italy (Fascist).

## Formation and mechanics

**Horn of Africa formable nation:** All Horn of Africa nations can form the Horn of Africa formable nation via decision (requires By Blood Alone).

**Shared focus tree (requires By Blood Alone):** Three primary branches:
- **Develop Capital State:** Nation development with bonuses to naval or air forces, and resources dependent on nation
- **The Armed Forces:** Improves irregular infantry and army bonuses; nation-dependent national spirits for combat ability, factory output, or research speed
- **The Nation:** Split into puppet and independent paths. Puppet path grants research bonuses and research slot. Independent path allows field marshal creation, war goals on other Horn nations, and Horn of Africa formation, or foreign alliances and volunteer sending

**National spirits:** Only Aussa starts with a national spirit. Shared focus tree can grant additional spirits depending on nation and ideological path.
