Chile is a minor democratic South American nation in 1936, politically unstable with access to multiple ideological paths. The country occupies a long, narrow strip between the Andes and the Pacific, heavily dependent on raw material exports. Its narrow geography and vulnerable military make it easy prey, but the Monroe Doctrine guarantees provide initial protection. Trial of Allegiance DLC enables a unique focus tree with multiple branches.

## Government and political situation

Chile begins in Liberal Democracy under **Arturo Alessandri Palma** (Partido Liberal), with 98% party popularity. Alessandri holds the trait *León de Tarapacá*, boosting stability (+5%), political power gain (+10%), and ideology drift defense (+5%).

Political rivals include the Partido Comunista de Chile (2% popularity, Carlos Contreras Labarca), Vanguardia Popular Socialista (0%, Jorge González von Marées), and Partido Ibanista (0%, Carlos Ibáñez del Campo). Alessandri's focus tree path *Endorse the Constitution of 1925* reinforces democracy via the 1938 presidential election, but competing paths enable non-aligned (Ibáñez coup), monarchy, Mapuche indigenous rule, or communist/fascist ideologies. The tree permits alliance shifts toward the Allies or Communist bloc.

Ministers and political advisors (Trial of Allegiance) include Pedro Aguirre Cerda (industrialization), Miguel Cruchaga Tocornal (senate), Carlos Ibáñez del Campo (non-aligned), and Jorge González von Marées (fascist leadership).

## Unique mechanics

**Trial of Allegiance focus tree** (requires DLC; generic tree without): Four primary branches plus industrial sub-branches and military shared focuses.

- *Look to Tradition* → Non-Aligned ideology, installs Carlos Ibáñez del Campo. Sub-paths enable monarchy (Antoine III as King of Araucanía and Patagonia), Mapuche indigenous rule with civil war, or alliance with France/invasion options.
- *Endorse the Constitution of 1925* → Democratic reinforcement via the 1938 election. Paths include increased presidential powers, realignment with Allies or Communists, request Pacific islands, or war with Argentina.
- *Dissolve COVENSA/Public Works Projects* → Industrial and resource-extraction focuses split into trade, construction, and currency devaluation sub-branches.
- *Denounce the State of Near Anarchy* → Fascist coup path. Sub-paths align with Germany (Operation Bolivar), other Axis members (Spain, Portugal), or form Pan-Hispanic faction alternative.
- *Trial of Allegiance Shared Military* → Special national spirits and military bonuses based on chosen ideological path.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering countries:** Peru (north), Bolivia (northeast), Argentina (east).

**Diplomatic status:** Protected by Monroe Doctrine guarantee from the United States (+35 opinion). As a democracy, Chile has:
- +10 opinion toward same ideology (other democracies)
- +20 opinion toward same ruling party
- -10 opinion toward Communists, Fascists, and Non-Aligned nations

**Factions:** None at start (can join Allies, Axis, or Communist International via focus tree).

**Key alliance options:** Can guarantee ideological allies, but cannot puppet or create collaboration governments in base democracy. Multiple focus paths enable faction alignment depending on ideological shift.
