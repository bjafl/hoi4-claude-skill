This page lists the 25 releasable minor countries and entities in Asia (excluding Indian and Indonesian Princely States, which have dedicated pages). Most can be released as puppets by their core owners; many are also released at game start if the "Asia Decolonized" Custom Game Rule is enabled. Release mechanics and faction shifts vary widely: some require specific focus tree completions, others are released automatically via decolonization paths.

## Ainu Republic

**Tag:** ANU | **Capital:** Sapporo | **Starting ideology:** Democratic | **Cores in:** Japan, Soviet Union

Shares cores with Ezo Republic and Far Eastern Republic. Elections every 4 years (next: January 1940). Political parties: Democratic (60%, ruling), Communist (25%), Fascist (3%), Non-Aligned (2%).

## Assyria

**Tag:** ASY | **Capital:** Mosul | **Starting ideology:** Non-Aligned | **Cores in:** Iraq

Can form Neo-Assyrian Empire and Mesopotamia. Elections every 4 years (next: January 1940). Political parties: Assyrian Church of the East / ACOE (50%, ruling, led by Mar Eshai Shimun XXIII), Assyrian Democratic Movement / Zowaa (30%), Assyrian Socialist Party / ASP (20%, led by Benjamin Arsanis), Babylon Movement / BM (0%).
