Iceland is a small island nation in the North Atlantic, geographically isolated but politically tied to Denmark as a dominion. It starts with minimal industrial capacity, no standing military, and limited resources. The country features a unique focus tree (via Arms Against Tyranny) allowing paths to independence across multiple ideologies, or cooperation with Denmark.

## Government and political situation

Iceland begins as a **democracy** under the Framsóknarflokkurinn (Liberal) party, led by Hermann Jónasson. Elections occur every 3 years; the next is scheduled for October 1936. Hermann Jónasson carries the traits Hot Tempered and Reformer, providing stability penalties but political power and democracy support bonuses.

## Unique mechanics

Iceland has a full unique focus tree (requires Arms Against Tyranny), divided into six branches:

- **Expand the Fishing Industry / Mineral Prospecting:** Industrial and resource bonuses.
- **International Trade:** Allows shifting from Isolation economy law to Civilian Economy, gaining Free Trade, off-map factories, and trade relations with major powers; improves intelligence agency.
- **The Kingdom of Iceland:** Democratic/non-aligned path. Offers choices between keeping King Christian X, inviting Prince Knud I as a co-equal partner, or becoming a republic. The rightmost path permits independence when Denmark is attacked.
- **Not Our King:** Communist and fascist paths to independence. Communists can foment uprisings in the UK (Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland). Fascists unlock the Ásatrú national spirit, which gains upgrades via decisions.
- **The Armed Forces of Iceland:** Military doctrine and experience bonuses.
- **Form the Nordic Council:** Joint branch for Nordic nations (Iceland, Denmark, Finland, Norway, Sweden). Any Nordic faction member can complete any focus once one creates a faction with another.

Iceland's research begins with 2 slots; the focus tree can unlock 2 more. Without Arms Against Tyranny, Iceland uses the generic focus tree.

Key decision available to fascist Iceland: purchasing the Ásatrú spirit through decisions for incremental buffs.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Land neighbours:** None (island nation). Greenland (Denmark's territory) lies nearby.

**Diplomatic:**
- Iceland is a **dominion of Denmark**. Denmark carries responsibility for Iceland's defence and foreign affairs under the 1918 Act of Union (valid until 1943 in-game).
- If Denmark is attacked or defeated, Iceland can declare independence via focus "Break the Crown" or take the democratic path ("Break with Monarchy") to assert control of defence and foreign affairs.
- The focus tree offers options to become a puppet state of the United States or United Kingdom temporarily (Democratic/non-aligned paths) or to join the Axis (Fascist path).
- Historical note: The UK conducted Operation Fork (occupation) in May 1940 in the historical timeline. The US provided occupation forces from 1941. The 1943 union expiration trigger may provide special mechanics.
