## Summary

Colombia is a minor South American nation in a favorable geographical position bordering both the Caribbean Sea and Pacific Ocean. It begins as a democracy under the Liberal Party with limited military and industrial capacity but significant oil reserves. The country is guaranteed independence by the United States via the Monroe Doctrine, constraining regional expansion but providing diplomatic security.

## Government and political situation

Colombia is ruled by the **Partido Liberal Colombiano (PLC)** with 99% popularity, led by **Alfonso López Pumarejo** in 1936. (Eduardo Santo assumes leadership in 1939.) Elections occur every four years with the next scheduled for February 1938.

Opposition parties exist but hold minimal support: **Partido Comunista Colombiano (PCC)** 1%, Conservador 0%, Fascist 0%. The Liberal party aligns with democratic ideology; shifting to communism or fascism requires significant political effort via national focus or appointed advisors. Switching to fascism avoids civil war if done via "population reforms" around the 1938 election; communism yields the alternate name "Colombian Socialist Republic," while fascism becomes "Nueva Gran Colombia" (referencing the historical Gran Colombia period when Venezuela and Ecuador were included).

## Geography and terrain

Colombia occupies a mountainous region with jungle provinces and difficult terrain causing high attrition. The Andes mountains run through Cundinamarca; most provinces feature jungle or mountain terrain except the La Guajira Desert in the north (represented as forest in-game) and plains in portions of La Libertad and Meta. The capital Bogota sits in mountainous Cundinamarca.

Major rivers: Cauca and Magdalena flow north into the Caribbean (La Libertad); Meta flows northeast to Orinoco; Amazon Basin rivers flow east to Brazil.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Land borders:** Venezuela (northeast), Panama (northwest), Ecuador (southeast), Peru (south), Brazil (east/southeast)

**Sea borders:** Caribbean Sea (north), Western Canal Zone (Pacific, west; controlled by United States)

**Diplomatic status:** Colombia's independence is guaranteed by the United States under the Monroe Doctrine. All neighboring countries (Panama, Ecuador, Peru, Venezuela) are similarly guaranteed. However, the US guarantee applies primarily to European intervention in the Americas; war between Latin American nations is not automatically prevented.

**Regional context:** Panama (Democratic) is directly northwest, split by the strategically important Panama Canal held by the United States with strong defensive forces. Venezuela (Fascist) lies to the northeast. Ecuador (Democratic) and Peru (Democratic but with rising Fascist influence in 1936, ~51% Fascist party popularity) are southern neighbors. Brazil (Non-Aligned) borders to the east. The British, Dutch, and French colonies in the Caribbean and Guiana region are nearby but not guaranteed by the US, making them potential targets if exploited during major-power conflicts.

## Military overview

**Army:** 6 infantry brigades (Brigada del Ejército type), each with 3 infantry battalions, 1 cavalry battalion, and engineer support. Total manpower: ~25.8K.

**Navy:** 2 destroyers of the Antioquia-class (Early Destroyer hull type, Light II engine); 15 convoys for trade supply. Total manpower: 500.

**Air Force:** 30 P-6 fighter aircraft (without By Blood Alone DLC; Inter-War Fighter if that DLC is active).

**Doctrine:** Uses generic doctrines (Grand Battleplan, Fleet in Being, Strategic Destruction).
