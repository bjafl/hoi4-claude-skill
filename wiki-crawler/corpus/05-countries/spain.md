Spain in 1936 is a young democratic republic convulsed by internal ideological division: the government is Democratic but faces pressure from Fascist, Communist, and Anarchist movements. A civil war is inevitable and will commence within the game year, with the player able to lead either Republican or Nationalist forces. The country holds the Iberian Peninsula with African colonial territories, giving it valuable tungsten and steel reserves critical to wartime production.

## Government and political situation

Republican Spain begins under **Democratic** control with support from:
- **Partido Socialista Obrero Español (PSOE):** 40.97% popularity, ruling party
- **Confederación Española de Derechas Autónomas (CEDA):** 36.97% popularity, Fascist
- **Comunión Tradicionalista:** 15.07% popularity, Non-Aligned (Carlist)
- **Partido Comunista de España (PCE):** 6.99% popularity, Communist

A civil war is inevitable; the countdown begins after elections in February 1936. The player can delay the conflict via decisions to increase Republican military readiness.

### Political Path Options

The focus tree branches into three main ideological paths:
- **Maintain the Second Republic:** Democratic branch supporting continued democracy and French alliance
- **The Anti-Fascist Worker's Revolution:** Communist branch with Soviet alliance or independent path
- **Regional Defense Council of Aragon:** Anarchist branch with unique collectivized economy and international brigades

Each victory in the civil war unlocks unique post-war focuses for reconstruction, annexation, or faction-joining.

**Possible leaders after civil war:**
- **Manuel Azaña** (Democratic, Figurehead President trait) — comes to power via event
- **José Díaz** (Communist, Veteran Communist trait) — via The Anti-Fascist Worker's Revolution focus
- **Julián Gorkin** (Anarchist, Militant Socialist trait) — via Masters of Our Own Fate focus if anarchist path chosen

## Unique mechanics

Spain's unique mechanics revolve around the **Spanish Civil War system:**

- **Civil War timer:** Countdown begins after February 1936 elections (can be extended or triggered early via focuses)
- **Garrison contested decisions:** Both Republican and Nationalist sides contest territory to build war support; player controls one side while AI controls the other
- **Multiple outcome paths:** Civil War can be won by Republicans (Democratic, Communist, or Anarchist), Nationalists (Fascist/Non-Aligned), or theoretically stalemate-end
- **Post-civil-war recovery:** Winning side gains access to unique post-war focuses (reconstruction, imperial expansion, faction joining)
- **International brigades and volunteers:** Foreign powers can send volunteers; Republican Spain can secure Soviet aid, Nationalist Spain receives German and Italian volunteers
- **Puppet release:** Both sides can release puppets (Republic of the Rif, Kingdom of Morocco, Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic, Equatorial Guinea) during or after the civil war

### Critical Decisions During Pre-War Phase

- **Imprison Primo de Rivera:** Fascist political pressure decision (Republican side)
- **Secure Northern Garrisons:** Nationalist focus giving free garrison control over Galicia, León, etc.
- **Disband the Army:** Republican focus triggering civil war early but with favorable army distribution

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Immediate neighbours:**
- **France** (north and African colonies; can guarantee Spain)
- **Portugal** (west; neutral)
- **United Kingdom** (via Gibraltar colonial holding)

**Pre-war diplomatic stance:** Neutral with most powers; internal divisions dominate. Republican Spain favors democratic nations; Nationalists have Fascist sympathies.

**Possible alliances:**
- Republican → Soviet Union (puppet risk) or France/Britain (Democratic guarantee)
- Nationalist → Axis (Germany/Italy provide volunteers)
- Anarchist → Independent or International brigades from leftist volunteers

## Focus Tree Overview

Republican Spain has a unique focus tree (La Résistance expansion required; otherwise Generic tree).

**Key pre-civil-war branch:** *The Popular Front* — gives army battalions and political power, takes longer than Nationalist focuses (70 vs. 28–35 days), incentivizing extension of pre-war phase.

**Post-civil-war branches (if Republican victory):**
- *Maintain the Second Republic:* Democratic path, potential French lend-lease
- *The Anti-Fascist Worker's Revolution:* Communist split with Soviet options (puppet vs. independent)
- *Regional Defense Council of Aragon:* Anarchist collectivist economy with uprisings
