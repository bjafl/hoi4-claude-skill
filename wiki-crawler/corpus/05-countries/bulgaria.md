Bulgaria is a minor Balkan power occupying 4 states in southeastern Europe. Heavily restricted by postwar treaties after WWI, it begins Non-Aligned under Tsar Boris III with limited military and industrial capacity. Its fate is to either pursue territorial restoration through fascism and Axis alignment, seek reform through democracy, or embrace communism under Soviet pressure. The Battle for the Bosporus DLC provides Bulgaria with unique mechanics and a detailed focus tree.

## Government and political situation

Bulgaria starts as **Non-Aligned** under **Tsar Boris III** (Despotism ideolo­gy), who leads the Tsar Boris Loyalists (55% popularity). Three other factions exist: the Democratic Party (10%, Nikola Mushanov), the Communist Party (5%, Georgi Dimitrov), and the fascist Union of Bulgarian National Legions (30%, Hristo Lukov).

The country can shift ideologies through its national focus tree:
- **Monarchist path:** Keep Tsar Boris III in power or restore Tsar Ferdinand I, gaining stability and military bonuses.
- **Democratic path:** Hold legislative elections and potentially ally with Greece and the Western powers.
- **Fascist path:** Embrace Hristo Lukov's militarism or adopt full Nazism under Aleksandar Tsankov; gain war goals and mobilization.
- **Communist path:** Overthrow the Tsar (when stability drops below 30%), embrace Georgi Dimitrov or Kimon Georgiev, and form the People's Republic or join the Comintern.

Alternatively, a **Zveno Coup** decision allows Kimon Georgiev to seize power as a military technocrat, providing economic and research bonuses.

## National spirits

Bulgaria begins with three crippling national spirits (all tied to the Treaty of Neuilly aftermath):

*Only applies with Arms Against Tyranny expansion enabled.

## Unique mechanics

Bulgaria's focus tree (requires Battle for the Bosporus) emphasizes:

- **Acquire Modern Tools:** Upgrade industry and military through negotiated rearmament, aircraft repair, naval tech, and the "Prussia of the Balkans" nationalist spirit.
- **Political struggle:** Three competing factions (Tsar loyalists, fascists, communists) offer competing paths to power, with each bringing different bonuses and diplomatic alignments.
- **Balkan confederation:** Pursue paths to form the Greater Bulgarian Federation, United Balkan Federation, or Third Bulgarian Empire, potentially annexing neighboring states.
- **Foreign pressure:** Choices to approach Germany/Italy, the United Kingdom, or the Soviet Union, each granting unique war goals and alliances.

Without Battle for the Bosporus, Bulgaria uses the generic national focus tree and starts with only 2 research slots.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

Bulgaria is bordered by:
- **North:** Romania
- **South:** Greece and Turkey
- **West:** Yugoslavia

In 1936, Bulgaria has no diplomatic relations but is subject to interference from neighboring powers. Yugoslavia may seek to annex Bulgaria (especially if pursuing Western or Communist paths). Romania, Czechoslovakia, Italy, and Germany all have potential claims or influence over Bulgarian territory. Bulgaria historically joins the Axis in March 1941, gaining territorial concessions (Macedonia, Thrace) but avoids participating in the invasion of the Soviet Union, instead engaging only the British and American navies in the Black Sea.
