Luxembourg is a minor democratic nation in Western Europe with minimal industrial capacity and manpower. Geographically positioned between France, Belgium, and Germany, it typically falls quickly to German expansion but can be prolonged through defensive preparations.

## Government and political situation

The Rietspartei (right-wing democratic party) rules under Joseph Bech, with elections scheduled for June 1937. Bech serves as both the political leader and provides traits that boost political power gain (+5%) and infrastructure construction speed (+5%).

Four other ideological parties exist:
- **Communist (5%)**: Kommunistesch Partei Lëtzebuerg (KPL), led by Charles Marx
- **Fascist (3%)**: Volksdeutsche Bewegung (VdB), led by Damian Kratzenberg
- **Non-aligned (13%)**: House of Nassau, led by Charlotte Wilhelmine

The democratic starting position grants positive relations (+10 to +20) with other democracies and slight negative relations (−10) with all other ideologies.

## Unique mechanics

**No unique focus tree:** Luxembourg lacks a unique national focus tree and uses the generic tree instead, with five branches: Army Effort, Aviation Effort, Naval Effort, Industrial Effort, and Political Effort. The Industrial branch can unlock up to 3 additional research slots (total of 5 available).

**No initial military:** Luxembourg starts with zero army divisions, zero naval units, and zero aircraft.

## Military industrial organizations

Luxembourg has access to specialized design companies:
- Tank Designer (reliability and armor research speed)
- Ship Designer (naval research speed)
- Light Air Company (fighter design)
- Medium Air Company (heavy fighter and tactical bomber design)
- Heavy Air Company (strategic bomber design)
- Naval Air Company (naval aircraft design)
- Artillery Designer, Infantry Equipment Designer, Motorized Equipment Designer

**No unique cosmetic names:** Equipment uses generic names.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Borders:** Germany (east), France (south), Belgium (north, west) — vulnerable to invasion if either Germany or France enters conflict with the other.

**Initial alliances:** None; the nation is unaligned but demographically democratic, making it targeted by German expansion via focus tree branches that push into BeNeLux.

**Diplomatic stance:** As a democracy, Luxembourg maintains positive relations with other democracies (Belgium, France, United Kingdom) but has no formal guarantees of military support; it typically falls within weeks if invaded by Germany, particularly via the BeNeLux route that bypasses French fortifications.
