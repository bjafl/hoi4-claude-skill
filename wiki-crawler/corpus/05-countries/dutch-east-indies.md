The Dutch East Indies is a non-aligned puppet of the Netherlands in Southeast Asia, strategically positioned in the archipelago with substantial resource wealth but crippled by depression-era economic crisis, weak military infrastructure, and puppet constraints. Despite governing a population of 48.53 million (core) with another 14.26 million non-core, the territory faces political repression, inadequate weaponry, and limited autonomy from its Dutch overlord. The Thunder at Our Gates DLC introduces unique mechanics and a dedicated national focus tree for potential independence or reformed governance paths.

## Government and political situation

The Dutch East Indies begins as an Integrated Puppet under Netherlands control, with autonomy locked at the highest puppet tier. The Gouverneur-Generaal dominates with 80% popularity; the Volksraad (People's Council) holds 11% despite its nominal quasi-parliamentary role following 1901 Ethical Policies reforms.

**Starting leader (1936, without Thunder at Our Gates DLC):** A.T. van Starkenborgh Stachouwer (Conservative, Moderate Governor) – provides +5% Stability but –5% Stability overall, –10% Consumer Goods Factories factor.

**Starting leader (with Thunder at Our Gates DLC):** Bonifacius Cornelis de Jonge (Conservative, Staunch Imperialist) – reduces Freedom gain and Autonomy progress (–0.40 daily) while increasing Civilian factory output to overlord (+15%).

**Political parties and leaders available (Thunder at Our Gates):**
- **Volksraad (Conservative, 11%):** Elected governors include Stachouwer (Moderate Governor, +5%/+10% Stability) and others with varied traits
- **Partai Komunis Indonesia (Communist, 9%):** Led by Tan Malaka or Musso; can push Marxism/Stalinism agendas
- **Partai Nasional Indonesia (Nationalist):** Mohammed Hatta (Proclamator) or Sukarno; can lead democracy or nationalist movements
- **Gouverneur-Généraal (Oligarchic, 80%):** Current ruling faction; emphasizes colonial control

**Notable paths:**
- Increase Autonomy to independence via focus tree (requires both Volksraad democratic support and gradual autonomy gains)
- Netherlands-Indonesia Union: Absorb the overlord and inherit forces, cored territory, and non-aligned ideology
- Communist/Fascist ideological flips via party support shifts

## Economic situation

**Starting laws:**
- **Conscription:** Volunteer Only (1.5% recruitable population)
- **Trade:** Export Focus (+10% construction, +5% research, +10% factory output, 50% resources to market, +20% civilian intelligence, +10% navy intelligence)
- **Economy:** Civilian Economy (35% factories to consumer goods, +30% conversion cost, –30% energy consumption, –25% fuel gain, –30% fuel capacity, –40% construction speed)

**Industry (1936):**
- Military factories: 3
- Naval dockyards: 2
- Civilian factories: 6 (–3 required for consumer goods)
- Fuel silos: 0
- Synthetic refineries: 1
- Building slots available: 62

**Economic constraints:** The Dutch East Indies economy has been severely depressed by De Malaise (Great Depression). Wage cuts, reduced exports (rubber, oil, cash crops), and competition from Japan have degraded revenue. Colonial policies prioritize welfare (land grants, public ownership of communications and finance) over industrialization, creating chronic underinvestment in military production.

## Unique mechanics

Without Thunder at Our Gates DLC, the Dutch East Indies uses the generic national focus tree (Army Effort, Aviation Effort, Naval Effort, Industrial Effort, Political Effort branches).

With **Thunder at Our Gates**, unique focuses unlock:
- **Independence path:** Gradual autonomy increases toward Free status, potentially leading to the "Declaration of Independence" event and Republic of Indonesia transformation
- **Netherlands-Indonesia Union:** Alternative path absorbing the Netherlands government and inheriting its forces, cored states, and leaders
- **Volksraad negotiations:** Decisions to increase democratic support and negotiate autonomy without immediate conflict
- **Various regional leader focuses:** Appoint provincial sultanates (Ternate, Siak, Pontianak, Gowa) or nationalist figures as heads of government
- **Industrial development via rubber sales:** Decisions to export rubber for civilian and military factory construction

**See [06-focus-trees/dutch-east-indies.md](../06-focus-trees/dutch-east-indies.md) for the full focus tree.**

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Borders:** Australia (east), Portuguese Timor (southeast), British Malaya (north)

**Initial diplomatic stance:**
- **Puppet of Netherlands:** Integrated puppet status; overlord guarantees all non-core territory and controls core foreign policy
- **Non-aligned faction:** Not part of any ideological bloc; can be recruited into German, Japanese, or Italian spheres if ideology flips or if the overlord falls

**Potential allies/rivals:**
- **Netherlands:** Overlord; controls policy until independence is achieved or union is formed
- **British Malaya / British Raj:** Regional colonial powers; neutral unless conflict spreads
- **Japan:** Rising regional power competing for trade and resources; potential threat to independence
- **China/Siam:** Distant regional powers; limited immediate diplomatic contact

**Autonomy constraints:** The "Netherlands Most Precious Jewel" national spirit prevents raising autonomy to Free (independence) tier without breaking the puppet bond through civil conflict or completing the Netherlands-Indonesia Union focus path.
