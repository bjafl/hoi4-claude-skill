Formable nations in Asia represent historical or ideological unifications created through national focus trees, decisions, and conquest requirements. They span the Middle East (Arabia, Mesopotamia, Persian Empire, Ottoman), South Asia (Hindustan, Akhand Bharat, Afghan-Pakistan Confederacy), East Asia (Chinese Empire, various Chinese Warlord Empires, Great Yuan), Southeast Asia (Greater Indonesian Confederacy, Federation of Malayan Peoples), and Central Asia (Turkestan, Turan, Timurid Empire, Siberia). Most require specific DLC and completed national focuses, plus territorial control of defined state lists.

## Arabia

| Ideology | Country name |
|---|---|
| Democratic | Arabian Federation |
| Communist | United Arab Socialist Republics |
| Fascist | Arabian Empire |
| Non-Aligned | Arabia |

Formable by any Arab nation in the MENA Region (Middle East and North Africa). Requires control of the Levant, Arabian Peninsula, and North Africa — includes historic Arab world territories. Based on the McMahon-Hussein Correspondence of WWI; the Sykes-Picot agreement historically negated this promise by dividing Arab lands between France, UK, Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Yemen, and Oman.

**Formation requirement:** Decision "Unite the Arabian Peninsula" after gaining cores on Saudi Arabia, Yemen, Oman, Egypt, Libya, Jordan, Palestine, Lebanon, Syria, Kuwait, Trucial States, Qatar, Tunisia, Algeria, Morocco, Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic, Mauritania, Sudan, Bahrain, South Arabia, Imamate of Oman, and Republic of the Rif territories. Must control Nejd, Madinah, Rub al Khali, Al-Qassim, Najiran, Jawf, Tabuk, Asir-Makkah, Dammam, North Yemen, Musandam, Oman, Muscat, and Dhofar. After formation, additional decisions integrate the Fertile Crescent, Levantine States, Western Mashriq, Eastern Maghreb, and West Saharan States for additional cores.

## Chinese Empire

| Ideology | Country name |
|---|---|
| Democratic | Chinese Constitutional Monarchy |
| Communist | United Chinese People's Republics |
| Fascist | Chinese Empire |
| Non-Aligned | Chinese Empire |

Formable exclusively by Manchukuo through focuses "Reclaim the Empire" (grants cores on all Chinese states controlled by China, Guangxi Clique, Northeastern Army, Guangdong Clique, Sichuan Clique, Hebei-Chahar, Shandong Clique, Yunnan, Qinghai Ma, Gansu Ma, Khotan Ma, Ningxia Ma, Shanxi, Communist China, Sinkiang, plus French, British, Portuguese, and Japanese territories in China) → "Claim the Mandate of Heaven." Requires conquest of all cored Chinese territories except Hong Kong (326), Kouang-Tchéou-Wan (728), and Taiwan (524). Upon formation, Manchukuo becomes Chinese Empire with darker yellow color and Qing Dynasty flag; leader Puyi adopts Emperor robes. To restore historic Qing borders: "One China Policy" focus, "Demand Mongolia" focus (annexes Mongolia and Tannu Tuva), and conquest of Outer Manchuria states (Vladivostok, Khabarovsk, Birobidzhan, Nikolayevsk, Amur) from Soviet Union.

## Chinese Warlord Empires

Warlords completing "Restoration of the Chinese Empire" focus form region-specific empires:
- **Great Yuan:** formed by Kumul Khanate or warlords led by specific Ma Clique commanders
- **Great Ma:** formed by Gansu Ma (led by Ma Buqing), Khotan Ma (led by Ma Hushan), Ningxia Ma (led by Ma Hongkui), or Qinghai Ma (led by Ma Lin or Ma Bufang)
- **Chinese Sultanate:** formed by Gansu Ma, Khotan Ma, Ningxia Ma, or Qinghai Ma if led by leaders other than the above
- **Empire of China:** formed by other warlords (catch-all)

| Ideology | Country name |
|---|---|
| Democratic | East India Company |
| Communist | East India Company |
| Fascist | East India Company |
| Non-Aligned | East India Company |

Formable by British Raj via focus "Look to the Future" (requires Graveyard of Empires DLC). Enables purchasing land using civilian factories. Follow-up focus "Rebranding" allows coring any held state with sufficient compliance and resistance. EIC can core states normally held by other nations if those nations complete specific focuses/decisions that grant cores (e.g., Afghanistan gaining cores if British Raj completes "Akhand Bharat"; Nepal if Akhand Bharat completed; many island and colonial territories have multi-nation core pathways). _Formation decision not detailed on wiki._

| Communist | Union of Malayan Peoples |
| Fascist | Greater East Asia Co-Prosperity Sphere |

## Greater Indonesian Confederacy

| Ideology | Country name |
|---|---|
| Democratic | Greater Indonesian Confederacy |
| Communist | Democratic Socialist Republic of Nusantara |
| Fascist | Melayu Raya |
| Non-Aligned | Nusantara |

Formable by British Malaya and Dutch East Indies (without Thunder at Our Gates DLC). Requires full independence and union of British Malaya + Dutch East Indies. Requires control of all Indonesian archipelago states (Sumatra, West Java, Kalimantan Barat, West Nusa Tenggara, Sulawesi, The Moluccas, Aru Islands, Dutch New Guinea, East Kalimantan, Bangka-Belitung, Padang, Medan and Riouw, Atjeh, Batavia, East Java, Bali, East Nusa Tenggara, Dutch Timor, Manado, Dutch Papua) and all Malayan states (Kuala Lumpur, Sarawak, Brunei, Sabah, Limbang, Johor, Perlis-Kedah, Kelantan-Terengganu, Pahang, Perak). Formation grants cores on Portuguese Timor, Christmas Island, Cocos Islands, Singapore, Penang, and Interior Borneo. Post-formation decisions integrate Papua (requiring Papua, Kaiser-Wilhelmsland, and Interior Papua control) and Northern Malays (requiring Southern Siam).

Formable by any Indian princely state, Nepal, or Bhutan (requires Graveyard of Empires DLC). Also gains claims on Burmese and Afghan states. Upon formation, rename to Hindustan with cores on all specified territories.

## Formation Notes and DLC Requirements Summary

- **No Step Back:** Siberia, Greater Mongolia, Turkestan
- **By Blood Alone:** _Not primary trigger for Asia formables_
- **Graveyard of Empires:** Hindustan, Akhand Bharat, Afghan-Pakistan Confederacy (partial), Persia/Persian Empire variants, Timurid Empire, United Arab Republic, East India Company
- **Battle for the Bosporus:** Ottoman Empire/Sultanate, Turan
- **No Compromise, No Surrender:** Association of South East Asian Nations, Federation of Malayan Peoples, Rattanakosin Kingdom
- **Thunder at Our Gates:** Greater Indonesian Confederacy, Federation of Malayan Peoples, Rattanakosin Kingdom, some Philippines mechanics

Most formable nations require control of historically significant territories and completion of specific national focus trees. Ideology determines the resulting nation name in most cases. Multiple nations may pursue the same formable (e.g., Hindustan by any Indian princely state, Nepal, or Bhutan), but only one can ultimately form it per game.
