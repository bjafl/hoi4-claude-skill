## Formable nations—Africa

Formable nations represent historical or alternate-history states that players can create by meeting specific territorial and political requirements. This page covers formable nations primarily formed through decisions in African regions, including North Africa, the Horn of Africa, East Africa, Central Africa, and Southern Africa. Formation requirements vary—some require focus tree completion, others only territorial control.

### Aksumite Empire

**Tag:** AKS  
**DLC:** By Blood Alone  
**Formable by:** Ethiopia

| Ideology | Country name |
|---|---|
| Democratic | Aksumite Empire |
| Communist | People's Union of Aksum |
| Fascist | Aksumite Empire |
| Non-Aligned | Aksumite Empire |

Ancient regional empire historically spanning the eastern coast of Africa and parts of southern Arabia. Ethiopia's first formable nation, unlocked after retaining Emperor Selassie and completing the focus "Restore the Empire of Aksum."

**Formation requirements:**
- Visibility: Has completed focus "Restore the Empire of Aksum"
- Prerequisites: Control all Ethiopian core states (Shewa, Hararghe, Bale, Sidamo, Illubabor-Kaffa, Welega, Gojjam, Begemder, Tigray, Wello) plus Eritrea and North Yemen
- Effects: Gains cores on South Yemen (659), Eritrea (550), North Yemen (293), Socotra (906), French Somaliland (268), Kassala (883), Khartoum (551), Blue Nile (886), Province of Aden (992), and Afar (908)

**Optional secondary decision:** Move capital to Tigray (costs 50 political power; adds 5 VP to Axum; upgrades state development level; only once per game).

### Al-Andalus

**DLC:** La Résistance  
**Formable by:** Morocco (and other Iberian nations)

| Ideology | Country name |
|---|---|
| Democratic | Islamic Republic of Al-Andalus |
| Communist | People's Republic of Andalusia |
| Fascist | Caliphate of Cordoba |
| Non-Aligned | Al-Andalus |

Moorish restoration of medieval Iberian sultanate. Upon formation, enables a cascade of decision chains to restore a unified Umayyad Caliphate across North Africa, the Mediterranean, and eventually the Middle East and Persia.

**Formation (Restore Al-Andalus):**
- Prerequisites: Fully independent; control Granada (173), Córdoba (789), Murcia (168), Valencia (167), Ciudad Real (175), Extremadura (170), Sevilla (169) from Spain; Beja (179), Santarém (795) from Portugal
- Effects: Gains cores on all Spanish states (18 total) and Portuguese states (7 total); capital moves to Córdoba (789)

**Post-formation decision chains:**
- "Restore the Sultanate of Africa" — gains cores on Moroccan, Tunisian, Algerian, and Libyan states
- "Restore the Emirate of Sicily" — gains cores on Mediterranean islands (Sicilia, Malta, Dodecanese, Crete, Cyprus, Sardegna, Corsica)
- "Recreate the Umayyad Caliphate" (requires both prior decisions) — gains cores on all Middle Eastern and Egyptian states
- "The Rashidi Expansion" — gains cores on Iran, Afghanistan, and Pakistan
- "The Northern Expansion" — gains cores on Caucasus and Anatolia

### Greater Ethiopian Union (and regional variants)

**DLC:** By Blood Alone  
**Formable by:** Ethiopia, Gojjam, Jimma

| Ideology | Primary | Gojjami variant | Jimman variant |
|---|---|---|---|
| Democratic | East African Federation | East African Federation | East African Federation |

| Fascist | Greater Ethiopian Union | Greater Gojjami Union | Greater Jimman Union |

## Formable Nations (Asia)

Formable nations in Asia represent historical or ideological unifications created through national focus trees, decisions, and conquest requirements. They span the Middle East (Arabia, Mesopotamia, Persian Empire, Ottoman), South Asia (Hindustan, Akhand Bharat, Afghan-Pakistan Confederacy), East Asia (Chinese Empire, various Chinese Warlord Empires, Great Yuan), Southeast Asia (Greater Indonesian Confederacy, Federation of Malayan Peoples), and Central Asia (Turkestan, Turan, Timurid Empire, Siberia). Most require specific DLC and completed national focuses, plus territorial control of defined state lists.

### Arabia

| Ideology | Country name |
|---|---|
| Democratic | Arabian Federation |
| Communist | United Arab Socialist Republics |
| Fascist | Arabian Empire |
| Non-Aligned | Arabia |

Formable by any Arab nation in the MENA Region (Middle East and North Africa). Requires control of the Levant, Arabian Peninsula, and North Africa — includes historic Arab world territories. Based on the McMahon-Hussein Correspondence of WWI; the Sykes-Picot agreement historically negated this promise by dividing Arab lands between France, UK, Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Yemen, and Oman.

**Formation requirement:** Decision "Unite the Arabian Peninsula" after gaining cores on Saudi Arabia, Yemen, Oman, Egypt, Libya, Jordan, Palestine, Lebanon, Syria, Kuwait, Trucial States, Qatar, Tunisia, Algeria, Morocco, Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic, Mauritania, Sudan, Bahrain, South Arabia, Imamate of Oman, and Republic of the Rif territories. Must control Nejd, Madinah, Rub al Khali, Al-Qassim, Najiran, Jawf, Tabuk, Asir-Makkah, Dammam, North Yemen, Musandam, Oman, Muscat, and Dhofar. After formation, additional decisions integrate the Fertile Crescent, Levantine States, Western Mashriq, Eastern Maghreb, and West Saharan States for additional cores.

### Chinese Warlord Empires

Warlords completing "Restoration of the Chinese Empire" focus form region-specific empires:
- **Great Yuan:** formed by Kumul Khanate or warlords led by specific Ma Clique commanders
- **Great Ma:** formed by Gansu Ma (led by Ma Buqing), Khotan Ma (led by Ma Hushan), Ningxia Ma (led by Ma Hongkui), or Qinghai Ma (led by Ma Lin or Ma Bufang)
- **Chinese Sultanate:** formed by Gansu Ma, Khotan Ma, Ningxia Ma, or Qinghai Ma if led by leaders other than the above
- **Empire of China:** formed by other warlords (catch-all)

## Formable nations/Europe

Formable nations of Europe are countries that can be created during a game via decisions, rather than played from 1936 start. They range from expansionist regional unions (Scandinavia, Greater Italy) to ideologically-variant empires (Alpine Confederation with four forms: Democratic, Communist, Fascist, Non-Aligned) to historically-themed recreations (Austria-Hungary, Roman Empire, Byzantine Empire). Formation typically requires territorial control and specific governmental conditions.

### Overview of formation categories

Formable European nations fall into three categories:

1. **By formable_nation_decisions.txt** (25 nations) — Primary method, triggered by generic "Formable Nation" decisions. Includes Austria-Hungary, Germany (reunified), Greater Germanic Reich, Holy Roman Empire, Roman Empire, and others.

2. **By other decision files** (14 nations) — Formed via decisions in other decision files (Alpine Confederation, Benelux Federation, Pan-Slavic Union, etc.), often tied to specific country focus trees or events.

3. **By other means** (45+ states/entities) — Created via puppet/annexation triggers, autonomous state decisions, or other game mechanics (Alpine Protectorate, British Alpine Territories, various Soviet republics, Papal State, etc.).

Most formable nations require specific DLC: By Blood Alone (Switzerland, Alps, Italy expansions), Arms Against Tyranny (Nordic expansions), No Step Back (Soviet-related), Götterdämmerung (German/Central European), Battle for the Bosporus (Byzantine/Balkans), La Résistance (Iberia, Franco-British).

- Communist: Alpine Union

### Release mechanics

Most African releasables share common requirements:
- **Standard release:** Released as puppet of colonial owner (France, UK, Portugal, Italy, Spain, Belgium, South Africa) by player decision or AI wargoal.

- **Spanish Civil War gate:** Spanish colonies (Equatorial Guinea, Morocco, Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic, The Rif) cannot be released until Spanish Civil War concludes.
- **Africa Decolonized rule:** Custom game rule enables release at 1936 start for most nations; changes some starting ideologies (noted per country).

## Indonesian Princely States

Indonesian Princely States are a group of releasable minor nations with cores in the Dutch East Indies. Most start as Non-Aligned sultanates and kingdoms with unique leaders and national characteristics. Most are not released by default with the "Asia Decolonized" game rule; only Timor-Leste and West Papua are affected by this rule. These nations can be released through liberation or independence wars.
