## Summary

Argentina is a middle-sized nation in South America with significant regional power. It begins non-aligned under the Concordancia party (led by Agustín Pedro Justo) and is guaranteed independence by the United States via the Monroe Doctrine. Despite starting relatively weak militarily, Argentina has prime expansion opportunities into numerous weaker South American neighbors and access to multiple political ideologies through its focus tree.

## Government and political situation

Argentina's 1936 government represents the aftermath of decades of political instability following the 1930 coup that installed José Félix Uriburu. Agustín Pedro Justo came to power through 1932 elections and pursued interventionist economic policies while suppressing political opposition.

Starting ideology is Non-Aligned under the **Concordancia** party (30% popularity) led by **Agustín Pedro Justo**, with trait *Fraudulent Industrialist* (Political Power Gain +5%, Construction Speed +15%, Stability -5%).

Three major opposition ideologies compete for power:
- **Democratic**: Unión Cívica Radical (24% popularity), led by **Marcelo T. de Alvear** (Harsh Democrat trait: Stability +10%, Daily Democracy Support +0.10, Democratic Diplomacy Acceptance +40)
- **Communist**: Partido Comunista de la Argentina (24% popularity), led by **Fanny Edelman** (Feminist Revolutionary trait: Recruitable population +1%, Daily Communism Support +0.10)
- **Fascist**: Union Nacional Fascista (19% popularity), led by **Nimio de Anquín** (Argentine Nationalist trait: War goal justify time -10%, War support +10%, Recruitable population +10%)

All opposition parties can come to power via coup. A Democratic path aligns Argentina with the Allies and reduces the Infamous Decade penalty. A Communist path triggers a civil war with options to either join the Comintern under Rodolfo Ghioldi or follow Fanny Edelman's anarcho-syndicalist path under Antonio Soto. A Fascist path puts nationalists in power with German and Italian support.

## Unique mechanics

Argentina gets a unique national focus tree with Trial of Allegiance DLC. Without it, Argentina uses the generic focus tree. The unique focus tree has 5 major branches:
- **A Call to Reform** — Communist paths with branching options for Comintern alignment (Ghioldi) or anarcho-syndicalist autonomy (Soto); unions South America under Communism or builds industry
- **The Clean Election** — Democratic path aligns with Allies, reduces Infamous Decade effects, may create Monroe Alliance faction
- **Roberto María Ortiz** — Historical Non-Aligned path attempts to mitigate political corruption; can lead to civil war with choice to elect Ramón Castillo
- **Work With The Nationalists** — Fascist path enables cooperation with Germany and Italy for regional expansion
- **Dominate the South** — Expansion opportunities via war goals or puppet states in South America and Western Hemisphere
- **Fourth Time's The Charm** — Hidden branch triggered when Fascist Argentina faces a Fascist German capitulation; installs Señor Hilter as leader to reconquer and core Germany as the Fourth Reich
- **Banco Central de la República Argentina** — Industrial focus branch builds industry, revisits British treaties, promotes immigration

Trial of Allegiance also includes shared military branches granting special national spirits and military bonuses based on chosen alignment.

See [06-focus-trees/argentina.md](../06-focus-trees/argentina.md) for the full focus tree.

## Geography and resources

Argentina owns 14 states dominated by plains and deserts. Buenos Aires is the capital with 152 victory points (5.6M population); Chubut has 231 VP; Región Mesopotámica has 132 VP; Santa Fe has 21 VP; Los Andes has 12 VP; San Juan y La Rioja has 12 VP; Río Negro has 12 VP; Mendoza has 2 VP; and six states have 1 VP each (Chaco Austral, Formosa, Santa Cruz, Tierra del Fuego).

Tungsten (35) is Argentina's dominant strategic resource, with coal (2) and steel (6) also available. Oil reserves (19) support initial naval and air operations, though limited rubber (3) restricts aircraft production without imports.

## Neighbours and diplomacy

**Bordering states**: Bolivia and Paraguay to the north, Chile to the west, Uruguay to the east, Brazil to the northeast.

**Diplomatic guarantees**: US guarantees Argentina's independence via the Monroe Doctrine, granting Argentina +35 opinion toward the US.

**Territorial claims**: Argentina starts with claims on the Falkland Islands and South Georgia, both held by the United Kingdom.

**Faction potential**: Democratic path may allow creation of the Monroe Alliance faction with other South American nations or alignment with the Allies. Non-Aligned path may pursue South American integration. Communist path can join the Comintern.
