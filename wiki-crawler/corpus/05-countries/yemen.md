Yemen is a minor non-aligned nation in the Arabian Peninsula, hemmed between Saudi Arabia to the north and British-controlled Aden to the east. Its 1936 government is the isolationist Mutawakkilite Kingdom under Imam Yahya Muhammad, which historically remained neutral throughout WW2. Yemen starts with minimal industrial capacity and resources but offers a foothold for regional expansion through the Arabia formation path.

## National spirits

Yemen does not have any national spirits.

## Unique mechanics

Yemen lacks a unique national focus tree and uses the generic national focus tree instead, with five branches available: Army Effort, Aviation Effort, Naval Effort, Industrial Effort, and Political Effort. The Industrial Effort branch can unlock up to 3 additional research slots beyond the starting 2.

**Camelry tech:** Yemen begins research with access to Camelry, a unique infantry variant available to Arab nations.

Yemen may form Arabia if it controls all required states.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering countries:**
- North: Saudi Arabia
- East: United Kingdom (controls Aden Province) and British Raj (controls Aden)

**Diplomatic claims:**
- Yemeni cores exist in Aden and Socotra, currently controlled by the British Raj and United Kingdom
- Historically, Yemen disputes its southern boundary with the British Aden Protectorate

**Potential diplomatic paths:**
- Can form Arabia as an Arab nation if territorial requirements are met
- Generic focus tree provides political paths toward democracy, communism, or fascism
