## Sheikhdom of Kuwait

A single-state British Integrated Puppet in the Middle East with 80K population and minimal industry. Kuwait has no starting military, no resources, and zero factories, making it one of the game's most resource-constrained minors. Its only strategic asset is the ability to form Arabia and Mesopotamia as an Arab nation, a feat requiring conquest of large swaths of the Middle East.

Kuwait consists of a single desert state (Kuwait, with capital at Kuwait City). No starting military of any kind; the country begins with only 5 convoys.

### Government and political situation

Kuwait starts as a Non-Aligned Integrated Puppet of the United Kingdom, with the House of Sabah controlling the government. Elections occur every 4 years (next scheduled January 1940).

**Ruling party:** The House of Sabah (Non-Aligned) — 58% popularity  
**Country leader:** Ahmad Al-Jaber Al-Sabah (Despotic) — Traits: The Military Sheikh  
Effects: Division Defense +15%, Cavalry Attack +10%, Cavalry Defense +10%, Recruitable Population +2%

**Other political parties:**
- Al-Tehalef Al-Watani Al-Demokrati (NDA) — Democratic — 40% popularity
- Kuwaiti Progressive Movement (KPM) — Communist — 1% popularity
- Popular Action Bloc (Hashd) — Fascist — 1% popularity

To flip ideologies and go independent, Kuwait must shift political support via ministers (150 political power per minister to hire) or through civil wars. The generic focus tree's Political Effort branch provides options to pursue other ideologies.

### National spirits

Kuwait does not have any national spirits at game start.

### Unique mechanics

As an Arab nation, Kuwait can form the Arabia and Mesopotamia decision by conquering the requisite territories. This is Kuwait's primary strategic objective and the driving mechanic for most gameplay—required conquests include Saudi Arabia, Yemen, Oman, Iraq, Turkey (possibly), and Syria.

Kuwait lacks a unique national focus tree and uses the generic focus tree, which offers five main branches: Army Effort, Aviation Effort, Naval Effort, Industrial Effort, and Political Effort.

**Geographic note:** Kuwait has a coastline but no naval bases at start. Building at least one naval base is necessary to deploy any naval units or receive faction members via sea.

### Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering countries:** Iraq (land), Saudi Arabia (land), Iran (maritime)  
**Diplomatic status:** Integrated Puppet of United Kingdom

Kuwait is guaranteed by the British and cannot be attacked by the UK or its faction without first forcing independence. The British guarantee on Iraq prevents early conflict with Iraq as well, creating a diplomatic bottleneck for expansionist play.

Player guidance (from wiki strategy section) suggests pursuing Communist ideology to gain access to manpower sources (Indoctrination focus +400 weekly; Ideological Loyalty army spirit +500 weekly), since joining the Comintern avoids needing territory from the Allies or Axis. Gaining independence requires either a civil war (purely a coup given one province) or gradually flipping political support to trigger elections. The narrow window to attack Turkey before it gains a British guarantee (between Reconfigure Turkish Foreign Policy and The Anglo-Turkish Agreement) is critical for maximizing puppeted manpower toward the Arabia conquest.

Lebanon is a French colony in the Middle East with 1.10 M population and one state (Beirut). Located on the Mediterranean coast, it borders French-controlled Syria to the east and British Mandatory Palestine to the south. It starts with no army, no air force, only 5 convoys, and is currently subject to France. Like most minors, it uses the generic national focus tree and can pursue the path to form Arabia if it gains independence and conquers the required territories.

Historical independence was declared in November 1941 under French pressure; in-game, independence requires breaking free from France through conquest, civil war, or diplomatic manipulation.

Lebanon's historical status as a French colony and its minimal starting military make it one of the weakest starting positions in the game. Surviving the early game requires careful diplomacy and significant reliance on ideology shifts and external support to gain any military footing.

## Mandatory Palestine

### Government and political situation

Mandatory Palestine begins in Non-Aligned ideology as an Integrated Puppet of the United Kingdom. Elections are held every 4 years; the next election is scheduled for January 1940.

**Political parties** and starting support:
- **High Commissioner for Palestine** (Non-Aligned, ruling): 60% — Sir Harold MacMichael

### National spirits

Mandatory Palestine has no starting national spirits.

### Unique mechanics

- **Generic focus tree:** Lacking its own unique national focus tree, Mandatory Palestine uses the generic focus tree with five branches (Army Effort, Aviation Effort, Naval Effort, Industrial Effort, Political Effort).
- **Releasable as Israel:** The Palestine state can be released as the independent nation of Israel.
- **Arabia formation:** An unmentioned but coded objective exists to form Arabia by conquering and annexing neighboring states.
- **No naval bases:** Despite having a coastline (shared with the Mediterranean and Red Sea regions), Mandatory Palestine starts with zero naval bases and must construct them to deploy any naval units or conduct naval invasions.

### Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Borders:** Lebanon and Syria to the north, Transjordan to the east, British Egypt to the west.

**Diplomatic status:** Integrated Puppet of the United Kingdom. This status prevents independent diplomatic action and subordinates Mandatory Palestine to British foreign policy and war declarations.

**Starting military:** Mandatory Palestine begins with no army units and no air force. It does possess 5 convoys, providing minimal maritime capacity. All military expansion must be built from scratch, with very limited industrial capacity to support it.

### Geography

Palestine contains four major cities: Al-Nasiriah, Jerusalem, Tel Aviv, and Umm al-Rashrāsh. Terrain composition is mixed desert (5 provinces), plains (3 provinces), urban (1 province), and lake (1 province).

Historically, Transjordan declared war on the Axis in 1939 alongside Britain and later contributed the Arab Legion to the British invasion of Iraq in 1941.

### Unique mechanics

**Displacing Ethiopia:** If Mohamed Yayyo remains in power and Aussa conquers all of Ethiopia, Aussa gains access to decisions that grant cores on all Ethiopian states and remove Ethiopia's cores, allowing direct assumption of the region without forming a new nation.

**Horn of Africa:** Aussa, along with Benishangul-Gumuz Nation, Gambela, Emirate of Harar, Oromia, Qemant State, Kingdom of Sidamo, and Tigray, shares the Horn of Africa national focus tree. Only Aussa is playable at game start; the others are releasable from Ethiopia. The focus tree provides three branches: Develop Capital State (development and bonuses), Armed Forces (army improvements and national spirits), and Nation (research and war goals within the Horn of Africa, leading to the formable Horn of Africa nation).

**Cores and claims:** Aussa holds cores on French Somaliland and the Ethiopian state of Wello.

### Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordered by:**
- Italy's colony of Eritrea (north)
- French Somaliland (east)
- Ethiopia (west and south)

**Strategic position:** Completely landlocked. Despite starting neutral, Aussa faces near-certain Italian annexation demands if Italy succeeds in Ethiopia (the historical path). Survival requires rejecting the ultimatum and maintaining a defensive posture in mountainous terrain while building strength. Italian invasion typically occurs 1–2 years into the game.

**Viable paths:** With careful play and defensive focus, Aussa can defend against Italy, build elite forces, and later join the Allies to assist in Ethiopia's liberation or pursue independent expansion via the Horn of Africa focus tree.

## Liberia

Liberia is a minor independent democratic nation in West Africa with a population of 750K. As one of only three fully independent African countries in 1936 (alongside Ethiopia and the Sultanate of Aussa), it occupies a unique geopolitical position bordering British and French colonial territories. Though poorly industrialized, Liberia would gain strategic importance to the Allies as a rubber supplier when Japanese expansion cut off Southeast Asian plantations. The country uses the generic national focus tree and has minimal starting military strength.

### Formation and mechanics

**Horn of Africa formable nation:** All Horn of Africa nations can form the Horn of Africa formable nation via decision (requires By Blood Alone).

**Shared focus tree (requires By Blood Alone):** Three primary branches:
- **Develop Capital State:** Nation development with bonuses to naval or air forces, and resources dependent on nation
- **The Armed Forces:** Improves irregular infantry and army bonuses; nation-dependent national spirits for combat ability, factory output, or research speed
- **The Nation:** Split into puppet and independent paths. Puppet path grants research bonuses and research slot. Independent path allows field marshal creation, war goals on other Horn nations, and Horn of Africa formation, or foreign alliances and volunteer sending

**National spirits:** Only Aussa starts with a national spirit. Shared focus tree can grant additional spirits depending on nation and ideological path.
