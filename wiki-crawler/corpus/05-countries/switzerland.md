Switzerland is a landlocked democratic minor power in Central Europe, surrounded by major powers: France (west), Italy (south), Germany (north), and Austria (east). It maintains a policy of strict neutrality but can pursue various ideological paths through its unique focus tree.

Switzerland has no starting factories and minimal resources, requiring trade or conquest for war production.

## Government and political situation

**Starting regime:** Democratic, led by the Federal Assembly of Cantons (collective leadership). Elections occur every 4 years; the next is scheduled for October 1939.

**Political parties:**
- Federal Assembly of Cantons (96% — ruling)
- Kommunistische Partei der Schweiz / KPS (2%)
- Fronten Bewegung / FB (2%)
- Freisinnig-Demokratische Partei / FDP (0%)

**Ideology paths:** Switzerland can shift to Communism, Fascism, or Non-Aligned through its focus tree (requires By Blood Alone). Key ideological leaders include:
- **Communist:** Fritz Platten (KPS) — daily +0.05 communism support
- **Fascist:** Robert Tobler (FB, starting agitator) or Rolf Henne (FB, Nazi-aligned)
- **Non-Aligned:** Henri Guisan (via "Guisan's Coup" focus) — Popular Dictator with +15% division defense

## National spirits

With By Blood Alone enabled, Switzerland starts with:

**Swiss Neutrality** — the defining spirit of 1936
- Improve relations opinion: -20%
- Join faction tension limit: +100% (cannot easily join a faction)
- Send volunteers tension limit: +20%
- Guarantee tension limit: +100%
- Justify war goal time on us: +250%
- Embargo tension limit: +250%
- Army Chief Cost: +200%
- Daily Democracy Support: +0.01

This spirit makes Switzerland extremely difficult to drag into war and locks it out of early faction membership. It can be removed through focus decisions (e.g., "Allied Gold" to join Allies; "Closer Ties with The German Reich" to go Fascist).

**Foreign Fascist Propaganda** — active threat from neighboring Fascist states
- Daily Fascism Support: +0.04

The combination of these two spirits — strict neutrality combined with rising Fascist influence — defines Switzerland's early-game tension.

## Unique mechanics

Switzerland has significant unique mechanics through its focus tree (requires By Blood Alone; generic tree used without the DLC):

**Swiss Guiding Principles (root focus):** Allows selection of which neighbor (Germany, Italy, or France) to fortify against, setting the tone for defensive strategy.

**Branching paths:**
- **Ban Foreign Nazi Propaganda:** Appeases democracies, reduces fascist opinion
- **Allied Gold / Purchase American Planes:** Pushes toward democracy, allows faction join (forms République Helvétique if puppet of France)
- **Gotthardbund:** Expansionist path; claims Austrian Vorarlberg and Liechtenstein; shifts toward communism or non-aligned; forms Alpine Confederation, Alpine Protectorate, or Alpine Union depending on ideology
- **A Swiss Navy:** Grants naval bonuses if Switzerland gains coastal territory
- **Support Allied Espionage:** (La Résistance DLC) Enables spy network support for democracies
- **Promote Henri Guisan / Frontier Defense Plan:** Boosts defensive capabilities and military production at cost of neighboring-major opinion
- **Federal Police Intelligence Department:** Cracks down on dissenting ideologies (reduces drift)
- **Purchase German Planes:** Appeases Fascists while improving air force
- **Closer Ties with The German Reich:** Path to Fascism; can form Schweizerische Reichsprotektorat (German puppet) or Alpine Supremacy
- **Expand Weapons Industry:** Improves army and air technology

See [06-focus-trees/switzerland.md](../06-focus-trees/switzerland.md) for the full focus tree.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering countries:** France, Italy, Germany, Austria.

**Diplomatic stance:** Switzerland pursues a neutral foreign policy with no faction membership or alliance in 1936. It can extend guarantees and sign non-aggression pacts, but the Swiss Neutrality spirit heavily penalizes these actions and prevents faction membership until specific focuses are completed.

**Claims:** With By Blood Alone, Switzerland gains a claim on Austrian Vorarlberg (enabling expansion via the Gotthardbund path).

Switzerland's geopolitical position is unique: completely surrounded by potential enemies or unstable neighbors, yet its neutrality status and the game's mechanics give it unusual protection. It cannot be easily subjugated but is equally locked out of early aggression or faction support, forcing a defensive or focus-tree-driven strategy.
