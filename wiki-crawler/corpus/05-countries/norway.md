## Summary

Norway in 1936 is a minor democratic power in Northern Europe with a population of 3.09 million and a neutral foreign policy. It borders Sweden and Finland and possesses significant aluminum resources (20 units), making it strategically valuable. Without Arms Against Tyranny DLC, Norway uses the generic national focus tree; with the DLC enabled, it gains a unique nine-branch tree allowing paths toward various ideologies and a potential Nordic Council faction.

## Government and political situation

Norway starts under **Democratic** governance with **Johan Nygaardsvold** (Arbeiderpartiet party) as ruling leader. Nygaardsvold holds the trait "Popular Reformer," providing +5% stability and +5% political power gain at the cost of -3% war support.

Elections occur every 3 years with the next scheduled for October 1936. The Democratic ideology permits guaranteeing other nations' independence and lowers world tension through guarantees, but prevents puppet creation, justifying war goals without world tension, and declaring wars on same-ideology nations without casus belli.

**Ideological paths available** (via focus tree):

- **Democratic (historical):** Continue Nygaardsvold's Government branch leads to "The Broken Gun" national spirit, cutting military industry.
- **Communist:** A Resurgent Labor Movement branch splits into Stalinist (allies Soviet Union) and Trotskyist/other communist paths.
- **Fascist:** The Stumbling Storting branch allows Quisling's fascist coup or Vidkun Quisling ascension.
- **Monarchist:** The Royal Chamberlain's Overture path restores King Haakon VII with Peder Anker Wedel-Jarlsberg as regent.

## Unique mechanics

Norway has a unique focus tree (requires Arms Against Tyranny DLC) divided into nine branches:

- **A Resurgent Labor Movement:** Communist path with Stalinist and Trotskyist options.
- **Vote of No Confidence:** Democratic rearmament branch; includes "Replace Laake" focus to arrest Vidkun Quisling and prevent his civil war. Forsvarets Intelligence Service requires La Résistance DLC.
- **Continue Nygaardsvold's Government:** Historical democratic path favoring civilian industry with "The Broken Gun" spirit.
- **The Stumbling Storting:** Fascist and Monarchist expansion paths; Quisling's Coup available under both democratic and fascist branches.
- **Start Rearmament:** Army branch modifying "The New Hæren" spirit.
- **Building an Independent Norway:** Industrial branch modifying "An Independent Norway" spirit.
- **Hærens Flyvevaaben:** Air force branch modifying "Hærens Flyvevaaben" or "Luftforsvaret" spirits.
- **Sjøforsvaret:** Naval branch modifying naval industry spirit.
- **Form the Nordic Council:** Shared joint branch for the five Nordic nations (Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Sweden, Norway); allows any faction member to complete focuses once a Nordic country joins another faction.

Without Arms Against Tyranny, Norway uses the generic focus tree. See [06-focus-trees/norway.md](../06-focus-trees/norway.md) for the full focus tree.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

Norway shares land borders with **Sweden** and **Finland** to the east. In 1936, Norway pursues a **strict neutral foreign policy**, refusing guarantees or military commitments to any power.

Without Arms Against Tyranny DLC, Norway remains geopolitically isolated. With the DLC enabled, the Democratic "Consolidate Power" sub-branch allows forming the **Nordic Council** faction with other democratic Nordic countries (Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Sweden), providing a mutual defense mechanism against external threats.

Norway begins with no guarantees and no faction membership. Its coastal position and aluminum reserves make it a target for German invasion (historically invaded 9 April 1940), but the focus tree offers diplomatic and military options to resist or accommodate such pressure depending on chosen ideology path.
