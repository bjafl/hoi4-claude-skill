## Summary

Hebei-Chahar is a Chinese warlord state in northeastern China, established under General Song Zheyuan to govern Hebei and Chahar provinces. With a large population but minimal industry, it begins under Kuomintang rule and faces pressure from the Japanese Empire, Manchukuo, and Mengjiang to the north. The country can pivot toward Communist or Nationalist alignment or attempt independence as a rival warlord state through its shared warlord focus tree.

## Government and political situation

Song Zheyuan rules as the starting leader under Kuomintang (Despotism). The country's political parties are:
- **Kuomintang** (100% popularity, ruling): Song Zheyuan leads
- **China Democratic League (CDL)**: 0%, Generic leader
- **Communist Party of China (CPC)**: 0%, Generic leader
- **Blue Shirt Society (BSS)**: 0%, Generic leader

After completing the "Secure Internal Politics" focus, Song Zheyuan can serve as leader of CDL (Socialism), CPC (Anti-Revisionism), or BSS (Rexism) ideologies, offering flexibility in ideological alignment. The country can change its name based on ideology: Republic, Soviet Republic, Free State, or Political Council if remaining a warlord subject.

## Unique mechanics

Hebei-Chahar shares the Warlords national focus tree with other Chinese warlord states. The tree offers three main branches:

1. **Secure Internal Politics** — Political alignment choices (Nationalist, Communist, or rival government).
2. **Develop the Capital State** — Industrial and economic development.
3. **Strengthen Warlord Authority** — Bonuses to cavalry or mountaineer units, and modification of the Warlord State spirit.

The country has access to multiple military industrial organizations (design companies) when specific conditions are met:
- **Sino-German Technical Mission** (Heavy Tank Designer): Requires peace with Germany or completion of "Chinese Panzers" focus.
- **Morozov Design Bureau** (Fast Tank Designer): Requires peace with Soviet Union and completion of "Hire Soviet Designer" focus.
- **CAMCO** (Medium Aircraft Designer): Available after "CAMCO," "Local Fighter Production," or "Local Bomber Production" focuses.

Herbei-Chahar does not exist in the 1939 start date but can be released as a puppet or independent nation.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Neighboring states:**
- **North:** Japanese Empire, Manchukuo (puppet of Japan), Mengjiang (puppet of Japan)
- **South:** China (Nationalist)
- **Southeast:** Shandong Clique (Chinese warlord)
- **West:** Shanxi (Chinese warlord)

The country begins independent but faces immediate pressure from Japanese expansion. Integration into the Chinese warlord system offers paths to join the Nationalist Chinese faction, Communist Chinese faction, or remain as a rival warlord state. Trade and military cooperation with Germany, Italy, or the Soviet Union is possible depending on chosen focuses.
