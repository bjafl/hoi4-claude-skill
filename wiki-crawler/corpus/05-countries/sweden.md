Sweden is a minor democratic nation in Northern Europe with strong industrial capacity and extensive natural resources, particularly iron ore. Rich in manpower and resources relative to other Scandinavian countries, Sweden pursues strict neutrality and is not faction-aligned at game start, though it faces ideological and strategic pressures throughout the war.

## Government and political situation

Sweden starts under the **Social Democratic Party** (Democratic ideology) with **Per Albin Hansson** as prime minister, enjoying 75% party popularity. The ruling party is characterized as having broad support and faces elections every 4 years, with the next election scheduled for September 1936.

**Democratic mechanics (starting ruleset):**
- Cannot declare war on same-ideology countries without a war goal
- Can guarantee other ideologies
- Guarantees lower world tension
- Cannot puppet countries
- Cannot create collaboration governments

**Alternative leaders available through focus tree:**
- **Axel Pehrsson Bramstorp** (Liberal, Bondeförbundet): Available via Defense Act event or 1936 election; brings +5% war support as "Rearmament Proponent"
- **Ernst Wigforss** (Democratic): Available if government falls; provides +10% civilian/infrastructure/railway/synthetic refinery construction speed
- **Gösta Bagge** (Conservative, Högerns Riksorganisation): Available via Defense Act or 1936 election; +6% research speed but -5% stability
- **King Gustaf V** (Centrism, the Royalist Movement, requires focus): Available after completing "King in Council"; starts with -10% political power gain but substantial bonuses to entrenchment and command power (+15% command power gain multiplier)
- **Nils Flyg** (Marxism, Socialistiska Partiet): Communist path leader; provides -10% justify war goal time
- **Birger Furugård** (Nazism): Available via Fascist focus path; provides -10% justify war goal time and +2% recruitable population but -10% stability
- **Tage Erlander** (Democratic): Backup leader if Per Albin Hansson's mobile HQ is destroyed

The focus tree offers paths toward Communist ideology (via "Strike the Capitalist Beast Down" focus), Fascism/Monarchism (via "Prevent a Red Dictatorship" and "The Old Enemy Stirs"), or deepening democratic commitment via Nordic Defense Council.

## Unique mechanics (Arms Against Tyranny)

Sweden has a unique national focus tree with 2 main branches and 15 subbranches:

**Defense Act Branch:** Allows balancing social policies against military/civilian industrial capacity; unlocks decisions on civilian or military industry focus, and Stability vs War support trade-offs.

**Government Weapons Contract Sub-branch:** Grants bonuses to army; unlocks unique equipment (Bicycle Infantry, Lynx Armored Car).

**Military production sub-branches:** Form SAAB (air force bonuses), Götaverken (navy bonuses), Seize Landsverk/Allow Landsverk to Operate Independently (defensive vs offensive military posture).

**Ideological paths:**
- **Continue the Second Industrial Revolution → Communist path:** Unlocks Communist advisors from other countries, allows fomenting communism in Nordic countries, and opens "Scandinavian Defense Union" (form a Communist faction with Nordic allies).
- **Democratic path:** "Democratic War Cabinet" sub-branch allows preparing for war and saving Denmark/Norway; "Nordic Defense Council" sub-branch enables forming a puppet union of Nordic nations under Swedish command.
- **Monarchist/Fascist path ("Prevent a Red Dictatorship"):** Available after "The Old Enemy Stirs"; centralizes power under King Gustaf V or pivots to Fascism.

**Nordic-specific mechanics:**
- "Scandinavian Defense Union" branch: Shared by Communist and Democratic paths; allows forming a faction or joining another faction of the same ideology.
- "Swedish Volunteer Corps / Åland Question / Finland's Cause is Ours:" Allows sending volunteers to Finland during war, claiming Åland, or defending Finland against Soviet invasion.
- "Form the Nordic Council" branch: Allows either decentralizing the Nordic Defense Council into a loose council or centralizing it into a puppet union ("Nordic Defense Council member" status).

**Diplomatic options:**
- "Delegation to Berlin" sub-branch: Requests German political support; Sweden may become a German puppet or resist and become "The New Order" (seek independence).
- "The Counter Revolution" sub-branch: Expansion toward Baltic and Nordic nations; if King Gustaf V is in power, unlocks option to crown him Gustavus Adolphus II and form the "Eternal Nordic Empire."

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering countries:**
- Norway (west, north)
- Finland (east)
- Denmark (south, across Öresund strait)

**Strategic position:** Sweden is surrounded by potential conflict zones (Finland-Soviet border, Norway-Germany proximity) but maintains legal neutrality. Historically allowed German military transit ("Permittenttrafik") and supplied Germany with iron ore (~58% of German iron ore imports by war's end), which historically enabled Swedish neutrality preservation.

**Faction status:** Unaligned; not member of Axis, Allies, or Comintern at 1936 start. Focus tree offers paths to join any faction or form Nordic bloc.
