## Government and political situation

**Ruling party:** Mongol Ardiin Nam (Communist), led by Anandyn Amar as head of government. The Comintern-aligned communist regime maintains control with 100% popularity at game start.

### Political pathways

Mongolia can pursue alternate ideologies through political events and decisions:

- **Democratic:** Mongolian Parliamentary Republic (0% starting popularity, generic leader)
- **Fascist:** New Mongol Empire (0% starting popularity). Special leader Jodbajab (Spirit of Genghis trait: cavalry attack +10%, naval bomber range +10%, naval attack +10%) available via event "Mongolian Pressure for Submission"
- **Non-Aligned:** Great Mongolian State (0% starting popularity). Jodbajab also available for this path via same event

### Notable Leaders

- **Anandyn Amar** (starting, Communist): No special traits
- **Khorloogiin Choibalsan** (Communist, 1939 event): Pan-Mongolian Stalinist trait — communism support +0.05 daily, recruitable population +5%, war support +10%, justify war goal time –10%
- **Jodbajab** (Fascist/Non-Aligned): Spirit of Genghis trait — cavalry attack +10%, naval bomber range +10%, naval attack +10%

### Starting Ministers and Advisors

**Government positions:**
- Sonin Zhanshan: Captain of Industry — civilian factory construction +10%, infrastructure +10%, railway +10%, synthetic refinery +10%

**Political advisors:** Includes Communist Revolutionary, Democratic Reformer, Fascist Demagogue (generic options), plus Compassionate Gentleman, Quartermaster General, War Industrialist

**Military advisors:** Theorists available for land, naval, and air doctrine (–10% doctrine cost each)

**Field commanders:** Horloogiyn Choybalsan (Army Morale Expert), Ölziytrygyn Badrak (Army Defense Expert), V. G. Pyrselov (Naval Maneuver Expert), Dashdorj (Commerce Raiding Expert), Sükhbaataryn Yanjmaa (All-Weather Expert), Gomgchigryn Bumtsend (Air Reformer Expert)

## National spirits

No national spirits. Mongolia begins with a blank slate.

## Unique mechanics

No unique mechanics documented on the wiki. Mongolia uses the generic national focus tree (Army/Aviation/Naval/Industrial/Political Effort branches) and standard game systems.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

### Bordering nations

- **North:** Soviet Union, Tannu Tuva
- **West:** Sinkiang
- **South:** Xibei San Ma, Shanxi, Mengkukuo
- **East:** Manchukuo

### Faction and diplomacy

**Faction:** Comintern (alongside Soviet Union and Tannu Tuva)

**Diplomatic vulnerability:** Mongolia is targeted by China's "Demand Mongolia" national focus, which allows China to claim Mongolian territory or invite Mongolia into a Chinese-led faction. The Soviets (per Waking the Tiger DLC) typically do not demand Chinese territory in peace conferences, making Soviet military support crucial for defending against Chinese expansion.

### Military-industrial organizations

Mongolia has access to research bonuses through hiring design companies: Industrial Concern, Electronics Concern, Tank Designer (Armor Company), Ship Designer (Naval Company), Aircraft Designers (light, medium, heavy, naval-focused), Motorized Equipment Designer, Infantry Equipment Designer (Small Arms Company), and Artillery Designer.
