## Summary

South America hosts a diverse collection of formable nations covering various historical reconstructions, ideological transformations, and indigenous liberation movements. These formables are primarily available through the Trial of Allegiance DLC via focus trees (especially Chile and Argentina) and decision systems, though some are base-game accessible. Formation typically requires controlling specific states and completing ideological/focus-tree prerequisites. Most formables offer ideology-dependent naming variants.

## Gran Colombia

A restored historical confederation unifying Colombia, Venezuela, Ecuador, Panama, Guyana, and Peru. Any of these five nations can form Gran Colombia; Peru gains additional cores on all Peruvian states when forming.

**Formation requirement (decision: Revive Bolívar's Legacy):** Control Pastaza (490), Loreto (491), Panamá (304), all Ecuadorean states (Ecuador 305, Galapagos Islands 649), all Colombian states (Meta 486, Cundinamarca 306, La Libertad 493), and all Venezuelan states (Zulia 489, Miranda 307, Bolívar 488).

**Cores gained:** Pastaza, Loreto, Panamá, Panamá Canal (685), Curaçao (695), British Guyana (687), plus all listed Ecuador, Colombia, and Venezuela states.

| Ideology | Country name |
|---|---|
| Democratic | Gran Colombia |
| Communist | Red Colombia |
| Fascist | Bolívar Empire |
| Non-Aligned | New Granada |

**Note:** Peru can form both Gran Colombia and Peru-Bolivian Confederation.

## Peru-Bolivian Confederation

A historical confederation (1836–1839) spanning Peru and Bolivia, available to either nation once it fully annexes the other.

**Formation requirement (decision: Revive the Confederation):** Own and control Loreto (491), Lima (303), Ucayali (494), Arequipa (492), Santa Cruz (487), La Paz (302), Tacna-Moquegua (947), plus originating from either Bolivia (with Peru non-existent) or Peru (with Bolivia non-existent).

**Cores gained:** All formation-controlled states plus Pastaza (490), Chaco Boreal (688), Antofagasta (506), Formosa (957), Los Andes (959), Arica y Tarapacá (951), Acre (940), Guaporé (942).

| Ideology | Country name |
|---|---|
| Democratic | United Peru-Bolivian Confederation |
| Communist | Peru-Bolivian Federal People's Republic |
| Fascist | Grand Peru-Bolivian Confederation |
| Non-Aligned | Peru-Bolivian Confederation |

## Guianan Federal Republic

Unifies Guyana, Suriname, and French Guiana under a single nation. Available to Guyana, Suriname, or Cayenne after they become independent from their colonial rulers (UK, Netherlands, France).

**Formation requirement (decision: Unite the Guianas):** Control Suriname (309), French Guiana (310), and British Guyana (687).

**Cores gained:** Suriname, French Guiana, British Guyana; also gains cores on Bolívar (488), Amapá (939), Rio Branco (969), Amazon (968) as hidden cores.

| Ideology | Country name |
|---|---|
| Democratic | Guianan Federal Republic |
| Communist | Guianan Soviet Socialist Republic |
| Fascist | Empire of Guiana |
| Non-Aligned | Grand Kingdom of El Dorado |

## Federal League

Uruguay's formable nation, restoring a confederation that existed 1815–1820. Requires annexing northeastern Argentina.

**Formation requirement (decision: Reform the Federal League):** Uruguay must be fully independent and control Paysandú (946), Montevideo (300), Cerro Largo (945), Región Mesopotámica (510), and Santa Fe (956).

**Cores gained:** Región Mesopotámica, Santa Fe.

| Ideology | Country name |
|---|---|
| Democratic | Federal League |
| Communist | Socialist Republic of Uruguay |
| Fascist | New Constitutional Uruguay |
| Non-Aligned | Federal League |

## Greater Paraguay & Greater Uruguay

Parallel formables available to Paraguay and Uruguay via a shared focus tree branch ("Beneath the Shadow of the Triple Alliance" for Paraguay; "Rekindle Old Gripes" for Uruguay). Players must choose which to pursue. Both require white-peacing Brazil and Argentina during the shared focus "Demand a Seat at the High Table."

**Greater Paraguay — Formation requirement:** Control Punta Porá (504) and Formosa (957).

**Greater Uruguay — Formation requirement:** Control Rio Grande Do Sul (502) and Región Mesopotámica (510).

**Cores gained:** All controlled formation states.

| Ideology | Greater Paraguay | Greater Uruguay |
|---|---|---|
| Democratic | Greater Paraguay | Greater Uruguay |
| Communist | Greater Paraguay | Greater Uruguay |
| Fascist | Greater Paraguay | Greater Uruguay |
| Non-Aligned | Greater Paraguay | Greater Uruguay |

## Plurinational State of Bolivia

Democratic Bolivia can adopt this national spirit through a decision, gaining bonuses to non-core territory.

**Formation requirement (decision: Embrace Plurinationalism):** Bolivia must be democratic and control at least one non-core state.

**Effects:** Add national spirit "Democratic Plurinationalism": +25% Non-Core Manpower, +15% Stability, +10% Division Defense on Core Territory, +0.05 Daily Democracy Support.

| Ideology | Country name |
|---|---|
| Democratic | Plurinational State of Bolivia |
| Communist | Bolivia Izquerdīsta |
| Fascist | Falangist Bolivia |
| Non-Aligned | Bolivia |

## Grand Federation of Amerindian Peoples

Chile's end-goal non-aligned path, forming through a civil war where Chile becomes the Mapuche State, then liberates indigenous peoples across the Americas and unites them. Accessible via focus path "Look to Tradition" → "Address the Mapuche Conflict" → "Avenge the Pacification of Araucanía" (civil war), then unlocking decisions to liberate and federate indigenous states throughout North/South America and Oceania, culminating in focus "Unified by Will."

**Formation requirement:** Complete focus "Unified by Will" after liberating and core-gaining indigenous subject states (Inca Empire, United Inuit States, Independent State of Samoa, etc.).

**Cores gained:** Nearly all states in North and South America, with notable exceptions: Buenos Aires (278), San Luis y La Pampa (958), Mendoza (511), San Juan y La Rioja (960), Tucumán (508), Los Andes (959), Chaco Austral (509), Santa Fe (956) cannot be cored.

| Ideology | Country name |
|---|---|
| All ideologies | Grand Federation of Amerindian Peoples |

## Reino De La Patria Nueva

## Second Argentine Republic

Argentina's democratic formable, available via completion of focus "The Second Argentine Republic" at the end of the democratic focus branch. Upon formation, Argentina guarantees the independence of all democratic South American nations.

**Formation requirement:** Complete focus "The Second Argentine Republic."

| Ideology | Country name |
|---|---|

## The Fourth Reich

Argentina's secret fascist formable, unlocked via hidden focus branch triggered by specific conditions: Argentina must complete focus "Work With The Nationalists" AND Berlin must fall (be occupied) while Adolf Hitler remains Germany's leader. Within one month of Berlin's fall, an event fires offering control of Argentina to "a strange visitor"; accepting reveals the hidden focus branch and allows forming The Fourth Reich via focus "Fourth Time's The Charm." As The Fourth Reich, Argentina gains cores on all German states, war goals vs. non-fascist South American nations, nuclear technology, and continues the focus path.

**Formation requirement:** Complete "Work With The Nationalists," wait for Berlin occupation with Hitler as leader, accept the visitor event, complete focus "Fourth Time's The Charm."

**Cores gained:** All German states.

|---|---|
| All ideologies | The Fourth Reich |
