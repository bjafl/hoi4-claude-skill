Siam is a minor Southeast Asian nation in a fragile political state after a 1932 coup established a semi-constitutional government. In 1936, it remains Non-Aligned under military control; historically, it shifts to Fascism in 1939. Small military and industrial base limit immediate power, but abundance of manpower and rubber resources provide strategic potential. Vulnerable to pressure from neighboring Japan, France, and Britain, yet positioned to potentially control Indochina.

## Government and political situation

Siam is ruled by the **Khana Ratsadon** (People's Party), a military organization that seized power via coup in 1932, forcing the absolute monarch Rama VII into constitutional government. By 1936, power has consolidated under the military wing led by **Phraya Phahon**, who holds the position of Prime Minister and serves as the starting leader. Phahon is characterized as politically pragmatic, granting +8% Political Power Gain and reducing economy laws cost by 15%.

Tensions between the military and civilian factions of the Khana Ratsadon remain high; one minor scandal could topple the government. Three other ideological parties exist with negligible support:

- **Khana Ratsadon - Civilian** (Democratic): Led by Pridi Banomyong, offers foreign affairs expertise with -25% improve relations cost.
- **Communist Party of Thailand**: Minimal presence; would enable Soviet alignment.
- **Fascist faction** (20% popularity): Led by Plaek Phibunsongkhram, offering +5% military factory construction and +5% land doctrine mastery. Historically becomes ruling ideology by 1939.

Alternative government names available: **Kingdom of Siam** (Non-Aligned), **Republic of Thailand** (Democratic), **Thai People's Republic** (Communist), **Siamese Empire** (Fascist).

Path to other ideologies proceeds via national focuses in the generic focus tree (or Thunder at Our Gates unique tree if enabled). Fascism historically becomes dominant in 1939.

## Unique mechanics

**Generic focus tree** (without Thunder at Our Gates DLC): Siam uses the standard focus tree with five main branches: Army Effort (army experience/research), Aviation Effort (air experience/bases), Naval Effort (navy experience/dockyards), Industrial Effort (factories/infrastructure/research slots), and Political Effort (ideology shifts).

**Thunder at Our Gates DLC**: Siam receives a unique focus tree introducing two competing national spirits representing the Military and Civilian wings of the Khana Ratsadon; maintaining balance is required to avoid civil war. The focus tree emphasizes limited wars to recover Indochinese territories from France without full-scale conflict.

**Formation of Rattanakosin Kingdom**: If Siam controls Laos, Cambodia, Bassac, and Lan Chang (currently French-held), it may form a larger Southeast Asian state, granting additional manpower and building slots.

**Indochina targets**: The game script enables Siam to declare limited wars (decisions, not full justification) on Vichy France, Burma, and British Malaya to recover occupied Indochinese provinces, with white-peace options once objectives are met.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering nations:** British Burma (west), French Indochina/Laos (east), British Malaya (south)
**Sea access:** Gulf of Thailand, Andaman Sea

**Diplomatic isolation:** Siam is diplomatically isolated, with relations primarily driven by ideology matching. Non-Axis nations (Allied, Comintern) begin with -10 opinion due to ideological difference.

**Great-power pressure:**
- **Japan**: Multiple focuses target Siam (Occupy Siam, Pressure Siam, Dominate Siam, Occupy Indochina, Spread the Revolution South). Japan may invite Siam to its faction or declare war.
- **France**: Controls neighboring Indochinese colonies (Laos, Cambodia, Vietnam). War is likely if Siam pursues territorial expansion.
- **China/Chinese factions**: Multiple Chinese shared-branch focuses can target Siam (Dominate Siam) if unified under Nationalist, Reorganized, or Communist rule.
- **United States**: May influence Siam via Secure Asia focus, potentially puppeting or invading.
- **United Kingdom**: Guarantees can be offered; controls Malaya and Burma.

**Historical trajectory:** Siam historically joined Japan's faction in 1941 but was occupied. In-game, the player may align with Japan (especially if war breaks out in China), join the Axis via Germany, or remain independent and conquer Indochina with limited wars before facing pressure.

## Military

**Army (1936):** 4 divisions of Royal Thai Infantry (9 battalions each), 36K manpower
**Navy (1936):** 1 Heavy Cruiser (Ratanakosindra), 1 Light Cruiser (Ratanakosindra), 7 Destroyers, 15 Convoys, ~3K manpower
**Air Force (1936):** 60 Fighters (base game) / 128 Fighters (By Blood Alone), 12 Close Air Support, 72 planes total

The navy is minimal but functional for regional control; the air force can be expanded via research and production. Both expand slightly by 1939 with additional cruisers and aircraft.

## Technology

Siam begins with tech in basic infantry equipment, early artillery, inter-war air frames, and early naval units. Access to both conventional and Japanese-licensed equipment names (Type 95 Light Tank, Ki-30 fighter, etc.). Research proceeds slowly with 2 initial slots; 3 more unlock via focus tree.

> ⚠️ Master table reports 9 states; wiki geography section lists only 7 named states (Isan, Lanna, Southern Siam, Western Siam, Central Siam, Eastern Siam, Bangkok State). The discrepancy may reflect unreleased or merged states in different game versions.
