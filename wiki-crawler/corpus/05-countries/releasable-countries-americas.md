Releasable countries in the Americas are minor nations that major powers can release as puppets, dominions, or independent states through various political paths. Most are currently under colonial control (UK, France, Netherlands, Denmark) or regional dominance (USA, Mexico, Canada). Most also appear as independent nations at game start if the "Americas Decolonized" Custom Game Rule is enabled. All start with the Monroe Doctrine national spirit.

## National spirits

All releasable countries in the Americas begin with:

**Monroe Doctrine** — _Not covered on the wiki._ Restricts their ability to join factions or be guaranteed.

## Bahamas

**Tag:** BAH  
**Capital:** Nassau  
**Initial ideology:** Democratic (75% Democratic, 1% Communist, 1% Fascist, 23% Non-Aligned)  
**Cores in:** United Kingdom  
**Release trigger:** UK must complete Decolonization. Released as Dominion if UK completes Self-Government For the Americas.  
**Available in Americas Decolonized rule:** Yes

Two states: Northern Bahamas (40,000 pop, 1 VP, Nassau) and Southern Bahamas (15,000 pop, Plains). Can form Antillean Confederation.
