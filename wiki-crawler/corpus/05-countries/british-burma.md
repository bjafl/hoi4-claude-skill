British Burma is a British colony in Southeast Asia with 8.63 million core population. It controls extensive tungsten reserves (212 reserves) but limited industrial capacity at game start (1 military factory, 3 civilian factories). As a subject of the United Kingdom, it is locked to democratic ideology by default but can pursue alternative paths through its focus tree or changes in overlord status.

## Government and political situation

Burma begins as a democratic subject-state of the United Kingdom, governed by The Dobama Asiayone party under Archibald Cochrane (a democratic conservative). As a colony, Burma is politically subordinate to British decisions on warfare and diplomacy.

Political paths available through focus and ideology shifts:

| Ideology | Party | Leader | Effect / Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Democratic (current) | The Dobama Asiayone (Thakins) | Archibald Cochrane (starting) / U Nu (post-decolonization) | Starting path; U Nu gains +10% stability, +5% political power gain, +0.05 daily democracy support upon Britain decolonizing |
| Communist | Burma Socialist Programme Party | Ne Win | +5% war support, +10% recruitable population, +0.05 daily communism support |
| Fascist | Mon Dynasty | Ba Maw | +10% war support, +0.05 daily fascism support |
| Non-Aligned | Konbuang Dynasty | Empress Myat Phaya Lat | Justify war goal time: -15%, +10% political power gain, +0.05 daily non-aligned support |

Burma lacks unique government mechanics beyond standard ideology-switching. Potential changes include independence (requiring British decolonization or conquest), Japanese puppet status (if occupied), or transition to other ideologies via internal pressure.

## National spirits

Burma does not have any national spirits at game start.

## Unique mechanics

Burma uses the generic national focus tree (5 branches: Army Effort, Aviation Effort, Naval Effort, Industrial Effort, Political Effort) rather than a country-specific one. The focus tree provides options to shift ideology and unlock additional research slots.

No country-specific decisions or events are documented on the wiki beyond standard political path mechanics.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Borders:**
- West: British Raj (United Kingdom)
- North: Yunnan (independent warlord)
- Northeast: China (Nationalist or other faction)
- East: French Indochina, Siam
- South: Sea access

**Status:**
- Colony (subject) of the United Kingdom at game start
- Can gain independence if Britain is forced to decolonize (e.g., through military defeat or specific events)
- Vulnerable to Japanese invasion once Japan enters the war (similar to other British Southeast Asian territories); wiki notes Burma can be prone to invasion by Yunnan if independent
- 1 air base and 2 naval bases provide minimal defensive infrastructure

Burma's strategic value lies in its tungsten reserves and its position blocking Japanese access to India via Siam. Historically, it became a major battleground in WWII after Japan's 1942 invasion, leading to occupation and a Japanese-backed puppet government (State of Burma under Ba Maw). The wiki notes that Ba Maw's fascist path mirrors this historical trajectory, while U Nu's democratic path reflects post-war independence movements.
