Northeastern Army (XIC) is a Chinese warlord state in northern China controlled by the Young Marshall Zhang Xueliang. Also known historically as the Fengtian Army, it occupies Manchurian and northern Chinese territory but sits amid a fractured Chinese political landscape, facing constant pressure from Japan, rival warlords, and ideological competitors. Its path is constrained: it can cooperate with Nationalist China, join the Communists, or stand alone—but non-1936-start dates eliminate it entirely.

## Government and political situation

Northeastern Army starts under the Kuomintang ideology with full stability in Zhang Xueliang's Kuomintang party (100% popularity). Zhang holds the title "Young Marshall" with traits reflecting his role: Stability −10% Defensive War Penalty, but Defensive War Penalty Stability Modifier +30%.

Three alternative ideologies are available after completing the **Secure Internal Politics** focus:

- **Socialism** — leads the China Democratic League (CDL), same traits
- **Anti-Revisionism** — heads the Communist Party of China (CPC), same traits
- **Rexism** — chairs the Blue Shirt Society (BSS), same traits

All three alternate leaders are Zhang Xueliang (representing ideological shifts by the same historical figure).

Zhang was historically tasked with suppressing Chinese Communist forces in 1935 but was eventually impressed by CCP discipline and patriotic messaging. In November 1936, he famously orchestrated the Xi'an Incident, kidnapping Nationalist leader Chiang Kai-Shek and extracting a nominal agreement to end the Chinese Civil War and resist Japan. However, Chiang repudiated these promises upon release, imprisoned Zhang, and deployed 37 KMT divisions to encircle the Northeastern Army. Historical divisions led Northeastern to formally dissolve and transfer its forces to Nationalist control by February 1937. In-game, the Northeastern Army does not exist in any 1939 start scenario and cannot be released.

## Unique mechanics

Northeastern Army's national focus tree is shared with other Chinese warlords (via the Warlords tree) plus one exclusive branch: **The Bandit Suppression Headquarters Branch**.

The **Bandit Suppression branch** splits into two distinct paths:

1. **Leftmost path** — Improves relations with the Chinese Soviet Republic and Sinkiang, pressures Nationalist China to form a United Front against Japan. May trigger the **Xi'an Incident** event chain (historically accurate to November 1936).
2. **Rightmost path** — Declares war on the Chinese Soviet Republic, requests Chiang Kai-Shek's relocation to Hebei-Chahar, and restores the Fengtian Clique. Unlocks war for control of all China and grants access to the Nationalist Chinese Warlord focus tree.

Three other warlord branches are available:

- **Secure Internal Politics** — Opens political alignment choices (Nationalist cooperation, Communist cooperation, or Opposition/independent government).
- **Develop the Capital State** — Industrial development (shared tree).
- **Strengthen Warlord Authority** — Modifies Warlord State spirit; grants bonuses to cavalry or mountaineers (shared tree).

Upon path completion, Northeastern Army gains access to either the **Nationalist Chinese Warlord** or **Communist Chinese Warlord** national focus tree depending on ideology choice.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering states:**
- Shanxi (east)
- China (southeast)
- Sichuan Clique (south)
- Qinghai Ma (west)
- Gansu Ma, Ningxia Ma, Chinese Soviet Republic (north)

Northeastern Army begins at peace with all neighbors. No starting diplomatic guarantees or faction memberships are mentioned on the wiki. Its strategic position is defensive but pivotal: control of northern Chinese territory and proximity to both the Communist-held areas (north/west) and Nationalist heartland (south) make it a potential linchpin in the Second United Front unification event chain if it chooses to cooperate.
