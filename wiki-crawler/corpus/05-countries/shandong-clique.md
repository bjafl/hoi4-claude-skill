## Government and political situation

**Starting ruler:** Han Fuju (Kuomintang/Despotism), trait: Unreliable Opportunist

Starting party (100% popularity): Kuomintang. Three opposition parties exist with 0% popularity: China Democratic League, Communist Party of China, Blue Shirt Society.

### Political dynamics and ideological paths

Han Fuju can shift ideologies after completing the "Secure Internal Politics" focus:
- **Socialism (CDL):** Han Fuju remains leader; trait Unreliable Opportunist
- **Anti-Revisionism (CPC):** Han Fuju as leader; same trait
- **Rexism (BSS):** Han Fuju as leader; same trait
- **Despotism (current state):** Han Fuju remains

A secondary leader, Sun Tongxuan, can come to power via the event "Han Fuju Flees Jinan," with traits Trickster and Infantry Leader.

## Unique mechanics

### Focus tree and ideology shifts

Shandong Clique uses the shared **Warlords national focus tree** with two major branches:

1. **Secure Internal Politics** — allows ideological divergence toward Cooperation With the Nationalists, Cooperation With the Communists, or Opposition (leading to a rival government)
2. **Develop the Capital State** — industrial branch
3. **Strengthen Warlord Authority** — modifies the Warlord State spirit; unlocks bonuses for cavalry or mountaineer divisions

At the end of the tree, access to shared Nationalist and Communist focus trees for warlords is available. See [06-focus-trees/china-warlords.md](../06-focus-trees/china-warlords.md) for the full focus tree.

### Industrial and military designers

Available through completion of specific focuses:
- **Sino-German Technical Mission** — Heavy Tank Designer (if controlling Liaoning 716 or at peace with German Reich)
- **Morozov Design Bureau** — Tank Designer (requires Hire Soviet Designer focus, at peace with USSR)
- **CAMCO** — Medium Aircraft Designer (via CAMCO focus and variants)
- **Jiangnan Shipyard Group** — Coastal Defense Fleet Designer (controls Shanghai 613)
- **Dagu Shipyard** — Raiding Fleet Designer (controls Beiping 608)
- Arsenal designers (Taiyuan, Liaoning, Hanyan, Mukden) unlock via territorial control

No Compromise, No Surrender DLC unlocks additional industrial concerns and designers (e.g., Shanghai Electronics, Shanghai Power Company, Bank of China, Yongli Chemical Industries).

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Direct borders:** China (south), Hebei-Chahar (west and north).

**Starting position:** Non-aligned warlord state, initially unaligned in major factions. The focus tree offers paths to ally with Chinese Nationalist or Communist governments, or to declare independence and establish a rival regime. The "Opposition" sub-branch allows instigating border clashes with neighboring Chinese states and (optionally) siding with Japan.
