## Summary

Qinghai Ma is a minor Chinese warlord state in northwestern China, ruled by Ma Lin as part of the Ma Clique collective. With minimal industry, no resources, and only 2.59M population, it begins in a precarious position but can leverage cavalry units and the Ma Clique's shared mechanics. The game requires the *No Compromise, No Surrender* DLC; without it, the country starts as the larger Xibei San Ma confederation. Qinghai Ma's primary strategic path involves consolidating the Ma Clique, securing early focuses for industrial development, and choosing between nationalist, communist, or independent alignment.

## Government and political situation

Qinghai Ma begins under the Ma Clique party in despotic form, led by **Ma Lin**. Three other ideologies are represented but unpopular: China Democratic League (socialism, led by Mao Sun), Communist Party of China (Stalinism, led by Hsia Sun), and Blue Shirt Society (Rexism, led by Hsia Jin).

**Key political paths:**
- Secure Internal Politics focus unlocks several options:
  - Send Ma Lin on Hajj: Makes Ma Bufang the ruling leader (Ma Bufang stat line: skill 3, attack 4, defense 2, planning 2, logistics 2, **Cavalry Officer** trait)
  - Diarchy option: Both Ma Lin and Ma Bufang can co-lead
- Party switches available via focus completion: CDL (socialism), CPC (Stalinism), BSS (Rexism), or remain Ma Clique (despotism)

Ma Lin traits: Devout Leader (PP gain +5%, stability +5%).

## Unique mechanics

**Form Xibei San Ma:** Qinghai Ma can unify the Ma Clique by conquering Gansu Ma, Khotan Ma, and Ningxia Ma via the decision **Unify the Hui States** (available after completing relevant focuses). This creates a larger warlord confederation and unlocks different country names and focus branches.

**The Military Governor Branch:** A unique focus line available only to Qinghai Ma that represents the power struggle between Ma Lin and Ma Bufang. The player can choose to send Ma Lin on a hajj (promoting Ma Bufang) or establish a diarchy.

**Sideline Family Conflicts Branch:** A shared branch with other Ma Cliques that allows the player to demand submission or set up war goals against Gansu Ma, Khotan Ma, and Ningxia Ma, facilitating their annexation and core integration. This expedites formation of Xibei San Ma.

**Develop Inter-Province Ties Branch:** Shared among all Ma Cliques to modify the Ma Clique Collective modifier and ultimately form the **Ma Clique Collective** faction.

**National focus tree options:** Qinghai Ma can complete the Warlords shared national focus tree (expanded with NCNS), with branches to cooperate with Nationalist China, Communist China, or remain independent with border clashes. At the focus tree's end, warlords may inherit the Nationalist or Communist Chinese focus trees.

> ⚠️ Focus tree branches (Power Struggle, Yan'an Incident, Proclaim Rival Government, etc.) are not fully documented in this summary. See [06-focus-trees/china-warlords.md](../06-focus-trees/china-warlords.md) for the complete focus tree.

**Technology cosmetic names:** Infantry equipment and tank designations are renamed to Chinese standards (e.g., Type 24, Type 36).

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Borders:** Gansu Ma (northeast), Northeastern Army (east), Sichuan Clique (southeast), Tibet (southwest), Khotan Ma (northwest).

**Faction status:** Part of the Ma Clique collective. Can form the **Ma Clique Collective** faction via focus completion, which provides shared bonuses and unifies the warlord states.

**Diplomacy:** Like all Chinese warlords and Nationalist China, Qinghai Ma is at war with the Japanese Empire in 1939 and may be drawn into conflicts with the Nationalist-Communist Chinese civil war. Guarantees or military access arrangements depend on chosen focus path (cooperation with Nationalist/Communist vs. opposition).

**Diplomatic situation note:** The Ma Cliques are historically aligned and mechanically tied; early coordination with sister warlords (particularly via the Sideline Family Conflicts branch) is strategically important for consolidating the region before larger factions (Japan, Nationalist/Communist China) intervene.
