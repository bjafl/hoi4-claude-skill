## Summary

Belgian Congo is a minor African nation under Belgian control in 1936, requiring the Götterdämmerung DLC for unique content. It controls 8 states, commanding substantial territory but suffering severe colonial penalties to stability, research, and political power. The country can pursue independence through multiple paths — democratic, communist, or monarchist — with vastly different post-independence outcomes.

## Government and political situation

Belgian Congo starts as a non-aligned colonial possession of Belgium with **80% Non-Aligned** popularity under the **Gouvernorat Général du Congo** party led by **Pierre Ryckmans**. A secondary Democratic faction (ABAKO) leads 20% under **Joseph Kasa-Vubu**, with Communist and Fascist movements at 0% and effectively dormant.

The starting government has no elections. Independence routes exist:
- **Democratic** (20% baseline): Via focus **Kasa-Vubu's First Cabinet**, leads to **Congo-Léopoldville** government under Kasa-Vubu with **Stability +20%** and **Daily Democracy Support +0.02**
- **Communist** (0% baseline): Via focus **Found the Congolese National Movement**, can reach 40%+ to unlock **The Lion Roars** branch, proclaiming a **People's Republic of the Congo**, aligning with the Soviet Union
- **Neutral/Monarchist** (accessible post-independence): If both Democratic and Communist fall below 40%, an **Assemble a Regency Council** branch allows election of a monarch, spawning six sub-branches: **Kingdom of Kongo**, **Kingdom of Bakuba**, **Kingdom of Loango**, **Kingdom of Burundi**, **Kingdom of Rwanda**, or **Kingdom of Mittelafrika** (pro-German option)

## Unique mechanics

Belgian Congo receives a **unique 9-branch focus tree** (requires Götterdämmerung):

- **Belgian Congo Branch**: Joint shared branch with Belgium; builds Force Publique, industrial infrastructure, and determines Congo's future
- **Congolese Businessowners Branch**: Removes **The Invisible Roof** spirit (requires independence)
- **Governor-General Pierre Ryckmans Branch**: Strengthens colonial regime; builds industry, develops states, decreases autonomy
- **Found the Congolese National Movement / National Committee for Liberation Branch**: Bifurcates independence — left side diplomatic (supports Democratic popularity), right side violent civil war (supports Communist/Fascist popularity, invites German or Soviet aid)
- **The Congo and the War in Europe Branch**: Becomes available if Belgium enters a war; provides resources to allies (UK, South Africa), gains US supplies, enables independence with British backing
- **Establish the Armée Nationale Congolaise Branch**: Military buildup branch; enhances national spirit effects, develops navy/air force/army
- **Kasa-Vubu's First Cabinet Branch**: Post-independence democratic path; lowers **An Uncontrollably Vast Nation** effects, builds industry, grants claims to Congo Basin (requires 40%+ Democratic popularity)
- **The Lion Roars Branch**: Post-independence communist path; forms **People's Republic of the Congo**, allies with Soviet Union, builds pan-African resistance; can form **African Union** on the right sub-branch (requires 40%+ Communist popularity)
- **Assemble a Regency Council Branch**: Post-independence neutral path (less than 40% of Democratic or Communist); selects one of six monarch sub-branches with distinct cultural and economic bonuses

Without Götterdämmerung, Belgian Congo is non-releasable; with it, the **Congo-Léopoldville** variant can be released via Africa Decolonized custom game rules, starting with only **An Uncontrollably Vast Nation** and **Force Publique** spirits.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

Belgian Congo is surrounded by **Belgium** (overlord), **France** (western border), **United Kingdom** (southern border via Uganda), and **Portugal** (southern/eastern borders). It controls releasable nations: **Burundi**, **Rwanda**, and **State of Katanga**.

As a Belgian puppet, it is initially non-aligned and subject to Belgian policy. Early game strategies typically involve building up the Force Publique and industry while deciding on a path to independence; the choice dramatically affects available alliances and military doctrine.
