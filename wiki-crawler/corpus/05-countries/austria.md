Austria is a minor Central European power with a population of 6.43 million. It borders Germany and Czechoslovakia to the north, Switzerland to the west, Italy and Yugoslavia to the south, and Hungary to the east. Austria begins under the conservative non-aligned Vaterländische Front party but has multiple ideological paths available through its focus tree, including democracy, communism, and monarchy, as well as fascism aligned with either Germany or Italy. The country faces severe economic constraints from the Treaty of Saint-Germain-en-Laye but possesses significant potential for industrial and military development through its extensive focus tree options added in the Götterdämmerung DLC.

## Government and political situation

Austria begins as a **Non-Aligned nation** under the conservative **Vaterländische Front** party with Kurt Schuschnigg as Chancellor. The ruling party represents moderate authoritarian corporatism (Austrofascism) but stops short of full Nazi ideology.

**Political parties and leaders:**
- **Vaterländische Front (Non-Aligned, 48% popularity, ruling)**: Kurt Schuschnigg (Chancellor), Joseph August von Habsburg (alternate)
  - Schuschnigg trait: "Abolished Austrofascism" (+5% stability, +0.05 daily democracy support, +10% surrender limit)
- **DNSAP (Fascist, 50% popularity, not ruling)**: Arthur Seyss-Inquart (Nazi Sympathizer: +0.10 daily fascism support), Edmund Glaise Horstenau (alternate)
- **Sozialdemokratische Partei Österreichs (Democratic, 1% popularity)**: Karl Renner (Resolute Social Democrat: +5% stability, +5% political power gain, +0.10 daily democracy support)
- **Kommunistische Partei Österreichs (Communist, 1% popularity)**: Alfred Klahr (Marxist Philosopher: +10% political power gain, +5% research speed, +0.05 daily communism support)

Austria has no elections. The player can pursue ideological shifts through the focus tree to embrace democracy, communism, monarchy, or strengthen fascism.

The Treaty of Saint-Germain-en-Laye is the most restrictive spirit, severely limiting military expansion until July 1940 when it expires.

## Unique mechanics

Austria has extensive unique mechanics tied to its Götterdämmerung DLC focus tree:

### Focus tree branches

Austria's focus tree offers seven major branches with multiple sub-branches:

- **Repeal the May Constitution**: Shift away from Austrofascism toward democracy, communism, or monarchy; opens communist, democratic, and monarchist sub-branches; can trigger civil war if pursuing communist path
- **Totalitarian Safety Branch**: Historical fascist path aligned against Germany with Italian support; modifies the Austrofascism spirit
- **Regulate the Finance Sector**: Recover from economic depression; increase resource extraction, infrastructure, and building slots
- **Österreichische Luftstreitkräfte**: Air force bonuses; air base levels; radar stations
- **Secret Rearmament**: Army bonuses; land forts; design companies; upgrade Österreichisches Bundesheer modifier
- **Re-Establish the Navy**: Naval industry and technology bonuses; upgrade Österreichische Kriegsmarine modifier
- **Indivisible and Inseparable (Austro-Hungarian joint branch)**: Cooperative branch with Hungary; unlocked via "The Danubian Federation" or "Demand Hungarian Submission" focuses; allows formation of Austria-Hungary

Key focus options:
- "The Danubian Federation": Create a faction including democratic or non-aligned Germany, the Soviet Union, or other Central European nations
- "Demand Hungarian Submission": Puppet Hungary and unlock the joint Austro-Hungarian branch
- "Reinstate the Herrenhaus" (Monarchist path): Reinstall Otto von Habsburg as leader or Ernst Rüdiger Starhemberg
- "Reform the Central Powers": Prevent Germany from pursuing Anschluss; allows formation of a Central Powers faction with Bulgaria and Turkey

### Starting modifiers

- Österreichisches Bundesheer (Army modifier)
- Österreichische Luftstreitkräfte (Air force modifier)
- Österreichische Kriegsmarine (Naval modifier, though Austria has no navy)

### Cosmetic names

Austria has unique names for equipment across different tech years (e.g., Steyr rifles, ADGR motorized vehicles, ADGZ armored cars).

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Land borders:**
- Germany (north) — primary threat; Germany will pursue Anschluss around 1937–1938 if Austria remains independent
- Czechoslovakia (north)
- Switzerland (west)
- Italy (south) — can be allied if pursuing fascist path
- Yugoslavia (south)
- Hungary (east) — can be puppeted via monarchist path

**Notable diplomatic considerations:**
- Austria has no starting alliances or faction membership
- Germany will demand Anschluss as historical pressure; this can be prevented by completing "Reform the Central Powers" focus
- Austria can join the Comintern (communist path), the Allies (democratic path), or form its own faction (Danubian Federation path)
- Italy may provide support to Austria if pursuing the Totalitarian Safety fascist branch
- Austria can guarantee or be guaranteed by other nations through focuses and decisions

Austria historically faces annexation by Germany around 1938–1939 but can extend independence through careful focus selection and alliances.
