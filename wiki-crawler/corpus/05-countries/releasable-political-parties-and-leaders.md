Releasable countries are nations that can be released as puppets or liberated by major powers during gameplay. Each releasable country has a defined starting ideology and political structure with available alternative government paths. This page catalogs the political leadership and party structures for all releasable nations.

## Structure

Each releasable country can shift between Democracy, Communism, Fascism, and Non-Aligned ideologies (or a subset thereof). The starting ideology is marked as "In power (default)". Alternative ideologies unlock through focus trees, events, or internal political shifts depending on ideology support and political power expenditure. Named historical leaders appear for certain ideologies; Generic leaders are used when named leaders are unavailable.
