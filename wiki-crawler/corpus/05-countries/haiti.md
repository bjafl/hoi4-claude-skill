Haiti is a small Caribbean nation on the island of Hispaniola with minimal industrial capacity and few natural resources. Economically crippled by post-US occupation recovery (1934 onwards), it begins the game guaranteed by the United States under the Monroe Doctrine. Despite its weakness, Haiti can pursue various ideological paths and is a candidate for forming the Antillean Confederation with neighbouring Caribbean nations.

## National spirits

**Monroe Doctrine**
Haiti's independence is guaranteed by the United States. This spirit reflects the defensive guarantee placed upon Haiti and is tied to both countries' allegiance to the Monroe Doctrine.

## Unique mechanics

_No unique mechanics documented on the wiki._ Haiti uses the generic national focus tree (no country-specific tree). The focus tree offers five branches: Army Effort, Aviation Effort, Naval Effort, Industrial Effort, and Political Effort. Through the Political Effort branch, Haiti can shift its ideology and unlock additional options.

Haiti has access to a roster of generals, admirals, and designers with various traits (e.g., Military Theorist, Cavalry Expert, Commerce Raiding Expert) and military industrial organizations for armor, aircraft, naval, and motorized equipment design.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering countries:**
- Shares Hispaniola island with Dominican Republic to the east
- Located west/southwest of Cuba (across sea)

**Diplomatic status:**
- **Guaranteed by United States** via Monroe Doctrine national spirit (both Haiti and the US guarantee each other's independence)
- **Faction:** Not aligned to any faction at game start
- **Trade:** Operating under Export Focus trade law (+10% construction speed, +5% research speed, +10% factory/dockyard output)
- **Conscription:** Volunteer Only (1.5% recruitable population at start)
- **Economy:** Civilian Economy law

**Historical context (for LLM context):** The US occupied Haiti from 1914–1934, leaving infrastructure improvements but crippling the economy. The Haitian Communist Party was banned in 1936 due to internal security fears. The 1937 Parsley massacre by Dominican forces against Haitians will occur mid-game. Haiti historically joined the Allies in 1941, providing limited shipping and manpower; the US financed rubber plantations there during WWII.
