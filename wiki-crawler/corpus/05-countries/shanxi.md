Shanxi (SHX), the Shanxi Clique, is a Chinese warlord state in northern China. Led by General Yan Xishan, it historically pursued neutrality and moderate modernization. In 1936, it is ruled by the Kuomintang under Yan Xishan's leadership, with access to alternate ideology focus paths (Nationalist, Communist, Anarchist). As a warlord state, it suffers significant penalties to political power, stability, research, and command power, but benefits from increased recruitable population and reduced attrition.

## Government and political situation

Shanxi begins under Kuomintang rule with Yan Xishan as both head of state and military leader. Three other political parties exist at 0% popularity:
- China Democratic League (CDL, Socialism) — Zhang Shen
- Communist Party of China (CPC, Anarchist Communism) — Luo Zhao
- Blue Shirt Society (BSS, Nazism) — Liao Ting

Via the "Secure Internal Politics" focus, Yan Xishan can become the leader of alternate ideologies (Socialism, Anti-Revisionism/Communism, Rexism/Fascism). The focus tree allows three political paths: Cooperation With Nationalists, Cooperation With Communists, or Opposition (which can lead to a civil war and adoption of any ideology).

Alternate government names reflect ideology: Shanxi Republic (democratic/socialist), Shanxi Soviet Republic (communist), Shanxi Yan State, or Shanxi Clique (if a warlord subject/integrated warlord subject of China).

## Unique mechanics

Shanxi accesses the shared **Warlords national focus tree** alongside other Chinese warlords. At the end of the tree, it may inherit Nationalist or Communist Chinese focus trees. With No Compromise, No Surrender enabled, the tree expands and grants access to new shared Nationalist and Communist branches.

Focus tree structure (base game):
- **Secure Internal Politics:** Base political focuses for warlords.
- **Cooperation With Nationalists:** Replace focus tree with Nationalist Chinese Warlord tree.
- **Cooperation With Communists:** Can replace and annex Chinese Soviet Republic with Soviet support; tree becomes Communist Chinese Warlord tree, inheriting all national spirits.
- **Opposition:** Instigate border clashes with other Chinese factions, side with Japan, then declare a rival government; civil war possible, with final tree determined by ideology.
- **Develop the Capital State:** Industrial focus branch.
- **Strengthen Warlord Authority:** Modify Warlord State spirit and boost cavalry or mountaineer divisions.

Yan Xishan grants bonuses when appointed as army chief (skill 3; traits: Career Officer, Infantry Officer): Non-Combat Out-of-Supply Penalties −6%, Army Experience Gain +0.30 daily.

Military industrial organizations and design companies unlock through focus completion or state control (e.g., Sino-German Technical Mission for heavy tanks, Taiyuan Arsenal for artillery, CAMCO for aircraft).

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering countries (1936):**
- East: Hebei-Chahar
- South: China (Nationalist), Northeastern Army
- Southwest: Chinese Soviet Republic
- West: Ningxia Ma
- North: Mongolian People's Republic, Mengjiang

**Status:** Warlord state, independent but militarily weaker than major factions. Japan will eventually present a wargoal. Shanxi can ally with Japan via focus or remain independent and compete with other warlords and China's unified factions.

**Note on 1939 onward:** By 1939, Shanxi ceases to exist as an independent country; all its states are owned by Communist China but controlled by Japan and its puppets.
