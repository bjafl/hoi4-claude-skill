Nicaragua is a minor Non-Aligned nation in Central America ruled by President Anastasio Somoza García after his 1936 coup. With only 0.68M population spread across a single state and minimal industrial capacity, it relies on the Monroe Doctrine guarantee from the United States. Nicaragua uses the generic national focus tree and can form the United States of Central America by controlling all independent Central American nations.

## Government and political situation

**Ruling party:** Non-Aligned (70% popularity) under Anastasio Somoza García, who seized power in a 1936 coup. He is Despotism ideology type. Cabinet ministers available for hire include Benjamin Lacayo Sacasa (Popular Figurehead, +15% stability) and Leonardo Argüello Vargas (Armaments Organizer, -20% civilian-to-military factory conversion cost).

**Political parties and potential leaders:**
- **Non-Aligned** (70%, ruling): Anastasio Somoza García — Despotism
- **Democratic** (15%): Partido Liberal Nacionalista (PLN), led by Juan Bautista Sacasa (Liberalism)
- **Communist** (10%): Francisco Leiva (Stalinism); would rename to Sandinista Nicaragua
- **Fascist** (5%): José Francisco Leiva; would rename to Nicaraguan Junta

No elections. Democracy and Communism are ideological options via generic focus tree Political Effort branch.

## National spirits

Nicaragua starts with:

**Monroe Doctrine:** The United States guarantees Nicaragua's independence. This national spirit prevents external conquest and reflects historical US dominance in Central America. Nicaragua plays as non-aligned in-game but is under de facto US protection.

## Unique mechanics

No unique mechanics documented on the wiki. Nicaragua uses the generic national focus tree with five branches (Army Effort, Aviation Effort, Naval Effort, Industrial Effort, Political Effort). Each branch grants experience, builds infrastructure, or unlocks ideology options.

**Formation option:** Nicaragua can form the **United States of Central America** by conquering all independent Central American nations (excluding Panama). This grants cores on conquered territories plus British Mexico (state 311), significantly boosting its power base.

Uses the generic national focus tree — see [01-mechanics/national-focus.md](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md).

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering states:** Costa Rica (southwest), Honduras (north). Pacific Ocean to the west; Caribbean Sea to the east.

**Diplomatic status:** Guaranteed by the United States via Monroe Doctrine national spirit. No faction membership at game start. Nicaragua is a minor nation with minimal military, industrial, and resource capacity.

**Historical note:** Nicaragua was one of five nations comprising the former United States of Central America (1824-1841). It was the first to secede in 1838. By 1936, under Somoza García's dictatorship, the nation focused on consolidating power. During WWII (declared 1941 in-game historical context per wiki), Nicaragua allowed Allied access to airfields and harbors but provided no combat troops, receiving obsolete Lend-Lease equipment as a result.
