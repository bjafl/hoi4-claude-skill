## Summary

Yugoslavia is a multinational Balkan regional power with a population of 13.78M, located in the western Balkans bordered by Austria, Hungary, Italy, Albania, Greece, Bulgaria, and Romania. It begins as non-aligned under Prince Paul's Regency Council, deeply fractured by ethnic tensions (Croatian, Macedonian, Slovene nationalism) and plagued by negative national spirits. The country's unique focus tree offers multiple ideological paths—remaining independent, joining the Axis, embracing communism, or allying with the Western bloc—with the potential to expand significantly in the Balkans through puppeting or annexing weaker neighbors.

## Government and political situation

**Ruling Party:** Regency Council (Non-aligned), led by Prince Paul acting as regent for the underage King Peter II (born 1923).

**Political Composition:**
- Regency Council (Non-aligned): 60% support
- Demokratska stranka (Democratic): 15% support
- Komunisticna partija Jugoslavije (Communist, Leninism): 15% support
- Jugoslovenska radikalna skupnost (Fascist): 10% support

**Path Options:**

- **Despotism → Democracy:** Via Milan Grol (Liberalism/Conservatism) or Peter II coming of age after the focus "End the Regency"
- **Despotism → Leninism:** Via "Abolish the Monarchy" focus; Josip Broz Tito becomes leader
- **Despotism → Fascism:** Via "Sign the Tripartite Pact" or "The Coronation" (Paul I variant)

Peter II as a leader carries traits: as "Underage Monarch" he grants +10% Stability but costs +25% for Conscription Laws, Trade Laws, Economy Laws, and Political Advisors; once he matures ("Legitimate Heir"), he grants +10% Political Power Gain.

These spirits can be partially or wholly removed via the focus tree, particularly through paths that grant autonomy to constituent states or reform the government into a Federal Republic (via communist path).

## Unique mechanics

**National Focus Tree:** Yugoslavia has a unique 6-branch focus tree:

1. **Western Focus** — Centralization vs. autonomy, choice between Non-aligned/Allies/Axis alignment
2. **Recognize the Soviet Union** — Path to communism; choose Comintern membership or independent "Yugoslav Federation"
3. **Industrialization Program** — Resource and factory gains; option to prioritize Serbian provinces or equalize development
4. **Modernize the Air Force** — Air force expansion; choose between domestic production or foreign licensing
5. **Army Modernization** — Army expansion and research bonuses
6. **Expand the Shipyards** — Navy expansion and research bonuses

**Notable Focuses:**
- "End the Regency" (after Sep 6, 1941 in vanilla timeline) — Peter II matures, gains +10% Stability and +10% Political Power Gain
- "Abolish the Monarchy" — Transitions to communism; requires 40% support
- "Yugoslavian path to Communism" (Titoism) — Independent communist path, guaranteed by Soviet Union but not in Comintern
- "Form the Federal Republic" — Removes all negative national spirits, grants cores on puppet states (if any)
- "The Coronation" — If Prince Paul is leader, can install puppet "Paul I" with fascism support

**Expansion via Focus Tree:** Completing reunification focuses ("A United Kingdom" branch) grants cores over puppeted territories (Hungary, Bulgaria, Transylvania if puppeted); not over Romania, Greece, or Albania.

See [06-focus-trees/yugoslavia.md](../06-focus-trees/yugoslavia.md) for the full focus tree.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Borders:** Austria, Hungary, Italy, Albania, Greece, Bulgaria, Romania

**Starting Alignment:** Non-aligned, no faction membership

**Diplomatic Status:**
- No starting alliances or guarantees
- Holds cores and claims on Italian-controlled **Istria, Zara, and Littorale**

**External Pressure:**
- **Germany:** Possesses national focuses "Demand Slovenia" and "Fate of Yugoslavia"; may partition Yugoslavia or puppet Croatia
- **Italy:** Can demand Dalmatia, Montenegro, or use decision "Ultimatum to Yugoslavia"; possesses "Officialize Bashkimi Kombetar" (removes Yugoslav cores from Kosovo, assigns to Italian puppet Albania)
- **France:** If France completes "Little Entente" national focus, Yugoslavia can join alongside Czechoslovakia and Romania

**Strategic Assessment:** Despite nominal insignificance, Yugoslavia is contested by all Balkan powers and both Axis and Allied blocs; the 3-power guarantee (if achieved via Little Entente) is critical for survival against Axis pressure.

## Strategies

**Powerhouse of the Balkans** (Non-aligned path): Complete "Western Focus" and "Reinforce Old Alliances," expand via justification or wait for world tension. Puppeting Hungary, Bulgaria, and others via focus tree "A United Kingdom" path creates a core manpower pool exceeding 24M and 30+ civilian factories, making Yugoslavia a major regional power capable of resisting German or Italian invasion.

**Road to Titoism** (Communist path): Pursue "Recognize the Soviet Union" branch to 40% communist support (via "Form Peasants Council" or "Soviet Economic Aid" with or without Anti-Communist raids), then "Abolish the Monarchy" and "Yugoslavian path to Communism." Complete "Form the Federal Republic" to remove all negative spirits. Yugoslav guarantees from Soviet Union, ~16 equipped infantry divisions by war start.

**Early Fascism** (Axis path): Begin "Western Focus" with Fascist advisors; disband non-essential units to win civil war at 100 PP. Target Austria first, then Hungary and Greece (latter pulls in Romania); war with Italy over cores is viable before Feb 1939. Completing "Reunited Kingdom" path grants buffs and manpower; swap to Democratic path for "King Coronation" stability and war support bonus.
