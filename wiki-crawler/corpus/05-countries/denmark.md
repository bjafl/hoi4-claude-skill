Denmark is a minor democratic nation in Northern Europe with 3.75M population. It controls 7 states including the Jutland peninsula, Danish islands (Fyn, Zealand, Bornholm), and overseas territories (Greenland, Faroe Islands, Iceland). Denmark starts neutral and isolated militarily, facing pressure from German expansion. The Arms Against Tyranny DLC provides a unique focus tree; without it, Denmark uses the generic tree.

## Government and political situation

Denmark is a constitutional monarchy under democratic rule, though the King (Christian X) is not yet head of government as of 1936. The ruling Socialdemokratiet holds 94% of political support.

**Starting ruling party:** Socialdemokratiet (Democratic)

**Other political parties and their leaders:**
- Danmark Kommunistiske Parti (Communist, 2%): Aksel Larsen
- Danmarks Nationalsocialistiske Arbejderparti (Fascist, 1%): Frits Clausen
- Bondepartiet (Authoritarian, 3%): Knud Bach

**Alternative leaders via events:**
- Christmas Møller (Conservative Politician) — war support +10%, military factory construction speed +5%
- Erik Scavenius (Liberal Politician) — improve relations cost -25%
- Vilhelm Buhl (Socialist Politician) — consumer goods factor -15%, economy laws cost -15%
- Aksel Larsen (Communist Politician) — communism support drift, consumer goods factor -20%
- Frits Clausen (Fascist Populist) — war support +10%, conscription cost -10%
- Christian X (Sovereign of Elefantordenen, via The King Assumes Power focus) — stability +5%, war support +5%, all military advisors cost -15%
- Frederik IX (Sovereign, if Christian X dies) — same bonuses as Christian X

Denmark pursues a neutral foreign policy. Iceland is a subject state (dominion).

## National spirits

With Arms Against Tyranny DLC enabled, Denmark begins with three national spirits:

**Neglected Military**
- Mobilization speed: -20%
- Surrender limit: -20%
- Planning speed: -10%

*Reflects Denmark's historical disarmament and underfunded military before German pressure.*

**Economic Crisis**
- Monthly population: -10%
- Weekly stability: -0.10%
- Daily democracy support: -0.05
- Daily unaligned support: +0.02

*Represents the interwar economic downturn and weak faith in democratic institutions.*

**Danish Industrial Capabilities**
- Consumer goods factories factor: +30%
- Production efficiency retention: -5%
- Factory output: -10%
- Dockyard output: -10%
- Equipment conversion speed: -5%

*Denmark's economy prioritizes civilian production; military industry must be developed from a weak base.*

Without Arms Against Tyranny, Denmark uses the generic national spirits.

## Unique mechanics

Denmark's unique national focus tree (Arms Against Tyranny) is divided into five branches:

**Continue the Kanslergadeforlig / Fortify Our Borders**
- Industrial development in mainland and overseas territories (Greenland, Faroe Islands)
- Ability to request trade deals with the United Kingdom or German Reich

**Samarbejdsregering** (Cooperation Government)
- Only available if Denmark becomes a puppet
- Sub-branches to seek independence (resisting overlord), sanction sabotages (aid from overlord's enemies), or align with Germany (cooperate with overlord for independence)

**Reaffirm Neutrality**
- Main political branch introducing Welfare vs Warfare Balance of Power mechanics
- Sub-branches for Democratic, Monarchist, and Fascist paths
- Prepare for war or maintain civilian welfare focus

**Sign Forsvarsforliget** (Defense Agreement)
- Military upgrades to army, navy, and air force
- Modifies national spirits: Hæren (army), Søværnet (navy), Flyvevåbnet (air force)
- Only available if Denmark is not a puppet (or has joined overlord's faction)

**Form the Nordic Council**
- Joint branch for Nordic nations (Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Finland)
- When one Nordic nation creates a faction with another, any member can complete any Nordic focus

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Direct borders:**
- Germany to the south (the primary strategic threat)
- Sweden to the east (naval crossing)

**Subject states:**
- Iceland (dominion)

**Overseas territories:**
- Greenland
- Faroe Islands

**Starting diplomacy:** Neutral foreign policy with no guarantees or alliances.

**Historical note:** Denmark was invaded by Germany in April 1940 as part of Operation Weserübung. Unlike Norway, the Danish military offered no resistance, and Germany maintained lenient occupation policies, allowing Denmark to retain its constitutional monarchy.
