A collection of 12 minor nations that can be released or liberated by major powers controlling territories in Oceania and the Pacific. All but Vanuatu are released automatically when the "Oceania Decolonized" Custom Game Rule is enabled. Most share cores with their overlords (e.g., Bougainville, Solomon Islands, and Papua New Guinea all share cores with Australia) and can form the Polynesia faction.

## Bougainville

**Tag:** BOU  
**Capital:** Buka  
**Starting ideology:** Democratic  
**Core holder:** Australia  
**Release method:** Oceania Decolonized rule (Yes)

Shares core with Papua New Guinea and Solomon Islands. Can form Polynesia through a decision.

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| States | 1 |
| Population | 60,000 |
| Victory points | 21 |
| Terrain | Jungle (4 provinces) |
