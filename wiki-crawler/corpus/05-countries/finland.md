Finland is a minor Scandinavian country with a strategic position between Germany and the Soviet Union. Historically neutral but militarily capable, it faces significant pressure from its Soviet neighbor and must carefully navigate the ideological and military conflicts of the 1930s and 1940s.

## Government and political situation

Finland starts under a Non-Aligned government led by **Pehr Evind Svinhufvud** (Kansallinen Kokoomus, 65% support). The country operates as a republic with regular elections. Other political parties present include:

- **Suomen Sosialidemokraattinen Puolue** (Social Democrat): 15% support, led by Väinö Tanner
- **Isänmaallinen Kansanliike** (Fascist): 19% support, led by Vilho Annala
- **Suomen Kommunistinen Puolue** (Communist): 1% support, led by Aimo Aaltonen

Finland can pursue various ideological paths through its focus tree (requires Arms Against Tyranny DLC for unhistorical branches):

- **Non-Aligned:** Historical path emphasizing neutrality with options to form Nordic alliances or seek security from major powers
- **Fascist:** Via civil war, allowing potential Axis alignment and Nordic dominion claims
- **Communist:** Via civil war, leading to Red Finland or alignment with the Soviet Union
- **Democratic:** Through the socialist path, enabling potential Allied alignment

The country may also convert to a monarchy under Prince Wolfgang von Hesse through the hidden "Finnish Throne" focus.

## National spirits

**Sisu** (starting spirit)
- Division Recovery Rate: +5%
- Division Defense on core territory: +10%

This spirit reflects Finnish resilience and determination, particularly relevant to its historical defensive capabilities during the Winter War.

## Unique mechanics

Finland gains unique mechanics and options through its national focus tree:

- **Greater Finland:** Can be formed through nationalist/fascist focuses, allowing expansion into neighboring Nordic and Baltic territories
- **Nordic Council:** Shared focus branch with other Nordic nations (Norway, Sweden, Denmark) enabling joint diplomatic and military actions
- **Nordic League:** Special decision to form a faction with other Nordic/Baltic states as an alternative to joining the Allies, Axis, or Comintern
- **Molotov Cocktails:** Anti-tank capability unlocked through industrial focuses
- **Unique military spirits:** Can upgrade national spirits for the Army, Navy, and Air Force through military budget expansion focuses
- **Military doctrine bonuses:** Access to unique doctrines and equipment names reflecting Finnish military design

Finland also starts with multiple viable ministers and military advisors including **Åke Henrik Gartz** (industrial specialist), **Aimo Lahti** (military industrialist), and others providing various economic and military bonuses.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Direct borders:**
- **Norway** (north): Neutral/Non-Aligned
- **Sweden** (northwest): Neutral/Non-Aligned
- **Soviet Union** (east): Communist, primary military threat

**Sea borders:** Baltic Sea to south and west

Finland occupies a critical strategic position between the Axis and Comintern blocs. As a minor power with modest industry but solid defensive terrain, it must carefully manage relationships with larger neighbors. Without established ideological commitment, Finland has significant diplomatic flexibility but faces constant pressure from the Soviet Union—historically manifesting in the Winter War (1939–1940) and later the Continuation War (1941–1944).

Historical note: Finland was one of only three European nations in WWII whose capital was never occupied (the others being the United Kingdom and the Soviet Union).
