## Summary

Amerindian Nations are a collection of releasable countries representing indigenous peoples across the Americas and Pacific. Most can be released via Chile's "Crusade Against Imperialism" decision or gain early-start eligibility through the "Americas Deimperialized" custom game rule. All start Non-Aligned by default (or Democratic with Americas Deimperialized enabled) with councils as ruling parties and multiple ideological pathways for government transition.

## Charrúa Nation

**Tag:** CHR  
**Capital:** Montevideo  
**Starting cores:** Uruguay  
**Starting government:** Non-Aligned (elections every 4 years; next February 1938)

Released via Chile's "Crusade Against Imperialism" decision. Also releasable at game start with "Americas Deimperialized" custom game rule enabled. Starting party: Charrúa Council (70% popularity). Unique leaders available with ideology switches: councils lead all four ideological variants (Democratic, Communist, Fascist, Non-Aligned).

## Confederated Canadian First Nations

**Tag:** CAN (as first_nations_canada)  
**Capital:** Ottawa  
**Starting cores:** Canada, France, United Kingdom  
**Starting government:** Non-Aligned with "Crusade Against Imperialism" release; Democratic with "Americas Deimperialized" enabled (elections every 5 years; next 1940)

Shares cores with Quebec and United Inuit States. Released via "Crusade Against Imperialism" decision. With "Americas Deimperialized" enabled, starts as Democratic under Liberal Party of Canada (98% ruling majority). First Nations Council leads across ideologies.

## Grand Kingdom of El Dorado

**Tag:** SUR (as united_guiana)  
**Capital:** Paramaribo  
**Starting cores:** France, Netherlands, Brazil, United Kingdom, Venezuela  
**Starting government:** Non-Aligned (no elections) or Democratic with "Americas Decolonized" rule

Also known as Suriname. Shares cores with Guyana, Great Pindorama, and Cayenne. Released via "Crusade Against Imperialism" or "Satellite Kingdoms" decisions. With "Americas Decolonized" enabled, releases as Republic of Suriname (Democratic, no elections). Three special Despotism leaders available via "Satellite Kingdoms" focus: Bartolomé Blanche, Gregorio Rodríguez Tascón, Quintín Lame (each grants +10% Stability, +10% War support).

## Great Pindorama

**Tag:** BRA (as indigenous_brazil)  
**Capital:** Rio de Janeiro  
**Starting cores:** Brazil (Second Brazilian Republic)  
**Starting government:** Non-Aligned with "Crusade Against Imperialism" release; Democratic with "Americas Deimperialized" enabled (elections every 4 years; next 1938)

Also known as Indigenous Brazil. Shares cores with Grand Kingdom of El Dorado, Guaraní Nation, Inca Empire, and Plurinational State of Bolivia. Released via "Crusade Against Imperialism" decision. With "Americas Deimperialized" enabled, starts Democratic under Provisional Government (49% initial, leads to 51% after shift).

**Tag:** CHL (as CHL_mapuche_state)

**Starting cores:** Argentina, Chile, United Kingdom
