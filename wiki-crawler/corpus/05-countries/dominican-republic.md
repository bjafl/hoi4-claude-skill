Dominican Republic is a small Caribbean island nation with 1.25M population controlling one state (Hispaniola) alongside Haiti. It begins 1936 as a fascist dictatorship under Rafael Trujillo with minimal industry and military, guaranteed independence by the United States via the Monroe Doctrine. The country lacks a unique focus tree and relies on the generic tree for all ideological and military advancement paths.

## 1936 starting position

**Tag:** DOM

**Geography:** Single state on the island of Hispaniola in the Caribbean, east of Cuba.

Starting military: 1 infantry division (División de Infanteria, 4 infantry battalions), 5 convoys. Manpower: ~1,250,000 total; ~4,000 used by current divisions.

**Economy:** Civilian economy law; Volunteer Only conscription; Export Focus trade law (+10% construction and research speed, +10% factory output, 50% resources to market).

## Government and political situation

Dominican Republic starts as **Fascist** with no elections. The ruling party is **Partido Dominicano** (100% popularity) led by **Rafael Trujillo**.

Opposition parties present but suppressed:
- **Democratic** (0%, leader Jacinto Gonzáles) — becomes ruling ideology if player chooses democratic path
- **Communist** (0%, leader Fernando Bayo) — becomes ruling ideology if player chooses communist path
- **Non-Aligned** (0%, leader Horacio Cabral) — alternative fascist/despotic path

Possible future leader: **Jacinto Peynado** (Fascist, available 1939) under Partido Dominicano.

Available government ministers include industrial/diplomatic organizers (Elias Brache, Manuel de Jesús Troncoso, Jacinto Bienvenido Peynado) and military staff across doctrine, maneuver, naval, and air specializations. No leader traits are listed for Trujillo or Peynado.

## National spirits

Dominican Republic starts with one national spirit:

**Monroe Doctrine**
- Effect: Independence guaranteed by the United States. (In-game: prevents United States from freely justifying war goals against DOM without triggering guarantor mechanics; solidifies US diplomatic protection.)

This guarantee persists unless the USA is conquered or DOM joins an opposing faction.

## Unique mechanics

No unique mechanics documented on the wiki. Dominican Republic uses the **generic national focus tree** for all advancement paths:

- **Army/Aviation/Naval Effort branches** grant experience and unlock research bonuses for respective military domains, plus construction of bases.
- **Industrial Effort branch** unlocks civilian and military factory construction (even beyond building-slot limits) plus infrastructure and additional research slots (+3 total).
- **Political Effort branch** enables ideological conversion and path selection (Democratic, Communist, Non-Aligned, or deeper Fascism).

See [01-mechanics/national-focus.md](../01-mechanics/national-focus.md) (generic tree) for the full generic tree.

Dominican Republic has no unique cosmetic unit names or equipment designer options beyond the generic military industrial organization menu.

## Neighbours and geography

**Direct neighbours:**
- **Haiti** (shares island, west of DOM)
- **Cuba** (nearby to the north, across water)

**Faction status (1936):** Unaligned. The Monroe Doctrine guarantee from the USA is diplomatic, not a faction alliance.

**Strategic position:** Island nation in the Caribbean. Borders on weak states (Haiti) and regional powers (Cuba, eventually United States involvement if war spreads). Suitable for regional conquest focused on Caribbean/Central American states (Costa Rica, Honduras, Nicaragua, Panama) and mainland South American expansion, though minimal starting military makes early expansion slow without focus-tree military buffs.

Historical note: After 1941, historical Dominican Republic declared war on Japan and joined the Allies; in-game, this is determined by player choice via political focuses and the faction system.
