## Summary

The British Raj (tag RAJ) is a non-aligned British colonial possession in South Asia, governing the Indian subcontinent with a core population of 251.19M and an additional 92.71M non-core population spread across 36 states (9 core, 27 colonial). Severely hampered by agrarian society, communal divisions (Marginalized Muslim Community spirit), and the Great Depression (with Graveyard of Empires DLC), the Raj begins under British suzerainty as a subject state but gains access to multiple independence paths through its focus tree, enabling transformation into a democratic dominion, communist state, fascist regime, or militarist sultanate. The game models India's complex political landscape with ideological competition between the Indian National Congress, Independence Movement, Communist Party, and Hindu nationalist forces.

## Government and political situation

**Starting government:** Freeman Freeman-Thomas (Despotism under British Imperial Rule) as head of state; actual governance is non-aligned ideology (Indian National Congress, 80% popularity).

**Key alternative leaders:**
- Lord Linlithgow (Despotism, The Last Viceroy) — can come to power via event "New Viceroy for the British Raj"
- Jawaharlal Nehru (Liberalism/Centrism, Socialist Modernizer) — via focus/event "A Question of Leadership"
- Mahatma Mohandas Gandhi (Liberalism, Spiritual Leader) — via independence completion or event
- Subhas Chandra Bose (Centrism/Fascism/Communism, Pragmatic Authoritarian or Nationalist Freedom Fighter depending on path) — via various focus routes
- Muhammad Ali Jinnah (Liberalism, All-India Muslim League Leader) — via event

**Political parties and routes to other ideologies:**
- Indian National Congress (Non-Aligned, 80%) — ruling
- India Independence Movement (Democracy, 17%)
- Communist Party of India (Communism, 1%)
- All-India Hindu Assembly (Fascism, 2%)

The "Onto the Road of Independence" focus branch is the primary path to leave British suzerainty. From there, the Mahatma sub-branch leads to democratic consolidation (Tryst with Destiny), the League Against Gandhism branch offers Fascist/Communist alternatives, and the Swaraj branch (requires "Onto the Road of Independence" completion) allows diplomatic or military independence. Remaining loyal to the Crown via the "His Majesty's Loyal Government" branch allows gradual autonomy gain without breaking the subject state status initially.

## Unique mechanics

**Focus tree structure (Graveyard of Empires):** The Raj has a 9-branch focus tree with multiple sub-branches, including:

- **Great Depression Price Controls** — Offers relief to the Great Depression while destabilizing the country; opens the Mughal and East India Company branches. The "Look to the Past" sub-branch leads to forming the Sultanate of Al-Hind; the "Revolt of the Princes" path unites Indian Princely States as puppets and enables war against the UK.
- **Onto the Road of Independence** — Primary independence pathway, unlocking political choice (Mahatma, League Against Gandhism, Swaraj routes).
- **His Majesty's Loyal Government** — Loyalty-to-Britain path requiring completion of three sub-branches (political, industrial, military) to become free without breaking subject status.
- **Keep British Generals / Indianize The Army** — Choice between relying on British officers and equipment or modeling the army on other major powers for themed bonuses.
- **East India Railways / Bombay Baroda and Central India Railway** — Infrastructure branch speeding supply hub, railway, and infrastructure construction.
- **Ammunition Factory Khadki** — Unlocks military factories and MIO (Military Industrial Organization) upgrades.
- **Royal Indian Artillery / Military Pensions** — Artillery, armor, and mountaineers buffs; enables Quinine technology (addresses Risk of Famine).
- **Local Recruitment Offices** — Grants manpower and reduces/removes Agrarian Society.
- **Indian Air Force** — Unlocks unique aircraft models and MIO variants.
- **Royal Indian Navy** — Naval industry and doctrines; choice between reliance on the UK or independent naval development.

**Ideological transformation paths:**
- **Mahatma sub-branch:** Democratic path allowing release of Pakistan and Bangladesh or keeping India united; leads to "Tryst with Destiny" with choice between Allies or neutrality, then annexation of Goa and French India, and economic/research bonuses.
- **I Am Death sub-branch:** Hidden path triggered if Gandhi survives assassination; turns India into an aggressive democracy with nuclear bonuses.
- **League Against Gandhism / Forward Bloc:** Fascist/Bose/Communist expansion paths. The Hindutva sub-branch forms Akhand Bharat; the Netaji path provides cooperation with Soviets or Axis (Communist or Fascist outcomes); the "Reds in the Shadows" branch triggers a civil war leading to Communist consolidation with Agrarian Socialism or Urban Industrialism modifiers, followed by "The Five Year Plan" multiplying all bonuses by 30%.
- **East India Company transformation:** The "Look to the Future" path converts the Raj into the East India Company (a corporate entity), enabling purchase and coring of foreign land with army, economy, and resource bonuses.

**Other mechanics:**
- Occupied starting states (Peshawar, Punjab, Quetta, Baluchistan, Sind in the west; Mandalay and Burma in the east) yield reduced manpower, resources, and industry until made core or released as independent nations.
- National spirit "Risk of Famine" can be mitigated through the "Royal Indian Artillery / Military Pensions" focus chain (Quinine technology).
- Multiple religious and ethnic leader options (Hindu nationalist Savarkar, Muslim League Jinnah, Sikh Tara Singh, etc.) provide targeted ideological and stability modifiers.
- Civil wars are available on ideological transition paths (independence toward democracy, communism, or fascism).

(Base game tree: simpler 3-branch structure focused on Provincial Elections, Lessons of the Great War, and Great Indian Peninsula Railway.)

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Borders:** Iran, Afghanistan, Sinkiang, Tibet, Nepal, Bhutan, China, and British Burma; also Portuguese colonial provinces, French colonies, Yemen, and United Kingdom (via Province of Aden).

**Diplomatic status:** The Raj begins as a subject state of the United Kingdom. Breaking this status requires completing one of the independence focus branches. Subjects of the UK include potential Portuguese and French colonies in India (Goa, French India, Pondicherry), which can be annexed if India becomes fully independent.

**Factions:**
- Historically aligned with the Allies (UK faction) as a subject, but the focus tree enables joining the Comintern (Communist path) or Axis (Fascist Bose path) via civil war mechanics.
- The "Swaraj" independence path offers a choice between Allies neutrality.

**Victory point concentration:** Hyderabad (151 VP) and West Punjab (115 VP) dominate the Raj's total; loss of these territories significantly reduces strategic standing. Bombay, Delhi, and West Bengal are secondary industrial/political centers.
