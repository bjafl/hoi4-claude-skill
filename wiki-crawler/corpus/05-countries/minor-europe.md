## Albania

Albania is a minor Balkan nation with just 1 million population and minimal military capacity. Located between Yugoslavia and Greece, it historically fell to Italian invasion in 1939 and does not appear in the 1939 scenario start. Italy has claims on Albanian territory and can release it as a puppet state (Regno d'Albania) via decision, granting it access to Albania's modest oil resources.

### Government and political situation

Albania is led by **King Zog** under the **House of Zogu** non-aligned ideology. In 1936, he holds 100% popularity and rules completely unopposed. The ideological pathway table shows potential transitions to other parties:

- **Democratic:** Eqrem Belishi (Pavarur party) → Republic of Albania
- **Communist:** Enver Hoxha (Partia Komuniste e Shqipërisë) → Socialist Republic of Albania
- **Fascist:** Tefik Mborja (Partia Fashiste e Shqipërisë) → Greater Albania
- **Despotism (House of Wied):** Prince Wilhelm of Wied (alternative non-aligned faction) → Principality of Albania

The House of Wied path may activate if **Hungary** completes its "Demand Wiedist Restoration" focus, promoting Prince Wilhelm with traits Stability +5.00%, War support +10.00%, Division Defense on core territory +10%, and Recruitable population +1.50%.

Albania ceases to exist in the 1939 start date after Italian annexation. The **By Blood Alone DLC** adds an Italian decision to release Albania as Regno d'Albania, available if Italy is non-aligned or fascist; the AI typically selects this option.

### National spirits

Albania has **no national spirits in 1936.** If released as a puppet by Italy in 1939 via the Regno d'Albania decision, it gains:

**Influx of Italian Settlers**
- Monthly Population: +20.0%
- Recruitable Population Factor: 10%
- Supply Hub construction speed: +30.00%
- Railway construction speed: +30.00%

### Unique mechanics

**No unique mechanics documented on the wiki.** Albania uses the generic national focus tree with five main branches (Army Effort, Aviation Effort, Naval Effort, Industrial Effort, Political Effort).

However, two foreign paths can unlock an **Austro-Hungarian joint focus branch** if:
1. **Austria** completes "The Danubian Federation" and "Take Albania" focuses, or
2. **Hungary** completes "The Lands of the Crown of Saint Stephen" and "Demand Wiedist Restoration," bringing Albania as a vassal.

This branch offers four sub-branches: Reforming the Common Army, Economic Cooperation Policy, Joint Industrial Planning, and Convene the Imperial Diet/United in the Face of Danger, granting various national spirits and modifiers for army, economics, and industry.

### Military

**Army:** 1 infantry division (Këmbësori Pjesëtim) with 9 infantry battalions and 1 reconnaissance support company; 9.5K manpower.

**Navy:** 5 convoys, 0 capital ships.

**Air Force:** None.

Albania's military is negligible. If released by Italy in 1939, it receives 2 infantry divisions (Milicia Fashiste Shqiptare) stationed in Tiranë and Korçë, each with 5 infantry battalions but no support companies; these units have a unique, non-modifiable template.

### Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Borders:** Yugoslavia (north and east), Greece (south).

**Key Diplomatic Facts:**
- Guaranteed by Italy (−25 opinion, Italy claims Albanian provinces).
- Grace relations with Greece: +5 opinion (though it deteriorates based on Greece's focuses; Greece also claims Northern Epirus).
- No starting alliances or faction membership.
- Italy considers Albania a target for invasion; Albania is prone to focus-based invasions by Greece and Italy.

If released as a puppet by Italy (By Blood Alone), Albania gains cores on Kosovo (802) and Debar (970) and claims on Montenegro (105), Kosovo, Debar, and Epirus (185).

Other nations can influence Albania:
- **Bulgaria:** via ideology or puppet subjugation
- **Soviet Union:** via ideology or puppet subjugation
- **Hungary (via Cisleithania):** "Demand Wiedist Restoration" focus; results in vassalization with cores on Kosovo and Debar
- **Austria:** "Take Albania" focus (annexation via focus days and decision)
- **Yugoslavia:** "Invite Albania" focus (faction invitation)

Albania has extremely limited industrial capacity and is entirely dependent on foreign aid or conquest to expand.

### Diplomacy and Neighbors

Ireland has a **core claim on Northern Ireland**, which is controlled by the United Kingdom. The island neighbor is also the United Kingdom. 

Historically, Ireland remained neutral throughout WWII despite pressure from both the Allies and Axis. The game reflects possible paths toward fascism or communism, though the default democratic stance emphasizes neutrality. Players can justify wars against the UK or other nations through the political focus tree.

### Strategies

#### Fascist Path

Recruit divisions early, hire a Fascist Demagogue, and pursue Political Effort → Fascist focuses. Once fascist, justify war goals to attack larger neighbors (UK, France, Spain). Joining the Axis provides access to German airbases. An alternative aggressive strategy uses collaboration governments to puppet smaller nations (Benelux, Hungary) for manpower, then uses paratroopers to invade the UK before Germany moves on Poland.

#### Allied Path

Build naval and air capacity to supplement weak ground forces (limited manpower). Approximately 16 divisions suffice for territorial defense; focus on subs, destroyers, and heavy cruisers for navy, and fighters + naval bombers for air. Provide convoy raiding and air support to Allied operations rather than large ground offensives. Coastal forts and AA defend Dublin against German invasion.

### Government and political situation

The Rietspartei (right-wing democratic party) rules under Joseph Bech, with elections scheduled for June 1937. Bech serves as both the political leader and provides traits that boost political power gain (+5%) and infrastructure construction speed (+5%).

Four other ideological parties exist:
- **Communist (5%)**: Kommunistesch Partei Lëtzebuerg (KPL), led by Charles Marx
- **Fascist (3%)**: Volksdeutsche Bewegung (VdB), led by Damian Kratzenberg
- **Non-aligned (13%)**: House of Nassau, led by Charlotte Wilhelmine

The democratic starting position grants positive relations (+10 to +20) with other democracies and slight negative relations (−10) with all other ideologies.

### National spirits

Luxembourg has no national spirits documented on the wiki.

### Unique mechanics

**No unique focus tree:** Luxembourg lacks a unique national focus tree and uses the generic tree instead, with five branches: Army Effort, Aviation Effort, Naval Effort, Industrial Effort, and Political Effort. The Industrial branch can unlock up to 3 additional research slots (total of 5 available).

**No initial military:** Luxembourg starts with zero army divisions, zero naval units, and zero aircraft.

### Military industrial organizations

Luxembourg has access to specialized design companies:
- Tank Designer (reliability and armor research speed)
- Ship Designer (naval research speed)
- Light Air Company (fighter design)
- Medium Air Company (heavy fighter and tactical bomber design)
- Heavy Air Company (strategic bomber design)
- Naval Air Company (naval aircraft design)
- Artillery Designer, Infantry Equipment Designer, Motorized Equipment Designer

**No unique cosmetic names:** Equipment uses generic names.

### Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Borders:** Germany (east), France (south), Belgium (north, west) — vulnerable to invasion if either Germany or France enters conflict with the other.

**Initial alliances:** None; the nation is unaligned but demographically democratic, making it targeted by German expansion via focus tree branches that push into BeNeLux.

**Diplomatic stance:** As a democracy, Luxembourg maintains positive relations with other democracies (Belgium, France, United Kingdom) but has no formal guarantees of military support; it typically falls within weeks if invaded by Germany, particularly via the BeNeLux route that bypasses French fortifications.

## Reichskommissariats

Reichskommissariats are special releasable puppet states that only Germany can form in controlled enemy territory after completing the focus Uplift the Rosenberg Office. Additional Reichskommissariats unlock via the focuses Mittelafrika, Create Asian Reichskommisariats, and Establish Protectorates in America. They serve to reduce the negative effects of Germany's Economy of Conquest national spirit. Reichskommissariats have no core states and all their territory remains occupied territory. Almost all game territory can be converted to a Reichskommissariat, with exceptions: Germany, Austria, Italy, France, Switzerland, Denmark, Sweden, Finland, Madagascar, Crete, the Bosporus Strait, and the Suez Canal.

### Overview of Reichskommissariat mechanics

All Reichskommissariats (except Protektorat Böhmen-Mähren and Generalgouvernement) start with the national spirit **Reichskommissariat Zivilverwaltung**, with two variants depending on the Reichskommissar:

**Reichskommissariat Zivilverwaltung (German)**
- Resource Gain Efficiency: +25%
- Weekly Manpower: +200
- Construction speed: +10%
- Lack of Resources Penalty: -10%
- Factory Output: +10%
- Overlord Trade Cost: -5%
- All autonomy gain: -10%
- Damage to Garrisons: -10%
- German Civilian industry to target: +20%
- German Military industry to target: +15%

**Reichskommissariat Zivilverwaltung (Collaborator)**
- Resource Gain Efficiency: +20%
- Weekly Manpower: +250
- Construction speed: +5%
- Lack of Resources Penalty: -15%
- Factory Output: +10%
- Overlord Trade Cost: -5%
- All autonomy gain: -5%
- Resistance Target: -10%
- Compliance Growth Speed: +10%
- German Civilian industry to target: +10%
- German Military industry to target: +10%

All Reichskommissariats begin as Fascist puppet states of Germany with 60% Fascism popularity. They inherit all German technologies and division templates, plus two unique division templates (Militärpolizei and Berittene Militärpolizei, each with 6 battalions and military police company) for garrison duties. All start with 3 research slots.

### Alfred Rosenberg modifier

After completing Uplift the Rosenberg Office, Germany gains access to the Alfred Rosenberg decision modifier, which increases as each new Reichskommissariat is formed (maximum bonus at 10 created):
