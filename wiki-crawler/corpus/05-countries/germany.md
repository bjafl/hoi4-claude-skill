The German Reich in 1936 is a major European power and one of the primary faction leaders. It is a fascist dictatorship under Adolf Hitler with the strongest industrial base in Europe outside the Soviet Union. Germany has extensive DLC content available (focus trees, national spirits, unique mechanics vary with Waking the Tiger, Man the Guns, Death or Dishonor, Together for Victory, and Götterdämmerung).

## Government and political situation

**Starting Leader:** Adolf Hitler (trait: Der Führer)
- Daily political power gain +10%
- Weekly war support (combat casualties) +0.10%
- Can upgrade Der Führer trait via Reorganize the Wehrmacht and/or Führerprinzip focuses

**Ruling Party:** NSDAP (40% popularity)

**Other Parties:**
- Deutsche Zentrumspartei (35% Democratic, leader: Konrad Adenauer)
- Deutschnationale Volkspartei (15% Non-aligned)
- Kommunistische Partei Deutschlands (10% Communist, leader: Wilhelm Pieck)

**Starting Stability:** 75% base (81% after modifiers)

**Starting War Support:** 30% base (35% after modifiers)

**Political Transformation:**
Germany can transition to other ideologies via national focuses:
- **Democratic** (Re-establish Free Elections or The Monarchy Compromise) — leaders include Konrad Adenauer
- **Communist** (The People's Victory) — leaders include Ernst Thälmann, Walter Ulbricht
- **Monarchist/Non-aligned** (Return of the Kaiser, Rally the Wehrmacht) — leaders include Wilhelm II, Wilhelm III, August von Mackensen

Multiple successor leaders available via focus "A Strong Successor" (Hermann Göring, Fritz Todt, Albert Speer, Joseph Goebbels, Rudolf Hess, Martin Bormann) or events (Heinrich Himmler, Eva Braun).

## Unique mechanics

Germany's scope of unique mechanics is extensive due to its major-power status and multiple DLC content branches:

- **Rhineland Focus:** Demilitarizes Rhineland and Moselland on completion
- **Anschluss:** Annexation of Austria (cores Austria and cores Vienna state)
- **Sudetenland:** Demand and annex Czechoslovak Sudetenland (Demand Sudetenland focus or negotiation with Czechoslovakia)
- **Territorial Claims:** Can gain cores on West Poland (Danzig, Posen, Katowice), Alsace-Lorraine, Luxembourg, via various focuses
- **Succession Events:** Oster Conspiracy event chain allows replacement of Hitler; succession via designated heirs
- **National Focuses:** Seven major branches including military modernization (Deutsches Heer, Luftwaffe, Kriegsmarine), economic recovery (Reichsautobahn), and ideological paths

See [06-focus-trees/germany.md](../06-focus-trees/germany.md) for the full focus tree; current version reflects Götterdämmerung content.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Direct Borders:** Denmark (north), Poland (east), Czechoslovakia (east), Austria (southeast), Switzerland (south), France (west), Luxembourg (west), Belgium (northwest), Netherlands (northwest)

**East Prussia (exclave):** Borders Lithuania, Poland

**Starting Alignment:** Leader of the Axis faction (no allies at game start, but Italy has positive relations)

**Diplomatic Situation:**
- Fascist ideology results in negative relations with most democracies and communists
- Treaty of Versailles demilitarizes Rhineland border region
- Can form German Empire (via focus), Greater German Reich or Holy Roman Empire (via decisions)
- Democratic Germany can form European Union (via decision)
- Multiple paths to gain territorial claims and cores on neighboring states via focuses and events

**Major Cities:** Berlin (capital), Frankfurt, Munich

**Major Ports:** Hamburg, Stettin, Bremen, Wilhelmshaven, Kiel
