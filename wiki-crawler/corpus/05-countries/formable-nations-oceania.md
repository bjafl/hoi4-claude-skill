Oceania contains two major formable nations: Australasia (formed by Australia) and Polynesia (formed by Pacific island nations). Australasia is intended as a unified regional power incorporating New Zealand and Melanesian territories. Polynesia can be formed by any compatible Pacific island nation and represents a united Polynesian-speaking federation. Both require control of specific island and coastal states to activate.

## Australasia

**Formation trigger:** Decision available to Australia (requires Thunder at Our Gates DLC and full independence).

**Formation requirements:** Australia must control all of the following regions:

| Region | Required states |
|--------|-----------------|
| Melanesia (West Papua New Guinea) | Dutch New Guinea (669), Dutch Papua (1057) |
| Melanesia (East Papua New Guinea) | Kaiser-Wilhelmsland (979), Papua (523), Bismarck (737), Bougainville (1070), Interior Papua (1073) |
| Melanesia (Solomons) | Solomon Islands (634), Nendo (734) |
| Melanesia (New Hebrides & New Caledonia) | New Hebrides (1071), New Caledonia (635) |
| Melanesia (Fiji) | Fiji (636) |
| New Zealand | Auckland (1079), Wellington (284), Marlborough (1080), Canterbury (723), Otago (1081) |

**Formation effect:** Australia becomes Australasia (name remains constant across all ideologies). Gains cores on all listed Melanesian states and, if New Zealand is not already a core of Australia, gains cores on all New Zealand states.

**Secondary decision — Integrate Indonesia:** After forming Australasia, Australia may select a second decision to annex Indonesian territory (requires full independence and Thunder at Our Gates). This grants cores on all Indonesian states: Interior Borneo (1022), Bangka-Belitung (1047), Padang (1048), Medan and Riouw (1049), Atjeh (1050), East Java (1051), Bali (1052), East Nusa Tenggara (1053), Dutch Timor (1054), East Kalimantan (1055), Manado (1056), Batavia (1058), Kalimantan Barat (334), West Java (335), West Nusa Tenggara (667), The Moluccas (668), Sumatra (672), Sulawesi (673), Aru Islands (738). Formation does not automatically claim Sarawak (333), Limbang (1059), Brunei (1023), Sabah (1024), or Portuguese Timor (721).

**Note on Reichskommissariat Australasien:** Germany can form a different entity called Reichskommissariat Australasien (led by Pavel Bermondt-Avalov or Eric Campbell) after completing the focus "Create Asian Reichskommisariats". This is a distinct formation from the Australian Australasia decision.

## Polynesia

**Formation trigger:** Decision available to eligible Pacific island nations (requires La Résistance DLC and full independence or dominion-level puppet status).

**Eligible formers:** _Not covered on the wiki._ Wiki lists the decision visibility for Kingdom of Hawai'i, Tahiti, Mariana Federation, Federated States of Micronesia, Independent State of Samoa, Solomon Islands, Melanesian Federation, New Zealand, Rapa Nui, Palau, Vanuatu, Bougainville, and Madagascar, but does not explicitly define which nations can form Polynesia or under what circumstances.

**Formation requirements (core decision "Unite Polynesia"):** Former must control all of the following states:

| Region | Required states |
|--------|-----------------|
| New Zealand | Wellington (284), Auckland (1079), Canterbury (723), Marlborough (1080), Otago (1081) |
| Hawaii | Hawaii (629) |
| Micronesia | Caroline Islands (684), Guam (638) |
| Polynesia | Tahiti (641), Samoa (726) |
| Melanesia / other Pacific | New Hebrides (1071), American Samoa (1072) |

**Formation effect:** Nation becomes Polynesia and gains cores on all Polynesian islands (30+ states spanning the Pacific). Country name varies by ideology:

| Ideology | Country name |
|----------|--------------|
| Democratic | Pacific Federation |
| Communist | Socialist Union of Oceanic Peoples |
| Fascist | Polynesian Empire |
| Non-Aligned | United Kingdom of Polynesia |
| Dominion | Dominion of Polynesia |

**Polynesian cores granted:** Wellington (284), Auckland (1079), Taiwan (524), Hawaii (629), Johnston Atoll (630), Midway Island (631), Wake Island (632), Marshall Islands (633), Solomon Islands (634), Bougainville (1070), New Caledonia (635), Fiji (636), Guam (638), Gilbert Islands (639), Tahiti (641), Phoenix Island (642), Ellice Islands (643), Iwo Jima (645), Saipan (646), Palau (647), Minamitorishima (648), Caroline Islands (684), Canterbury (723), Marlborough (1080), Otago (1081), Nauru (725), Samoa (726), Line Islands (727), Nendo (734), Pitcairn Island (270), Easter Island (948), New Hebrides (1071), American Samoa (1072).

**Secondary decision — Request Control of Pacific Islands (New Zealand only):** Available to New Zealand after completing the focus "Maori Volunteers" and while fully independent or dominion-level puppet. New Zealand can demand Pacific islands from faction members, triggering an event "New Zealand Requests Control of Polynesia" to a random faction ally that controls at least one Polynesian state. Does not apply to Hawai'i, Tahiti, Mariana Federation, Federated States of Micronesia, Independent State of Samoa, Solomon Islands, Melanesian Federation, Rapa Nui, or Palau.

**Dominion formation:** Polynesia can form even as a dominion (puppet nation), enabling scenarios where a puppet nation in the Pacific forms the Polynesian federation while retaining dominion status.

**Historical context:** In the "Oceania Colonization Status - Polynesian Empire" alternative history bookmark, Kingdom of Hawai'i starts as independent and already united as Polynesia, with New Zealand annexed.
