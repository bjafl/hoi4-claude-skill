## Summary

Iran is a large West Asian nation with 12.60 million core population, controlled by the Pahlavi Dynasty under Reza Shah Pahlavi. Despite declaring neutrality at the start, Iran faces internal instability from ethnic resistance in Azerbaijan and Kurdistan, while the Anglo-Iranian Oil Company imposes severe economic penalties. Iran possesses a unique focus tree (requiring Graveyard of Empires) offering paths to Communist, Democratic, or Fascist ideologies—or consolidation as a Monarchist power that can rebuild the Persian Empire by annexing neighbors.

## Government and political situation

Iran is ruled by **Reza Shah Pahlavi** (Despotism ideology), leader of the Pahlavi Dynasty party (60% popularity). Other major parties include the National Front of Iran (20%, Mohammad Ali Foroughi), Ultra-Nationalist Movement (15%), and the Tudeh Party (5%, Iraj Eskandari—Communist).

Possible country leaders accessible through the focus tree:
- **Mohammed Reza Pahlavi** (Despotism, Legitimate Heir): +10% Stability, +10% Political Power Gain (via [The Assassination of Reza Shah] focus).
- **Mohammad Ali Foroughi** (Conservatism, Renaissance Man): +10% Political Power Gain, +5% Research Speed (via event Election Season).
- **Mohammad Mosaddegh** (Conservatism, Anti-Shah Democrat): +10% Political Power, -15% Unaligned Diplomacy acceptance, +0.05% daily Democracy support, +5% Stability (via event Election Season).
- **Abol Gashem Kashani** (Despotism, Supreme Leader): -10% Political Power, +0.02 daily Unaligned support, +10% War support (via [The Islamic Revolution] focus).
- **Iraj Eskandari** (Marxism, Devoted Communist): +10% Factory output, -10% Resistance Activity Chance (via [The Iranian Socialist Revolution] focus).
- **Davud Monshizadeh** (Nazism): via [March On Saadabad] focus.

## National spirits

When Graveyard of Empires is enabled, Iran starts with five national spirits:

**Anglo-Iranian Oil Company** — Represents British control of oil resources. Resource Gain Efficiency: -15%, Factory output: -10%, Resources to Market: +20%.

**Rural Feudalism** — Underdeveloped economy. Political Power Gain: +5%, Recruitable population Factor: -5%, Construction Speed: -2%, Research Speed: -5%.

**Islamic Traditions** — Religious conservatism. Stability: +10%, Research Speed: -10%, Political Advisor Cost: +25%.

**Azerbaijani Resistance** — Northern ethnic uprising. Stability: -10%, War support: -10%.

**Kurdish Resistance** — Eastern ethnic insurgency. Stability: -10%, War support: -10%.

Ethnic resistances can be removed through focus tree branches (Unification Initiative removes both after Forced Secularization). Islamic Traditions can be replaced by choosing secularization or Zoroastrianism paths.

## Unique mechanics

Iran possesses a unique focus tree (Graveyard of Empires required; base game uses generic tree). The tree branches into seven major ideological/military paths:

**Establish a National Front Branch** — Opposition to the Shah, leading to Communist or Democratic paths. The Communist path includes Industrial Aid (research bonuses, oil) and The New Economy (military bonuses). A shared International Solidarity sub-branch (accessible to both Communist and Democratic paths) unlocks volunteer recruitment and colonial uprisings.

**The Pahlavi Imperium Branch** — The Monarchist path with Political Power bonuses. To Stand With Giants opens trade/research with Germany and bans Communism (or pursues industry). Forced Secularization applies to multiple branches. A Legacy of Greatness establishes the Shah as center of the nation, allowing assassination of Reza Shah to install Mohammed Reza Pahlavi or choosing to keep Reza Shah and form the First Iranian Empire. The Unification Initiative removes ethnic resistance spirits.

**Rally Behind Mosaddegh Sub-branch** — The Democratic path: constitutional monarchy or republic route, with industry bonuses, Allies membership, and options to annex Iraq/Afghanistan. The constitutional monarchy path may trigger civil war leading to [The Islamic Revolution].

**Unite the Ultra-Nationalists Sub-branch** — The Fascist path triggering civil war, reinstalling a new Shah. Allows alliance with Germany, Japan, or solo expansion, with end-goal to form Greater Iran and expand into the Middle East and India.

**The Fight for Iran Branch** — Only available if Iran enters defensive war. Splits into Force Them Back (attack bonuses, peace options) and Dig in and Defend (defense bonuses, fortification, and conditional surrender routes). If Iran becomes puppet, Rebuilding Iran branch offers loyalty or independence plotting. United Kingdom puppet route grants [Reinstate the Qajar Dynasty] focus (Mohammad Hassan Mirza as Shah). Soviet/UK invasions via Operation Countenance events gate [Retake North Iran/Consolidate British Territory] focuses.

**Modernizing Iran Branch** — Industry branch: research bonuses, railway network, construction/infrastructure, civilian factories and oil production (four main paths).

**The Saadabad Pact Branch** — Joint branch shared with Afghanistan, Iraq, and Turkey; builds national spirit with industry and military bonuses.

**Restructure the Army Branch** — Army technology and industry. Includes Iranian Special Operations sub-branch for special forces and intelligence.

**Establish the Air Force Branch** — Air force technology and doctrines.

**Establish the Navy Branch** — Naval technology, industry, and doctrines.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Borders:** Iraq (west), Turkey (west-northwest), Soviet Union (north-northeast), Afghanistan (southeast), British Raj (east). Also controls the Persian Gulf coastline.

**Ideology and faction:** Non-aligned in 1936 with no elections. No starting faction membership; can flip to Communist (join Comintern), Democratic (join Allies), or Fascist (join Axis) via focus tree paths.

**Diplomatic guarantees:** None at start. Historical pressure: both Soviet Union and United Kingdom are mentioned in context of intervention. Anglo-Soviet invasion occurred historically in 1941, leading to the Anglo-Soviet invasion of Iran event chain (gated by UK's Secure Iran or Soviet's Preemptive Invasion of Iran focuses).

## Additional character designers and military advisors

Iran has access to 25+ personnel slots (ministers, military theorists, generals) under Graveyard of Empires, including notable figures like Ali-Akbar Davar (Popular Figurehead, +15% Stability), Mohammad Mosaddegh (Silent Workhorse, +15% Political Power), and Teymur Bakhtiar (Illusive Gentleman, +1 operative slot). Without Graveyard of Empires, generic advisors are used.

**Military industrial organizations (MIO):** Tehran Armor (medium tank designer), Shahbaz Aircraft (aircraft designer), Qorkhaneh (infantry equipment), Iranian Shipyards (escort fleet designer), plus licensable designers from Czechoslovakia (Skoda), Germany (Henschel), Sweden (Bofors), and generics.

## Starting laws

- **Conscription:** Volunteer Only (1.5% recruitable population)
- **Trade:** Export Focus (+10% construction, +5% research, +10% factory output, 50% resources to market)
- **Economy:** Civilian Economy (35% consumer goods burden, -30% factory conversion cost)
