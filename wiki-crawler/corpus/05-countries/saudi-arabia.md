Saudi Arabia is a minor Non-Aligned power in the Middle East, ruled by the House of Saud under Abdulaziz Ibn Saud. A largely desert kingdom with negligible industry but substantial oil reserves, it plays a peripheral role in most campaigns. The country uses the generic national focus tree and lacks unique national spirits, making it straightforward but limited in gameplay options.

## Government and political situation

Saudi Arabia starts as **Non-Aligned** under the House of Saud party at 100% popularity, led by **Abdulaziz Ibn Saud** (trait: Despotic, Unifier of Central Arabia — reduces war goal justify time by 25%, boosts war support by 10%).

The political path is unlocked through the generic focus tree; Saudi Arabia can shift toward **Democratic** (ruled by Arabia's Republican government), **Communist** (ruled by a Soviet-backed Republic), or **Fascist** ideologies. Historical note: the master stats indicate a Fascist shift occurred by 1939 in the data model, though no specific focus branch is documented on the wiki.

## National spirits

Saudi Arabia does not have any national spirits.

## Unique mechanics

No unique mechanics documented on the wiki. Saudi Arabia uses the generic national focus tree, which provides standard branches for Army, Aviation, Naval, Industrial, and Political Effort.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering countries:** Iraq, Transjordan, Kuwait (north); Yemen, Oman (south). Also bordered by British colonial territories in the Persian Gulf.

**Terrain:** Mostly desert and mountains to the east; difficult for rapid maneuver.

**Arabia formation:** Saudi Arabia may form the Arabia nation-state if it controls all necessary territories. Due to its limited starting strength and manpower, this requires either favorable world conditions (e.g., United Kingdom pursuing decolonization in its focus tree) or aggressive military campaign planning.

**Diplomatic position:** At game start, Saudi Arabia is unaligned, with no recorded guarantees or faction membership. As a minor power with negligible industrial base, it attracts little diplomatic attention early on.
