Ecuador is a minor democratic country in South America with limited industrial capacity and a small military. It borders Colombia to the northeast, Peru to the south, and has coastline on the Pacific Ocean. Its strategic position and small military make it vulnerable to larger neighbors, but the Monroe Doctrine guarantees offer some diplomatic protection.

## Government and political situation

Ecuador starts as a **democratic state** under the **República del Ecuador**, governed by **Federico Páez**. Elections are held every 7 years; the next is scheduled for December 1940.

## National spirits

**Monroe Doctrine**  
Ecuador begins with the Monroe Doctrine national spirit, which guarantees the country's independence under U.S. protection. This provides diplomatic benefits but does not prevent Ecuador from being a target of war goals or invasion.

## Unique mechanics

No unique mechanics documented on the wiki. Ecuador uses the generic national focus tree, which provides standard branches for military effort (army, aviation, naval), industrial expansion, and political choices. The generic tree can unlock up to 3 additional research slots (Ecuador starts with 2).

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering states:**
- Colombia (northeast border)
- Peru (south border)

**Diplomatic guarantees:**  
The United States guarantees Ecuador's independence via the Monroe Doctrine national spirit.

**Historical tensions:**  
The game notes a historical Ecuadorian-Peruvian War in 1941, which may trigger as an event. Ecuador's weak military and limited industrial base make it vulnerable to Peruvian aggression; the wiki player guide suggests defensive fortification strategies and eventual alliance-building through ideology shifts (communist or fascist) to justify expansion.

Ecuador is not aligned with any faction at game start and lacks the military or industrial capacity to pursue major conquest without significant focus tree development and potential ideology changes.
