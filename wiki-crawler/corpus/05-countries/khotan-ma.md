Khotan Ma is a Chinese warlord state in northwestern China, ruled by Ma Hushan and part of the Ma Clique faction. With ~480K population and no starting industry or resources, it is the weakest of all Chinese warlord states. It borders Gansu Ma and Qinghai Ma to the east, Tibet and the British Raj to the south, and Sinkiang to the west and north. Despite severe starting disadvantages, it has a unique national focus tree enabling transformation into the Khotan Emirate and pursuit of an Islamic sultanate path separate from the main Chinese political factions.

## Government and political situation

Ma Hushan leads Khotan Ma as an authoritarian despotist ruler of the Ma Clique warlord faction. Competing ideologies are present as minor parties (China Democratic League, Communist Party of China, Blue Shirt Society) at 0% popularity; the Ma Clique holds 100% political support.

The national focus tree offers paths to switch government ideology via the "Secure Internal Politics" branch: players can align with Nationalist China, Communist China, or pursue an independent rival government path (Japanese-aligned or opposition route). After completing the unique "Shizang's Coup" focus, Ma Hushan is replaced by Mahmut Muhiti (Skill 2 with Emir of Khotan traits), and the country transforms into the Khotan Emirate with access to additional socialist, communist, rexist, and despotist variants. This path grants claims on Soviet Central Asian territories and enables formation of the Chinese Sultanate if further focuses are completed.

## Unique mechanics

Khotan Ma has two exclusive national focus tree branches unavailable to other Ma warlords:

**Undermine KMT Rule branch (Khotan-exclusive):** Removes the Ma Clique political structure and forms the Khotan Emirate, placing Uyghur ethnicity in power. Grants claims on Soviet Central Asian territory. A central focus allows the United Kingdom and British Raj to send volunteers. Rightmost section grants combat bonuses against Chinese Soviet Republic and Sinkiang, plus a cavalry division template.

**Call for Aid/Strike the Governor branch (Khotan-exclusive):** Enables war declarations on Sinkiang and Kumul Khanate (if released) for control of the Sinkiang region. Success creates the Sinkiang Ma Clique sub-state.

**Shared Ma Clique mechanics:**

- **Rename to Xinjiang Ma:** Automatically triggers if Sinkiang is conquered.
- **Form Xibei San Ma:** Unify all four Ma Clique warlords (Gansu Ma, Ningxia Ma, Qinghai Ma) via the "Sideline Family Conflicts" focus branch.
- **Form Great Ma:** Conquer all of China; available to any Ma warlord including Khotan Ma.
- **Switch to Chinese Sultanate:** After completing "Shizang's Coup" focus, complete additional focuses to form the Chinese Sultanate faction (Islamic path alternative to Nationalist or Communist China).

Khotan Ma uses the shared Warlords national focus tree as a base, with unique and Ma Clique–shared branches, and gains access to Nationalist or Communist Chinese warlord focus trees at the end depending on political choices. See [06-focus-trees/china-warlords.md](../06-focus-trees/china-warlords.md) for full tree structure.

**Cosmetic equipment names:** Unique names for Mountain Infantry I research and historical infantry equipment (Type 24 in 1936, TsingTao MP18 in 1939, ZB vz.26 in 1942).

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering states:**

- **East:** Gansu Ma (Ma Clique ally), Qinghai Ma (Ma Clique ally)
- **South:** Tibet, British Raj (non-aligned)
- **West/North:** Sinkiang (warlord rival, primary conflict zone)

Khotan Ma begins in the **Ma Clique Collective** faction (shared with Gansu Ma, Ningxia Ma, Qinghai Ma). The "Battle for Sinkiang" national spirit prevents joining other factions until removed; this spirit can be shed by pivoting away from Ma Clique politics via Secure Internal Politics focus tree branches.

**Military position:** Khotan Ma begins with only 3 divisions (1 infantry, 2 cavalry) totaling 7.17K manpower—severely underequipped and unable to fully man even these units. Infantry and cavalry divisions are removed in the 1939 start date, indicating the state does not survive to that date in historical play without player intervention. Success requires rapid conquest and integration of neighboring warlords or Sinkiang to gain territorial production capacity.

**Strategic note:** Khotan Ma is documented as the weakest Chinese warlord and one of the weakest nations in the entire game. Early expansion toward Sinkiang or internal consolidation of the Ma Clique is essential to avoid early defeat or absorption.
