The Philippines in 1936 is an archipelagic puppet state of the United States undergoing democratic transition toward independence. Though nominally sovereign, as a subject state it begins with severe military and economic constraints—minimal factories, weak army, and heavy consumer-goods burden. It serves as a valuable naval position and source of chromium.

## Government and political situation

The Philippines in 1936 is a democratic commonwealth transitioning toward independence from the United States. Elections occur every 6 years; the next is scheduled for September 1941.

## Unique mechanics

**No Unique Mechanics Documented on Wiki** for base game. With **No Compromise, No Surrender** DLC, the Philippines gains:

- **Capture of Manila event:** Triggers when any invading force (enemy or ally) occupies Manila, with narrative and potential effects.
- **Focus tree independence paths:** Multiple branches to shift from puppet status toward autonomy or revolution (Declare Military Emergency, petition for independence via focus Reino de Filipinas, align with Japan or USA, legalize Communist Party, etc.).
- **Design company access:** After declaring independence or military emergency, gains conditional access to US-aligned designers (Springfield Armory, North American Aviation, etc.) or Japanese-aligned designers (Kokura Arsenal, Mitsubishi, Sasebo Naval Arsenal) depending on diplomatic alignment.
- **Military staff and theorists:** Access to specialist commanders with varying traits (Commando, Jungle Rat, Career Officer, etc.) tied to focus completion.

See [06-focus-trees/philippines.md](../06-focus-trees/philippines.md) for the full focus tree.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering Countries:** None directly (archipelago); nearest major neighbors are China (north), Japan (north), British Malaya (west), Dutch East Indies (south).

**Current Status:** Subject state of United States; guaranteed by USA.

**Starting Faction:** None (subject of USA); can only join Allied faction after independence or via special events.

**Key Diplomatic Mechanics:**
- As puppet, **cannot declare war independently** without breaking puppet status.
- **Autonomy gain is severely restricted** (−0.1 daily from American Interference spirit); independence requires completion of specific focuses.
- Potential paths to independence: Democratic process (elections), military coup (Declare Military Emergency), or Japanese invasion (Second Philippine Republic puppet state).
- Upon Japanese occupation in historical 1942–1945 scenario, can become Second Philippine Republic (separate tag, Japanese puppet).
