Tibet is a mountainous, landlocked minor country in South Asia with minimal industrial capacity and military strength at 1936. It borders multiple Chinese warlords and regional powers, making it vulnerable to expansion pressure from stronger neighbors. The country follows the generic focus tree and begins under a non-aligned government led by the religious hierarchy.

## Government and political situation

Tibet starts as **Non-Aligned** under the **Retting Rinpoche** party, led by **Jamphel Yeshe Gyaltsen** (Tulku trait). The government name is "Tibet" and there are no elections.

## Unique mechanics

Tibet lacks a unique national focus tree and uses the generic focus tree instead. The generic tree includes five main branches (Army Effort, Aviation Effort, Naval Effort, Industrial Effort, and Political Effort), providing choices on military doctrine, industrial expansion, and political alignment.

Tibet does not have any unique cosmetic names for its equipment.

## Neighbours and diplomatic position

**Bordering countries:**
- East: Sichuan Clique, Yunnan, British Burma
- South: Bhutan, British Raj, Nepal
- North: Khotan Ma, Qinghai Ma

Tibet pursues a neutral foreign policy but is strategically vulnerable. Early threats include encroachment by the Sichuan Clique (likely to attack Chamdo) and pressure from Chinese warlords and unifying Chinese states through the shared Chinese focus tree. The country is prone to submission or territorial conquest demands from Manchukuo, Communist China, and various Chinese warlords that access nationalist or communist focuses.
