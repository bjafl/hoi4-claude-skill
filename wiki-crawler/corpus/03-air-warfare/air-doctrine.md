## Summary

Air doctrine is bought with **air experience** from the **Officer Corps**. It has two layers: one of three mutually exclusive **Grand Doctrines**, and four **Doctrine Tracks** (Fighter, Strike, Medium, Heavy Aircraft) in each of which you pick one **Subdoctrine**. Subdoctrine milestones are unlocked with **Mastery**, earned by flying missions, not with experience. **Each grand doctrine and each subdoctrine costs a base 100 air experience.**

> ⚠️ This describes the **post-1.17** doctrine system. Any guide referring to Strategic Destruction / Battlefield Support / Operational Integrity as linear XP-cost trees with per-node experience prices is describing the pre-1.17 system and is obsolete.

---

## How the system works

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Grand Doctrine | Only **one** can be enabled at a time. Gives an immediate bonus plus one **milestone per track** unlocked when that track is fully mastered. |
| Doctrine Tracks | Four: Fighter Aircraft, Strike Aircraft, Medium Aircraft, Heavy Aircraft. Each holds a set of subdoctrines shared across all three grand doctrines. |
| Subdoctrine | Only **one per track** may be enabled at a time. Grand doctrine choice does **not** restrict which subdoctrines are available. |
| Mastery | Earned per track when the relevant aircraft see action, at a **reduced rate while training**. Spent on the five milestones of the selected subdoctrine (100 / 200 / 300 / 400 / 500). |
| Unselected tracks | If no subdoctrine is selected in a track, up to **200 Mastery** is held in reserve. |
| Cost | **100 air experience** base per grand doctrine and per subdoctrine, modifiable by spirits, advisors, focuses, events and decisions. |
| Switching | You may change doctrine tree at any time, but **all bonuses from previously unlocked doctrines are lost**, and doctrine-exclusive Officer Corps spirits are automatically switched. |

### Which aircraft feed which track

| Doctrine Track | Gains Mastery from |
|---|---|
| Fighter Aircraft | Fighters, Interceptors |
| Strike Aircraft | Close Air Support, Naval Bombers |
| Medium Aircraft | Tactical Bombers, Heavy Fighters, Scout Planes |
| Heavy Aircraft | Strategic Bombers, Naval Patrol Bombers |

## Grand Doctrines

| Grand Doctrine | Immediate bonuses | Concept |
|---|---|---|
| **Strategic Destruction** | Strategic Bombing **+20.0%**; Escort Efficiency **+25%** | Mass bombing of factories, refineries and transport infrastructure deep in the enemy hinterland |
| **Battlefield Support** | Fighter Detection **+15.00%**; Air Support Mission Efficiency **+20%** | Close army–air force cooperation; air strikes as flying artillery for fast ground operations |
| **Operational Integrity** | Interception Detection **+20.00%**; Air Support Mission Efficiency **+10%** | Win and hold air superiority, then dominate with general-purpose medium bombers on operational strikes |

> ⚠️ Strategic Destruction's **Escort Efficiency +25%** is on a modifier the wiki elsewhere states **has no effect in game** (see [Air warfare overview](air-warfare-overview.md#the-other-mission-efficiency-modifiers)). Treat half of Strategic Destruction's headline bonus as possibly dead weight.

## Terminology

| Modifier | What it actually does |
|---|---|
| Air Superiority Mission Efficiency | Improves air attack, air defense and agility for planes on Air Superiority |
| Interception Mission Efficiency | Same, for planes on Interception |
| Air Support Mission Efficiency | Same, for planes on Close Air Support — **does not boost Ground Attack** |
| Naval Mission Efficiency | Same, for planes on naval missions |
| Air Superiority | Increases the contribution of planes on Air Superiority to the zone's air superiority |
| Escort Efficiency | Nominally reduces disruption from enemy attacks — **the wiki states this modifier has no effect** |
| Bomber Defense | Increases air defense for planes on Strategic Bombing |
| Strategic Bombing | Improves strategic bombing damage for planes on that mission |
| Strategic Bombing Visibility | Counteracts enemy detection while on Strategic Bombing, reducing how many enemy planes can respond |
| Fighter Detection | Increases the detection contributed by planes on Air Superiority |
| Interception Detection | Increases the detection contributed by planes on Interception |
| Ground Support | Increases the scaling rate of the Enemy Air Support bonus from air superiority |
| Enemy Air Support | Modifies how badly enemy air superiority hurts your ground troops |
| Ace generation chance | Raises the probability of generating aces on missions |
| Night-time strategic bombing penalty | Reduces night's negative effects for strategic bombing, notably the Ground Bombing Targeting penalty |

Detection, disruption and damage mechanics are in [Air combat](air-combat.md).

## Which branch suits which strategy

**Inferred throughout this section.**

### Pick your Grand Doctrine by what your air force is for

| Your strategy | Grand Doctrine | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Land-war blitz** — you want the enemy's divisions to lose defense and breakthrough, and your CAS to hit hard | **Battlefield Support** | Air Support Mission Efficiency **+20%** is the largest single mission-efficiency bonus available, Fighter Detection +15% multiplies your fighters' effective numbers, and its milestones give Ground Support +20% (which scales the whole enemy-air-support penalty) plus Air Superiority +15% |
| **Win the sky first, then bomb operationally** — fighter-led, tactical bombers as the workhorse | **Operational Integrity** | Interception Detection +20% is the best defensive-detection bonus in the game, and its Medium track milestone gives Tactical Bomber Strat. Bombing **+20%** — the strongest tac-bomber payoff anywhere |
| **Strategic bombing campaign** — you intend to burn enemy industry | **Strategic Destruction** | Strategic Bombing +20% up front, another +20% from the Heavy track milestone, Bomber defense +25% from the Medium track, and Naval Mission Efficiency +15% on the Strike track. Discount the Escort Efficiency +25% (no effect) |

### General rules of thumb

- **Do not switch grand doctrines casually** — switching wipes every unlocked bonus and forces Officer Corps spirit changes. Decide by 1937.
- **Mastery only accrues for tracks whose aircraft actually fly.** Selecting a Heavy Aircraft subdoctrine with no strategic bombers earns nothing; leave it unselected and up to 200 Mastery banks for later.
- **Training earns Mastery at a reduced rate**, so peacetime pilot exercises do build toward the first milestone or two.
- **Detection bonuses are undervalued.** Attacking planes are capped at 3× the number of *detected* enemy planes, so Fighter/Interception Detection converts idle aircraft into fighting aircraft — often worth more than a flat stat percentage.
