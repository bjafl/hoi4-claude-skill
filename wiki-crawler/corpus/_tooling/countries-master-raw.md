

===== PAGE: Countries =====
Countries are the acting entities of Hearts of Iron IV. Each is controlled by a player or by AI and pursues its own national focus tree during a game as well as choosing various political Ideas, engaging in Diplomacy, Construction, Research, Production, and the command of its military units. There are 344 countries (not including formable nations) in the game, 99 of which appear at the 1936 start.

Each country belongs to one of four political Ideologies, which can change over a game, though there are in most cases no ways to increase the Non-Aligned ideology without modding.

## Major countries

Countries in HOI4 are either major countries or minor countries. In a war, a faction will only surrender when all its participating major countries capitulate. The major countries in each faction are shown by golden frames around their flags in the war overview screen. Wars that have at least 3 major countries participating are considered major wars, which can unlock certain conditional modifiers.

Major countries also receive the following, additional benefits:

- Becoming eligible as targets for certain events and other countries' national focuses, usually to their benefit
- Becoming eligible for various decisions, most notably the decisions associated with hosting governments in exile and, with [Arms Against Tyranny]Arms Against Tyranny, the Diversify Elite Forces decision
- With [La Résistance]La Résistance, are much harder to target with coup operations
- During peace conferences, can receive various cost discounts to certain demands and apply cost penalties to non-major pursuing certain demands

Justifying a war goal against a major country at war is discounted, and the amount of world tension generated from various sources is scaled based on how powerful the number 2 major country by total factory count is. The AI also treats major countries differently, being more willing to embargo them and less willing to accept their conditional surrender.

The major countries in 1936 are:

- [Flag of Germany] Germany
- [Flag of Italy] Italy
- [Flag of Japan] Japan
- [Flag of Soviet Union] Soviet Union
- [Flag of France] France
- [Flag of United Kingdom] United Kingdom
- [Flag of United States] United States

As the game progresses, more countries can become major countries.

### Becoming a major country

All countries that lead a faction are immediately designated as major countries.

Otherwise, a country is considered a major based on its factory count. If a country has at least 35 factories and either is in the top 7 by factory count or has more than 70% of the average factory count of the top 7 countries, it will be considered a major country.

Which countries should be majors is evaluated every month. If a country is at war, this monthly check cannot result in the country losing its major status. Becoming a faction leader will bypass this, making the country major immediately.

Notes:

1. A tag is a three-letter code for a country that doesn't vary with localization or with changes in ideology, unlike a name (GER is always the same country, even though it's called the [Flag of German Reich] German Reich when fascist and [Germany] Germany when democratic). Console commands require referring to countries by tag, and they're sometimes also used as fan jargon.
2. Any countries created after the game starts, either by civil war or collaboration government by a foreign power, will be given a tag D## (with the ## ascending based on the number of new nations already created).
3. The number of military/civilian factories and dockyards in this list are the amount which each country actually owns. The number of available factories/dockyards may be different depending on the Compliance level in non-core states, certain State Modifiers, or if the country is an overlord or a puppet, hence why they may differ from the in-game numbers.
4. The number of resources in this list is the total base amount from each state each country owns. Rubber gained from synthetic refineries are also included. These numbers are then increased due to infrastructure levels, supply hubs and Compliance level, hence why the in-game values seen in the Trade interface may differ from those in this list, even at the start of the game.

## Releasable countries

  Main article: Releasable countries

Releasable countries are countries that do not exist as independent nations at game start. They can be released by countries who own their core territory through the Occupied Territories screen, through certain national focuses, events, or decisions, or forced to do so by a victorious power in a peace conference. They can also exist at game start through various game rules.
