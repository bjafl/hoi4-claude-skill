## Skill levels explained

- **Genius** — Highest tier, significant combat bonus
- **Unique** — Mid-tier, distinctive bonus or niche specialty
- **Good** — Lower tier, minor combat bonus

## Ace types

- **Fighter Ace** — bonuses to fighter aircraft combat
- **Support Ace** — bonuses to dive bombers, ground attack, or other support roles
- **Bomber Ace** — bonuses to strategic bombers
