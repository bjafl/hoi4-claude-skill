The Arms Against Tyranny DLC adds 19 achievements, focused on Nordic/Northern European countries (Sapmi, Norway, Denmark, Iceland, Sweden, Finland) and military industrial organization (MIO) management. Achievement difficulty ranges from Easy to Very Hard.

## Sapmi united by the fire

**Condition:** As Sapmi, own and control every Sapmi core.

## Norway Jose

**Condition:** As Norway, own and control every Mexican core.

## Pinot Noirway

**Condition:** As Norway, own and control Bourgogne (27).

## Cheese your fighter.

**Condition:** As Norway, with Peder Anker Wedel-Jarlsberg as country leader, own Telemark (921), Gloucestershire (338), Normandy (15), Lombardia (159), and Holland (7).

**Strategy:** Rush monarchy path early, build minimal military industry, use claims on Denmark/Sweden to expand Scandinavia, then justify on UK (likely already at war with Axis for fast justification). Follow the monarchy focus chain: Appoint Prince Olav to Army Staff → Power to the Royal Guard → Encourage Recruitment → For the King and Prince → Shield of the North. Prioritize 55% war support before completing The Kingdom of Norway. Maintain non-aggression with Germany while conquering UK. Very difficult due to reliance on focus timings and RNG; set historical AI.

## Med plutonium 2

**Condition:** As Denmark, drop a nuclear bomb on Sweden.

**Strategy:** Go fascist and join Axis before WW2 to stay safe from German aggression; sit through entire game until nukes, fighters, and strategic bombers are available, or take The Danelaw alternative path.

## Everything is awesome.

**Condition:** As Denmark after January 1st, 1941, reach Maximum Welfare Spending on the Balance of Power without being puppeted or capitulating.

**Strategy:** Go fascist and join Axis for safety; repeatedly take Balance of Power decisions toward Welfare side; hire/fire advisors to influence the balance.

## The Danelaw

**Condition:** As Denmark, control all cores of United Kingdom.

## Vinland

**Condition:** As Iceland, fully control Newfoundland (331).

**Strategy:** Go fascist or communist; build small army and cheap submarines; build fleet to establish naval superiority; USA usually garrisons Newfoundland with 1 division if they take it via focus; plan naval invasion accordingly.

## Brexit

**Condition:** As communist Iceland, start uprisings in Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland; each must be at war with UK.

**Strategy:** Disable La Résistance DLC for ease; follow communist path through Embrace the Workers' Revolution; boost Communist party popularity in UK; take Infiltrating the British Isles focus; save Political Power for uprising decisions in each region. If keeping La Résistance enabled, attempt to roll back to Patch 1.13.7 to reduce UK counterespionage investment.

## Two Arms Against Tyranny

**Condition:** As Finland, capitulate the Soviet Union while faction contains only Finland and one other Nordic country.

**Strategy:** Follow Uralic Brothers United guide but do not disband faction or join Axis after uniting Scandinavia; release Iceland as puppet (counts as Nordic despite minimal industry). Prevent other countries from joining faction.

## Uralic brothers united

**Condition:** As Finland, have Hungary, Estonia, Sapmi, Karelia, Mari El, Udmurtia, Komi, Ostyak-Vogul National Republic, and Yamalian Republic as subjects.

## Lone Wolf

**Condition:** As Finland, form Greater Finland focus and own every core and claim without ever joining a faction.

**Condition details:** Must own and control Murmansk (213), Onega (215), Olonets (216), Norrbotten (666), Finnmark (925), Luga (208), Leningrad (195), Volkhov (244), Tikhvin (264); must not complete Right-Wing Policies or Suomalainen Sosialismi; Soviet Union must not complete Beaten, but not Defeated.

## No one crosses the finnish line

**Condition:** As non-aligned Finland, survive until September 19th, 1944 without losing control of a single core territory.

**Condition details:** Must not lose any starting core state entirely; must not complete Right-Wing Policies or Suomalainen Sosialismi; Soviet Union must not complete Beaten, but not Defeated.

**Strategy:** Focus on industry and civilian factories early; build supply hub and level 1 land forts along Soviet border; hire advisors strategically to unlock specific buffs; plan to reach Partial Mobilization law early; train 10-width infantry divisions (4 Infantry + Medium SP Anti-Air); use captured Soviet equipment extensively after Salvaged and Retooled focus; garrison all ports to defend against naval invasions (especially Åland and Petsamo); collaborate with USSR to reach 90% compliance for easier capitulation by mid-1942; then wait until 1944 for achievement trigger. Alternative: establish non-aggression pact with USSR in early 1939 to prevent Winter War entirely (they will not attack), then wait until 1944.

## Danzig queen

**Condition:** As Sweden, own and control Danzig (85).

## Caramelldansen

**Condition:** As Sweden, puppet Japan.

## The return of the king

**Condition:** As Sweden with King Gustaf V as country leader, fully control all your starting cores; do not have Escaping the mainland national spirit.

## Satisfactory

**Condition:** Have at least one MIO per category at level 10+.

**Condition details:** Tank MIO size > 10 with one light/medium/heavy tank equipment type or armor technology; Ship MIO size > 10 with carrier/cruiser/destroyer/battleship/capital ship equipment or corresponding ship technologies; Aircraft MIO size > 10 with fighter/CAS/naval bomber/carrier aircraft equipment or corresponding aircraft technologies; Material MIO size > 10 with infantry/artillery/anti-tank/anti-air/motorized/mechanized/armored car equipment or corresponding technologies.

**Strategy:** Best done as USA with strong economy and research abilities; progress toward next MIO stages gained by researching with assigned MIO and/or producing equipment with assigned MIO; requires reaching MIO level 11 for some categories.

## Cod wars

**Condition:** As Iceland, capitulate United Kingdom.

## The Magic School Bus

**Condition:** With the Per Albin Bus, travel from Stockholm to Cape Town in less than 19 days as Sweden.
