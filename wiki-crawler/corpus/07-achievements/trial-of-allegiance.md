## Summary

Trial of Allegiance achievements focus primarily on South American nations (Brazil, Argentina, Chile, and others), with challenges ranging from territorial conquest to specific government transformations. Most require controlling vast regions in South America or extending influence beyond the continent.

## Rumble in the Jungle

**Condition:** Playing as Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Paraguay, Peru, Uruguay, or Venezuela; controls all of: Amazonas (495), Pastaza (490), Loreto (491), Ucayali (494), Santa Cruz (487), Guaporé (942), Pará (938), Bolívar (488), Meta (486), British Guyana (687), Suriname (309), French Guiana (310).

**Strategy:** Continue from Lamento Boliviano by justifying on Bolivia and conquering them.

## And the snake smoked

**Condition:** Playing as Brazil; controls Brandenburg (64); is in faction with United Kingdom; one of: Germany has capitulated OR Germany does not exist.

**Strategy:** If Germany is the last major in its faction, capitulating it will end the war and the achievement will not fire. Capitulate Germany before Italy when possible.

## Red Hot Chile Peppers

**Condition:** Playing as Chile; controls California (378); ruling party is Communist.

**Strategy:** Can be done at the same time as Chilean Empire.

## Bad Ending - The whole world is now Brazil

**Condition:** Playing as Brazil; controls all states in the world.

## A Land of Fire

**Condition:** Has dropped a nuclear bomb on Tierra del Fuego (953).

**Strategy:** Easy with Chile or Argentina. Use research bonuses from military focus tree to get nuclear weapons early. For Chile, need fighters to guarantee air superiority. For Argentina, release Y Wladychfa Gymreig as free country then justify war goal.

## I'm Home!

**Condition:** Playing as Brazil; controls all cores of Portugal: Porto (180), Guarda (181), Lisbon (112), Santarém (795), Beja (179), Madeira (697), Azores (698).

**Strategy:** Subjugate Portugal via monarchist path, or go communist/fascist to get foothold in Iberia via Spanish Civil War. If subjugating, take Spain and give to Portuguese puppet to lower autonomy faster.

## Proactive Defense

**Condition:** Playing as Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Paraguay, Peru, Uruguay, or Venezuela; controls Maryland (361); ruling party is Communist.

## The Merry Band

**Condition:** Playing as Brazil; has completed focus Cangaço Coup; controls East Midlands (129); ruling party is Communist.

## Somehow He Has Returned

**Condition:** Playing as Argentina; Señor Hilter is current country leader.

**Strategy:** Play historical, go fascist, build fascist support until taking Guardia Nacional, then wait for Germany to capitulate. Event fires soon after.

## URSAL

**Condition:** Playing as Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Paraguay, Peru, Uruguay, or Venezuela; ruling party is Communist; controls specified states from: Colombia (La Libertad, Cundinamarca, Meta), Venezuela (Zulia, Miranda, Bolívar, Curaçao), Ecuador (Ecuador, Pastaza, Galápagos Islands), Peru (Loreto, Lima, Ucayali, Arequipa, Tacna-Moquegua), Bolivia (Santa Cruz, La Paz), Paraguay (Chaco Boreal, Paraguay), Uruguay (Paysandú, Montevideo, Cerro Largo), Chile (Arica y Tarapacá, Antofagasta, Atacama, Santiago, Araucanía, Aysén, Magallanes, Easter Island), Argentina (all specified territories including Buenos Aires, Falkland Islands), Brazil (extensive Amazon and Atlantic states including Falkland Islands, British Guyana, Suriname, French Guiana).

## Islas Malvinas

**Condition:** Playing as Argentina; controls Falkland Islands (299) and South Georgia (720).

**Strategy:** Time requirement is not necessary; achievement unlocks upon occupation by force or peace deal.

## Lamento Boliviano

**Condition:** Playing as Argentina; does NOT control Santa Cruz (487) and La Paz (302); controls specified states from Colombia, Venezuela, Ecuador, Peru, Paraguay, Uruguay, Chile, Argentina (extensive), Brazil (extensive), Falkland Islands, British Guyana, Suriname, French Guiana.

## Revenge for the triple alliance

**Condition:** Playing as Paraguay; is at war with Brazil, Argentina, and Uruguay; these three countries have declared war on each other within 1 month.

**Strategy:** Avoid civil war and flip communist. Use ~70 Political Power to justify war goal on Uruguay. Take focus Beneath the Shadow of the Triple Alliance to get other two war goals around same date. Declare on all three.

## The Dragon Rises

**Condition:** Is Y Wladychfa Gymreig; controls Wales (122).

## King of What?

**Condition:** Playing as Chile; ruling party is Non-Aligned; has completed focus Crown Antoine as Sovereign King; controls Ile de France (16).

**Strategy:** When civil war fires, choose divisions option and rush Mobilize the Veteran Legionaries. Do not finish war with Argentina but cause casualties. When WW2 starts, attack France and join Axis. Once France capitulates, ask Germany for control of Paris (or gift them conquered Argentine territory for war score).

## True Condor Legion

**Condition:** Playing as Bolivia, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, or Venezuela; has sent volunteers to Germany.

**Strategy:** Pick country with authoritarian government like Venezuela or Peru. Spam 2-width divisions to reach 30-division threshold for volunteers. Send volunteers when Germany attacks Poland historically. If world tension too low, justify on USA.

## Chilean Empire

**Condition:** Playing as Chile; controls all mainland Pacific-border states in South America, North America, and Asia (extensive list including California, Oregon, Washington, British Columbia, Alaska, and multiple Asian states including Kamchatka and Vladivostok).

## Reconquistadors

**Condition:** Playing as Argentina or Chile; if Argentina: has completed focus Unión Nacional Fascista; if Chile: has completed focus Restore the Old Kingdom; controls all Spanish cores (Galicia through Granada, extensive Iberian Peninsula).

**Strategy:** For Argentina: go fascist, build industry and army, get military access from Italy or join Axis, naval invade Spain when civil war ends and army ready. Spain has equipment deficit so overrun easily.

## Hispanics of the world, unite!

**Condition:** Playing as Chile; has completed focus The Hispanic Alliance; is faction leader; faction contains at least 8 of: Mexico, Guatemala, Cuba, Dominican Republic, Puerto Rico, El Salvador, Honduras, Nicaragua, Costa Rica, Panama, Colombia, Venezuela, Ecuador, Peru, Bolivia, Paraguay, Uruguay, Argentina.

**Strategy:** Rush fascist route, take Hispanic Alliance focus to invite fascist/non-aligned Hispanic nations (80% acceptance). In 1936, eligible: Argentina, Dominican Republic, El Salvador, Guatemala, Nicaragua, Peru, Uruguay, Venezuela; Bolivia and Paraguay may flip later. Take focus when as many eligible as possible—once declined, extremely difficult to recruit (need 30% fascism, government, +100 opinion, 100% pressure, and border).

## Cisplatine War 2, Electric Boogaloo

**Condition:** Playing as Argentina or Brazil; if Brazil: at war with Argentina, in faction with Uruguay; if Argentina: at war with Brazil, in faction with Uruguay.

**Strategy:** Play Argentina historical, go fascist, take Establishing a Foothold to get war goal on Uruguay, defeat and puppet them. At 60% fascism, continue focus tree and create Argentina First faction or join Axis. Take The Old Enemy focus for war goal on Brazil. Declare war to unlock.

## America Decolonized!

**Condition:** Playing as Chile; has released Guaraní Nation, Inca, Miskito, Maya, United Inuit States, Charrua, Itza, Nahua, Isthmo-Amerindian State; has reorganized Bolivia, Brazil, Mexico, Guianas, Canada, United States.
