This DLC group contains 19 achievements spanning multiple nations (Australia, New Zealand, Siam, Indonesia, UK) with a mix of difficulty levels (VE, E, M, H). Many focus on territorial conquest and specific military requirements (capturing generals/admirals, sinking specific ships, deploying heavy tanks).

## Et tu, Paulus?

Capture a field marshal.

**Condition:** Has captured an enemy field marshal.

**Strategy note:** On historical, Finland deploys general Aksel Airo in mid '37 and will eventually promote him to field marshal while he remains deployed. Justify on them as the Soviets at game start and encircle the HQ immediately; keep him encircled until around mid-1938 when Finland will promote him despite being surrounded, then capture him. Switzerland also deploys a field marshal that remains in the field through 1945.

## Hunt for the Desert Fox

Capture Erwin Rommel.

**Condition:** Has captured "Erwin Rommel".

**Strategy note:** Best done as Austria or Czechoslovakia, making yourself Germany's single focus and making Rommel easier to locate. Alternatively, play as Soviet Union on historical, justify on Poland and Germany immediately, then rush them down.

## The 7th State

As Australia, own and fully control New Zealand.

**Condition:** Playing as Australia. Owns and controls: Otago (1081), Canterbury (723), Marlborough (1080), Wellington (284), and Auckland (1079).

**Strategy note:** Follow the Democratic path and let Labour win elections 3 times, then take the focus "Reclaim the Lost Territory" (requires DLC).

## The rightside up

As Australia, own and fully control Jan Mayen.

**Condition:** Playing as Australia. Owns and controls Jan Mayen (914).

**Strategy note:** Follow the Fascist path with P.R. Stephensen and join the Axis via "Nauru's Return to German Hands". Justify and declare on Norway with Germany's help before WT exceeds 25%; Norway will have no guarantees. Take Jan Mayen in the peace deal.

## A succulent Chinese Meal

As Australia annex and own one province in China.

**Condition:** Playing as Australia. Owns and controls any core state of China.

**Strategy note:** Follow the Fascist path with P.R. Stephensen and join the Axis via "Nauru's Return to German Hands", then justify on China and help Japan capitulate the Chinese United Front. Alternatively, play Democratic and annex a Chinese state Japan or Manchukuo controlled when the Japanese faction loses.

## Java-Script

As the Dutch East Indies (or Indonesia), research the Advanced Computing Machine and fully upgrade the Cryptology Department (while your capital is on the island of Java).

**Condition:** Playing as Dutch East Indies. Has researched Advanced Computing Machine. Has completed all three Agency Upgrade Machine-Assisted Decryption levels. Capital is West Java (335), Batavia (1058), or East Java (1051).

## Maphilinda

As Indonesia or the Philippines, form Maphilindo and control all Indian core states.

**Condition:** Playing as Dutch East Indies or Philippines. Either: Has global flag PHI_maphilindo_flag OR has cosmetic tag INS_maphilindo. Owns and controls: Southern Madras (423), Northern Madras (424), Mysore (425), Orissa (426), Hyderabad (427), Gujarat (428), Bombay (429), East Bengal (430), West Bengal (431), Assam (432), Rajahsthan (433), Arunachal Pradesh (434), Bihar (435), Central Provinces (436), Central India (437), United Provinces (438), Delhi (439), Bahawalpur (989), Manipur (990), Gwalior (991).

## Waltzing Matilda

Deploy 25 Heavy Tank Battalions as Australia.

**Condition:** Playing as Australia. More than 24 Heavy Armor battalions deployed.

**Strategy note:** At game start, hire a Chief of Army with political power. Use the army XP to design a basic inter-war heavy tank and add 4 heavy tank battalions to your starting armored division template. Convert all 7 starting divisions to this template. Divisions do not need to be fully equipped for the achievement to trigger.

## Old Zealand

As New Zealand, control and own the states of Brabant (Zeeland) and Sjaelland (Zealand).

**Condition:** Playing as New Zealand. Owns and controls: Brabant (35) and Sjaelland (37).

## Hail to the King

As Siam, form Greater Thailand before King Ananda is 15 years old.

**Condition:** Playing as Siam. Has completed focus "The Greater Thai Empire". King Ananda is less than 15 years old.

## ThaiTanic

As any nation, sink Siam's pride of the fleet.

**Condition:** Not playing as Siam. Sank the Pride of the fleet of Siam.

**Strategy note:** Easy as UK or USA. Wait until Siam joins Japan attacking the Allies, then put your larger navy on patrol missions around Siam to catch and kill the heavy cruiser HTMS Ratanakosindra.

## Thai fighters

As Siam, have 10 aces.

**Condition:** Playing as Siam. Has more than 9 Aces.

**Strategy note:** Join the Japanese faction and fight through India and the Middle East to Europe. Get airbase access from Germany and send 10-15 fighter wings to contest the English Channel.

## From Rags to Admiral Stripes

Promote a Ship Captain to Admiral.

**Condition:** Promoted a ship captain to admiral.

**Strategy note:** Can be achieved in minutes from game start. Choose a country with a large navy such as UK and promote an admiral after building up command power.

## The Man of Medan

Sink 100 convoys as Indonesia.

**Condition:** Playing as Dutch East Indies. Has destroyed more than 99 convoys.

**Strategy note:** Stay loyalist and focus on building Dockyards and a submarine fleet. Once Germany invades the Netherlands, send subs to convoy raid in the Mediterranean where they will slowly rack up kills.

## Noone Will Notice the Difference

As Indonesia, station Gustav Adolf Ilgen in Stockholm.

**Condition:** Playing as Dutch East Indies. The Army HQ of Gustav Adolf Ilgen is in state Södermanland (141).

**Strategy note:** At game start, obtain military access from Sweden by improving relations. Build the HQ and send the commander over.

## ANZAC

As Australia or New Zealand, have an Army HQ in Greece and ensure that Greece doesn't capitulate until 1945.

**Condition:** Playing as Australia or New Zealand. After Jan 1st, 1945. Greece has not capitulated before Jan 1st, 1945. Any army leader is in Attica (47), Dodecaneso (164), Crete (182), Thrace (184), Epirus (185), Peloponnese (186), Aegean Islands (187), or Central Macedonia (731).

**Strategy note:** Best done as Australia on Democratic or Fascist dominion path. Defend Greece and keep your HQ there; Greece will kick your divisions out after war ends but the achievement fires anyway. If Axis surrenders before 1945, annex the Dodecanese from Italy and station HQ there, or justify on Greece post-war and get the HQ into a Greek state before they capitulate.

## A Bridge too Far

As the UK, liberate Brabant, and have Bernard Montgomery liberate Friesland while maintaining a force in Arnhem.

**Condition:** Playing as UK. Control of Friesland (36) changes while UK or ally controls Brabant (35) and Bernard Montgomery leads an army or army group with a division stationed in Arnhem (9363).

**Strategy note:** Montgomery does not need to be deployed in an Army HQ.

## Saving Private Bryansk

Capture a General in the state of Bryansk.

**Condition:** Captured an enemy general in Bryansk (224).

**Strategy note:** As the Soviets, justify and declare on Poland at game start with a fallback line behind Bryansk. Wait until AI puts a general in or near Bryansk, then encircle and capture. Alternatively, retreat during Barbarossa or advance as Germany, but justifying on Poland is faster.

## Grand Siam

As Siam, capitulate 4 nations within one year.

**Condition:** Playing as Siam. Has capitulated 4 nations within 1 year.

**Strategy note:** Easiest after completing "Hail to the King". Release, justify on, and conquer Laos, Cambodia, Champa, and Champasak. Alternatively, after conquering French Indochina, build up for Japan to declare war, submit and join their faction, then capitulate Yunnan, Guangxi, Guangdong, and British Burma.
