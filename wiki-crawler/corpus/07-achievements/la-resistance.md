Summary: La Résistance achievements center on espionage, national focus tree completions, and controlling key territories. These achievements require mastery of the spy agency system, fascist/communist conversions, and military conquest across Europe and Asia.

## BFFs

**Condition:** Playing as Portugal. In faction with United Kingdom.

Strategy: Go democratic and wait for world tension to rise. If La Résistance is enabled, you can rush towards Join the Allies focus as well.

## Go Ahead, Macau My Day

**Condition:** Playing as Portugal. Any of the following countries: China, Communist China, Guangxi Clique, Yunnan, Shanxi, Xibei San Ma, Sinkiang is a subject of Portugal. Is a collaboration government. Macau (729) is a capital state. Own all core states of China and Manchukuo.

Strategy: Turn on Historical AI Focuses. Rush Restoration of the Monarchy and The Kingdom Reunited in the focus tree to get cores on Brazil. Build an army of ~48 divisions toward Spain. After Germany attacks Poland, justify a war goal on Spain and join the Axis. Once Germany defeats France, invade the UK. When UK capitulates, give Hong Kong and Guangzhouwan to your Chinese puppet. Justify war on Chinese factions and secure all Chinese core states under your Macau collaboration government.

## Play It, Sam

**Condition:** Intel Network Strength in Casablanca (461) is more than 50%.

Strategy: Establish an agency and set operatives on "Establish Spy Network" mission in Casablanca. The achievement triggers once network strength exceeds 50%. More operatives assigned to the same region speeds up network development.

## Spies Trade

**Condition:** Playing as British Raj. Is Spy Master. In a faction with United Kingdom.

Strategy: Increase autonomy towards Dominion. Create an intelligence agency and upgrade it three times with any upgrade. Once you become a Dominion, the "Become Spymaster" option appears next to the agency icon. AI will always accept.

## Why Die For Danzig?

**Condition:** Playing as Germany. France: Ruling party is Fascist. Is not at war with Germany. Controls Danzig (85). Germany has not declared war on France. Germany is not at war with France.

Strategy: Boost fascism in France from the start. Complete Danzig for Slovakia to gain control of Danzig without going to war with France. If France hasn't flipped by that point, start a fascist coup. The achievement fires when the coup succeeds. Note: declaring war on France invalidates the achievement; however, joining a war through an ally (e.g., if Italy declares war first) is permitted.

## I SEE EVERYTHING

**Condition:** Any major country: Cipher is not fully decrypted. Civilian intel greater than 89%. Army intel greater than 89%. Airforce intel greater than 89%. Navy Intel greater than 89%.

Strategy: Decryption must not be complete or cracked for the achievement to fire. If you select decryption and wait 30 days without completing it, the achievement will trigger once all intel categories reach 90%.

## Shaken, Not Stirred

**Condition:** Started a coup in any nation using an Operative with the seducer trait.

Strategy: Start as any non-aligned country and recruit a spy with the seducer trait. Dutch East Indies is the easiest target—high non-aligned popularity at start and won't build a spy agency early.

## Listen Very Carefully, I Shall Say This...

**Condition:** Have British and French Operatives complete an Operation together.

Strategy: At least one participating operative must be French and at least one must be British. Check occurs upon operation completion.

## Man of a Thousand Faces, Every One the Same

**Condition:** Have any operative get captured twice.

Strategy: Soviet Union has the highest operative capture rate. Expand operative slots and send them all to the Soviet Union. After one gets captured, rescue and redeploy to Soviet Union again. Once captured a second time, the achievement fires.

## Turing Complete!

**Condition:** Playing as United Kingdom. Germany is considered a Major Country. Has decrypted all major countries in faction with Germany.

Strategy: At game start, create your spy agency and unlock all cryptology tech. You only need to decrypt Germany as they are the only Axis major.

## One King, Two Crowns

**Condition:** Playing as Spain. Is Carlist Spain. Conquer all of France.

Strategy: Play on Patch 1.9 Husky for this strategy (1.9.1+ made it significantly harder). Fight the Spanish Civil War to establish Carlist Spain, then build 48 divisions toward France. Secure a naval invasion route from Bilbao and Spanish ports to French coastlines. After defeating France in the civil war, rush the focus tree to Claim the French Throne and declare war. Execute coordinated naval invasions to capture Paris, Calais, and other key French cities.

## We Will Rock You

**Condition:** Playing as Spain. Owns Gibraltar (118).

Strategy: Turn historical AI off. The United Kingdom may take the Revisit Colonial Policy branch containing the focus Withdraw from Contested Territories, which gives Gibraltar to Spain for free and makes it a core state. If this occurs during the civil war, Gibraltar may not transfer to player Spain until after winning the civil war.

## Well, I Didn't Vote for You

**Condition:** Playing as Spain. Is Regional Defense Council of Aragón. No other country with the original tag of Spain exists.

Strategy: Anarchist Spain is created from Masters of Our Own Fate focus or the Government Crackdown event. Focus on building military factories in Valencia at the start. If you control Valencia when the Anarchist uprising fires, you will be given control. Easier if Nationalist Spain is weakened by the Carlist rebellion. Keep control of northern states (Asturias and País Vasco) as long as possible to weaken Nationalists.

## Our Chief Weapon is Surprise...

**Condition:** Playing as Spain. Started more than 4 coups (requirement is 5+ coups total).

Strategy: Join a major faction with many members to gain spy slots. Do not join the faction's wars so you can trade with all countries and build spy networks faster. Stage coups against non-faction nations.

## Die, Perfidious Albion!

**Condition:** Playing as France. Ruling party is Fascist. Controls all European cores of United Kingdom and Ireland.

Strategy: Rush the fascist focuses. Upon taking Utilize the Leagues, boost fascist and non-aligned popularity. Hire the fascist demagogue and spam "Rally the Leagues" decision. Avoid "Unleash La Cagoule." Once 50% combined popularity is reached, continue toward Expand to the Suez. Take Political Unity focus immediately to prevent civil war. Declare war on Britain and annex them—your starting army is strong enough for conquest.

## Second Time's the Charm

**Condition:** Playing as France. Controls Moscow (219). Napoléon VI is the current country leader.

Strategy: Rush toward Proclaim the Third Empire focus to get Napoléon VI. Wait until Germany declares war on Poland, then declare on the United Kingdom. Accept invitation to join Germany's faction. Your starting army and fleet are sufficient to destroy the UK. Guarantee Finland when USSR justifies war on them. Accept the call to arms and invite the rest of the Axis to prevent the "Great Patriotic War" buff for the Soviet Union. Secure enough war score to ask allies for control of Moscow.

## Don't Die for Your Country

**Condition:** Playing as Germany in a 1939 start. Controls all European cores of Poland and France. Has suffered fewer than 475 casualties in the current wars. Has not paradropped.

Strategy: Paradrops set the country flag "has_paradropped_flag" which prevents this achievement. Avoid all paratroop operations. Use blitzkrieg tactics with swift encirclements in Poland and France to minimize casualties while securing all core territories.

## Warszawo, Walcz!

**Condition:** Any country is at war with the player and Poland. Has successfully staged the Warsaw uprising.

Strategy: Start as Soviet Union, justify and declare war on Poland, and call Tannu Tuva into the war. After capitulating Poland, give them all Polish states and kick from faction. Justify war on them but do not capitulate them. Since they lack means to suppress resistance properly, wait until resistance in Poland hits 90%.

## Tour de France

**Condition:** Not playing as France. Controls all French states in Europe. Has more than 2200 infantry equipment in armies. Has more than 220 support equipment in armies. Has more than 22 bicycle battalions.

Strategy: Mainland France includes Corsica (1). Occupy all French states while fielding at least 20 fully equipped bicycle regiments. Equip your armies with sufficient infantry and support equipment.

## You Will Never Kill Me Alive

**Condition:** Have an operative die with the Suicide Pills agency upgrade active.

Strategy: Build an intelligence agency and acquire the Suicide Pills upgrade. Keep sending operatives on missions or assign them to "Build Intel Network" activities (preferably in the Soviet Union). Eventually one will die and the achievement will trigger.
