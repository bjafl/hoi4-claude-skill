## Summary

Hearts of Iron IV features 293 achievements across 17 DLC expansion groups, all playable in single-player Ironman mode (regular difficulty or higher, starting in 1936 unless noted otherwise). This index provides a quick reference for all achievements organized by DLC, with the exact unlock condition for each.
