## Summary

The X Anniversary Update introduces one achievement focused on Germany. This achievement requires dominating Europe while managing surrender progress, combining territorial conquest with a unique mechanical condition.

## Steiner

**Playing as:** Germany
**Condition:** After 1941.06.22, has had more than 30% surrender progress while at war with Soviet Union, France and United Kingdom, has not capitulated, and controls Moscow (219), Ile de France (16), and Greater London Area (126).
**Strategy:** Win the war without peacing out. Wait until after 1941.06.22. Declare war on Switzerland and let them occupy you to 31% (surrendering to a minor power to accumulate surrender progress). You only need to have had more than 30% surrender progress after 1941.06.22 once, and it does not need to coincide with being at war with all three major powers.
