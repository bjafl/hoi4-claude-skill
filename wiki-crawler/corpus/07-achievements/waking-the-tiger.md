## Summary

Waking the Tiger achievements cover playing as China, Chinese warlords, Manchukuo, Japan, and additional countries (Germany, Australia, Colombia, Greece). Conditions range from simple territorial conquest to complex multi-step campaigns like annex Mexico then Europe before 1945, or breaking free from Japan as Manchukuo to annex the former overlord.

## Awake and Angry

**Condition:** Playing as China. Japan forced to sue for peace after losing all of Manchuria and Korea. Owns all core states of China and Manchukuo except Kouang-Tchéou-Wan (728), Macau (729), and Hong Kong (326).

**Note:** These three provinces do not need to be conquered, allowing achievement without war against the Allies.

## Romance of the Three Kingdoms

**Condition:** Playing as China. Integrated Guanxi Clique through making them a puppet. Integrated Yunnan through making them a puppet.

## Sun Tzu Reborn

**Condition:** Playing as any Chinese nation (China, Chinese Soviet Republic, Guangxi Clique, Yunnan, Shanxi, Xibei San Ma, Qinghai Ma, Sinkiang, Khotan Ma, Ningxia Ma, Gansu Ma, Sichuan Clique, Guangdong Clique, Northeastern Army, Shandong Clique, Hebei-Chahar, Reorganized National Government, Kumul Khanate). Any army leader is a field marshal with skill level 9 or higher.

## The People Have Stood Up!

**Condition:** Playing as Chinese Soviet Republic. Mao Zedong is the current country leader. Owns all of: Fujian (595), Zhejiang (596), Shandong (597), Jiangsu (598), Jiangxi (600), Kham (601), Hunan (602), Guizhou (603), Sichuan (605), Anhui (606), Henan (607), Beiping (608), Nanjing (1035), Hebei (614), Hubei (620), Qingdao (743), Xian (744), Zunyi (748), Huangshan (749), Changde (750), Liangshan (751), Ganzi (752), Gannan (753).

## Made in China

**Condition:** Playing as Chinese Soviet Republic. At least 101 military factories.

**Note:** Off-map military factories do not count.

## Tojo Shot First

**Condition:** Playing as Japan. United States has NOT completed Nuclear Fission. Dropped a nuclear bomb on a core state of the US.

## Sunrise Invasion

**Condition:** Playing as Japan. Before 1 January 1945. Annexed Mexico before taking any territory in Europe. Owns a coastal state in Europe.

**Strategy:** Attack Mexico early before the US gets too powerful. Justify against Mexico (generates lower world tension than justifying on the US directly). Build marines, transports, and prepare naval invasions to the Philippines and from Marshall Islands to Honolulu, then from Honolulu to Los Angeles. Early execution (before end of 1936) finds light resistance. Plan for heavy convoy use and assign task forces to convoy escort. Naval invade Philippines and Honolulu first, then Los Angeles. Capture Philippine land and make it capitulate. Once in Los Angeles, consolidate forces there and grind down the US (they won't capitulate until Pennsylvania and Maryland are taken). Annex Mexico before the US capitulates. After defeating the US, attack China if time permits before Germany invades Poland, otherwise prepare for war against the Allies and naval invade Britain from Canada.

## The Dragon Swallowed the Sun

**Condition:** Playing as Manchukuo. Annexed Japan.

**Strategy:** Requires breaking free from Japan (via the Independence War focus) and then annexing them militarily. After independence, research marines, naval transports, and submarines. Build submarines to exceed the Japanese fleet strength on the Sea of Japan. Increase relations with the US and take the Train Marines focus. When Japan goes to war with the Allies (do not join their faction), naval invade Japan via Korean ports near Hiroshima or north of Osaka. Capture key ports and upgrade them for troop throughput. Tokyo and territories west may be sufficient to force capitulation. If Japan gets puppeted by the Allies or Soviet, pre-emptively puppet and annex them via the autonomy system. Alternatively, on historical mode, build up industry and paratroopers, trigger Independence War when Japan begins Strike the Southern Road, deploy armies to Korea/China, use paratroopers on Nagasaki and Honshu bridgeheads, then ship main forces from Korea to conquer the home islands while Japan is distracted with China and the Philippines.

## Hail to the Qing

**Condition:** Playing as Manchukuo. Has completed focus Claim the Mandate of Heaven.

## The Good, the Bad and the Weird

**Condition:** Playing as Manchukuo. At least 5 infrastructure in each of: Heilungkiang (714), Liaobei (715), Jehol (610), Kirin (328), Sungkiang (717). At least 16 Oil.

**Note:** All required oil can be generated by Heilungkiang after researching Excavation IV and completing both Prospect for Resources decisions.

## Battlecry

**Condition:** Playing as a warlord (Guangxi Clique, Yunnan, Shanxi, Xibei San Ma, Qinghai Ma, Sinkiang, Khotan Ma, Ningxia Ma, Gansu Ma, Sichuan Clique, Guangdong Clique, Northeastern Army, Shandong Clique, Hebei-Chahar, Kumul Khanate). Owns all core states of China, Manchukuo, and Japan except Kouang-Tchéou-Wan (728), Macau (729), and Hong Kong (326).

## Panda-monium

**Condition:** Playing as a warlord (Guangxi Clique, Yunnan, Shanxi, Xibei San Ma, Qinghai Ma, Sinkiang, Guangdong Clique, Shandong Clique, Sichuan Clique, Ningxia Ma, Gansu Ma, Northeastern Army, Khotan Ma, Hebei-Chahar). At war simultaneously with: Guangxi Clique, Yunnan, Shanxi, Xibei San Ma, Sinkiang, China, Chinese Soviet Republic.

**Strategy:** Play as Sinkiang, join the Comintern, and justify/declare war on a warlord once the Chinese United Front is formed.

## Make a Man Out Of You

**Condition:** Playing as a warlord (Guangxi Clique, Yunnan, Shanxi, Xibei San Ma, Qinghai Ma, Sinkiang, Guangdong Clique, Shandong Clique, Sichuan Clique, Ningxia Ma, Gansu Ma, Northeastern Army, Khotan Ma, Hebei-Chahar). At least 1,000,000 manpower in deployment queue.

**Strategy:** Start as Guangxi Clique, reach 5 Army experience. Create 1-width divisions and rush-train as many as possible with highest recruitment priority. Deploy once they reach minimum training level, then switch to normal template. Note: Can only train 75% of field manpower with 100K minimum, so need at least 1,333,334 total manpower on field.

## Monarchy is Back in Fashion

**Condition:** Playing as Germany. Wilhelm II is the current country leader. Italy has Vittorio Emanuele III as the current country leader.

**Strategy:** Requires Götterdämmerung to be disabled. Follow the Return of the Kaiser and Focus on the True Enemy paths in the Oppose Hitler branch until reaching the Assassinate Mussolini focus (AI always agrees). Wilhelm II must remain your leader throughout and cannot die before focus completion.

## Our Other Place in the Sun

**Condition:** Playing as Germany. Completed focus Our Place in the Sun (or if Götterdämmerung enabled, still requires this focus). Conquered an island in the Caribbean.

**Note:** Requires Germany to follow the Return of the Kaiser branch. Curaçao from the Netherlands does not count as it is part of South America.

## Australia-Hungary

**Condition:** Playing as Australia. Owns all core territory of Hungary.

## My Ships Don't Lie

**Condition:** Playing as Colombia. Took the decision to form Gran Colombia. At least 10 Battleships. At least 10 Carriers.

## I Swear I'm Not a Byzantophile

**Condition:** Playing as Greece. Formed Byzantine Empire.

**Strategy:** Easiest via the Byzantine path in the Greek focus tree introduced in Battle for the Bosporus (see Istanbul is Constantinople. Again.).
