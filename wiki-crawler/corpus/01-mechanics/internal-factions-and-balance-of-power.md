## Summary

Two separate systems model domestic power struggles. **Internal factions** are scripted organizations (Congress, the Blackshirts, the Imperial Army, the Ustaša …) that a specific country must appease, fight or integrate, mostly through focuses, decisions and events. **Balance of power (BoP)** is a numeric −100%…+100% slider with a left side and a right side; each range on the slider grants its own modifier set, and only six countries have one. Both are DLC-gated country content.

## Internal factions

An internal faction is an organization pushing its own agenda, usually against the government. Some advisors and generals belong to a faction and are only hireable while that faction is friendly. Factions can influence the country through national spirits, and some can take power outright.

## Balance of power

- A country can have **one balance of power at a time**, with a **left side** and a **right side**; the scale runs **−100% to +100%**.
- Each side is divided into ranges; every range carries its own modifier block and can have activation effects (events, leader changes, national-spirit swaps).
- Each BoP has one **middle range** belonging to neither side.
- A BoP can only be replaced by another BoP **on the same side** — e.g. the right-side Mussolini BoP can be swapped for the right-side Italo Balbo BoP, but never for the left-side Communist one.
- BoP value is moved by national focuses, events and decisions (each country has its own decision set).

Six countries have a balance of power:

### Finland — Public Trust (requires Arms Against Tyranny)

Left = Low Public Trust, right = High Public Trust.

| Range | Name |
|---|---|
| −100% to −60% | Very Low Trust |
| −60% to −10% | Low Trust |
| −10% to 10% | Medium Trust |
| 10% to 60% | High Trust |
| 60% to 100% | Very High Trust |

Public Trust is tied to the current Finnish leader. The effects of Trust itself are constant, but **the lower the Trust, the cheaper it is to retire the sitting leader**. Every leader has a Personal Agenda trait defining which player actions raise or lower Trust (see the Personal Agenda trait table in [Politics, ideology and government](politics-ideology-government.md)). The default succession is Pehr Evind Svinhufvud → Kyösti Kallio → Risto Ryti → Juho Kusti Paasikivi; if Finland is at war with a major power, Carl G. Mannerheim can be installed.
